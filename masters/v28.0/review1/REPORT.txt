The three worst faults across all fifteen pictures
1. The man in white's robe is blown out to a flat white blob with no folds, and from above he reads as a white lump, not a person. Glaring, and it is in nearly every shot he is in: phone 3.50, 11.80, 14.00, 15.40, 16.50 and 25.50, and desktop 7.00, 11.80 and 25.50.
2. The red serpent itself has a crumpled, faceted surface and no readable head or face, with see-through copies stacked round its outline and torn shard edges. Glaring where it fills the screen as a smeared red mass (phone 4.20 and 5.80), and noticeable in phone 11.80, 14.00 and 25.50 and desktop 4.20 and 7.00.
3. In phone 22.00, two-thirds of the frame is a blank wall of mismatched flat panels with the two men crammed into the bottom-right corner. Glaring. On desktop the same empty plain wall takes a third to almost half the screen in 4.20, 7.00 and 25.50.

Close behind those three: a thin, straight white line hangs across the doorway at knee height in phone 8.50 to 25.50 and desktop 11.80 and 25.50; the staff is never gripped and floats; and the purple light bars pass through the grey figures' bodies.
