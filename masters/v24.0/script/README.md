## The fairness read (Chad's standing rule: no practice and no practitioner is ever the villain)

Every line of the master, Pong and Pa Noi was read against the rule.

- **The master** greets, asks about the shop with interest, praises it as merit and a living at once, reads kindly,
  advises merit and a calm mind as well as offering help, tells him to choose whichever piece he likes and not to be
  shy (CP13, L5-1: ไม่ต้องเกรงใจ — the piece is a gift, and play does ask him to take one), accepts the
  offering with anumodana, says he may close his eyes, blesses, and wishes him well. When he is asked about the figure
  (B) he answers sincerely and offers to teach; when the player bolts (C) he says it is all right and to go carefully;
  when the player leaves early (D) he is unoffended. Nothing he says threatens, mocks, pushes a sale or does harm.
- **Pong** is eager and sincere: proud of a master he has never met, a dealer who hears the business part loudest. His
  shorter translation of the reading drops the merit and the calm mind — not hiding, just his own ear; the player may
  decide otherwise (the ledger's MEANING), which is the lesson. In C he defends the master ("He is a good man!"); in D he
  admits he never asked who the teacher was either.
- **Pa Noi** is hospitality from first to last: water, the tray, mangoes, the card, an umbrella.
- **The takhian tree** is named by Pong as a folk custom, respectfully ("Don't point, ah"), and never commented on.
- **The figure** is never explained by anyone. The master calls her "that one" (เขา — no gender, no kind), and Pong
  keeps it so.

## The silence plan (the v21.30 rule: no stretch of 1.5 s or more without a voice unless a sound with a reason holds it)

- **The film**: the shutter's rumble, the LEDs, the pen, the phone, then the theme over the rice country (O5 has no
  voice by design: the music holds it), the diesel and the drops, gravel and thunder.
- **The yard**: no score, by design — the luk thung radio from the stilt house, the TV soap, wind in the palms, frogs,
  thunder, the dog; Pa Noi's calls punctuate the waiting.
- **The room**: no score — rain on zinc, the wall fan's click, the tube's hum, the fridge; the chant itself holds the
  work; in the dark the rain's roar, the thunder and the murmured chant hold WHAT CHANGED?.
- **The hush**: one second of nothing (deliberate, the decision's moment), then the roof ticking, drips, the frogs.
- **Endings**: A — the pao's three breaths, the drips, the frogs, Pong's chatter on the walk, the diesel; B — the long
  look is held by the rain's last ticks and the fan, the pickup by Pong's excitement; C — his heartbeat and breathing,
  the cup, the dog's bark, the wipers; D — the frogs and the dusk paddy, Pong's thought at the shelter. Each ending's
  music cue enters under its last third. Checked against estimates at CP9 and against the takes at CP12.
