player of episode 1 can see. Chad approved the plan
(docs/V7.0-EPISODE2-PLAN.md) on 9 Sep 2026 — "can you begin building

## What it is

Until now a chapter was a walk to ONE object, a decision, four scenes. The
kit is everything a chapter may ask of the engine BETWEEN its film and its
decision, so episode 2 can be the day and the night before the choice:
fall in, make the bed against a clock, talk to the bunk, lie down, hear the
shower, hold the blanket, dig the scrape, take the stag.

Every seam is OPTIONAL and DECLARED. Chapters 1–5 declare none of it and
run exactly the path they always ran — that is the law since v4.0 and it
is what the full suite proves. The verbs reach a chapter as `ctx.kit`
inside `build()` and `api.kit` inside a scene.

| seam | how a chapter uses it |
|---|---|
| **Hotspots** | `stage.hotspots = [{ id, pos, radius, prompt, onInteract(), once, enabled() }]` — the badge names the nearest one in reach and in view; E, or a tap on the badge, fires it. The pile is untouched and still comes first. |
| **Objective, timer, waypoint** | `kit.objective(text)`, `kit.timer(secs, onEnd)` → `{ stop, left }`, `kit.waypoint({x,y,z})` — a HUD line top-centre, a countdown beside it, a diamond projected in the world (clamped to the edge when off-screen, gone when behind). |
| **Events** | `kit.event({ kind, ... })` → a promise of `{ ok, score, hits, misses, t, early, skipped, aborted, delta }`. Kinds: `tap`, `timed` (a window that opens later — fire on the order), `mash` (a bar that decays; press fast), `hold` (keep pressing; release or drift fails it), `stabilise` (hold; the score is how steady), `heartbeat` (tap on the beat, n beats), `focus` (put the tap or the reticle on a light before it fades, n targets, world or screen positions), `sequence` (items shown one after another, faster and faster — Chad's standby bed; an item is `{ label, icon }`, the icon a canvas element or a hosted asset key). `award: { stat, lo, hi }` moves Sanity or Awareness by the score — never Wisdom, the trial game's rule. |
| **Torch** | `torch: { on, angle, color, red, intensity, distance }` in DATA; F or the HUD button toggles; `kit.torchOn(bool)`, `kit.torchRed(bool)`. A spotlight parented to the camera, no shadow pass. |
| **Presence** | `kit.presence(0..1)` — a chapter with `ghost: null` drains sanity through the same bar; the banner says the chapter's `words.presence` ("Something is here.") unless HER drain is showing it. Her loops and whisper stay hers. |
| **Conduct** | `kit.conduct({ s, a, note })` — banked in play, capped at ±10 per stat, applied with the choice and shown as one line under the stat rows ("In the field: You turned the light on. (+3 Awareness)"). `kit.award(stat, delta)` for an immediate stat move with the drain's own tick. |
| **Pose** | `kit.pose('lying' \| 'standing', { y, yaw, span, pitchLo, pitchHi, secs })` — the eye sinks to the pillow, the neck narrows, the yaw is clamped to a span, walking stops. |
| **Daylight** | `kit.daylight(preset, secs)` — the sky, fog, lights, stars, sun, clouds and the viewmodel's rig tweened in play; `applyDaylight()` is now a wrapper over `applyDaylightD(d)`, the body the tween drives. |
| **Decision clock** | `kit.decisionClock(secs, onExpire)` before the decision opens — a bar in the panel head; on expiry the chapter's callback, or −8 sanity by default, and the panel stays open. |
| **Phase, choices** | `kit.setPhase(v)` / `kit.getPhase()` — a bookmark saved with the run; `kit.choices()` — the letter picked per chapter this run. |
| **Haptics** | `kit.haptic(pattern)` — `navigator.vibrate` behind one function. |

**Inside a film**: `api.event(at, opts, onDone)` starts one at `at` while the
film runs on; `api.eventWait(at, opts, onDone)` PAUSES the film until it
resolves. While an event is live, Space / Enter / E belong to it, not to
the skip; the Skip button still ends the film, and a skip resolves the
event as skipped.

**On the phone**: a `tap`/`timed` event is a button at the bottom-centre;
a touch counts only on the button, a mouse anywhere (under pointer lock it
lands on the canvas). Every other kind takes the whole screen, which is
what suspends the stick and the look for its duration. Drift for `hold` is
the finger's movement plus the look's radians.

**Saves**: `worldState()` gains `phase`, `conduct` and `choices`; a save
from before v7.0 loads with all three empty; a bad one is capped, typed
and cleaned (`statetest` 7c).

## The one rule the code is shaped by

A verb MUTATES kit state; the FRAME (`kitFrame`, from `tick`) is the only
thing that touches the DOM. The kit block sits before `CHCTX` because
`build()` runs during module init — before `$`, `ui` or `state` exist —
and a chapter must be able to call `kit.objective()` from inside it. The
fixture does exactly that, and `fixturetest` checks the HUD got it.

Two boot paths, and the second was the bug found first: `restart()` and
`setChapter()` run `kitReset()`, which applies a chapter's declarations,
but a FRESH boot reaches play through neither — so the booting chapter's
torch is applied in `kitInit()`, on the first frame.

## Verified

`fixturetest` grew from 13 promises to 48: the objective set from
build(); the phase saved; a hotspot named by the badge, fired by
`interactNow`, and the badge restored away from it; the torch declared,
toggled by F; the presence drain and its banner rule; every one of the
eight event kinds, passing and failing (tap, tap timing out, timed early,
timed on the order, mash, mash decaying to nothing, hold, hold released,
stabilise, heartbeat, focus, sequence); an event opened from a hotspot
awarding +6 awareness; the pose lying and standing, walking stopped; a
daylight tween landing on its fog; the timer firing and its HUD hiding; the
waypoint shown; the decision clock running, costing 8, and gone on
dismiss; the conduct banked at the switch reaching the card. `statetest`
gained the three save fields. `chaptertest` learned `torch` and
`words.presence`, and both it and `textsync` and `build.py` now find
chapters in subfolders. The full 23-harness suite ran green on the build.

Three harness races were mine, not the engine's, and are written down in
LEARNINGS: an event that resolves on its first frame is never seen
"started"; a heartbeat window narrower than a frame cannot be hit on a box
that steps 0.05 s; a focus dot must be placed before it can be tapped
(the engine now places it on the frame it starts).

## Also in this release — the episode plumbing

- `build.py`, `textsync` and `chaptertest` discover chapters in
  SUBFOLDERS: episode 2 goes in `src/chapters/e2/`, keys stay the file's
  stem (`e2c1`).
- **The single-file build carries episode 1 only.** It inlines everything
  it carries; ten episodes would stop it opening on a phone. `dist/` ships
  every chapter as before.
- The per-episode sound-pack tier (EPISODES-PLAN §8) is NOT built yet:
  there is nothing to compute until two episode-2 chapters share a sound.
  It lands with the first that do (v7.3, the range and the bunk).
