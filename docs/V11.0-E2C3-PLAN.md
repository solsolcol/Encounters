## 1 · The chapter, in the source's words

S1·05, the first half (docs/source/site-case-files.md):

> During infantry training we had an outfield exercise deep in the jungle.
> That night I was at the very rear of my section, facing away from the
> others and looking out into the dark.
>
> Then I felt something strange. Something was pressing against my leg.
> Not just a sensation — there was actual weight to it. I shone my torch
> straight down. Nothing. I could not see anyone, could not hear
> footsteps, and there was not even the sound of vegetation moving around
> me.
>
> But the pressure was still there. That alone was strange enough. I
> swung the torch towards it, and as the beam passed across, whatever was
> there was gone. The jungle was silent again.

The site's own film shows what the prose does not: **a pale, grey-skinned
soldier in an old uniform crouched against a tree beside him, one hand on
his leg**, then gone when the beam swings (docs/source/video-transcripts.md).
The other platoon and the bicycle are the SAME night in the source; they
are chapter 4's, and he does not know about them yet.

The lesson (S1·05): *Sometimes you feel something before you see anything
at all ... none of that proves on its own that something spiritual is
present. Always consider the ordinary explanations first. In an unfamiliar
place, especially at night, stay observant without letting curiosity or
fear take the wheel.*

## 2 · The place

**Secondary jungle at night.** The first chapter of the episode not on
Tekong's camp, and the first in the game with no walls.

- **The track** — 60 m of trodden path winding in from the drop-off,
  1.5 m wide, roots and leaf litter, the tree kit (v6.15, all four
  kinds) planted dense on both sides with undergrowth cards between the
  trunks, so the walkable world is the track and nothing else. Blockers
  are the tree line itself (a corridor of invisible boxes 2 m off the
  path, walktest-proved).
- **The harbour** — a clearing 12 m across at the track's end, six
  **shell scrapes** in a ring (a 1.8 × 0.6 m trench 0.4 deep, spoil on
  the outer lip), packs and rifles on the lips. HIS scrape is the rear
  one, the one facing back down into the dark AWAY from the track — the
  source's "very rear of my section, facing away from the others".
- **The dark** — fog near 3 m / far 20 m at night, a black sky, no moon.
  The torch (the kit's v7.0 declaration, `torch: { on: true, red: true,
  angle, distance }`) is the only light the player carries; the harbour
  has two red chemlights on the commander's scrape and nothing else. **The
  torch is what you see by.**
- **The tonner** — a pocket 90 m off for the film only: a stretch of
  laterite road through the same trees at last light (an EVENING daylight
  in the pocket, the world's night outside it), the truck's tail-gate and
  bench, the section opposite.

Daylight declaration: the harbour at NIGHT for play (near-black, a cold
blue hemisphere at 0.08, no key, no sun), the film's pocket painted for
dusk. The kit's `daylight` tween brings the last light down over the walk
in (§4, phase 1).

## 4 · Play — the night, in phases

Every phase is a saved `phase` (v7.3's law) and every beat has a
receipt. The player's stat surface is the kit's: objective, waypoint,
timer where named, the torch, presence, events.

### 1 · `harbour` — TAKE YOUR SCRAPE (~40 s)
Objective: *"Move to the rear scrape — red light only."* Waypoint on his
scrape. The torch starts RED. **White light is a mistake the chapter
answers**: hold white for more than 1.5 s and the commander's whisper
comes across the harbour (**s3white**: *"WHITE LIGHT. Off."*), −3
Awareness conduct, once per 20 s. Red light is dim and this is the
lesson of the torch before the lesson of the leg: you can see less than
you want to, and that is the correct amount.
The buddy is at the next scrape (**b3here**, on approach: *"Eh, here. You
take the back one."*). Reaching the scrape's zone (the v8.7 disc, red)
turns the lens OUT into the dark over 0.55 s — the way the bed zone
turned him to the balcony — and begins phase 2.

### 2 · `dig` — DIG IN (mash, 10 s)
A brief panel first (v8.7's law): *"Dig your shell scrape. Press as fast
as you can. Stop and the spoil slides back."* Then **DIG**, the kit's
`mash`: the bar decays, the press fills it, the entrenching tool
(`digtool` ×N) and the buddy's digs a beat off his (**b3dig**: *"Faster
lah, sergeant coming."*). Award Awareness `lo −4 / hi +6` on the graded
ladder (v9.3). The scrape deepens with the bar (the trench mesh sinks in
three steps). On completion the camera settles to the kneel height
(0.95 m) in the scrape — the chapter's play pose from here — and the
rifle prop lies on the lip in front of him (the `rifle.glb` viewmodel is
NOT in this chapter; it is a prop, never held — rifle mode is chapter
4's).

### 3 · `stag` — FIRST STAG (~70 s, three small beats)
Objective: *"Watch your arc."* Presence is OFF here; nothing drains. The
three beats are the ordinary night, so the extraordinary one has
something to be different from:
1. **The radio check** (`timed`, a 4 s window): the handset crackles
   (**r3check**, the sergeant's voice through a lowpass: *"Charlie Two,
   this is Charlie Zero, radio check, over."*). Press inside the window:
   **n3radio** (*"Charlie Zero, Charlie Two, okay, over."*), +2
   Awareness. Miss it and the commander's whisper comes instead
   (**s3wake**: *"Two. WAKE UP."*), −3.
2. **The micro-sleep** (`tap`): the screen dips twice over three seconds
   — the eyes closing — and a tap opens them; a slow tap costs −2
   Sanity. The buddy: **b3sleep** (*"Oi. Don't sleep."*).
3. **The branch** (`focus`, one target): a snap on the LEFT (`branchsnap`,
   panned), a flicker in the undergrowth 6 m out — put the torch on it
   before it fades (2.2 s). It is a branch. **n3branch** (*"...branch."*).
   +3 Awareness for catching it.

### 4 · `pressure` — THE PRESSURE
No warning and no sound: **the phone buzzes** (a 180 ms pulse, the kit's
haptics) and the camera's PITCH is nudged down 0.14 rad over 0.4 s by
the chapter (CHCTX carries `pitch` since v8.7) — the look down is a
reflex the game performs, because Master Z did not decide to look, he
looked. The torch is on his own boot and trouser. Nothing.
**n3press** (jamesTeen): *"Something was pressing against my leg. Not
just a feeling... there was weight to it."*
PRESENCE begins (`kit.presence`, the banner's words are the chapter's:
*"Something is pressing on your leg."*) and drains slowly — 0.6 a
second, a chapter-declared rate — for as long as the beam is off the leg.
Objective: **HOLD THE BEAM ON IT.**

**HOLD THE BEAM** — the kit's `stabilise`, 8 s: keep the reticle inside
a 6° cone on the leg while the screen tempts you — *LOOK UP* flashes at
the top edge at 2.5, 4.5 and 6.5 s and the reticle is pushed by a slow
drift he has to correct. The score is steadiness; award Sanity `lo −8 /
hi +6`. **And while he holds it, the film's image**: at the edge of the
beam, where red light stops being light, a shape resolves over 2.5 s — a
hand on the trouser, an arm, and against the tree beside him **a
crouched soldier, grey, in an old uniform, no expression**, the same
`fbosling` ghost as the man in the shower and the man at the flagpole,
parked on the kneel take, ghostified in the v9.6 paler look so it reads
at all in red light. It is in the player's peripheral vision by
construction: the stabilise cone is on the leg, and the figure is 30°
off it. A player who holds sees it out of the corner of the eye. A player
who LOOKS UP at it (the temptation) fails the hold — and sees it plainly
for the frame before it goes.
On the hold's end (pass or fail) the chapter SWINGS the beam itself
(yaw driven 0.6 s onto the tree): gone. The presence drain stops on that
frame. Silence — the jungle bed ducked to nothing for 2 s, the first
true silence in the chapter.
**n3gone** (jamesTeen): *"For a moment, I thought I saw something. Then
whatever was there disappeared."* — the source video's own words.
Then the decision opens by itself (as e2c1's fear does), the "pile" being
the tree at the beam's edge.

## 5 · The decision — the trial's HALLWAY set, ×3 as every chapter is

The trial's set is about light, darkness, other people and provocation,
and fits a jungle at 2 AM unchanged. The deltas are the trial's ×3
(docs/source/trial-game-chapters.md, "Chapter 2 · THE HALLWAY"):

| | choice | S | A | W | teaching (the trial's, verbatim) |
|---|---|--:|--:|--:|---|
| A | PUT THE TORCH ON IT BEFORE YOU MOVE | +9 | +18 | +21 | Courage does not require deliberately removing your own safety advantages. |
| B | REACH DOWN INTO THE DARK | −12 | +9 | −9 | Making observation harder is not bravery. |
| C | ASK YOUR BUDDY IF HE FELT IT | +6 | +21 | +24 | Independent context can narrow possibilities without forcing a conclusion. |
| D | "WHO'S THERE?" | −6 | +6 | −12 | Provocation and investigation are not the same thing. |

No critical mistake in this set (the trial marks none). The best wisdom
is C, and it is the source's own instinct: your experience and somebody
else's account are two different things, and the way to get the second
is to ask.

## 6 · The four scenes

Every scene begins in the scrape, kneeling, facing out. Hands hidden in
all four (the torch is the "hand"). Times are the shape, not the numbers.

**A · THE BEAM HELD (~22 s).** He does not move. The torch goes WHITE
(the one time the chapter allows it without the whisper — the commander
sees the beam and says nothing) and comes up off the boot slowly, the
way a hand that does not want to is made to, onto the tree. **The figure
is fully in it for one second**: old uniform, grey face, no expression,
looking not at him but at the ground where his leg was. Then only the
tree, the bark, an ant. The beam holds on the bark for four seconds
because he cannot make it move. **n3A1** (*"...nothing."*). Back to red.
The buddy, from the next scrape, low: **b3A** (*"Eh. What you shining
at?"*). **n3A2**: *"Nothing. Go back to your arc."* Card.

**B · THE HAND INTO THE DARK (~20 s).** The torch stays down. His hand
goes off the rifle and down past his own knee into the leaf litter beside
the leg — the camera tilts to follow it, red light on the back of his own
hand — into cold leaves, and the leaves are moving, **something drawing
back from his fingers** through the litter the way a thing does that was
there. `leafdraw` (a slow drag through dry leaves, panned away). The hand
comes back fast. He looks at it in the torch. **n3B1**: *"Cold. Something
was there. Something moved."* Presence banner for 3 s. **n3B2**: *"I
should have looked first."* Card.

**C · "FELT WHAT?" (~26 s).** He turns his head — not the body — to the
next scrape. **n3C1** (whispered): *"Eh. You feel that?"* The buddy
(`Idle_6` → a head-turn): **b3C1**: *"Feel what?"* A beat. **n3C2**:
*"...my leg. Something pressed on it."* The buddy looks down at his own
legs with his torch, then out. **b3C2**: *"Nothing here. Maybe root. Or
you fell asleep."* — and then, not laughing: **b3C3**: *"Just watch your
arc. I watch mine. Relief at four."* Two red torches facing out into the
same dark for the rest of the scene, the camera pulling up and back over
both scrapes: two men, two beams, the tree between them with nothing
against it. Card.

**D · "WHO'S THERE?" (~22 s).** His voice, not whispered, across the
harbour — **n3D1**: *"Who's there?"* — and the jungle answers with
silence, then the commander's hiss from the far scrape, **s3hiss**:
*"TWO. Shut up."*, and the buddy's, **b3D**: *"Siao ah?"*. The pressure
LIFTS on the frame his voice ends — exactly as if answered — and the
presence banner clears. He knows it, and that is worse. **n3D2**: *"It
stopped. Right when I asked."* The camera holds on the black in front of
him where the beam's edge was, and something in the undergrowth 8 m out
is moving away — leaf cards shifting in a line, no figure. Card.
