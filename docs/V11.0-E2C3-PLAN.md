shell scrape stuff, i will rather focus on the pressure experience, but of

## 1 · The chapter, in the source's words

S1·05, the first half (docs/source/site-case-files.md):

> During infantry training we had an outfield exercise deep in the jungle.
> That night I was at the very rear of my section, facing away from the
> others and looking out into the dark.
>
> Then I felt something strange. Something was pressing against my leg.
> Not just a sensation — there was actual weight to it. I shone my torch
> straight down. Nothing. I could not see anyone, could not hear
> footsteps, and there was not even the sound of vegetation moving around
> me.
>
> But the pressure was still there. That alone was strange enough. I
> swung the torch towards it, and as the beam passed across, whatever was
> there was gone. The jungle was silent again.

The site's own film puts **a pale, grey-skinned soldier in an old uniform
crouched against a tree beside him, one hand on his leg**, gone when the
beam swings (docs/source/video-transcripts.md). The other platoon and the
bicycle are the SAME night in the source; they are chapter 4's, and he
does not know about them yet.

The lesson (S1·05): *Sometimes you feel something before you see anything
at all ... none of that proves on its own that something spiritual is
present. Always consider the ordinary explanations first. In an unfamiliar
place, especially at night, stay observant without letting curiosity or
fear take the wheel.*

## 2 · The place

**Secondary jungle at night** — the first chapter of the episode off
Tekong's camp, and the first in the game with no walls.

- **The harbour**: a clearing about 12 m across at the end of a track,
  six **shell scrapes already dug** in a ring (a 1.8 × 0.6 m trench,
  0.4 deep, the spoil on the outer lip, a pack and a rifle on each lip),
  the section RESTING at them — sitting on the lips, lying back on the
  packs, one man on his side asleep. HIS scrape is the rear one, the one
  furthest from the track and **closest to the jungle**: from it the
  section is one way and the trees are the other, three metres off.
- **The walkable world** is the clearing and the first two metres into
  the tree line on every side — enough to step off the ring and stand
  among the trunks, not enough to leave. The tree kit (v6.15, four
  kinds) planted dense, undergrowth cards between the trunks, an
  invisible ring of blockers 2 m inside the trees (walktest-proved).
- **The dark**: fog near 3 m / far 20 m, a black sky, no moon. Two red
  chemlights on the commander's scrape. The torch (the kit's v7.0
  declaration) is the only light the player carries, and it is WHITE
  here — this chapter is about curiosity, so the player is given the
  full beam. **The torch is what you see by.**
- **The track and the tonner**: the film's pockets — a stretch of laterite
  road through the same trees at last light (an EVENING daylight inside
  the pocket), and the track's mouth where the file steps off.

Daylight declaration: NIGHT for play (near-black, a cold blue hemisphere
at 0.08, no key, no sun); the film's pocket painted for dusk and the walk
in tweened down to the night (`kit.daylight`).

## 4 · Play

Two phases before the decision. Each is a saved `phase` (v7.3's law).

### 1 · `look` — LOOK AROUND (the torch, ~90 s of curiosity)
Objective: **"Look around. Shine the torch into the jungle."** and a
counter, *0 / 6*, that is the v10.0 counter-as-beats (1/6 · 2/6 … each
one an OBJECTIVE COMPLETE flash, v8.7).

Six **torch spots** — the v7.0 hotspots, but the "press" is the BEAM:
a spot is *seen* when the torch has been on it (inside 4° of the reticle,
within its range) for 0.8 s. No button. The hotmark diamond marks each
one out to 16 m as it does in c1 and c2, so the player knows where to
look and the chapter is not a hunt. Each spot has a line from him,
narration in the dark to himself, and the six are placed so that seeing
them WALKS him round his own scrape and turns him every way but one:

| # | spot | where | his line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a strangler fig, roots like a cage | 6 m out, straight ahead | *"Everything out here looks like something else."* |
| 2 | a fallen log, fungus on it | 5 m, left | *"Wet. Everything is wet."* |
| 3 | a gap between two trunks, going nowhere | 9 m, right | *"You could walk in there and never come out."* |
| 4 | the buddy at the next scrape, his eyes shut | 4 m, behind-left | **b3here** (buddy, not opening his eyes): *"Eh. Torch off, lah. Save battery."* |
| 5 | the commander's chemlights | across the ring | *"Nobody's sleeping. Everyone's pretending."* |
| 6 | his own boots, in his own scrape | at his feet | *"Just a scrape. Just a night. Sleep when you can, they said."* |

Spot 6 is LAST by construction (it enables when the five are seen), so
the last thing the objective makes him do is put the torch on his own
legs — and he is looking at the ground in front of him, **his back to
the jungle**, when the pressure comes. The order of 1–5 is free.

The section is alive while he looks: the buddy on `Idle_6` sitting on
his lip, two men lying back (`Sleep_Normally` on the pack, the c1 rig),
the commander kneeling on his signals take at 0.5 rate, one man's
cigarette (an emissive dot, no smoke). Ambience: `junglenight` (insects,
all night, keyed to nothing), a distant `tonner` once at 40–70 s on the
chapter's stream, the dread `e2dread` at 1.0 under everything (v10.7).
Presence OFF. Nothing drains. This is the ordinary night.

### 2 · `pressure` — THE PRESSURE
On the sixth spot's line ending (+1.5 s):

1. **The camera SHAKES** — 0.45 s, a hard kick and a decaying tremor (the
   faint's shake at a quarter, v3.2's own code) — with **`stingpress`**
   (a new sting: one low hit and a rising string, 2.5 s) and, under its
   tail, **`ghostlaugh`** (c1's young laugh, v10.6's take, once, panned
   BEHIND him, 0.5) and a 180 ms buzz. The torch is still on his boots.
2. **n3press** (jamesTeen, on the frame the shake ends): *"Something was
   pressing against my leg. Not just a sensation — there was actual
   weight to it!"*
3. Objective changes (COMPLETE for the look, then the new order in
   sodium): **"Shine the torch down. On the ground."** — the waypoint on
   the ground at his own feet. PRESENCE begins (`kit.presence`, the
   chapter's words: *"Something is pressing on your leg."*), draining
   slowly while the beam is off the ground — the only drain in the
   chapter, and it stops the moment he obeys.
4. When the beam is on the ground at his feet (pitch under −0.85 with the
   torch on) for 1.0 s: **n3look** (jamesTeen): *"Nothing around... no
   one... no footsteps... not even the sound of vegetation moving around
   me."* The jungle bed DUCKS to a fifth under this line — the source's
   silence, made audible.
5. **n3still** (jamesTeen, 0.8 s after): *"But I still feel the pressure
   on my leg..."* — the banner stays up through it.
6. The decision opens by itself on the line's end (the c1 fear's path;
   the "pile" is the ground at his feet). He is looking down at his own
   boots in the torch when the four options arrive, the jungle at his
   back.

## 6 · The four scenes

Every scene begins where the decision opened: in the rear scrape, the
torch on his own boots, the jungle behind him. Hands hidden in all four.

**A · THE TURN (~18 s) — Chad's scene.** The torch stays on the ground
and the view **turns 180° backwards**, the beam sweeping the litter round
his legs as it goes (yaw 0 → π over 1.1 s, an ease that starts slow and
whips) — and in the half-second BEFORE the turn completes, at the edge of
the beam and still in the corner of the frame, **a transparent soldier is
already running**: `fbosling` ghostified at 0.55 alpha on `Running` at
full rate, from 1.2 m behind his scrape straight out into the trees, 2.6
m/s (the take's own speed), gone into the fog inside 1.4 s — before the
turn is square, so the player never gets a full look. **`ghostrunleaf`**
(running footsteps in leaf litter, fast, receding — NEW, not c1's
wet-tile `ghostrun`), panned with him, and the laugh's tail once more,
far off. The beam lands on the ground where he was: leaf litter, and a
pressed-down patch the size of a boot — then the litter lifts back by
itself (a plane scaled up over 0.6 s). The pressure LIFTS on that frame;
the banner clears. **n3A1** (jamesTeen): *"...gone. Whatever it was. As
soon as the light touched it."* A beat on the empty trees. **n3A2**: *"I
didn't chase it."* Card.

**B · THE HAND INTO THE DARK (~20 s).** The torch stays down. His hand
goes off the rifle and down past his own knee into the leaf litter beside
the leg — the camera tilts to follow it, the beam on the back of his own
hand — into cold leaves, and the leaves are MOVING, **something drawing
back from his fingers through the litter** the way a thing does that was
there (`leafdraw`, a slow drag through dry leaves, panned away). The hand
comes back fast. He looks at it in the torch. **n3B1**: *"Cold. Something
was there. Something moved."* The presence banner for 3 s more. **n3B2**:
*"I should have looked first."* Card.

**C · "FELT WHAT?" (~26 s).** He turns his head — not the body — to the
next scrape. **n3C1** (whispered): *"Eh. You feel that?"* The buddy
(`Idle_6`, the v5.03 head-look onto him): **b3C1**: *"Feel what?"* A beat.
**n3C2**: *"...my leg. Something pressed on it."* The buddy puts his own
torch on his own legs, then out at the trees behind the player's scrape.
**b3C2**: *"Nothing there. Maybe root. Or you fell asleep sitting."* Then,
not laughing: **b3C3**: *"Face the front. I face mine. Relief at four."*
The pressure lifts while he is talking, unremarked. Two beams facing out
into the same dark; the camera pulling up and back over both scrapes,
the trees between them with nothing against them. Card.

**D · "WHO'S THERE?" (~22 s).** His voice, not whispered, across the
harbour — **n3D1**: *"Who's there?"* — and the jungle answers with
silence, then the commander's hiss from the far scrape (**s3hiss**: *"TWO.
Shut up."*) and the buddy's (**b3D**: *"Siao ah?"*). The pressure LIFTS on
the frame his voice ends — exactly as if answered — and the banner
clears. He knows it, and that is worse. **n3D2**: *"It stopped. Right when
I asked."* The camera holds on the black behind him where the beam's edge
was, and eight metres out the undergrowth is moving away in a line —
leaf cards shifting, no figure, no footsteps. Card.

### The torch is an item (engine)

The torch had been a chapter DECLARATION since v7.0: `torch: { on: true }`
and the button was there from the first frame. Chad's mechanic needs the
torch to be a THING — picked up, carried, equipped — and the game already
has a bag with a hand slot that holds the beads or the phone. So:

- `torch.item` (an inventory id) on the declaration. When present, the
  torch exists for the PLAYER only while that item is in its slot:
  `torchAvail()` reads the hand slot, `torchAvailSync()` derives the
  `hasTorch` body class (which shows the button), switches the light off
  if the item leaves the hand while it is on, and clears the bag's pulse
  once the item is equipped. It runs from `invPaint` (every inventory
  change), `applyState` (a resume) and `torchSetup` (a chapter change).
- `torchToggle` (F, the button) is gated on it. `kit.torchOn()` from a
  chapter or a scene is NOT: a film owns its light, and a save from before
  this release (phase `look:`/`pressure`/`decide` with no torch in the bag)
  still plays, because `applyPhase` equips it on the way in.
- Five bag verbs on the kit: `give(id)` (adds, sets `invUrge`), `take(id)`,
  `equip(id)` (into its slot, swapping whatever was there to the bag),
  `has(id)`, `equipped(id)`, plus `urge(id|null)`. Episode 1 calls none.

  first time the bag opens, the torch is the thing that lights up.
