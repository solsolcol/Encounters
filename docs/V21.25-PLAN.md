# v21.25 · THE GALLERY IS READ IN ORDER

Episode 3 chapter 3's masters' gallery (`kit.gallery`, the twenty-ninth seam). Chad's two messages of 22 Oct 2026,
verbatim:

> "There is a problem with the monk gallery window, if i accidentally moved to the next monk and moved back to the
> previous one, the next button to skip to the next monk after the next, if i already finished all monks and went back
> to an earlier one, the button below becomes continue, and ends the whole window even if i wanted to go to the next
> monk. It should not have this kind of behaviour, it should always go to the next monk on the next page rather than
> have this kind of skipping behaviour. This is extremely disappointing, i already told you to do a deep debug and you
> still missed this. I said this gallery mode is of critical importance, and the player must read all the stories, amd
> have no skipping behaviour."

> "Continue is misleading, i expect continue to show me more but it ends the whole window. Use another word. Also i
> dont want players to just mindlessly and quickly scroll through all the stories or to spam next, or spam flip or spam
> continue to close the whole window. I have told you time and time again this gallery mode is very important. Please
> treat it seriously, you are not serious enough"

## 1 · What was wrong, exactly (v21.24, measured against the code)

1. **NEXT skipped.** `galEndTarget()` / `galPrimary()` sent NEXT to "the next page NOT YET READ after this one". A page
   counted as read after 1.2 s on screen (`GAL_DWELL`), so a page visited by accident (an arrow, a swipe, the wheel's
   pull) counted, and NEXT from the page before it jumped over it.
2. **CONTINUE ended the window from any page.** Once every page had counted, the foot button and the end mark said
   CONTINUE on EVERY page, and pressed, closed the window — going back to an earlier master and pressing it ended the
   gallery instead of opening the next one.
3. **"Read" meant "on screen for 1.2 s".** Five presses of NEXT (or five flips), 1.2 s apart, read the whole gallery:
   six seconds, no story read, then CONTINUE.
4. **Nothing stopped spam.** NEXT, the arrows, a swipe and the wheel turned a page whatever had been read; only the
   0.45 s guard after opening and the wheel's own hold limited them.
5. **"Continue" says "show me more".** It ended the window.

## 2 · The rules (v21.25)

**R1 · Read in order.** A page may be opened only if no unread page stands before it: the FRONTIER is the first page
not yet read, and pages after it are locked (their thumbnails say so). Going back is always allowed.

**R2 · NEXT is always the next page,** and it opens only once THIS page is read. Before that it is shown locked, filling
as the story is read. The same holds for every forward route: the → arrow, A/D, a swipe, the sideways wheel, the
wheel's pull at the end of a story, Page Down at the end, Enter, Space at the end, End, the digits, the thumbnails.

**R3 · A page is READ when its story has been read to its end at a reading pace.** A reading cursor moves down the
story at `GAL_PACE` words a second (8 — about twice an average adult's silent reading, ~238 words a minute, so a
genuine reader is never held), and never past the bottom of what is on screen. The page counts when the cursor
reaches the story's last line (and it has been open at least `GAL_DWELL`). Scrolled straight to the end, the cursor
still has to get there: a 460-word story cannot count in less than ~57 s. A short story that stands whole on the
screen counts in its own reading time. The ring round the page's thumbnail and the NEXT button both fill with the
cursor, so the reading is seen.

**R4 · The window ends only on the LAST page, once EVERY page is read,** with a button that says so: **Close gallery**
(the end mark under the story: "All 5 read · Close the gallery"). On the last page while it is still being read, the
button is shown locked and filling, as NEXT is. Esc and the X still close the window at any time — a player must be
able to leave a window — but the chapter counts the masters only when every page was read (v21.10's gate, unchanged),
and reopens on the frontier.

**R5 · No spam.** A turn while the last one is still turning (`GAL_TURN` ms) is refused, by every route. A press of a
locked NEXT (or a locked turn) answers once — the button shakes, a soft tick, the hint says "Read the story to its end
first" — and repeated presses within `GAL_TURN` do not repeat it. Closing has its own guard (0.8 s after the last page
counts, kept from v21.23).

**R6 · Old states stay sane.** A gallery reopened with pages read out of order (a save from v21.10–v21.24, when any
order was allowed) opens on the frontier; R1/R2 hold from there. Opened with every page read, it opens on the first
page, NEXT is NEXT and the last page closes.

## 4 · Record

**Two more messages arrived while it was built**, and shaped it:

> "It should not be done to the point where users have no idea how to flip or proceed to the next one, it needs to
> remain intuitive"

> "Also the background music of the shop clashes with the music of this gallery mode. The background music should be
> softened heavily while this gallery mode opens, and priority is on the music of this gallery mode." — and: "Does the
> shop music return after the gallery mode completes and closes"

So the pace was set at **10 words a second** (600 a minute — the upper edge of skimming, 2.5× an average adult's silent
reading), not the 8 first drafted: a genuine reader must never meet the gate. And the lock SHOWS itself everywhere it
applies, so it is never a mystery: both Next buttons fill like a bar as the story is read and light once (with the read
tick) when it counts; a press too early flashes the button and the line under the window says "Read on — Next opens
when the bar fills" (on a phone on its side, where that line is hidden to save height, it floats over the foot); under
the story, while it fills, "Opens when the bar fills"; the → arrow dims and stops nudging; a later page's thumbnail is
dimmed with a small lock; ← always works. The window's opening line says the rule: "Read each story to its end, then
tap Next".

**CP1 · the engine (src/main.js), done.**
- `galFront()` — the frontier; `galMayTurn(k, dir)` — on only from a read page, never past the frontier (it also
  refuses ← from the first page round to the last past unread pages); `galNext()` — `{ k, close, locked }`: always the
  next page; on the last page, every page read, the close; while the last page is read, the close waits; read with an
  earlier page unread (an old save's order), round to the first.
- `galTurnTo(k, dir)` — every turn by every route goes through it: the open guard, the turn gap (`GAL_TURN` 500 ms,
  refused quietly), the reading brought up to the instant, the gate (refused with `galNope`). `galGo`, `galJump`,
  `galPrimary` all call it; the wheel's sideways swipe and pull hold the words only when a turn happened.
- `galDwell(now)` — the reading cursor: `cur = max(cur, min(visible fraction of the story, cur + dt × GAL_PACE /
  words))`, the visible fraction measured in layout units (the boxes' scale during the window's entrance divided
  out), 4 px of grace, `dt` capped at 1 s and reset on a tab's return; a page counts at `cur` 1 and `GAL_DWELL` on the
  page; each page keeps its cursor while the window is open (`gal.curs`), so going back to check something loses
  nothing.
- `galEndPaint()` — the foot's button (`Next` / `Close gallery`, classes `locked` / `fin`), the side arrows (`locked`),
  the end mark (`locked` / `last`, its kicker and name); `galReadPaint(k)` — the ring round the thumbnail and `--rd` on
  both buttons; `galNope(reading)` — the flash, a soft low tick, a 14 ms buzz and the line, once per `GAL_TURN`.
- The words: `gallery.finish` "Close gallery", `gallery.endFinish` "Close the gallery", `gallery.lockRead`,
  `gallery.lockOrder`, `gallery.endWait`; `gallery.hint` / `gallery.hintKey` say the rule; `gallery.continue` retired.
  Sheet v112 (`masters/v21.25/masterz-text-v112.xlsx`): exactly those eight cells against v111.
- shell.html: `#galGo.fin` (gold, breathing — it was `.galBox.done #galGo`, gold on every page once all were read),
  `#galGo.locked` / `.galEnd.locked .galEndGo` (the fill), `.woke`, `.nope`, `.galNav.locked`, `.galThumb.locked` with
  a lock drawn in CSS (no image: the strict CSP), `.galEndWait`, `.galHint.say` for a phone on its side.

**The music (src/chapters/e3/e3c3.js `mixBeds`), done.** Measured first, in the phone band (300 Hz – 5 kHz): with the
window up the radio sat at 0.3 × 0.2 → −41 dBFS RMS and the gallery's bed at 0.55 → −26 — 15 dB apart, and still two
melodies in different keys, colliding in every quiet bar of the khim; and the radio sank only as fast as the bed rose
(a 1 s ease). Now the radio has its own duck, `galDuck`, eased in over 0.18 s and out over 0.8 s, taking it to 8 % of
its level (−22 dB more), and the gallery's bed comes up to 0.7 (+2 dB): about 26 dB apart. The air and the mall's
murmur, which carry no melody, sink to a fifth as before. The radio is never switched off — only held down — so when
the window closes it comes back on its own, over about two seconds.

## 5 · v21.26 — the review of the live gallery, and what it changed

An adversarial review read the live v21.25 gallery against Chad's four asks: no page can be skipped, nothing can be
spammed through, the rules stay intuitive, and the radio steps aside for the gallery's music and comes back after it.
Its four claims about the rules all held: there is no way past the reading, no skip, no turn faster than one per half
second, and the radio is back after every close. It found nine things to put right. Each was checked against the
code or a probe before it was touched, and each is in v21.26 (deployed `6ada2e5319f2e874ebcd88f0`, byte-verified
236 of 236 files):

1. **The words named the wrong thing.** "Read on — Next opens when the bar fills" and "Opens when the bar fills": the
   only thing on screen that looks like a bar is the 1-of-5 strip under the thumbnails, and it does not fill while a
   story is read. The words name the button now: "Keep reading — Next fills up as you read", "Keep reading — Close
   gallery fills up as you read" on the last page, and "Fills up as you read" under the story. Sheet v113 has exactly
   these three cells against v112 (`gallery.lockRead`, `gallery.endWait`, and the new `gallery.lockReadLast`).
2. **The fill was faint.** A locked button's wash runs from 18 % to 46 % gold, which is hard to see over the dark
   lacquer on a phone. A bright line now runs along the foot of the locked Next and the end mark's button
   (`linear-gradient(90deg, #FFD98A calc(var(--rd) * 100%), …)`, 3 px), and it grows with the reading.
3. **The arrows went round.** On the last page → turned to the first master (read, so allowed), beside a button that
   said Close gallery. Two ways to leave the last page that looked like the same thing. The arrows stop at the ends now:
   → is locked on the last page and ← on the first, and the arrow keys and A/D stop there too (`galGo` no longer wraps).
4. **A refused press shook the wrong control.** `galNope` always flashed Next. It flashes what was pressed now: the
   arrow, the thumbnail, or Next and the end mark. The locked arrow's shake had a rule that matched nothing
   (`.galNav.locked.nope svg`); it plays now. A refused thumbnail gets its own small shake (`galThumbNope`).
5. **On a phone on its side the opening rule was never shown.** The hint line is hidden there to save height, and
   v21.25 floated only the refusal line. The opening rule ("Read each story to its end, then tap Next") now floats over
   the title (`top: 6px`) for its few seconds.
6. **Reading was lost on an accidental close.** Each page's cursor lived on the open window (`gal.curs`), so a story
   half-read, then Esc, then the row pressed again, began at zero. The cursors are kept for the session, keyed by the
   page's id (`galCurs`, `galPid`). A page already read is read either way; this is about the bar the reader had filled.
7. **The radio came back too soon.** Its duck was released the moment the window's state ended, while the gallery's bed
   was still fading. The two rose and fell at about the same rate, so for half a second after every close the two
   melodies sat within a few dB of each other again. The radio is now held down until the gallery's bed is under 0.12
   (`galHeld` in e3c3's `mixBeds`).
8. **Close gallery wrapped on the narrowest phones.** At 360, 375 and 390 px wide the button dropped under the
   thumbnails on the last page, and the foot grew from 64–73 px to 101–115 (`dbg-galfoot.mjs`). The thumbnails are now
   `min(34px, 8.4vw)` with a 6 px gap. The button has 10 px sides, .74rem type, .06em spacing and `white-space:
   nowrap`, which makes it 127 px where it was 145. Measured with all five pages read, on the last page and on page 2,
   at 360×780, 375×812, 390×844, 412×915, 667×375 and 844×390: on the thumbnails' row every time, and the foot 63–72 px.
9. **Two fixture checks were coin tosses.** "Not read at once" waited a fixed time in the harness, and "Esc early"
   depended on a short third story being read before the Esc landed. The first now measures in the page (a 20 ms
   watcher records when the page counted, and the check asks that it was ≥ 1150 ms). The second uses its own three pages
   with a long third story. Three checks were added: `galleryNopeOnPressed` (the shake lands on the control pressed),
   `galleryReadingKept` (a reopened window keeps the bar) and `galleryArrowsStopAtEnds`.

**A probe law, paid for in the foot measurements.** The first candidate layouts all measured exactly as the shipped one
(145 px, wrapped). CSP took the blame first, but the hosted page sends no policy. The game's stylesheet sits inside
`<body>` (shell.html's `<style>` comes after `<body>`), so rules a probe appends to `<head>` come earlier in document
order and lose every tie of specificity. The candidates were measured with `!important`. LEARNINGS has it.
