# v6.10 — The leaf in his palm

Chad, on v6.9 ("better now"):

> But for the leaf picking up, after picking it up, instead of the leaf
> dangling from the hand and his head looking at it, can you just make it
> so that the leaf is resting on his palm? And the camera shows the leaf
> directly resting flat facing up in his palm? And just close up of the
> palm.

That is the five-dollar note's shot (v6.6, 3c: the walk-pick take parked
a beat after the grab, the note flat on the fingers, a macro into the open
hand), and this release gives it to the leaf.

## Measured before anything moved

The pick take — the one that bends him to the leaf — was measured ten
times a second from the grab to the pocket (`dbg-palm.mjs`: the hand
bone's world axes, the palm being its +z):

| film time | what the take is doing | palm's +z, up component |
|---|---|---|
| 9.8–10.0 | the hand closes on the leaf | 0.47 → 0.43 |
| 10.3 | the lift | 0.04 |
| 10.6 (the cut) | the lift | −0.12 |
| 11.1–13.4 | the held pose (v6.8's hero frame at 12.8) | −0.50 to −0.62 |
| 13.5–14.1 | the hand drops to the pocket | −0.65 → −0.10 |

There is no frame after the grab with the palm up: the take carries its
object palm-DOWN, which is why v6.6 pinched the leaf at the fingertips.
The walk-pick take, parked at 5.85 (the five's frame), holds the palm
0.71 up; in pocket one at 12.0 it measures 0.67 up in world, the hand
11.7 cm above the lawn.

## What changed (all in `src/chapters/ch1.js`)

- **The pose.** At the cut (10.6) a `take(10.6, 14.0, 'walkpick', 0,
  5.85)` parks him on the five's frame, and a step stands him at
  `standFor(LEAF, RY1, 0.58, 1.16)` so that frame's hand lands where the
  leaf was picked. The pick take keeps running underneath (a later take
  wins) and pocket two's `alert` takes over at 14.2, in black. The head
  look to the hand ends at the cut: the head is out of frame.
- **The leaf.** A second in-hand copy, `palm`, beside v6.8's `pinch`:
  stem at (0.012, 0.030, 0.024) in the hand bone's frame, `rotation
  (0.33, 0, 0.15)` — the five's plane exactly (the heel of the palm at
  z 0.020, the curled fingertips at z 0.053, a 19° rise; 4 mm clear at
  both ends), the blade down the fingers, turned 0.15 about its own
  normal. The leaf's own frame, read from the GLB without a browser: the
  stem at the origin, length +y (±32767 quantized, × the node's 0.025 ×
  `mk()`'s 2.2 = 0.11 m), width 6.5 cm, 0.9 mm thick, face +z; the blade's
  centre sits 13 mm off the stem's line, which the x puts back over the
  palm. `stage.leafShow(mode)` shows one copy by name; the grab step shows
  `pinch`, the cut step `palm`, and a seek re-fires both in order.
- **The shot.** `shotPalm(10.6, 14.0, 0.30, 0.22, 0, 0, 0.02, 0.02,
  smoothK, 0.085)`: the lens along the palm's normal, 0.30 → 0.22 m, a
  slow push-in. `shotPalm` gained an `aim` (default 0.05, the five's
  call unchanged): the leaf's centre is 8.5 cm down the fingers, and on
  the phone's centre crop the 5 cm aim and the 0.20 m end put the blade's
  edge on the right side of the frame.
- **The fade** back to 13.4–14.0 (v6.8 had pulled it to 13.15–13.7
  because the pick take dropped the hand toward the pocket under the
  fingertip shot; a parked hand never drops).
- Replaced: v6.6's `shotFingers` on the leaf (the lens beyond the
  fingertips, the leaf hanging between it and his face). The helper
  stays; nothing else used it.

Turns tried at 12.0 (`dbg-leafyaw.mjs`, 960 × 540): 0 and 0.25 both rest
on the hand with the fingertips showing under the blade; 0.5 takes the
blade off the hand onto the grass; −0.25 slides it off the heel. 0.15
ships.
