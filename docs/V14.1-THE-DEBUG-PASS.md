## 1 · The two that no harness could see

### The fifty-one trees were NaN

`plantTrees` composes each instance with `sp.h * (0.88 + rnd(i,1)*0.30)`.
Every chapter in the game passes `h`. This one passed `s`, meaning a scale —
so every matrix composed with `undefined * jitter`, which is **not** a small
tree and **not** a missing tree: it is garbage geometry, black shards, one
clump of which stood in the film's fourth shot.

Measured on the shipped build:

| chapter | finite instances | NaN | bounding spheres |
|---|---|---|---|
| e2c5 | **0** | **51** | 9 of 9 null |
| e2c4 (control) | 173 | 0 | all present |

A NaN matrix makes `computeBoundingSphere` return null, so the stand was
invisible to the culling checks too — the bug hid itself from the one class
of test that might have caught it.

Fixed in three places, because the class is what matters and not the typo:

1. the chapter passes `h: 7.2 * (0.85 + hash(i,17)*0.5)` — a real height in
   metres times the author's intended spread (the kit normalises every tree
   to height 1.0 at prep, v6.15, so a bare multiplier multiplies nothing);
2. the engine defaults a non-finite height to 6.0 m **and says so on the
   console**, once;
3. **`walktest` fails any chapter whose instance matrices or mesh positions
   go non-finite.** It is the right harness because it is the only one that
   walks into every episode-2 chapter with a live scene.

### The apron was telling the player "Something is burning ahead..."

```js
const chWord = (k, fallbackKey) => (CH.words && CH.words[k]) || T(fallbackKey);
```

`''` is falsy, so a **declared empty** word fell through to chapter 1's
fallback. Two chapters declare `approach: ''` to mean "no floating label on a
person" — episode 2's cookhouse since **v10.0** and its apron since **v14.0**
— and both have been naming a fire that exists in neither.

The intent was written down three times and never implemented: e2c2's comment,
e2c5's comment, and the engine's own gate, added at v13.0 —

```js
/* v13.0: and only when the chapter HAS a word for it. An empty
   `words.approach` is the sheet's own "remove that text" (EDITING-TEXT),
   and an empty floating box is not a removal. */
if (d < 6.2 && onScreen && chWord('approach', 'world.burning'))
```

— against a helper that could not express it. This is v12.5's label bug
("only when it is actually on screen", written in v2.1's comment and never
tested) in its second form.

A presence test, not a truthiness test. It holds for every word the helper
serves, because that is also the sheet's contract (*"An empty cell removes
that text"*), and every other chapter declares non-empty words, so nothing
else moves. Measured on the shipped build: the prompt's text is `""` and the
box is hidden, in play and at the decision.

## 3 · The interactions

**The counters went dead at point-blank.** Anchors at `z = ARMS.z - 0.7` =
**6.20** — the exact `maxZ` the chapter's bounds stop the player at — and
y 1.30. So walking straight up to a counter, which is the only thing anyone
does, put the anchor directly under the lens: `hotspotVisible` projects to NDC
and it left the frame, the badge went out and the press fell through. The
badge only ever lived in a band from about 0.44 m to the radius, which a
player crosses on the way in.

The v7.5 law, met a second time: **the anchor of a thing you walk up to sits
at EYE height, on the thing.** `{ x, y: 1.55, z: <counter> }` — 0.70 m out and
0.07 m under the eye from the closest legal standing spot. Measured there,
facing the block, all three offer their badge.

**Two of the three clerk lines were dropped.** `sayLine` refuses a line while
another is playing and returns false; `handIn` ignored the return. The
armskote's `c5arms` is 5.04 s and the walk from the armskote to the stores is
about two seconds, so on the natural left-to-right route `c5store` was thrown
away with no error. They go through the chapter's own queue now (the v9.5
count-off's shape) — a held line lands as soon as the mouth is free, which
reads as the previous clerk finishing behind your shoulder. `beginTalk` no
longer empties that queue, because the only thing that can be in it at that
moment is a clerk line the player walked away from.

**And:** `kit.give` left the bag button pulsing its equip urge for the whole
clearance — for the two things the chapter is about handing BACK; the office's
waypoint diamond was planted inside the bench's own blocker; `armsdoor` was

### Why the theme died, and the law under it

Every cue a scene fires joins `cineVoices`:

```js
const src = snd(smp[0], smp[1] * vol);
if (src) { if (cine) cineVoices.push(src); ... }
```

and `cineEnd` calls `stopCineVoices(!c.skipped)`, which keeps only the sources
in `liveVoices` — the voice takes, which is how `n5close` runs on under the
outcome card. `e5theme` is not a voice take, so it was ramped to zero over
0.30 s and stopped, 3.7 s after it started.

**A scene cannot hand a sound over to the outcome card unless that sound is a
voice take.** So a closing bed has to be cued far enough back that the part
you want heard is heard INSIDE the scene — under the last answer, which is
where a closing bed belongs anyway. Measured first: this one is flat at about
−24 dBFS RMS for its first 36 s, so there is no swell to miss and nothing to
re-cut. At 0.75 under the v5.27 duck it sits about 17 dB below the cast.

## 5 · One parse per asset

`admintee` was GLTF-parsed **twice** — once for the storeman and once for the
apron extra — so the chapter held two full copies of a 44,539-triangle rig and
its 2048 px sheets, while `cloneSkinned` sat destructured out of `ctx` and
unused.

Now a per-key promise holds the parsed original, **every** rig takes a clone,
and the original is never added to the scene and never touched — which is the
v9.1 law ("every copy is made before any is touched") in the form that cannot
be got wrong: no copy is ever taken from a mutated one. `SkeletonUtils.clone`
shares geometry and materials, so `dispose()`'s sweep over `world` still frees
them. Measured on the shipped build: the two admintee rigs' skinned meshes
carry the **same** geometry uuid.
