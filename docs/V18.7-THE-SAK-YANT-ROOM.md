## The retarget bug (LEARNINGS)

`tools/retarget.mjs` used each SOURCE parent's world REST rotation instead of
its animated one, so any take whose hips or spine move came out wrong. A rig
retargeted onto ITSELF came out 126° off at the feet; after the fix, 0.0°.
Proven across rigs: the old Ajarn's `Sitting_Answering_Questions` retargeted
onto Krukai matches Mixamo's own copy of that take on Krukai, by photograph.
