# v6.11 — The wrist turns

Chad, on v6.10's close-up (the leaf on a hand borrowed from the note's
take, down by his slipper):

> why cant you make the palm facing up naturally

## What the takes have

`dbg-takes.mjs` posed every frame of his five takes at ten a second and
read the right hand's height, its palm (the bone's +z) and how far ahead
of him it was:

| take | frames with the palm ≥ 0.5 up |
|---|---|
| walk | 0.7–0.9 s, the arm swing: hip height, beside the body |
| alert | none |
| walkpick | 5.6–5.9 s, the grab: 0.12–0.23 m off the ground (v6.10's frame) |
| look | none |
| pick | 0.8–0.9 s (passing the body on the way down) and 1.3 s (0.28 m) |

Nothing holds a palm up at a standing height. The turn had to be added.

## Two probes that lied, and the one that did not

The first wrist trial reported the palm oscillating through three full
turns over 0.25 rad steps. Cause: `PropertyMixer.apply` only calls
`setValue` when its two accumulators differ, so the mixer had stopped
writing the parked take's bones, and each trial's twist was multiplied
onto the previous trial's result. The second trial posed the two wrist
bones in one call and measured them in the next, but `cine.seek()` only
pauses the clock — the tracks are re-applied every frame — so it measured
two bones at the held frame on a body the film had put back into the
lift, and concluded a wrist split could never reach the palm up; its
twelve "candidate" renders were twelve copies of the film's own frame.
The third (`dbg-wrist2.mjs`) posed the whole body and measured every
candidate inside one synchronous call:

| split (forearm share) | angle for the palm straight up | up | fingers after |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.00 | −2.20 rad | 0.996 | (0.12, −0.09, −0.99) |
| 0.75 | −2.22 rad | 0.996 | (0.02, −0.09, −1.00) |
| 0.50 | −2.18 rad | 0.999 | (−0.07, −0.04, −1.00) |
| 0.25 | −2.20 rad | 0.999 | (−0.12, 0.05, −0.99) |

The hand's +y sits 11° off the forearm's axis in the forearm's frame
(0.14, 0.98, −0.12), so every split is a twist about nearly the same
line: the fingers stay forward and the palm comes fully up. The base
palm through the lift, for the ramp: (1.00, 0.02, −0.05) at 10.4,
(0.98, −0.12, 0.16) at 10.6, (0.87, −0.50, −0.05) at 11.2, (0.81, −0.57,
−0.18) at the held frame.

## What changed (all in `src/chapters/ch1.js`, plus one line of `package.json`)

- **`boyPalm = { k, ang: −2.04, split: 0.75 }`** and `boyPalmApply`, called
  in `boyPose` right after the mixer: the forearm's and the hand's
  quaternions are set from the clip's own tracks (`createInterpolant`,
  evaluated at the take's time, slerped by the blend weight when two takes
  run) and then multiplied by `ang · k · share` about each bone's own +y.
  −2.04 is 0.92 of the vertical angle: the palm 0.98 up, tilted 10° toward
  his right, so `shotPalm`'s yaw (a horizontal bearing from the camera to
  the palm) has a bearing to take. Three quarters on the forearm, a
  quarter at the wrist. The clip's values are re-applied even at k = 0, so
  a bone never keeps a stale twist.
- **The film:** `palmUp(10.45, 11.25)` ramps k on a smoothstep across the
  lift; shot 1b runs to 11.0 and shows the hand rising and turning; the
  cut at 11.0 swaps the pinch for the flat leaf and opens the palm macro
  (v6.10's shot and aim); `take(12.8, 14.0, 'pick', 0, 4.4)` parks the take
  on its held frame so the hand never drops toward the pocket under the
  macro; the fade is 13.4–14.0. The hand look to the hand runs to 13.2 as
  in v6.9. `palmOff()` at pocket two's step, and `filmReset` zeroes k.
- **The pinched leaf hangs by physics** through the lift: v6.8 had fixed
  its length on world-down and its face toward the lens in the hand's
  frame for the 12.8 pose, and once the wrist turned under it the leaf
  went behind the palm at 10.8 and out past the thumb at 10.9. A track from
  the grab to the cut recomputes both directions every frame from the
  hand's world orientation and the camera's position.
- **`npm run build`** runs `node --check` over every chapter file before
  esbuild. The chapters are copied, not bundled, and a chapter with a
  broken constant had gone through the build without a word.
- v6.10's borrowed walk-pick frame and its stand are gone.

## Verification

41 frames at ten a second, 10.0–14.0, on contact sheets, then the turn
again at full size (10.3–11.2) after the hang fix, and the phone crop at
10.7, 10.8, 10.9, 11.0, 11.2, 12.0 and 13.2: the hand comes up on its own
take with the leaf dangling from the fingers, turns over, and from 11.0 the
close-up looks down at a palm held at chest height with the leaf flat on
it, his body and the lawn behind, the wrist clean. `cine` and `chapter`
green. No engine seam. No word moved; sheet v39 stands.
