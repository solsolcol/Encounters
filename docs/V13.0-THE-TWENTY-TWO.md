## The engine's share, and why episode 1 cannot feel it

`src/main.js` changed in four places and every one is unreachable from
episode 1 **by construction**, not by care:

- `WEAPON_DEFAULTS` gains `unlimited` (default false), `adsSecs` and
  `adsPos`; `weaponFire` / `weaponReload` / the ammo pill read `unlimited`.
  Episode 1 declares no weapon at all.
- the aim is a tween (`adsK`, stepped on wall time in `weaponFrame`) instead
  of a snap, and the weapon comes onto the sight line under it. Same gate.
- `evBrief` accepts any `demo` value, not only `'drag'`. Episode 1 declares
  no events.
- the canvas's `mousedown` returns early while a kit event is open. Both
  halves of that line — the event and the weapon — are episode 2's only.

`shell.html` gains the AIM button's size and word, the `bar` demo's CSS, and
two rules that hide the ammo pill and the reload button under
`body.wpnNoAmmo`.

## The three laws this release paid for

**THE SESSION IDS ARE THE ONLY HANDLE ON A GENERATION.** The six ElevenLabs
nodes had completed, and their session ids had never been written down — and
`creative_get_flow_run_status` takes session ids, not node ids. The six had to
be re-run to get them back. They go in `masters/<v>/sessions.json` the moment
a run starts now, before anything else.

**A FRAMING NUMBER IS MEASURED IN NDC, NOT IN DEGREES ON PAPER.** The flare's
launch point was placed at 17.2° off the lane by arithmetic and photographed
at **ndc.x 1.44** — 44 % past the right edge of a portrait phone — because the
camera's own yaw settle (+0.16 rad) turned the lens the other way. The
arithmetic was right about the world and silent about the shot.

**A FIXED WORLD SIZE IS NOT A SIZE.** The climbing flare was in frame,
correctly placed, fully opaque — and **seven pixels** on a 390 px phone,
because a 0.5 m ball at 88 m is 0.3°. It is scaled by its distance from the
camera now and holds 38–50 px the whole way up. The first contact sheet showed
an empty sky and every number said the flare was there.
