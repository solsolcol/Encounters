## 0.1 · The chapter on one screen

1. **The story is the case file's** (S1·16): a man asks Grandmaster Pho to see his colleague, Grace, at her home (R3); she keeps going back to a
   man who treated her badly; Pho finds a love spell and breaks it while Master Zavier holds the holy water; she comes back
   to herself. At three that morning, at home, something crushes his head from every side, looking for whoever helped her.
   He calls his teacher, who sets a barrier round him over the phone. *"I had thought I was only there to assist my teacher."*
2. **Four movements:** THE ROOM (evening) · THE BREAKING · HOME · THREE O'CLOCK. The decision sits at three o'clock, his
   phone in his hand. Four endings, each a whole story; in every one the barrier and the dawn.
3. **The look:** the spell is a thin RED cord (Master Z's own trial gives the keepsake a reddish cord); his teacher's
   protection is WHITE thread and Pho's GOLD. Evening is warm lamplight; three o'clock is the city's cold glow; dawn is gold.
4. **The horror comes back as PRESSURE, not monsters:** the picture closes in from every side, the glass doors tick as if
   pressed from outside, and in Spirit Vision his own footsteps light up red from the front door to his bed.
5. **Three new ways to play:** KEEP THE WATER STILL (the ritual), the PRESSURE walk (it presses hardest while you move;
   standing still eases it), CLOSE THE CIRCLE (lay the white thread round yourself while your teacher chants on the phone).
6. **Places:** a shophouse row at blue hour and Grace's own rented flat above it, where the evening happens (Chad, R3:
   "it should be the lady's house"; new); his own home, a high-floor flat with the city outside its glass (new).
7. **People:** Grandmaster Pho; Grace and Marcus (new, made here his way). The man and the master who made the spell are
   never seen, and nobody is made a villain.

## 1 · Where it sits in the story

**What the last chapter left him.** Episode 4 ended one afternoon in Grandmaster Pho's room in Singapore: the collector's
twenty pieces in a black bag sealed with white thread, and the card's sentence, "Cultivation, control, experience and
discernment — that is the real path, and there is no shortcut through any of it." He is still, in his own mind, the one
who carries the bag and the string (chapter 5's plan §3). This chapter opens on the next case, on another day, after
dark, at a stranger's door (R3: her own home), so nothing re-shows a journey (v24.56).

**What this chapter is.** Episode 5 is *The Signal And The Translator*: the years his gift is tested, and the casebook's
hinge between the two episodes is this chapter's last sentence. Until three o'clock he believes the work is his
teacher's and he only holds the bowl. The chapter is the night that belief breaks.

**What it leaves for the four after it** (v26.0: an ending never runs ahead). It stops at dawn the next morning, the white
thread on his wrist. It does not teach him to help anyone on his own: chapter 2 (S1·17) is him trying exactly that, a
friend taken over in front of him, forty-five minutes alone, then a VIDEO call to his teacher in Thailand, and his teacher
murmuring the language he himself once murmured when his Lersi came in (its period is his third year, "I was still an
amateur": so here too he does nothing on his own). So here the call is a VOICE call on the speaker, his teacher is in Singapore, and nobody speaks in
that language. Chapter 3 (S1·18, a hotel room), chapter 4 (S1·19, the tree, where he leads) and chapter 5 (S1·20, the
mirror, the season's finale) are not foreshadowed by name. Pho's last line ("From now on, we prepare first") is the next

where other practitioners are involved. One more thing worth saying plainly, whatever you believe about spells: when
someone keeps returning to a person who treats them badly, that pattern also has very ordinary explanations, and it can
be dangerous in very ordinary ways. Whoever they are, they need people around them who will listen and help them stay

## 3 · What the research settled (docs/research/V29.0-LOVESPELL.md; the second reader wins)

- Said as popular belief, in a character's mouth: love charms (เสน่ห์) exist and are believed to work through a person's
  photograph, name, birth date or something of theirs; holy water is made with chanting and traditionally traced to the
  story of Ānanda sprinkling water from the Buddha's bowl round Vesālī (so the breaking's chant is the Ratana Sutta); a
  candle's wax may be dripped into the water; white sacred thread is strung round a house while monks chant, against
  evil spirits, and tied on the wrist; the Jinapañjara is one of Thailand's most loved protective chants.
- **Not attested, so never said as belief:** that a spell's maker looks for whoever undid it (only that sorcery can
  rebound on whoever made it, ของย้อน), and that three o'clock is a special hour. Both stand in the chapter only as Master
  Z's own account of his own night: what happened to him, and what his teacher told him then. No line calls three o'clock
  anything but the time.
- Love-spell services are advertised in Singapore today: nothing of that trade is shown (no seller, no price, no recipe).

## 4 · Creative direction

**4.1 · The idea in one line.** What you lift off someone, for a moment, you are holding. The chapter's axis in the
episode is RETURN: a consequence finds its way home along the path you walked.

**4.2 · Tone.** Spiritual education, never cheap horror (v23.0). The evening is quiet, sad and kind: a frightened woman,
a worried friend, a teacher who never raises his voice. The night is dread, built from pressure and sound, never from a
face in the dark: nothing jumps out, nothing is shown that the case does not say (v9.5: what is already there is the
beat). The dawn is warm and human.

**4.4 · The camera's grammar.**
- Play and most of each film are **his eyes** (the grown Master Zavier is never seen: v24.56; first person even in the
  circle, his own crossed legs and hands under the lens). A film may cut away from his eyes only to an insert of a thing
  (the cord, the bowl, the plate, the wax) or to a person who is looking at him (v21.30). The opening's crane down the
  street, before he steps out of the taxi, is the one exception: it watches Marcus wait for them.
- **Evening: still and patient.** Slow pushes, held frames, eye-level, a teacher's pace. **Night: tight.** The lens
  narrows as the pressure rises (play), handheld drift in the films (a smooth function of the clock, never noise), low
  angles from the floor in the circle. **Dawn: wide and level again.**
- **Phone and desktop.** Every shot below names what it must HOLD on both screens; a portrait phone sees a third of the
  width (v21.8), so subjects stand near the centre and a phone gets the wider lens in rooms (v24.21). Lenses are given as
  vertical degrees, **phone / desk** (the game's play lens is 72).
- **Slow motion only once in play's story** (the cord snapping) and once in each ending (the threads meeting the gold),
  each on the film's own clock.

**4.5 · The motifs.**
- **The red cord.** The keepsake's cord (the trial's own "reddish cord"); in Spirit Vision the spell is a thin red line
  from Grace's chest out under the door, pulsing slowly toward it. When the cord snaps, a wisp of red leaps across the
  table and slips round the wrist of the hand holding the bowl, and is gone (shown once, quickly; nobody in the room
  sees it). At night red threads come under his door and through his glass, and follow his footsteps. Red appears
  nowhere else in the chapter's light.
- **The water.** The bowl he must keep still; the same water, bottled, on his altar at night, trembling with each wave,
  and still again the moment the barrier holds.
- **The white thread.** Pho ties the packet with it; at night he lays his own spool of it round himself (he carries it
  for his teacher, as he carried the bag and the string in episode 4); at dawn his teacher ties it on the same wrist the
  red touched. The chapter's first object in his hands is a lighter; its last is that thread.
- **The gold.** Grandmaster Pho's alone (v25.5): only the barrier glows gold.

**4.6 · Nobody is the villain, and her story is told with care** (v21.30). The man who treated her badly is never seen
or heard: only his messages lighting up her phone, their words never shown. The master who made the spell is never seen and
never named; Pho does not curse him and will not say who he is ("Who it was does not matter tonight"). Grace is never
blamed and never mocked: Pho's first words about her are "She is not weak". Marcus is a good friend, awkward and
persistent. The case's own last sentence is spoken in the room (Pho to Marcus: "Don't let her meet him alone") and is on
the card, because the ordinary danger is real.

## 8 · Play

**8.1 · THE ROOM** (phase `room`, about 3 minutes, in Grace's front room; he kneels at his place and does not walk, as

1. "Pour water into the bowl." (a press on the jug at his knee: his hands pour it into the brass bowl on the cloth.)
2. "Light the candle." (a press on the lighter.)
3. "Watch Grace. Find what is pulling her." Four things, each a press on the thing itself (with the aim dot on a

   - **her phone** (it lights up every 20 seconds): Z (thought) "Every time it lit up, she leaned toward the door."

   - **her reflection** in the window, beside the shutter's edge (seen from where he kneels): Z "In the glass, she was

   - **her hands** on her bag's strap, twisting toward the door: Z "Her hands never stopped. Like she was already

   - **the packet** in her open bag: Z "Something in her bag. Wrapped in cloth, tied with red cord." **P:** "Ask her

     ask where it came from; don't touch it.)
4. "Open Spirit Vision and look at Grace." (the eye button asks, as chapter 4 taught it; this beat is never left to

Twice, on the phase's clock (40 s and 110 s), Grace stands up: **G:** "I should go." **M:** "Grace, please." **P:**
"Sit. A little longer." She sits. Each time her reflection turns first, and in Spirit Vision the red line pulls taut.
When the four are found and the thread seen: the film THE KEEPSAKE (§9.2).

**8.2 · THE BREAKING · "Keep the water still"** (about 90 seconds; the hold-it-steady minigame of episode 3 chapter 4's

  ring. Words: phone "Hold the bowl steady. Drag to keep the light inside the ring." desk "Hold the bowl steady. Move the

- The lens looks down into the bowl in his hands (phone 50 / desk 44). The candle's reflection on the water is the mark;

  Between surges Pho dips his fan in the bowl and flicks water over Grace and the packet (the scene's part: nothing to

- Graded on the share of the time the light stayed in the ring; **never failed**: when it stays out too long, a little

**8.3 · THREE O'CLOCK · the PRESSURE walk** (phase `night`, about 3 minutes).
- He wakes in bed at 3:00. Order 1: "Something is pressing on your head. Get up." (a press.) Order 2: "Get to your
  phone." It is on its charger on the dining table, about 9 m away, through the bedroom door and the passage; marked
  from the moment it is the goal (v14.8).
- **The pressure** (0 to 1) comes in waves, one every 7 to 9 seconds (up by 0.15 over 1.5 s, then back a little). It
  presses harder while he moves and while Spirit Vision is open, and eases slowly while he stands still: so the way to
  the phone is walked between the waves. It is SEEN (the picture pinched in from all four edges, a vignette, colour
  draining from the edges, a slight colour split: §13), FELT (the heart on the HUD and the phone's buzz, 70 to 140 a
  minute) and HEARD (the world's sound closing down to his blood and breath). His steps slow as it rises. What the phase
  obeys is on the screen (v8.1): the pinch itself, and the heart.
- **Told once, plainly** (a short tip, no timer): "It presses hardest when you move or have Spirit Vision open. Stand
  still and it eases."
- **Never a fail state:** at full pressure he sinks to one knee for two seconds and it falls back to 0.7. Nothing ends
  the phase but the phone, and the phone is always reachable (v7.4).
- **The beat that is never left to chance** (v21.17): at the bedroom door the strongest wave forces Spirit Vision open
  by itself for four seconds: **his own footsteps glowing red from the front door to his bed**, and thin red threads
  coming in under the front door and through the glass, feeling along the floor and following them. Z (thought) "My own
  footsteps. Something was following them." After that he may open it himself, at a price.
- **What he meets** (on the phase's clock, each with a reason): the glass of water on his bedside trembling with each
  wave; the pictures in the passage tapping the wall; the glass doors ticking and creaking as if pressed from outside;

  headache. Something was pressing in from every side." / "The water I brought home was shaking."
