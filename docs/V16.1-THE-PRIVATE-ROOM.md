## Decisions (mine, stated for Chad)

- **The room is a pocket** 250 m west of the wat (x −250), the way the film's
  memories are: the far plane is 160 m, so inside the room the wat is not drawn
  at all (and the wat never sees the room). `bounds.minX` goes to −262; in the
  wat the west compound wall still stops the player (it was 1 m inside the old
  bound). Its floor is at the sala's height (0.16), so every `SALA.floor + …`
  in the yant code is still true there.
- **The yant dais moves whole** into the room (the rods, the ink, the lamp,
  the stool, the Ajarn, the man under the needle); the sala's dais stays
  where it was and becomes the MONK's, dressed with a monk's things (the alms
  bowl, the lustral-water bowl and its whisk, the fan, the white string, the
  thermos).
- **The monk is a stand-in** (the botak recruit, recoloured saffron, seated)
  until Chad's model; added to docs/E3-MODELS.md.
- **One engine seam, `kit.warm()`**: the curtain's own warm (textures, every
  program, the light states, one drawn frame) run on demand under a black the
  chapter has raised, with the loading word if it takes more than a moment.
  The fixture declares it; fixturetest proves it.
- **Lit without a light** (v9.4): the room's lamps are emissive with painted
  pools; the room's look is a `kit.daylight` preset (warm, dim) applied in the
  black and handed back when he leaves.
