and the reveal should show the real thing.**

## 1 · The flashbacks show what they remember

Chapter 4's scene A is VISUAL RECALL (v4.91): three black-box dioramas
forty metres off the flat, each built to its own chapter's recipe, so the
boy is looking at chapter 1, chapter 2 and chapter 3 as he remembers
them. Two of the three were still built out of what those chapters had
before the real art arrived.

**The notes.** The five drifted notes in the void-deck memory were the
CANVAS-DRAWN note — `makeHellNote()`, the placeholder chapter 1 shipped
with until v3.8 gave it the photographed one (`assets/hellnote.webp`).
So the memory of the void deck contained the one object in the frame that
was never in that room. It now loads the real art through the same
`loadImageTexture()` path chapter 1 uses, with chapter 1's own brightening
(colour x1.75, emissive 0.20 + the emissive map) — which is not decoration:
a saturated print at this light level collapses into a dark tile and reads
worse than the flat card it replaced. Measured in the running game, all
five notes: `{w:1024, h:614, kind:"bitmap", colour:1.75, emissiveMap:true}`.

It builds with the drawn note and SWAPS when the bytes land, exactly as
chapter 1 does, so a slow download costs the photograph and never the
notes.

**The bed.** The bedroom memory was five boxes — a frame, a mattress, a
blanket, a pillow and a headboard — while chapter 2 itself has had Chad's
real bed since v5.17. It is the same `bed` asset now, loaded into the
memory group, and the five primitives are hidden only once the model
arrives (a failed download leaves the boxes, never an empty diorama).

Scaled **by WIDTH to 0.95 m**, which is chapter 2's own `BED.w`, and never
by length — the same rule v5.17 wrote down, and for the same reason: the
bed's left edge is one wall of the GAP, and the gap is what this memory is
about. The dark strip at x -0.87 is right beside it. Measured in the
running game: `{hidden:5, shownMeshes:1, tris:4900, w:0.95, h:0.824,
len:2.121, floor:0}` — a 0.95 x 2.12 bed standing on the memory's floor.

Both assets are additions to chapter 4's `assets` list, which is what puts
them in its preload set. Neither is in the chapter's PRESENT: there is
still no hell note in the flat in chapter 4 (the tang-ki finds it in
chapter 5), and no bed — they exist only inside the two memories.

## 2 · The reveal

The film's find was: he reaches under the thinking chair, and the shot
cuts to the note held up in his hand in the morning light. v5.07 built
that shot and it took real work — the note rides his right hand BONE, the
hand is put where the shot wants it by freezing the magic take one fifth
in, and a solver aimed a plane in a bone's frame at the lens, because a
hand bone's axes are nothing a person can reason about. It worked, and
Chad is right that it reads as a man **trying** to hold up a note rather
than a man showing you one.

So the shot is gone and the beat is now: he stands, he carries it the
short step to the table, he **sets it down**, and the film cuts to the
wood.

**Where it lands is not a new number.** It is `NOTE_HOME` — the spot the
chapter's own play state already puts the note in, on his end of the table,
on the room-facing edge, clear of the pot and both cups. The film now ends
with the note exactly where the chapter begins with it, instead of
somewhere the engine has to move it from at `restore()`. Measured: it
travels chair -> hand -> table and lands at (0.98, 0.776, -0.27) at
scale 1, which is NOTE_HOME to the millimetre.

**Where he STANDS to reach it** is measured against the furniture the v5.03
audit boxed. (0.72, 0.28) is 0.37 m off the table's near edge, 0.11 m
clear of the thinking chair's collision column, and 0.61 m from the note:
a reach with a lean, not a walk through the dining set. That distinction
is the whole reason v5.03 exists — cutscene paths obey no collision, so a
path that looks fine is not the same as a path that is fine.

The beats, in order:

Two numbers worth keeping. The mid-shot camera started at 0.9 m behind
him and his robe was most of the frame with the table he was walking to
out of it; it sits **1.6 m** back now (`MIDCAM` at -0.25, 1.50, 1.55,
pitch -0.274), so the walk has a room in it. And the turn is done the
short way — `d = atan2(sin(d), cos(d))` — because the raw difference
between his facing at the chair and his facing at the table crosses PI
and he would have spun the long way round.

`aimNoteInHand` is **deleted** with the shot it existed for. `noteToHand`
stays, because he still carries the note; what is gone is the machinery
that made a plane in a bone's frame face a camera. Its counterpart
`noteToTable` is four lines: unparent, re-home, scale back to 1 (the hand
parenting divides it by the bone's ~95x world scale), copy the position
and rotation the chapter already stores.

## 3 · The sound

`noteset` — a sheet of paper set down on a hard wood surface, a light
contact with a little slide. 0.99 s, 16,344 bytes mp3 / 8,535 bytes opus,
peak-matched at -11.2 dB to the takes around it, metadata stripped, both
encodings, in chapter 5's pack (only ch5 names it, so `build.py` computes
it into ch5's pack, not the shared one). Its `STING_SAMPLE` row is beside
`notepull`, the sound of the note coming OUT from under the chair — the
two halves of the same movement.

Verified the cue actually fires, which needed one correction to how the
film is probed: **`__enc.cine.seek()` sets `cine.paused = true`**, so
seeking past a cue never fires it. Calling `cine.resume()` after the seek
and letting the film run across 30.2 gives `{"kind":"noteset",
"how":"muted"}` in the cue log.
