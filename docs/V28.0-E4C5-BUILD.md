## 2 · The rules of the road (his, from this week; CLAUDE.md has them in full)

- ONE generation of each model. A model that comes back with a fault is NOT made again: it is recorded with its
  pictures and put to him (v28.0, R8). A job that failed outright is re-run unchanged.
- The effort is fitted to the work (R13): max for design and judgement, low or medium for routine; no helper for what a
  few steps can do.
- Every build is a full release, deployed the moment it boots its chapter; the tests after, on the live build.
- At most two browsers at once: every browser goes through `sh tools/bslot.sh <command>`.
- Several helpers work in ONE tree: a helper saves only its own paths, with `sh tools/gitsave.sh "<v28.0 message>"
  <paths>`; never `git add -A`. Only the engine's builder runs `npm run build` until the tracker says otherwise.
- THE MACHINE IS SHORT OF ROOM (2.0 GB free at the start). A build is always `ENC_NOZIP=1 ENC_DISTLINK=1 npm run build`
  (no release zip; the assets hard-linked into dist). Before each model, `df`: under 700 MB free, stop generating and
  report. The local copies of older chapters' sources (8.6 GiB, all on the sources branch) may be cleared only on his
  word: asked 21 Nov.
- Privacy and keys: CLAUDE.md's rules and the plan's (no key printed, no signed link in a file, no real face or name to
  a picture service, his photographs never committed).

## 3 · Master Zavier's kit, as redesigned for R14 (the direction; docs/V28.0-FIGHT.md is the specification once written)

He is defence and support (R3), and each of his acts does something Grandmaster Pho's cannot:
1. BIND: a bubble that locks one Prai where it is. It cannot flee, dart or hide. Fire always lands on a bound one, and
   the golden shockwave gathers every bound one it passes, at once.
2. SMELL: only he finds a Prai that has gone unseen; Grandmaster Pho cannot aim at what is not revealed.
3. THE CAGE LENT: his Lersi's cage over his teacher for a few seconds: the shockwave's wind-up cannot be broken in it.
Cooldowns run on while the other is played, so switching is rewarded. Neither wins alone. Gold stays Grandmaster Pho's.
