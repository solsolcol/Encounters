# v28.0 · THE SEAMS FOR THE TAG MODE (60 to 65)

Built 21 Nov 2026 for episode 4 chapter 5's third-person tag-team fight (docs/V28.0-E4C5-PLAN.md §5, §12; Chad's R3, R14).
This is the reference for whoever puts the fight into the chapter (M8). The engine knows NO ability: it owns the camera,
the input, the two bodies' movement and collision, the buttons, the marks and the save; the chapter owns the Prai, what
every act means, the pacing and the words. The fixture (`src/chapters/chtest.js`) declares all of it and plays a
plain-shapes fight with it; `fixturetest.mjs` proves it on, off and absent (section 8).

Every seam is opt-in and a pass-through when absent: no chapter before episode 4 chapter 5 declares `tag`, calls
`kit.button`, `kit.retry` or names the `sour` tint, so every branch these add is behind a flag that only those calls set
(section 9 says what episode 1 can reach, line by line).

## 1 · Seam 60 · THE TAG MODE

### 1.1 The declaration (the chapter's DATA)

```js
tag: {
  heading: 0,                                // the court's heading (radians, yaw.rotation.y): the lens always faces down it
  boom: { back: 4.0, up: 3.1, pitch: 26 },   // metres behind and above the PLAYED one's feet, degrees down (a landscape screen)
  portrait: { back, up, pitch, fov },        // an upright screen's own (absent: `boom` for both)
  fov: 72,                                   // the lens while the mode is on (vertical degrees)
  camBox: { minX, maxX, minZ, maxZ, minY, maxY },   // where the lens may stand; it slows to a stop at its edges
  soft: 0.6,                                 // metres over which the box's edges ease the lens
  glide: 0.4,                                // seconds from behind one to behind the other at a switch
  lock: 0.6,                                 // no other switch for this long
  radius: 0.42,                              // each body's disc: the two never stand closer than both
  speed: 1.2,                                // a body's pace (m/s), unless the body names its own
  turn: 9,                                   // how fast a body turns to face where it walks
  eye: 1.62,                                 // the pivot over his feet ("where he is" for every distance)
  keys: { switch: 'KeyF' }
}
```
Every field is optional; the numbers above are the defaults (the plan's §5.6 camera). Any of them may also be given to
`kit.tag(opts)` for one opening. Without a `tag` declaration `kit.tag` refuses.

### 1.2 The verbs

| verb | what it does |
|---|---|
| `kit.tag(opts)` | OPENS the mode. `opts.bodies`: `[{ id, obj, name, pic, speed, radius, faceOff, acts: [{ id, slot, label, icon, key, waitSay }] }]`; `opts.played` (a body's id), `opts.onAct(report)`, `opts.targets` (a list or a function answering one, below). Answers `true`, or `false` outside play, in a film, without the declaration, without a body, or while it is already on. An open minigame is ended (aborted), as a film ends one |
| `kit.tag(null, how)` | LEAVES it. `how`: `'restore'` (the default: the rig back exactly as the mode found it), `'body'` (first person where the played one stands, looking where he faces), `{ x, y, z, ry }`, or `null` (left alone). Under a film, the film's own snapshot is written instead, so its end puts the rig there |
| `kit.tag()`, `kit.tagState()` | READ: `{ on, live, played, t, lock, gliding, held, bodies: [{ id, x, z, ry, v, moving, played }], count }`. `v` is the ground a body covered this frame (m/s): what its walking take's rate follows (feet that do not slide: CLAUDE.md v26.0). `lock` is the switch's lock still to run, to the millisecond rounded UP: it reads 0 exactly when a switch is free (one function, `tagLockLeft`, for the read, the refusal and the ring) |
| `kit.tagSwitch(to, { force })` | a switch the chapter asks for (to the next, or the body named). Refused inside the lock or under the switch's own lock (`kit.tagAct('switch', { blocked })`) unless `force`; never outside live play |
| `kit.tagAct(id, p)` | an act's state on its button: `wait` (0..1, the share of its wait still to run: its ring), `fill` (0..1, a hold's progress), `pulse` (it asks: grows and shrinks), `blocked` (a sentence: dimmed, and a press says it), `hidden`, `on` (lit), `label`, `icon`, `waitSay` (words for a press inside the wait). The id `'switch'` is the switch's own (`pulse`, `blocked`). An act hidden or locked while it is held is let go (its up said, `cut: 'hidden'` or `'blocked'`) |
| `kit.tagBody(id, p)` | a body's: `speed`, `root` (it does not walk), `face` (an angle it turns to and keeps; `null` gives it back to its walk), `name`, `pic`, `share` (0..1 or null: the ring on its mark at the screen's edge) |
| `kit.tagPlace(id, x, z, { secs, ry })` | a body moved by the chapter (the unplayed one stepping aside, a fight begun again): eased over `secs` of the mode's clock, or at once; refused into a wall or out of bounds |
| `kit.tagMarks(list or fn)` | the targets: `[{ id, at: [x, y, z], r, color, picked, dim }]`. Each is a ring drawn over the point while it is in the picture; a tap or a click within `r` px (default 32, at least 18) is a pick |
| `kit.tagCount(left, of)` | the count under the objective (`of` dots, `left` of them lit; one goes out as it falls); `null` takes it off |
| `kit.tagHold()` | the run held again as it stands now (the place a Continue lands stays where the mode opened) |

Act ids must be unique across both bodies and must not be `'switch'` or `'target'`. One act per slot per body. A key
named by an act or the switch is the mode's while it is live; F, V, E, Space, R and Q are swallowed in the mode even when
no act names them (the torch, the eye, the hand and the weapon are not this mode's).

### 1.3 What the chapter is told (`onAct`)

| report | when |
|---|---|
| `{ act, who, phase: 'down', by, t }` | an act pressed and allowed (`by`: `'key'`, `'tap'`, `'click'`) |
| `{ act, who, phase: 'up', by, t, cut }` | let go; `cut` when the engine ended it: `'switch'`, `'pause'` (a menu, a minigame, a film, the tab hidden), `'retry'`, `'leave'`, `'hidden'`, `'blocked'` |
| `{ act: 'switch', from, to, by, t }` | the played one changed (`by` `'kit'` for `kit.tagSwitch`) |
| `{ act: 'target', id, who, by, t }` | a target's mark tapped or clicked |

Refused presses are never told (a wait or a lock: the button shakes, and a lock says its sentence).
**One act a frame**: presses are queued and the frame takes ONE, the main first (then second, third, switch, target);
the rest are dropped and their lift says nothing. A press and its lift inside one frame give its down and its up.

### 1.4 The rig, the lens and the walk

- `yaw` stays "where he is": the PLAYED one's head (`obj.position` + `eye`), turned to `heading`; `pitch` is the boom's.
  Every distance in the engine and the chapter (hotspots, the ghost, sound, a chapter's "me") is the played one's.
- The lens goes back on a boom, `camera.position` (nothing else writes it), DERIVED on the frame: behind and above his
  feet, eased inside `camBox`, clamped to `bounds`, and pulled in front of every blocker between it and his head (in at
  once, out eased). It is exactly zero outside the mode and under every film (`playCineFn` zeroes it on its first line).
- At a switch the lens glides from behind one to behind the other (a smoothstep of the mode's clock over `glide`): the
  switch's own frame shows it exactly where it stood.
- The stick (the left 45 % of a phone's screen) and W A S D / the arrows move the played body BY THE SCREEN (up is away
  down the court), at its `speed`, eased as the walk is, on the mode's clock. It slides along the chapter's blockers (the
  engine's own test, asked from where IT stands: `collideFrom`), never closer to the other body than their two discs,
  rides `stage.groundAt` and turns to face where it walks (`atan2(vx, vz) + faceOff`: a Mixamo body faces +z at 0).
- THE LOOK IS DEAD: a drag and a moved mouse turn nothing; no pointer lock (a click is a pick or the main act).
- The played one's covered pace drives the engine's footsteps (`stage.stepSound`), as the player's walk does.

### 1.5 What steps aside while it is on

The first-person hands' pass (never drawn: the mode adds itself to the pass's own test; `handsRoot.visible` is not
written), the weapon (no `weaponUp`, no reticle, no shot, no reload, no aim), the bag (its button, and `I`), the torch's
button and F, the eye (its button, and V), the hotspots, their marks and the badge, the pile's prompt, the red edge, a
button of the chapter's own (seam 61), the hint line. The hands are taken back at the opening as a film takes them
(`kit.pray`, `kit.handPose`, `kit.hold`, `kit.hands`).

### 1.6 Saves, clocks, films, losses

- **The save is HELD** as the run stood when the mode opened (its stats, the bag, the ward, the phase, the conduct, and
  `at`: where he stood in first person) until it is left: every autosave, a film's save and a return to the title write
  that. A Continue lands there, in first person, and the chapter (reading its phase) briefs the fight again. The mode's
  hold comes before a minigame's (`ev.save0`).
- **The clock** (`kit.tagState().t`) is wall time while the mode is LIVE (in play, no window or minigame, no film, the
  tab seen), a tenth of a second at most a frame. Under a menu, a panel, a film or a hidden tab nothing moves and nothing
  the chapter drives from this clock fills. Drive every wait, cooldown and Prai from it.
- **A film from play inside the mode** (`kit.film`): the mode is suspended (no boom, no input, no presses held), and
  resumed at the film's end (the rig derived from the played body wherever the film left it; the hands still not drawn;
  nothing saved but the held run). **Leave the mode before** a film that wants the first-person hands.
- **A decision** (`startDecision`) leaves the mode first (`'restore'`). **The engine's own loss** (`lose`, `kit.lose`)
  leaves it (`null`) before its film. **kitReset** (a restart, a Continue, a change of chapter) leaves it (`null`): the
  caller places the rig.
- **A touch already down** when the mode goes live (at its opening, after a menu or a film) is not its stick and not a
  tap until it is lifted.

### 1.7 Traps for the chapter (read before M8)

1. Do not put the two bodies' own boxes in `stage.blockers` while the mode is on: a body inside its own box cannot move.
   The mode keeps them apart itself (`radius`).
2. The engine writes the PLAYED body's `position` (x, z and y from the ground) and `rotation.y` every live frame, and
   both bodies' y. Move a body through `kit.tagPlace`, never by writing its position (the played one would fight it).
3. Levels of detail chosen by the distance from the LENS (chapter 4's `peopleLevels`) see the lens 4 to 5 m off: pin the
   heroes' levels for the fight (the survey's 12.10), and make their culling spheres cover every take they play (12.18).
4. Spirit Vision's eye is the lens (src/vision.js): the mode hides the eye and refuses V, but a chapter that opens the
   vision in the mode gets its wave from behind him. Not changed (the survey's 12.3).
5. The torch hangs on the lens: a chapter with a torch puts it out before the mode (F is the switch).
6. Act keys must not be W, A, S, D, the arrows, Escape, M or I; the switch's key wins over an act's.
7. `kit.health`'s bar keeps the first-person HUD's margins (fixed in lifeFrame): not moved for the mode's cluster.
8. Captions: the chapter shows a spoken line's words with `kit.captions` at the line's own start; the engine does not
   read src/voicelines.js at run time (nothing does), so "one copy of the words" is the assembler's job (the chapter's
   caption table made from the same source as the registry's rows).
9. A body keeps its way across a pause: a walk let go under the menu (or a minigame, or a film) coasts on for a moment
   (about 0.6 s of the mode's clock) once play is back, as his own first-person walk does. A chapter that wants both still
   after a film places them (`kit.tagPlace` stops a body dead).
10. A step that would cross a wall or the other body's disc is refused whole, as the first-person walk's is: on a slow
   frame (a tenth of a second at most) a body stops up to one step short (12 cm at 1.2 m/s; 2 cm at 60 frames a second).
11. The other one's mark at the screen's edge stands inside the mode's margins (an upright screen: under the count, above
   the cluster; a landscape: clear of the buttons' column) and above a standing caption with room for a refusal's words
   over it (the caption's measured top, `--capTop`, plus 62 px).

## 2 · Seam 61 · A BUTTON OF THE CHAPTER'S OWN (`kit.button`)

`kit.button(id, { icon, label, key, wait, pulse, locked, hidden, on, onPress })`; every field optional and kept until
changed; `kit.button(id, null)` takes it away. A round button in the right-hand column under the torch's and the eye's
(each 56 px a step), for a sense the chapter gives him in first-person play (SMELL, on N). `icon`: what an event's item
takes (a canvas, a function that makes one, or a hosted asset key); with none, the first letters of `label` are drawn.
`wait` 0..1 its ring (a press inside it shakes the button and is not told); `pulse` asks (the eye's language); `locked` a
sentence (dimmed; a press says it, never pressed); `onPress({ id, by })` is told of a press by a tap or a click
(`'tap'`) or its key (`'key'`). Refused outside play, under any window, minigame or film, and in the tag mode (hidden
there: the mode's own buttons are its acts). On a phone the objective box narrows to clear the column, as it does for
the torch and the eye (`body.hasCBtn`). kitReset takes every one away.

## 6 · Seam 65 · A LOSS THAT BEGINS AGAIN (`kit.retry`)

`kit.retry({ title, line, button, more, onPress })` → a Promise of `{ button: 'again' | 'more' }`, or `false` (refused
unless the mode is on and holding the run, in play). Its own card and screen state (`retry`): the words the chapter's
(`title`, `line`, `button`; `more` a second button only when given), the sheet's (`hud.retryLabel`, `hud.retryAgain`)
when it gives none; the court stays seen behind a veil. The press that lost cannot also retry: the buttons wait 0.6 s.
On BEGIN AGAIN (a click, Enter or Space): the run put back EXACTLY as the mode holds it (his stats, the bag, the worn
piece's charge, the bookmark and what play had earned: the held save through `applyState`), play again, and the chapter
told (`onPress`, then the Promise). Nothing in the world is moved: the chapter begins its fight again itself (its Prai,
its count, `kit.tagPlace` for the bodies). kitReset closes an open card (its Promise answers `null`).
