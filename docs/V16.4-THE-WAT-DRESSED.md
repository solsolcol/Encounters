  material; `LOW` thins pigeons, flags and bells.
- Nothing downloads: all canvas / primitives. Two new sounds only.
- Photographed from where the player stands, desktop AND phone, before
  release; photos to Chad before it goes live.

## Chad's changes mid-build (30 Sep)

1. *"the previous red gold roof pattern was better, apply that pattern back to
   this new version of the roof"* — the gables are the red-and-gold carving
   again (matGoldC); the stepped roof, the porch gable, the gold blades and
   the naga heads stay. The mosaic panel (makeGablePanel) is deleted.
2. *"remove the mural, it looks bad"* — gone, with its dado; makeMural deleted.
3. *"the ceiling inside is too much, remove that star pattern and make it
   simple and clean"* — plain pale teak boards (makeCeilPlain).
4. *"elevate the temple grounds, with stairs at the front leading up to the
   temple. The existing naga should align on the left and right of the
   stairs just like a real thai temple."* — THE SALA ON A HIGH BASE:
   `SALA.floor` 0.16 -> 1.10, a six-riser staircase down the front, the
   naga balustrades running down both sides of it and rearing at the foot.
   Needs an ENGINE SEAM (the 21st): `stage.groundAt(x, z)` — the eye rides
   the ground (eased, snapped on a teleport) and collision samples 1.0 m
   above the ground rather than above zero; a pose's `y` is measured from
   the ground. Absent -> 0 everywhere, so every other chapter is unchanged
   by construction. The private room, the walkway and the kuti rise with
   the floor (they are built at SALA.floor). Every absolute height in the
   sala, the room, the scenes and the poses is audited; walktest learns the
   ground.
5. *"Remove the flags, there are no flags in temples"* — the six flagpoles
   (the Thai flag and the dhamma-wheel flag) are gone with their painters;
   the long hanging Lanna banners (tung) stay — offered to Chad as a
   one-word removal if he counts them as flags too.
