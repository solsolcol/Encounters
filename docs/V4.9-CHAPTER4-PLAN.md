## Traps to not re-learn

- Films start on BLACK and own their fade (playCineFn startFade=1 — v4.6).
- The sit is not an engine feature: E opens the decision as always, and
  EVERY scene begins with the sit-down beat (camera to 1.18 at the table)
  so the player always sees themselves sit. Scenes B/C/D then stand up.
- Flashback sets live in ch4's OWN world; the camera cuts to them through
  black. Fog must be widened by the scene and restored by the scene.
- Sounds reused from other chapters (clock, fan, doorcreak, seat) simply
  become SHARED in the computed split — deliberate, costs ~100 KB at boot.
- No hell note ANYWHERE in ch4 — not on the table, not in flashback MEM1.
- Door signs are written down before staging: front door hinge at its −x
  jamb, shut 0, OPEN −1.45 (swings INWARD into the flat, toward −z).
