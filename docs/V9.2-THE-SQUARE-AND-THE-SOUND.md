## 1 · The green blocks, and the float

**THE BLOCKS.** v9.1 hid the frame, the mattresses, the pillows, the sheets,
the folded blankets and the wire bases behind Chad's bunk model, and Chad
still had green boxes on some beds. Photographed from his own eye at the foot
of bed one, what was left is what `supersede` had never covered: the POSTS
and the RAILS. They are the only bed parts the model does not restate, and
they had survived every pass because from across the room a green post
against a green frame reads as the frame.

`supersede` now takes `bedGeo.post`, `bedGeo.rail` and `bedGeo.railEnd`
alongside the six it already held — sixteen meshes to twenty-one.

**THE FLOAT**, and the measurement that mattered. The first attempt made it
WORSE: 2.0 cm off the bed became 4.2. The deck height came back as **0.591**,
which is `BUNK.deckLo` 0.592 — the mattress top in the MODEL's own
coordinates, before the group that holds it has been scaled or placed.

> `Box3.setFromObject` updates the object it is given and its DESCENDANTS.
> It never updates its ancestors. Measuring a child of a freshly positioned,
> freshly scaled parent reads the parent's stale matrix and gets the answer
> in the wrong frame entirely.

One `m.updateWorldMatrix(true, true)` before the traverse. Measured after:
**statues 0.0 cm off the mattress, rigs −0.1 cm** — from 2.0.

The deck is read from the model rather than assumed, and every sleeper is
re-seated through `restOnDeck` / `settleBed`, so a sleeper who arrives before
the bunk model does is put right the moment it lands. A rig placed against a
number the model has not supplied yet is the whole class of bug here.

Everything Chad listed as "should stay the same" is the same code: the

| | v9.1 | v9.2 |
|---|---|---|
| the line | the balcony, x 7.6 | the square, **x 14.0** (Chad's pick) |
| they face | the square | the other block (+x), unchanged in sign |

The two of them go PAST the rank and turn back down it — `ry` −π/2 faces −x,
which is the line, the balcony and the bunk door behind it. That is the
inspection Chad described, and it is the opposite of where they used to
stand. They reach it round the rank's flank rather than through it: `to.via`
adds waypoints to `marchLegs`, which is why nobody is walked through a man.

The parapet's removal is what makes the walk possible at all, and removing it
outright rather than cutting a gate in it was Chad's instruction. The roof
columns and the two end walls stay, so the balcony still reads as a balcony.

The square itself got what the fall-in now needs: a yellow line where the
rank forms, the car-park bays already there from v8.9, and a far block for
them to face.

## 4 · The sound

Chad asked for more, everywhere, and named one thing he wanted: other
platoons marching outside. Three sounds, ElevenLabs flow
`Qa5DO5sUaMlbpNPyD5E8`, two takes of each, and **the pick was measured, not
guessed** — there are no ears in a session.

| | source | why this take |
|---|---|---|
| `platoonmarch` | march2 | cadence autocorrelation **0.881** against march1's 0.602, and it has the SHAPE of a pass-by: 1-second RMS runs −63 → −32 at six seconds → −71 |
| `campamb` | camp1 | camp2 puts **84.4 %** of its energy under 120 Hz, which is rumble, not a treeline; camp1 is 62.5 / 15.6 / 20.4 across the bands |
| `e2day` | day1 | day2 puts **86 %** of its energy in 120–500 Hz, exactly where his voice lives — the v5.27 masking reasoning; day1 keeps its energy under 120 |

**Two model parameters that are not prose.** Asking for "14 seconds" in the
prompt returns a **1.04-second clip**. `duration_seconds` and `loop` are real
fields on `eleven_text_to_sound_v2` and have to be passed as
`model_parameters`; the first run of all six came back one second long
because the request was only ever written in English. `eleven_music_v2` has
no loop flag at all, which is why `e2day` is crossfade-looped by hand:
`acrossfade(S[4:27], S[0:4], d=4)` ends where it began, so it wraps with no
seam. Both loops were then checked at the JOINT rather than by RMS —
level within 0.7 dB, maximum sample step at the wrap under the file's own
99.9th percentile, so no click.

**Where they play.**

- `e2day` is the day's music, at `0.26 * (1 - nightK)`, against `e2bed`'s
  `0.30 * nightK`. The two cross at lights out, so neither starts into
  silence. It exists because from the end of the film to lights out the
  chapter had **no music at all** — which is precisely the half of the
  chapter Chad was playing when he asked.
- `campamb` is keyed to `outK`, 0 at his bed and 1 on the square: 0.150
  indoors, 0.330 on the balcony, 0.410 out on the tarmac. Running it flat
  would say the bunk and the parade square sound alike, and the whole of the
  fall-in is the walk between them.
- `platoonmarch` is an EVENT, not a bed — every 46–82 s, dealt from the
  chapter's own deterministic stream, louder the further out he is, and only
  in the DAY's phases. Nobody marches a platoon at three in the morning; the
  night belongs to the shower.

**Priced against the narration**, the way v5.27 did it:

```
DAY, indoors    v9.1  -24.4 dBFS      v9.2  -19.8 dBFS   (+4.5 dB)
DAY, the square                       v9.2  -17.6 dBFS
NIGHT, shipped, shower on             v9.2  -13.7 dBFS
```

The loudest day mix is **3.9 dB quieter than the night the chapter already
ships**, and his voice keeps **12.9 dB** of margin through the v5.27 duck —
inside the 10–15 dB that pass targeted. More sound, and not one decibel of it
taken off him.

**Verified on the shipped build**: the first pass-by fires at day **19.6**,
inside its own 16–30 s window; the next is booked 78.9 s out, inside the
46–82 range; `outK` runs 0 at his bed to 1 on the square; and at night the
ticker goes quiet (`nextMarchIn` null) and no further pass-by arrives.

Getting there cost two probes' worth of wrong answers, both worth writing
down. **`__enc.stings()` cannot see `worldSfx`** — the cue log is filled by
`sting()`, and `worldSfx` calls `snd()` directly, so the log has been blind to
the whistle and the push-ups since v7.1 and was blind to this; `stage.ambient()`
is the readable surface now, the same move as v5.29's `seatStats()`. And
**every harness runs MUTED**: `let muted = !HAS_TOUCH`, and headless Chromium
has no touch, so `snd()` returns null for everything. That was settled by
asking three sounds that have shipped since v7.1 — `whistle`, `b1day`,
`bunkday` — the same question at the same instant; all three returned null
too, which puts the fault in the environment rather than in the new cue.

## 5 · What reset() had to learn, a fourth time

The law from v8.1, v8.2 and v8.7: **a value stated in a clock must be cleared
by whatever resets that clock, and so must a position stated by a phase.**
`marchAt` is stated in `dayClock`, so `reset()` clears it along with
`marchSeed`, `marchN` and `outK`. Without it a replay would wait out a
pass-by timer belonging to the run that just ended.
