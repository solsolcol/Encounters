# v5.02 — THE MOTHER WALKS (four Mixamo takes, retargeted offline)

Chad's four downloads: Female Start Walking, Female Stop Walking, Standing
Idle, Talking. They are the first real animation any character in this game
has had — every model so far shipped a POSE and slid.

## What was wrong

`mother.glb` ships one clip whose usable part is a standing idle. So in
chapter 2 she glided into the bedroom with her feet planted, stood
motionless through five seconds of dialogue, and glided out backwards; in
chapter 5 Ma slid to the kitchen the same way. v4.8 tried to cover the
dialogue with procedural head-nods, and v5.01 found those had never run at
all (the head-bone regex — see LEARNINGS).

## The bake, and why it is offline

`scratchpad/anim/bake.py` (kept with this doc's history, not shipped)
rewrites the four clips onto HER skeleton and merges them into one
`assets/motheranim.glb`, 224 KB, four named clips: `idle`, `talk`,
`walkstart`, `walkstop`. Offline because three.js binds animation tracks to
bones BY NAME: Mixamo's `mixamorig:Hips` is not her `mixamorigHips_01`, so
without the rename nothing binds and — the failure mode this repo keeps
meeting — nothing happens, silently. Renaming at bake time means the
runtime does nothing but play a clip: no retarget code, no per-frame cost,
no new engine seam.

Three transforms, each measured rather than assumed:

1. **Node rename.** Core bone name (`Hips`) matched to her own node. All 65
   of her body joints exist in every clip; nothing is missing.
2. **Position scale.** Her rig is 12.4x the clips' (hips rest 12.929 against
   1.043) — the ratio is READ from the two files. Rotations are unit-free
   and pass through untouched, which is why only the hips needed it.
3. **In-place.** Both walk takes carry root motion (+1.33 m and +1.45 m of
   Z). The scenes already glide the group, so a travelling clip would move
   her twice. X and Z are pinned to their first value; Y is kept, because
   the vertical bob is what sells a step.

Finger tracks are dropped: 30 of her joints, half the file (487 KB -> 224),
and invisible at every distance she is ever seen from.

## The trap this cost an hour

Her grounding is measured from POSED bones (the v3.8/v4.7 law) — but it was
posed by the model's OWN idle, and **that clip rides ~0.8 m above its bind**
(the same quirk as the lead-in note in ch2's loader, seen from the other
end). Ordinary Mixamo takes stand at bind height, so the offset tuned to the
old pose overshot and buried her to the neck: measured, her toes came out at
**-0.79 m**. Both chapters now RE-GROUND after the swap — pose changed, so
measure again. Feet land at 0.000, height 1.583.

## Where they play

| where | clip |
|---|---|
| ch2 scene B, 8.2 | `walkstart` — in through the door, 0.96 m in 1.8 s (0.53 m/s against the take's 0.48, so nothing skates) |
| ch2 scene B, 10.0 | `idle` — she arrives and settles |
| ch2 scene B, 10.5 | `talk` — her line, with shoulders and hands, not just a nod |
| ch2 scene B, 11.6 | `walkstop` at rate **-1** — there is no backing-up take, but a walk settling to a halt, reversed, IS one: a stand breaking into a backwards walk, her face still to camera |
| ch5 film, 23.0 | `walkstart` — she withdraws to the kitchen, 2.6 m in 4 s |
| ch5 scene D, 9.0 / 24.8 | `walkstart` to the altar's edge, then `talk` on her thank-you |

`mumPlay(name, rate)` / `maPlay(name, rate)` is the entire interface, and
both reset to `idle` in `reset()` and `restore()` so a restarted chapter
never resumes mid-stride. The procedural head-nod survives as the FALLBACK
and is skipped whenever the real clips loaded — a failed download costs her
walk, never the scene.

## Still outstanding

kitchen -> table in ch5's film on a two-keyframe pose. His skeleton is the
same Mixamo standard, so the same bake serves him the moment Chad picks
clips — a walk and a bow would finish him.
