# v5.18 — THE RIVER (the boy re-voiced, every take, every chapter)

Chad's ask, verbatim:

> Valf voice was horrible and inconsistent, sometimes sounding like a girl
> and sometimes adult. Can we regenerate all the main player character
> voicelines across the entire game to River instead? Make sure everything
> is covered, everywhere, leave nothing behind. Do not accidentally delete
> anything.

He is right about VALF, and the diagnosis matters more than the complaint:
eleven_v3 re-rolls a voice's *character* per take, and VALF's range is wide
enough that "child, playful" landed on a girl in one line and a grown man
in the next. Across 79 takes spread over five chapters, the drift is what
the player hears — not any single reading. **River Faith**
(`v6KgbPaQh6lAmMpmmtcH`) was one of the second-round candidates at v5.15
and is narrower: youthful and timid, and it stays there.

## What changed, exactly

- `SPEAKERS.james.voice` in `src/voicelines.js`: `loY1uopAz31XyhAEhNSa`
  (VALF) → `v6KgbPaQh6lAmMpmmtcH` (River Faith). That is the ONE place the
  voice is named; everything downstream reads it.
- **All 79 of the boy's takes** regenerated in River, encoded to the
  contract, level-matched and installed — 78 into `assets/audio/` and
  `assets/audio-opus/`, plus chapter 1's separate `assets/voice.mp3`.
- `secs` re-measured for all 79 (76 rows moved; `vscoff`, `v3chair` and
  `v4tired` came back the same length to the centisecond).
- Two cutscene cues re-timed against the new lengths (below).

**Not one word changed.** The prompts are the v5.15 texts, character for
character — this is a re-voice, not a re-write. That is also why sheet v23
is identical to v22 in every TEXT cell.

## Coverage — "leave nothing behind"

`LINES.filter(l => l.who === 'james')` out of the registry, which is the
same list `chaptertest` checks against the shipped files, so a take that
exists in the game but not in the list is a test failure, not an oversight.

Verified three ways after installing, not assumed:
- every `secs` in the registry matches `ffprobe` on the shipped file to
  within 0.06 s (97 lines checked — the other 18 belong to Ma, the auntie
  and the tang-ki and were left alone);
- every changed `.mp3` has a changed `.ogg` beside it (78 and 78);
- no take peaks above −0.5 dB (the loudest is `vscare1` at −1.90, the
  quietest `v2wake2` at −11.50) — each one peak-matched to the file it
  replaced, the v4.8 rule.

## River is faster than VALF, and one thing follows from that

Total spoken time: **331.5 s → 273.3 s**, −58 s over 79 takes. 67 takes got
shorter, 12 longer. The extremes: `vchantline` −5.43 s, `vD` −3.76;
`vghost` +1.36, `vrelief` +1.05.

A shorter take can only *free* room in a timeline, so the static overlap
scan (`masters/v5.18/overlapscan.mjs` — every cue in every film and scene,
its measured length against the next cue and the scene end) found no new
collision anywhere. The two moves it did want are both `vrelief`, the one
wordless exhale, which went the other way:

- `ch1` scene C (walking away): `sfx(4.60, 'vrelief')` → **3.15**. At 4.52 s
  it would have run a second and a half past the fade into the outcome card.
- `ch2` scene B (the mother leaves): `sfx(20.0, 'vrelief')` → **18.9**. It
  still starts well after Ma finishes at 15.6, and now ends at 23.4, inside
  the scene that is written to outlast it.

Nothing else in five chapters needed touching.

## The traps this run hit

- **Which River?** The workspace has more than one voice whose name starts
  with River, and the premade middle-aged one (`SAz9YHcvj6GT2YYXdXww`) is
  not the one Chad heard. The id was recovered from the v5.15 candidate
  round — the actual generation call — rather than from a name lookup. A
  voice id is never resolved by name when a previous session's call has it.
- **eleven_v3 still fails about one line in three, at random.** Re-run with
  the text COMPLETELY unchanged; do not reword a line that failed. That is
  the v4.1 rule and it held again here.
- `vrelief` is a wordless exhale and must be prompted with the v4.8 recipe
  (`[exhales, shaken, relieved] Hoohh... hahh.`), not with tags alone.

## The record
