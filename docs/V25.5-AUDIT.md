### 5.1 He could still walk out of his shop (C4: my miss)

His words: "do not allow the player to exit the shop in the gameplay, i was able to walk out of the shop into other
shops." v25.5 put a wall across the shop's open front and I tried it at the front, at seven points. I did not flood.

The audit flooded the real collision grid from where he stands, in every play phase, on the build that was live:
**40,167 cells reached, 257 square metres, the whole of the chapter's bounds**, for a shop whose free floor is 17. The
way out was the side walls. The shop was a film set in chapter 1 (never walked), and when chapter 2 made it walkable
only what stands on its floor was made solid; beside the wall cabinets the wall itself was a picture. Four gaps, the
same on both sides: by the door (z 2.25 to 2.97, 0.72 m wide) and behind the counter (z -2.71 to -0.87, 1.84 m).
"Into other shops" is exactly that.

- **The fix:** each side wall is solid from the back wall to the front wall's outer face (masters/v25.0/parts/
  b_build4.js). Nothing is drawn; nothing else moves.
- **Measured after:** 2,542 to 2,652 cells, 16.3 to 17 square metres, x from -2.24 to 2.25 and z from -2.67 to 2.94,
  in all five phases tried (the shop by day, the night before and after the light, the clearing before and after).
  Every one of the eleven things he presses is still within its own reach from that floor.
- **So it cannot come back:** a stage may now name the ground he must stay on (`stage.keepIn`), and walktest fails if
  its flood reaches a cell outside it. Proved by breaking it: on a throwaway copy with the side walls taken out it
  fails ("240.4 square metres reached outside it"); on the real build it passes.
- **The picture of it:** tools/probes/walkmap.mjs and walkmap.py draw the flood from above, before and after.
