# v5.31 · TWO FACES

Chad, on v5.29's tent:

> chapter 3 audience, the seated female with pink shirt, and the granny by
> the burner, seem to be overly compressed that they look weird now, take a
> look at them, they should not look so deformed.

## What they were

The pink shirt is `sitwoman` (three of the nineteen seats); the granny by
the burner is `scold` (`standers[2]`). Both are Meshy models shrunk at v5.29
through `tools/prepwoman.mjs`, and both were accepted from a wide shot of
the tent. Photographed from the player's eye in the game they read as
whole figures; rendered head-and-shoulders in a bare viewer (same light,
same lens, `dbgviewer.mjs`) they read as Chad said:

| asset | source | shipped at v5.29 | what that looked like |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sitwoman.glb` | 591k tris, 8192² sheet | 26,309 tris, 512² JPEG, 1,338 KB | the face a smear — eyes and nose gone, the gingham a pink blur, lumpy shoulders |
| `scold.glb` | 257k tris, 2048² sheet | 22,395 tris, 768² JPEG, 1,257 KB | the nose a wedge, the cheeks in planes, the chin a polygon, the hair spikes |

The woman's is the worse of the two for one reason: her sheet is a single
8192-pixel atlas for the whole body, and shrinking it SIXTEEN times to 512
left her face a few dozen pixels. The granny's is the geometry: 257k to 22k
is a ratio of 0.087, and a face is where a simplifier's error shows first.

## What they are now

Same tool, same recipe, gentler numbers, chosen from a matrix rendered
side by side (ratios 0.08 / 0.12 / 0.28 / 0.5 on 1024 and 2048 sheets):

| asset | ratio | sheet | tris | size | delta |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sitwoman.glb` | 0.08 | 1024² JPEG 312 KB | 47,295 | 2,340 KB | +1,002 KB |
| `scold.glb` | 0.15 | 1024² JPEG 334 KB | 38,620 | 1,903 KB | +646 KB |

At 47k the woman has a face — eyes, nose, mouth, the gingham check legible,
smooth shoulders, proper hands; at 39k the granny's nose and chin are round
and her hair reads as hair. The next steps up (71k / 72k, 3.5 MB / 2.7 MB)
were not visibly better at the player's closest distance, and the 2048
sheets add a megabyte each for nothing a phone can show. Chapter 3's
download grows by 1.6 MB, which is the price of two faces.

Both keep their one clip (`Sit_Cross_Legged`, `Stand_Talking_Angry`), their
skin, and `KHR_mesh_quantization`; posed size is unchanged to the
millimetre (1.288 → 1.291 m seated; 1.702 → 1.703 m standing), so nothing
in `ch3.js` moved — the same `seatKind()` and the same `standers[2]`
loader read the same files by the same names. Credits unchanged (Meshy AI,
CC0). No text changed, so no new sheet: v31 stands.

The commands, for the record (from `scratchpad/dl/`, the v5.29 sources):

```
node tools/prepwoman.mjs sg_woman.glb        sitwoman.glb 0.08 1024 256 "Armature|Sit_Cross_Legged|baselayer"   1
node tools/prepwoman.mjs granny_scolding.glb scold.glb    0.15 1024 256 "Armature|Stand_Talking_Angry|baselayer" 1
```

## A correction to v5.29

That build's doc said the woman "would not simplify below ~26k triangles
... the real floor is topological". It is not: at the tool's own defaults
the same source gives 47k at ratio 0.08 and 165k at 0.28. The 26k file came
from a far lower ratio than the doc believed. The doc carries the
correction now, and LEARNINGS the rule: when a number contradicts the
tool's documented behaviour, re-run the tool before writing the number
down as a property of the model.

## Also in this build

`leaktest` and `fixturetest` were the only two harnesses on Playwright's
default 30-second navigation timeout; every other harness gives the page
180 s. Both failed on `page.goto` twice during v5.30's run — the boot
preloads do not finish in 30 s when two browsers share the box — and
passed the moment they ran alone, with the live site's preloads diffed
against the build's and found identical. Both now set the same 180 s.
