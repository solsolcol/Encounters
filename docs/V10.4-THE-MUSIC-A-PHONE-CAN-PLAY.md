## Why the eerie music was still too soft after two gain raises

The v9.7 dread bed was chosen on the MASKING measure: 80.7 % of its energy
under 120 Hz, so it sat under his voice rather than on it. That is exactly the
band a phone speaker cannot reproduce, and Chad plays on a phone. Raising its
gain (0.26 → 0.62 at v10.3) raised a signal his device discards, so it stayed
"too soft" through three asks. The fix is a different sound, not a bigger
number: a new `e2dread` picked on the PHONE-BAND measure (`masters/v10.4/
make.sh`), levelled to −6.0 dBFS peak (the beds sit at −10.5), and run at 0.85
in both chapters. Under a spoken line the v5.27 duck still dips every
non-voice source to 0.40.
