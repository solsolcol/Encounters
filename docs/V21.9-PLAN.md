## The plan (his second message)

- **CP1 · the engine seam, `pressAim` (the twenty-eighth).** A hotspot that is
  answered by a PRESS on what the player is looking at: no dwell timer; offered
  to E and the badge only while the reticle is inside its `aim`; when two cones
  hold the reticle, the one it is nearer (as a fraction of each cone) wins; a tap
  or an unlocked click on its MARK presses it; a click under pointer lock presses
  the one the badge names. Its mark is the press mark ("!"), and the one under
  the reticle grows and ripples. Opt-in: no hotspot before v21.9 declares it.
  The fixture declares two (`sign`, `plaque`, cones overlapping) and
  `fixturetest` proves: not offered off-aim; offered on-aim; the nearer cone
  wins; resting on one runs no timer at all; E presses it; a tap on the other's
  mark presses that one.
- **CP2 · the shop.** The four look-spots become `pressAim` with a `hits` box
  each (a tap or click on the thing itself presses it too); their reach equals
  where their mark is drawn, so a mark on screen can always be pressed; the
  framed amulets move to the staff's counter. **All four** must be pressed —
  the objective counts them (`Look around the shop · 2/4`, each one a beat) and
  the owner calls on the fourth. The three ways the browse used to end by itself
  are gone: two looks were enough, walking up to the owner's counter called him,
  and 45 s of wandering called him — each left markers on screen never pressed.
  The count rides the phase string (`browse:wall,tim`), so a Continue lands on
  the same count.
- **CP3 · release.** Build, boot-check, photograph the markers and a press on a
  phone and a desktop, deploy and byte-verify, harnesses (chapter, fixture, walk,
  text, csp, hosted, cine, leak), sheet v104 (one UI word and one voice-line
  description), docs, commit and push, report.
