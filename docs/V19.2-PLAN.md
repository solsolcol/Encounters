## Found so far (before the workflows report)

1. **The signs were drawn squashed flat.** `makeSignTex(…, aspect)` stretches
   its glyphs vertically by `aspect`, which e3c1 passes as the plane's
   WIDTH ÷ HEIGHT (2.65, 3.7, 4.25). e3c2 passes HEIGHT ÷ WIDTH (0.32, 0.18,
   0.2), so the chant-book shelf's sign ("บทสวดมนต์ · CHANT BOOKS") and both
   ROADEYE signs in the film are a thin dashed line (photographed:
   phone-look/011-shelf-h3).
2. **The stat bars flash over the black at the end of every opening film.**
   `cineEnd` un-hides the HUD before `playChapterCard` has covered the screen,
   so for the card's fade-in the bars sit over the film's black (photographed:
   phone-film/059-film-t55.08). Engine-wide — every chapter with a film.
3. **The film's first shot reads as a toy.** Scalloped black zigzags and a grey
   stick with a black cone for the rod (phone-film/005).
4. **The temple-floor glance meets a face.** The glance meant for "the knees
   either side" frames the whole upper body of the man beside him at arm's
   length: his decimated face and hair (white flecks at the temple), and the
   viewmodel's hands drawn through his chest (phone-film/052).
