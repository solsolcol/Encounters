## Why this is a better chapter, not just an edited one

The first version hid her in the crowd — a seated figure facing the wrong
way. The revision inverts the game's whole power relationship: **the thing
that has been hunting you since chapter 1 stands out on the open tarmac,
in full sun, facing the tent, and will not cross into it.** Nothing in
three chapters has frightened her before. So the tent means something,
the ceremony means something, and the player spends the whole chapter
inside the one place she cannot follow — with the knowledge that home is
on the other side of the car park, and she is between him and it. That is
chapter 4 (BACK HOME) loaded and cocked, by one image, in one shot.

The turned chair stays, EMPTY. It faces the open back of the tent — which
is to say, it faces her. Nobody sits in it. Nobody moves it. The tent
knows.

## Engine seams (each defaults to today's behaviour)

1. **`ghost: null` — a chapter with no haunting** (the eleventh leak).
   `applyGhostTerritory()` sets `GH.off` when a chapter declares `ghost:
   null` (undefined still means "chapter 1's numbers", untouched).
   `updateGhost()` parks in `hidden` with reveal/hauntK zero; 
   `ghostDrainRate()` returns 0. The CUTSCENE cast is untouched — the
   intro drives her mesh directly, which is the one place she appears.
2. **Daylight reaches the hands.** The viewmodel rig was hard-coded to
   midnight (`vmHemi 0.55` cold blue, re-asserted EVERY FRAME in
   `updateViewmodel`, plus `vmKey 0.50` re-set at three restore points) —
   which is why the hands read near-black against a bright sky. Now
   `VM_REST` is mutated by `applyDaylight()` from the chapter's `daylight`
   (`vmHemi: [sky, ground, intensity]`, `vmKey: [color, intensity]`), and
   every hard-coded site reads `VM_REST`. Defaults are the exact midnight
   values.
3. **Sun and clouds.** `daylight` grows `sun` and `clouds` (both default
   0 — chapters 1/2 pixel-identical). The sun is a halo + disc sprite pair
   like the moon's, positioned along the chapter's own key-light direction.
   Clouds are seven soft canvas-blob sprites on the upper dome, drifting
   very slowly (the whole group yaws), opacity scaled by the declaration.
4. **`voiceLine` may name a pack sound.** `voiceDecode()` tries `packJson`
   first, then falls back to `assetBytes` — so ch1's separate `voice`
   asset still works and ch3 can say a line from its own pack (`v3play`).

## The new film and scenes (beat sheets)

**INTRO (~38 s).** 0–22.6 unchanged (black → glare → the walk in → the
drum STOPS). 22.4–26.0 the medium's head goes back, nobody reacts
(unchanged, new `suona` at 20.4). Then the new back half: the camera
comes off the front and finds the ODD chair — turned, and **empty**
(`chair` creak, new line `v3chair`). The camera lifts along the chair's
facing, out through the open back, into the sun — **and she is out there**,
tiny, dead still, facing the tent (`v3out1`, then the four-word reveal
`v3out2`: "She's not coming in."). No sting under her. No ghost sound.
The ceremony's silence does it. Fade; the drum restarts alone in the
black (kept — best beat in the film).

**A · OBSERVE QUIETLY (~17 s).** Stillness pays in information. The
trance escalates (drumroll, flags, bellring) — and mid-scene the medium's
head snaps up and he looks STRAIGHT at the player, holds, goes back to
the altar. `v3seen`. The crowd never moves. Being seen by the god is the
cost of watching.

**B · JOIN THE RITUAL (~15 s).** The whole escalation kept (anjali,
drum crowding in, strobing tubes, the lean). At the stop: half a second
of nothing — then **the medium is face to face with him**, head tilted,
eyes shut (`trancehum`, `gongdeep`, `suona`, `v3grip`) — and the auntie
hauls him out (`v3aunt5` kept). The possessed man replaces her outright.
`medium.position` is now in snap/restore/reset so the borrow is safe.

**C · QUESTION WHAT YOU SEE (~29 s).** The conversation, nearly
untouched — it was already the chapter working. The whisper cue is gone
and the ending changes: after "do not sit in the back row tonight" the
camera comes round to the back row and just HOLDS on the empty turned
chair, out through the tent to the bright car park beyond. Nothing moves.
The rattle is cut — nothing of hers is inside the tent, that is now the
rule the whole chapter stands on.

**D · LEAVE THE GATHERING (~17 s).** Out, tent falling quiet behind him
(kept). He turns for one look — and the drum stops, mid-pattern, forty
people silent in the sun. Nothing else happens. That nothing is the
scene: he leaves with the question instead of the answer, `trancehum`
under the last backing-away, one far `gongdeep`, `v3left` ("I could
still hear the drum from the lift. I told myself that was normal.") —
which is chapter 4's doorstep.

## Traps found on the way (also in LEARNINGS)

- The viewmodel lights are reasserted every frame — a one-time fix in
  `applyDaylight` alone would flicker back to midnight within a frame.
- `snap()` did not carry `medium.position`; any scene that borrows the
  medium's body must add it or a skipped scene teleports him permanently.
- scJoin's vm-light tracks wrote absolute night values; they now start
  from `VM_REST` so the hands do not DIM at the start of the loudest
  scene in a bright chapter.
- The `strings` sting is, in practice, HER leitmotif (every reveal since
  v3.3 uses it) — a ghost-free chapter must not cue it or she is present
  by grammar even when absent in pixels.
