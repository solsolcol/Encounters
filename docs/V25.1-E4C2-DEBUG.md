## 1. The method (CLAUDE.md, the deep-debug method, v18.5; v24.2, v24.5, v12.3)

A debug request covers the whole chapter in depth, not only what he named. Every lens is read-only and has one concern;
every finding is a LEAD until it is verified (default to refuted, quote today's line, ask whether a player can reach
it); verdicts and what was left alone are written down; my own regressions are named as mine. The lenses are mine, read
by me: no reviewer agents (his 7 Nov "Dont spawn reviewers").

- **V · THE PICTURES.** Every film and ending sampled every 2 s on the phone crop (390×844, touch) and every 3 s on a
  desktop (1280×800); every play beat photographed from where a player stands (day, night, lit, watched, walked up, the
  clearing standing and kneeling, the decision), with the HUD as it is; looked at for what is fake, floating, clipped,
  doubled, blank, popping, mis-lit, mis-scaled or unnatural. A picture's oddity is measured, never dismissed.
- **L1 · SAVE AND RESUME.** Every phase's Continue (done at CP13: 14/14), the same-chapter replay through the real path
  (reset() read for what it forgets), the way in from chapter 1's end and from the selector.
- **L2 · GEOMETRY AND FRAMING.** Collisions and blockers against what is drawn, props resting on what they stand on
  (nothing floating, nothing sunk), surfaces sharing a plane, every film's lens against the set's solids.
- **L3 · SOUND AND TIMING.** Every press has its sound, beds by place and beat, music under every film and ending, no
  voice over another, no silent stretch, levels.
- **L4 · MODELS, RIGS AND MOTION.** Lersi Chin, Pong and the extras: takes, feet (sliding, sinking), hands (through the
  body, the robe), seated poses, turns (snaps), looks (neck limits); the props he holds.
- **L5 · WORDS AND HUD.** Every word on screen true and fitting a 390 px phone; prompts and badges only while live;
  objectives; the pick, the decision, the cards; the captions; the drawn screens (laptop, phone, notebook).
- **L6 · THE ENGINE SINCE THE LAST REVIEW.** main.js's changes for v25.0 (the grey tint seam) and what the chapter asks
  of the engine.
- **L7 · REGRESSION AUDIT OF THE LAST PASS.** v25.0's late fixes (the tie re-staged, wristShare, the wai's lift, the
  water, the save letters, the warning on a Continue) against everything they touch.
