## 0.1 · The plan on one screen

**The story (Master Z's S1·12).** For years he has carried four questions nobody could answer. He goes to a Wai Kru in
Udon Thani with the group from the forum. Among all the masters there, one man in white holds his eyes. The next
morning he is brought to him, tells him everything, and is answered. What Grandmaster Pho teaches first is control:
to open and to close at will.

**How it plays (about 15 minutes).**
1. **Film** (47 s): the thread, his notebook with the questions, a white shirt into a bag, dawn in Udon Thani.
2. **The Wai Kru** (play): carry a tray of flowers to the teachers' altar, sit among the devotees, join the homage.
   His eyes go along the row of masters and stop on one. Then, as the chanting rises, his hands begin to move by
   themselves, as they did in episode 3, and settle when he looks at the man in white (**NEW**).
3. **The check** (play): he does what chapter 2 taught him. He asks the people who know the man before he goes near
   him.
4. **The next morning** (play): one bow, then one press, "Tell him everything". He is answered. What the answers were
   stays between teacher and disciple, in Master Z's own words; what we hear Grandmaster Pho say is only what the case
   file makes public: control first.
5. **OPEN AND CLOSE** (the one new way to play): hold to open, and the world comes in; let go to close. Three breaths.
   It cannot be failed. For the first time in the game, the things that have happened TO him are in the player's own
   hand.
6. **The decision** (Chad's own four, §4.4). His teacher has shown him the first step and told him where the road
   goes, and waits for his answer: accept your path, challenge the path, ask why, or ask to make it stop. None of
   them leaves the path. Each is a different road to the same place: he accepts his path and his mission (§4.5),
   and one ending all four share shows it a year on (§4.6).

**What it needs from Chad** (§2.3): what Grandmaster Pho may be heard saying; the wording and ranks of the decision's four (§4.4); a yes or no
on the two NEW beats; photographs or memories of the real Wai Kru, if Master Z has them; a voice for Grandmaster Pho
by ear; and models (§6.1) for which stand-ins are proposed until his arrive.

**How it is built** (§11): direction first (his answers, the research refuted, the voice), then the whole chapter with
a commit at every checkpoint, then one build, one deploy and one round of checks.

## 3 · Direction

### 3.1 · What the chapter is for

Episode 4 is three kinds of room. Chapter 1 is a stranger's room that watches him. Chapter 2 is his own shop in a
fog. Chapter 3 is **stillness**: the one chapter of the episode where nothing is against him, and the only difficulty
is inside. The player has spent seventeen chapters having things happen to him: the heartbeat, the shaking hands, the
cold at the edge of sight, the picture that lags his eyes. This chapter hands him the switch. That is the whole
design: the effects the player has learned to dread become something he does on purpose and can stop.

So the chapter must be the brightest and calmest place in the game (white cloth, marigolds, morning), it must hold no
scare, and its one moment of fear (the pull) must come from him and be answered by a look.

### 3.2 · The place: one ground, four hours of it

A lay master's samnak on the edge of Udon Thani, on the morning of its Wai Kru. One open-sided ceremonial pavilion:
white posts, a white cloth ceiling tied under a steel roof, marigold garlands, woven mats over a swept floor. By
Chad's standing rulings for a Thai temple, kept here too: white, yellow and gold, red only as an accent; white pillars; no green; no
flags; no long banners; no mural; no halo behind the Buddha; nothing Chinese.

- **North end, centre:** the teachers' altar, in tiers under white cloth. A Buddha image at the top; below it the
  bai sri, the trays of flowers the devotees bring, fruit, sweets, betel, candles and incense.
- **West side:** the monks' platform (their seats are not beside the Buddha on the same wall, his ruling).
- **East side:** the masters' low dais, a row of seats with Isan triangular cushions. Grandmaster Pho sits at its
  near end, a little apart, on the plainest seat.
- **The floor:** the devotees on mats inside a ring of sacred thread strung on posts round the ground.
- **South edge:** where he arrives. Rows of sandals, the table of trays, the food tables.
- **Beyond:** a frangipani in flower and a tamarind (both new), the samnak's house, a paddy horizon with sugar palms.
  He cannot leave the ground (§12).

The same ground is seen four ways, and nothing else is built: **day one** (the ceremony, the crowd), **day two** (the
morning after: the mats being rolled, a few people, Grandmaster Pho receiving), **night** (the film between the two days: the
altar's candles), and **a year on** (the coda: day one again, from nearer the front). The endings also see day two in
the afternoon and at dusk: the same set, its light moved.

Fresh by his rule: no place in the game is an open ground under white cloth. What it takes from earlier chapters is
listed in §6.4 and declared in the chapter (`reuse`), as chaptertest's FRESH check asks.

teacher. Not about luck, prices or prediction: nobody asks for them. Not a re-telling of earlier chapters: his telling is seven sentences.

### 5.1 · One new seam: the event kind `breath` (the forty-fourth)

Hold to open, let go to close, in a number of breaths. Declared by the chapter:
`{ kind: 'breath', low: true, breaths: [{ enough, edge, cue }, …], onLevel(k), onBreath(report), onDone(result) }`.

- A level k from 0 to 1 that rises while anything is held and falls when it is let go, handed to the chapter every
  frame (`onLevel`). The chapter lays its own world on k: its beds' gains, the daylight's strength inside the same
  light count, the gold, and past the band the pulse, the shake and the lag.
- A band per breath (`enough` to `edge`, in seconds, both seconds wide: nothing here tests touch latency) and three
  results: closed in time, closed for him, not opened. Charges go through `evCut()` and are paid on the spot.
- The hold is timed by the events' own timestamps and counts only the time the finger was really down (v24.2). While
  it is open all input is its own; the long press is not the browser's.
- Its instructions come first with a START (v8.7). Its own moving picture, `breath` (a ring that opens under a held
  finger and closes when it lifts): a row in `EV_DEMO` and a class in shell.html, or chaptertest fails the kind.
- Opt-in, and a bit-identical pass-through for every chapter that does not use it. The fixture chapter declares it
  and `fixturetest` proves it, on a fake clock with a person's reaction delay (v21.2). It ships in the same release.
- **Episode 1 cannot reach it: unchanged by construction.** Episode 1 runs as the control all the same.
- If Chad chooses "watched" (Q4), none of this is built and P9 is a film.

The episode's plan gives the same kind to chapter 4, so it is built once for both.
