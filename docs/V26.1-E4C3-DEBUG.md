## 1. The method (CLAUDE.md: the deep-debug method, v18.5; v24.2, v24.5, v12.3)

A debug request covers the whole chapter in depth, not only what he named. Every lens is read-only and has one concern;
every finding is a LEAD until it is verified (default to refuted, quote today's line, ask whether a player can reach
it); verdicts and what was left alone are written down; my own regressions are named as mine. The lenses are mine, read
by me: no reviewer agents (his 7 Nov "Dont spawn reviewers").

- **V · THE PICTURES.** Every film and ending sampled on the phone crop (390×844 points, touch, two pixels a point) and
  a desktop; every play beat photographed from where a player stands; looked at full size for what is fake, floating,
  clipped, doubled, blank, popping, mis-lit, mis-scaled or unnatural. A picture's oddity is measured, never dismissed.
- **L1 · SAVE AND RESUME.** Continue at every phase, a same-chapter replay through the real path (reset() read for what
  it forgets: v26.1 added state — the kids' stops and toss queue, the step stream, the sky's look, the taper's hold),
  the way in from chapter 2's end and from the selector.
- **L2 · GEOMETRY AND FRAMING.** Blockers against what is drawn; things resting on what they stand on; surfaces sharing a
  plane; every film's lens against the set's solids; the v26.1 edges (groves, house, tanks, motorbikes, compressors)
  against the films that look that way.
- **L3 · SOUND AND TIMING.** Every press has its sound; beds by place and beat; music under every film and ending; no
  voice over another; no silent stretch; the new steps; levels.
- **L4 · MODELS, RIGS AND MOTION.** Grandmaster Pho, Ton, the masters, the monks, the dancers, the children, the crowd,
  the woman with the taper: takes, feet, hands, seats, turns, looks; the repainted sheets at every level.
- **L5 · WORDS AND HUD.** Every word true and fitting a 390-point phone; prompts and marks only while live; objectives;
  the decision and the cards; the captions; the text-size lock on the HUD.
- **L6 · THE ENGINE SINCE THE LAST REVIEW.** main.js's v26.1 changes (PHONE_RATIO, pixelRatioFor/applyRender, textZoom,
  the grass rows, the diag rows) and what the chapter asks of the engine.
- **L7 · REGRESSION AUDIT OF THE LAST PASS.** v26.1's own changes against everything they touch (the repainted bodies,
  the levels, the kneel window, the kids, the taper, the sky and edges, the steps).
