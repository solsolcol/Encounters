## 1. The source

S1·08 *The Journey To Seek The Truth* (docs/source/site-case-files.md 125–140):
Buddha Day, an amulet shop he often visits; paying respect to the main Buddha,
something came into his body; fully conscious, could not stop; a traditional
dance "as though dancing for the god to see"; people rushed in to stop him and
could not, gave up, let him dance about fifteen minutes; afterwards worried —
trance? possessed? — they urged him to see a medium that night. At the medium
the same presence took over again: the body moved forward and BOWED to him; the
medium said, without much hesitation, it was a ghost possessing him; at that
moment the nature of it changed — something monkey-like came through, he ROARED
at the medium and could feel its anger. The medium performed rituals; the
experiences did not simply stop. Ghost? Thevada? Hanuman? He did not know.

The episode axis (CONTROL): chapter 3 is **VOICE** — a presence dances him and
roars through him; he loses the rest. The player has LESS control here than in
chapter 2: in chapter 2 he could fight the hands; here he cannot stop the body
at all — only stay aware (the minigame) — and at the medium his legs walk him
forward on their own.

### 2.2 PLAY A — the amulet shop, Vesak noon
- **browse** — "Look around the shop." Look-spots (dwell) and presses: the LP
  Tim's own case behind the counter (a line), the wall of framed amulets, the
  shop's Buddha altar, the photo wall of famous masters, the counter's tray.
  The owner calls him over when he has looked at two, or when he walks up.
- **gift** — at the counter the owner takes the LP Tim Khun Paen out of its case:
  "Today Buddha Day... I want you to have this one." → `kit.unlock('timkp')`
  (ITEM UNLOCKED, the model turning) and `kit.give('timkp')`.
- **wear** — "Open your bag and wear the LP Tim Khun Paen." The bag pulses
  (v11.6 / v14.7 flow, derived from the bag every frame; taking it off asks
  again with no COMPLETE). Worn → OBJECTIVE COMPLETE; the owner: "Good. Keep it
  close. Come — the chanting is starting." It takes the NECK box: a worn Phiboon
  goes back to the bag (the two never stack — v14.13; flagged to Chad, §7).
- **chant** — the celebration starts: the staff lights the altar candles, the
  customers in white kneel on mats before the Buddha, the radio's chant comes
  up and the owner leads "Namo tassa...". "Join them at the altar" → the free
  mat glows with the engine's "!" (a `place` hotspot, e3c2's shape); a press
  kneels him (`kit.pose`), the hands go into añjali (`kit.pray`).
- **respect** — the chant runs; he bows with the room; on the third Namo the
  music under it CHANGES (a Thai melody comes up that nobody else hears), the
  hands part from the clasp by themselves (`kit.hands`), he RISES (rooted —
  nothing the player does moves him), and the lens begins to turn.
- **trance (the minigame — STAY PRESENT)** — a `focus` event, briefed: "Something
  has come into your body. You cannot stop it — but you are still here. Tap each
  thing you notice, as it shows itself." Six targets in the shop, projected
  through the camera the DANCE is turning: the Buddha's face, the owner's face,
  a candle, the staff girl's hands, a phone held up filming, the wall clock.
  A hit is awareness; a miss costs sanity on the spot (the LP Tim's 50 % guard
  applies). The spiritual reason is the case file's own sentence: "I was
  conscious the whole time. I knew what I was doing and I knew what was
  happening around me" — the body was taken, the awareness was not. Win or
  lose, the body dances on.
- **the cutscene takes over** — `kit.film('trance')` (THE TWENTY-SIXTH SEAM, §5).

### 2.3 THE TRANCE — a cutscene from play (~45 s), then transport
1. From his eyes: the hands high in the dance, the Thai melody full, the shop
   turning slowly past — altar, counter, faces, the glass front.
2. "Eh — eh! Are you okay?!" The owner and a staff man come at him and take his
   arms; the lens pushes on through them; "Hold him!" — "Cannot! So strong!"
3. They let go and step back. A customer prays faster. The staff girl lifts her
   phone. Outside the glass, passers-by stop; a crowd at the door.
4. TIME COMPRESSED: the wall clock passes again and again, its minute hand
   jumping 12:10 → 12:25; the light on the floor moves; the owner: "Let him...
   don't touch. Let him finish."
5. The music stops. The body stops. Down on his knees; the hands fall; breath.
6. Faces over him. Owner: "You know what you were doing just now?" Him (gasping):
   "I knew... I knew everything. I just couldn't stop." Staff girl: "Was he in
   trance?" Auntie: "Possessed, maybe... something unclean." Owner: "Tonight you
   go see a medium. I know one, a good one. Go tonight, don't wait."
7. Black (kept). Under the black: transported to World B, its night light on,
   warmed (`kit.warm`), every rig landed; then the black lifts. Him: "That
   night... I went."

### 2.4 PLAY B — the medium's shrine, that night
A shophouse temple: the roller shutter half up, red lanterns over the five-foot
way, inside a tiered red altar with deity statues, a great urn, incense coils
hanging from the ceiling, the medium's carved chair and his table (talismans,
brush and ink, a bell, a command flag, a sword, cups of tea, oranges), a drum
and a gong, plastic stools for the waiting, the assistant at a small desk with
the queue book, a ceiling fan, fluorescent tubes and red altar lamps.
- **arrive** — "Light incense at the urn." A press at the joss-stick box: three
  sticks lit at the candle, three bows (the lens dips three times), placed in
  the urn; the smoke rises.
- **wait** — "Wait for your turn." Sit on the free stool (a `place` hotspot). He
  WATCHES two consultations, each read with complete certainty: (1) a woman —
  "Your house... the main door facing the wrong way. Something follows your
  husband home." → a talisman burned into a cup of water: "Drink, three days."
  (2) a father with a small boy — "Your son got a fright. Something saw him." →
  the bell rung over the boy's head, a talisman stamped on his shirt.
- **called** — the assistant: "Next. You — come." THE BODY WALKS: the player's
  controls are overridden (`kit.root`, the chapter drives the lens), the hands
  lift a little, the presence walks him forward to the table and BOWS him to
  the medium, deep and formal. Him: "No... not again. My legs were walking on
  their own."
- **reading** — the medium looks, barely a beat: "It is a ghost possessing you."
- **the roar** — on that word the nature of it changes: a gold flash, the screen
  shakes, a golden monkey-warrior SHADOW thrown over the medium and the altar
  from behind the lens, and a ROAR comes out of him; the lens lunges at the
  medium; the devotees scatter back; the medium recoils, grabs the bell and the
  sword; the assistant beats the drum; the rituals begin.
- **decide** — the decision opens by itself over the rituals: "What do you make
  of the medium's reading?"

### 2.6 The four endings (each 35–60 s; the outcome card over its own last picture)
- **A** — He wais: "Then please... drive it out." Bell, sword passes round him, a
  talisman burned, its ash in water: "Drink." He drinks. Assistant: "Come back
  seven nights. Bring these things." Outside under the lanterns his hand stirs
  in his pocket. "I did everything he told me... and it didn't stop."
- **B** — "You're wrong. It's not a ghost." Silence; the drum stops; the
  medium's face hardens — and it surges again: a second roar, the gold flash,
  stools scraping back, the assistant shoving him out: "Go! Go out!" The
  shutter rattles down behind him. "I answered his certainty with my own... and
  whatever it was heard me."
- **C** — A polite wai: "Thank you, Sifu." Out into the night lorong, a coffee
  shop's lights, a bus going past. "I thanked him and told myself it was
  over... I didn't want to think about it."
- **D** — A wai: "Thank you, Sifu." Out under the lanterns, his hand finds the LP
  Tim on his chest; he looks up past the shutter at the night sky. "A ghost, he
  said... maybe. Maybe not. I couldn't prove it either way." "If I wanted the
  truth, I would have to go and find it myself."
- All four end on the closing line that loads chapter 4 (amulet shops):
  "After that night, I started visiting other shops... other masters... looking
  for answers."

### 2.7 The teaching (`core`) — S1·08's lesson, verbatim
"Not every practitioner will read the same experience the same way. Every
medium, master and practitioner interprets through their own knowledge, training
and tradition — and being called a master does not make an interpretation
infallible. I could not prove it was a ghost. I could not prove it was not. I
could not even prove it was something else entirely. What I realised that night
is that I could not simply accept the first explanation I was handed. If I
wanted the truth, I had to go and seek it, learn it, and find it for myself.
That is worth remembering whoever you consult, and about anything you are ever
told with certainty."

## 5. Engine seams (all unreachable from episodes 1 and 2 by construction)

1. **`kit.film(key, { onDone })` — THE TWENTY-SIXTH SEAM: a cutscene FROM PLAY
   that hands back to PLAY.** A chapter declares `films: { key: fn }` (each in
   the cutscene language). `kit.film` plays it through `playCineFn` (cine
   state, letterbox, Skip from 3.0 s), keeps the black at its end
   (`keepFade`), and returns to `state = 'play'` — no card — calling the
   chapter's `onDone` UNDER the black so it can move the player to another
   place and lift the black itself. The engine warms each film's cues at the
   chapter's entry (the CUE_RE read, as for scenes); `chaptertest` walks every
   film like an intro (cue timing, warming); the FIXTURE declares one and
   `fixturetest` proves it (cine during, play after, the camera where onDone
   put it and not the snapshot's, the HUD back, skip returns to play too).
2. **The `focus` kind finished** (no shipped chapter has used it): its OWN
   briefing picture (`spot` — a ring appearing at places and a fingertip
   landing on each; EV_DEMO's `look` shows a different game), stakes on the
   v9.3 ladder (a miss is paid on the spot through `evCut`, so the LP Tim
   halves it), and world targets that may MOVE (a getter, re-projected every
   frame). Fixture proof extended.
3. Nothing else is planned. If the build finds another leak, it is added here
   with its reason before it is written.
