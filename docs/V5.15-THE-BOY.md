  overlap scan of every film and scene in all five chapters: each voice cue

  voice cue ends, and any cue past the scene's end. Shift only what must
  move; camera beats stay. Probe with `cinetest`.
- **CP5 — the release.** Build; full suite in batches; docs (this file,

  VERSION 5.15; deploy from inside `dist/`; verify; bundle refresh (a
  whole-cast re-voice is a milestone).
