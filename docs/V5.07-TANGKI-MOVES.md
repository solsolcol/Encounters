# v5.07 · THE TANG-KI MOVES

Chad's notes after v5.06, in his words, and what each became:

> *"Can you reduce the height of the medium? he's like more than 1.5x the
> height of a normal person."*

He was 2.09 m. The old man's rig has no `HeadTop_End`, so its topmost bone
IS the head joint, and v5.06 scaled THAT to 1.68 — the base of the skull at
a tall man's crown, the skull above it, and the topknot and hairpin above
that. Measured in ch5 beside Ma (mesh top 1.585): his mesh top was 2.092.
`PRAY_H` / `TANG_H` are now **1.50 at the head joint**, which lands the
skull at ~1.63 and the hairpin at 1.867 — a slightly short old man whose
topknot just clears the player's 1.62 m eye line. Same number in both
chapters, on purpose.

> *"in the intro chapter cutscene for chapter 5, put the hell note in his
> actual hand instead of it floating in air."*

Done by two measurements. The note now rides his **right hand bone**
(`stage.tangHand`), seated along the bone's finger axis — measured as
(-0.69, 0.72, 0) in bone space, 45° between -x and +y, which is why the
note carries a 0.76 rad roll — a centimetre off the palm, face turned out,
and scaled by 1/95 because the bone inherits the model's scale. And the
hand is PUT where the old floating note was by freezing the magic take one
fifth of the way through: sampled across the take, that frame has the
right hand 0.45 m forward of the hips at 1.44 m — the shot the floating
note was faking. `tangPlay(name, ts, fade, once, at)` gained the `at`
argument for exactly this: a take parked on one frame is a pose library.
The insert camera is re-aimed at the note's new home, (1.34, 1.45, 0.57),
0.62 m from it (it was 0.69; a try at 0.45 made the robe's flared cuff most
of the frame and put his face out of it): a 15 cm note at the fingertips, a
sixth of the frame, with his face in the right of frame — a man holding up
a note. The note sits 16 cm above the hand bone and 10 cm toward the lens;
closer than that and the cuff cut through it.

One trap on the way, found only by looking at the ART beside the shot:
the first working insert showed a tan card with mirrored glyphs in his
hand, and no edit to the note changed it — a flip about y, a different
palm side, the photographed art confirmed loaded at 1024×614 — because
the card was not the note at all. It was the robe's flared CUFF, with the
hem calligraphy on it, and the note was inside the sleeve. Six
centimetres along the fingers is inside this model's cuff; the note now
sits 11 cm out and 5 cm off the palm toward the lens — and "toward the
lens" was measured with him facing the chair as the film has him, after a
first sign taken from a probe that had him facing the other way put it
behind the sleeve instead. The model's
signboard and talismans (its second material, skinned to the same hands)
also step out of the shot — `setTangProps(v)` from the pull to the black,
restore()/reset() put them back — so nothing else is in that hand.

> *"idle for at the medium event before pressing options, one is magic
> attack for when user press option 1, 2, and 3 at the medium event."*

ch3's rest clip is now Standing Idle 03; scenes A, B and C call the
two-handed magic take (2.83 s) at their climbs (A at 2.4 inside the trance
climb; B at 3.6 as it goes wrong; C at 8.7 so that when the auntie says
"watch his hands" and the boy looks, they are doing something). Scene D
keeps the idle. The drum still sets his rate. `medPlay(name, ts, fade)` is
the switch; a fade of 0 is a HARD CUT that bakes the pose at once, which B
needs at 8.9: `drumBeat` is 0 there, the mixer never advances, and a
crossfade would have left him mid-spell on the lens.

> *"catwalk turn, in chapter 5, where the medium is walking around the house
> or cutscene and needs an animation for when he is turning around."*

The take rotates his HIPS 178° to the right over 2.92 s (measured in the
engine by forcing the action's time, because this box runs ~1 fps and the
engine clamps dt to 0.05 s, so a clip's end can never be *waited for*
here). `mkTurn(api)` makes `turnTo(t0, ry0, ry1, ts, then)`: the group's
yaw is walked from ry0 to ry1 + π across the take, wrapped the short way,
and when the take ends the group snaps to ry1 as the take is cut for
`then`, whose hips carry no yaw — the visible facing is continuous through
the cut. Used at the film's one turnaround (177°, from the kitchen dark to
the chair), scene D's 115° corner at the table's end, and scene D's turn
from the altar to the boy (166°). Scene A's 38° corner is WALKED instead —
yaw eased over the first stride — because a half-turn take on a corner
spins him past and back.

> *"standard walking animation in-place."*

Every glide he makes now plays the walk, rate-matched to the glide's
distance over its time (0.3 for the shuffle round the table, 1.1 for the
run to the altar). The bake pins X and Z on ALL of his takes, because the
catwalk turn walks 1.9 m and the torch grab steps 0.7 m toward its wall,
and a take that travels under a group that also travels moves him twice.

> *"grab torch ... Just show the hell note disappearing from the table when
> the medium approaches the table, then he walks to the altar, play this
> animation, and have a fire cracking sound. There is no need to show the
> hell note being burnt at the altar because it looks very fake."*

Scene D: the note vanishes at the bottom of his lean (5.2) and is never
seen again; he walks to the altar (shuffle, turn, stride); the torch take
plays once and holds (13.6–18.2); the match strikes into the reach; the
fire takes with `noteburn` — which was always 12 seconds of paper burning,
i.e. the crackle Chad asked for, no new sound needed — and the altar
light lives with it. The world-space burning note, its shrink and its
curl are gone. Bell, room lift, Ma's thank-you unchanged.
