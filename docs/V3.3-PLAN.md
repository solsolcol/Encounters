## The variant machine (replaces standing→dart only)

- Appearance 1: behind the burner (unchanged) → stand → FLEE (dart away).
- Appearance 2: always `chase`.
- 3+: weighted pick — chase .30, cross .30, close .20, corner-flee .20 —
  never the same variant twice in a row.
- Per-variant: spawn point (frustum-validated), motion track, duration,
  chunk drain, sounds:

| variant | motion | chunk | sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| flee (first + corner) | stand 1.4–2.2 s then dart AWAY, k⁴, low hover, fade last 40 % | 4 | swoosh (pan) |
| chase | spawn 11–14 m dead-ahead-ish, glide AT the player, ease-in (k³), fade from 60 %, vanish ≥ 4.2 m away | 7 | swoosh + sobbing (pan) |
| cross | spawn at −30° edge, glide to +30° edge (or mirrored), 6–9 m out, ease-in-out, fade in 15 % / out last 25 % | 5 | swoosh (pan follows her!) |
| close | 2.2 m dead ahead, stand 1.1 s, fade in place, never moves | 10 | gscream + sobbing |

- Every appearance: rotating James reaction (vscare1..4) unless the vghost
  line is still owed; ~25 % extra gscream on non-close variants.
- Chunks apply at the reveal moment, only in state 'play', shown as the
  HUD tick numbers, each with a pulseSpike.
- Upright rule: `ghost.rotation.y` (facing player) is the ONLY rotation
  ever touched; mixer NEVER advances while she moves (frozen glide);
  slow advance (0.25–0.3×) only while standing.
- GHOST_MIN_DIST guard applies to flee/chase/cross; `close` deliberately
  breaks it (2.2 m) — ghosttest's distance floor is now 1.5 m.

## Directional audio implementation

- `snd(name, vol, rate, pan)` — optional 4th arg inserts a StereoPanner
  (guarded: skip if createStereoPanner missing).
- `ghostPan()` — ghost position → camera-local x/(|z|+1.5), clamped ±1.
- Loops: `packLoops[name]` gains a lazy panner; `loopPan(name, v)` slews
  it; updateAudioFrame sets whisper+ghostloop pan every frame.
- James reactions are the player's own voice: centered, via the narration
  channel (never over another line), rotating index for variety.
