## A. What changes (the short list)

Corrected or refuted:

1. **The white Buddha's name (row 2).** ปางห้ามมาร is *a* textbook name, not *the* one. ปางประทานอภัย is equally a book
   name for a raised right palm, seated or standing. The dictionary lists neither. Write: ปางห้ามมาร (also ปางประทานอภัย).
2. **Its near twins (row 7).** The list is right but short. Add ปางขับพระวักกลิ (a waving hand), ปางปลงอายุสังขาร (the
   hand laid on the chest), ปางโปรดพุทธมารดา (fingers joined or bent) and ปางแสดงโอฬาริกนิมิต (the LEFT hand raised):
   a mirrored model becomes another image.
3. **"pointed curls" (row 8).** The quoted sources give small curls, and the hall's are large round knobs. Say *a modern
   image in the Lao manner*, not Lan Xang.
4. **สะดุ้งมาร (row 30).** The dictionary accepts the name. Do not call it a mistake.
5. **Eight spokes (row 36).** "the 8 spoked wheel represents the noble eightfold path" is said of the Indo-Tibetan
   tradition in the source. The Thai dictionary allows 8 or 12. Eight is what the photographs show (row 40).
6. **The small white figure (row 47).** Probably a monk; not certain.
7. **The backdrop's middle (row 50).** A head AND a robed shoulder, not only a face.
8. **The backdrop's side figures (row 52).** The hermit reading is unlikely: the main figure wears a wide flat brim.
9. **The fan and laymen (row 57).** "nothing I read has a layman holding one" overstates: the note's own source says the
   first palm-leaf fan was used by monks and laypeople. The long-handled ceremonial fan is the monk's.
10. **The Commons fan (row 62).** The same kind, not a twin: copy the hall's own design.
11. **The green columns (row 66).** ต้นบายศรี / บายศรีหลัก is right. พาขวัญ is the kind built up on trays: drop it for
    these. An odd number of tiers: seven or nine.
12. **Nagas (row 69).** "an odd number of heads from one body" is practice, and a stated rule only for Khmer nagas; the
    hall's big group has five heads on five separate necks.
13. **The brown bai sri (row 80).** That "it is not fresh banana leaf" does not follow from the basins. Material unknown.
14. **The note's eight corrections to the build sheet (row 88).** Five stand as written; three carry a caution.
15. **REFUTED: the thread's colour (row 85).** It does not read dark "only where it crosses the gold cloth and the
    terracotta wall". It is dark in four frames, against the white thumb too. The build sheet's "thin dark cord" was right.

Lowered to "cannot be told" (each is a question in section E):

16. **The thread's name (row 83).** Tied like a thread, but dark: an old sai sin gone dark with smoke is the likely
    reading; it is not proven.
17. **The white Buddha's legs (row 15)** are hidden in every frame, and **its palm's slight turn (row 12)** cannot be
    measured. Build right leg over left and the palm straight out.
18. **Three names (rows 64, 71, 72):** the stacked silver and gold drums (ฉัตรเงิน ฉัตรทอง), the dark green towers
    (ขันหมากเบ็ง) and the white bird (หงส์). Build each from the photographs.
19. **Whose fan it is (row 58).** That it "marks the place where invited monks chant" is an inference.

## C. What the note lacks and the build needs

1. **The small standing Buddha is bigger than the build sheet has it.** It stands about two thirds of the wheel's
   diameter tall (0.69 in h05, 0.62 in h10), not 0.25 m against a 0.6 m wheel.
2. **The green wheel is flanked** by slim green cones with a bud on top (a white bud at its left, a gold one at its right).
3. **The stacked drums** are taller than they are wide (about 1.3 to 1 in h04), about nine to a column. The build
   sheet's 0.14 m a drum against 0.2 m across does not match. Each has a rolled rim at its top, and the pole shows in
   the small gaps between them. The finial is a flat pointed blade on a thin rod.
4. **The green columns:** a green satin pole; a small gold-rimmed disc under each tier (the encyclopedia's
   "แป้นรองบายศรีแต่ละชั้น"); green ribbon fronds of bead-tipped fingers curling up, with yellow flowers; a yellow bud on top.
5. **On the white Buddha's hand:** the thread's knot and its loose ends hanging down the palm; the garland's white tassel.
6. **The fan stands in a low stand** with small spired posts (two show at its foot in h02). The Commons example stands
   in one of the same kind.
7. **Not in the note or the build sheet, and not identified:** a dark wooden staff with a small carved figure at its
   head, standing beside the green Buddha before the clock (h02, h03); a pair of small two-tier umbrella-like ornaments
   at the gold Buddha's knees (h05, a few pixels wide: low confidence).
8. **Sources to drop or swap:** the lottery-section page (row 11); the wealth headline (row 70: the name only); the
   wealth-cult journal where the encyclopedia says the same (rows 37, 79).

**What would be wrong to show** (each from a row above):

- The thread as a bright white line, or as a cable. Every frame shows it thin, dark and tied.
- The green Buddha with a flame as well as a crown, with a robe or shawl, or with a hand on the knee.
- The lettering on the gold Buddha's base; the banknotes' print; any real person's face in the frames on the walls.
- A likeness of the spectacled monk before the owner says who he is and that he may be shown.
- The backdrop's side figures: they may be portraits of real people.

## D. Modelling briefs

One for each image that will be modelled. Each line is a thing a picture can be checked against.
References: R = my folder `web/verdicts/`; F = the folders beside it, `web/01` to `web/13`.

### Brief 1 · The white Buddha with the raised hand (D1, D2)

Type: seated Buddha, right palm raised (ปางห้ามมาร, also ปางประทานอภัย), a modern image in the Lao manner.
- **Seat:** cross-legged and flat, knees wide and low, right leg over left (the default; not visible in the photographs).
- **Right arm:** upper arm down beside the body, elbow bent, forearm upright. The hand is before the right chest at
  chest height, a little forward of it.
- **Right hand:** PALM OUT to the viewer. Four fingers straight, together, tips level. Thumb straight up beside the
  forefinger, not touching it. No curl, no ring.
- **Left hand:** in the lap, palm UP, fingers pointing to the image's right.
- **Head:** rows of large rounded knob curls; a tall narrow flame finial; arched raised brows; downcast eyes with dark
  pupils; a small closed smile; long ears whose lobes turn outward; a small dark dot between the brows; shallow neck lines.
- **Body:** right shoulder bare; the robe's edge is a modelled line from the left shoulder down under the right arm;
  smooth, no folds. Pearl silver-white all over.
- **Cloth (a separate thing):** gold lattice brocade from the LEFT shoulder across the chest; plain gold satin over the
  left arm and lap.
- **On the hand:** a thin dark thread, three to five turns low on the four fingers just above the palm, a knot toward
  the little finger, loose ends down the palm, then one sagging line off to the image's left. A yellow garland looped
  over the thumb, a white tassel at its end.
- **Lotus:** one round drum; two offset rows of large upturned petals, gold with silver-grey edges; a flat ledge on top.
- **Wrong if:** see section C.
- References: R `01-white-buddha` (a, b the posture and the white finish; d the Lao hand and face; e the hand close;
  c the popular name only; f a contrast). F `01-white-buddha` (the labelled bronzes).

### Brief 2 · The green Buddha in its costume (L17, L16)

Type: a replica of the Emerald Buddha in the summer costume.
- **Body:** deep green, glossy; seated cross-legged, back straight; both hands in the lap, right over left; bare torso
  under the jewellery.
- **Crown:** TALL, stepped, pointed, with a thin spire and an ear piece at each temple. No flame finial.
- **Shoulders:** one pointed upturned ornament on each.
- **Chest:** a broad collar; two chest pieces crossing to a large pendant (ทับทรวง).
- **Arms:** an armlet on each upper arm; bracelets at each wrist.
- **Waist:** a belt (รัดพระองค์) with a short hanging piece at its middle.
- **Knees:** one curved gold piece with an upturned tip at each knee end.
- **All ornaments:** gold; on the real image they are set with coloured stones.
- **Base:** tiered gold: a cushion-like top tier in leaf relief, a red band, a lower gold tier; a red-and-gold brocade
  panel hanging down the centre front.
- **Before the knees:** two small sprays, one silver and one gold, in little blue vases.
- **Wrong if:** a robe or shawl; a flame instead of or as well as the crown; a hand on the knee; any body colour but green.
- References: R `02-green-buddha-summer-costume` (four replicas; a has the nearest base). F `02-emerald-buddha` (the real
  image in all three costumes).

### Brief 3 · The gold Buddha (L9)

Type: seated Buddha, ปางมารวิชัย, gilded, wearing a cloth.
- **Seat:** cross-legged, right leg over left.
- **Right hand:** lying over the right knee, back of the hand to the viewer, fingers pointing straight down, not gripping.
- **Left hand:** palm up in the lap (under the cloth in the photographs).
- **Head:** small curls; a tall thin flame finial; calm oval face.
- **Body:** right shoulder bare; a flat robe flap from the left shoulder to the navel. Gilded all over.
- **Cloth (separate):** gold satin with an edging of small beads or braid, over the left shoulder, the left arm and the
  lap, hanging over the base's front.
- **Base:** tiered gold; leave its front band plain where the real one is lettered. On a dark wooden stand.
- **Wrong if:** the hand lies palm-up on the knee (that is ปางประทานพร); a hand is raised; the left hand is on the knee.
- References: R `03-gold-buddha-maravijaya` (a the classic gilded form, ignore the frame behind it; b a cloth on a
  seated image; c the hand and the legs). F `03-gold-maravijaya`.

### Brief 4 · The small standing Buddha before the green wheel (L12)

Type: standing Buddha, most probably ปางถวายเนตร (the Sunday image).
- **Stance:** upright, feet together, weight even.
- **Arms and hands:** both arms straight down in front; hands crossed before the tops of the thighs, right over left.
- **Head:** eyes open, looking straight ahead; a pointed finial.
- **Robe:** to the ankles, hanging straight. Gilded.
- **Size:** about two thirds of the wheel's diameter.
- **Wrong if:** a bowl at the waist (ปางอุ้มบาตร); hands on the chest (ปางรำพึง); arms at the sides; a raised hand.
