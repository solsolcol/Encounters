## 1. What chapter 1 is

S1·06 "How It All Began · Part One", SHORT, per Chad: the natural progression
after NS, then straight on. The case file: a software engineer who became a
dashcam businessman, looking for better luck — amulets, blessings, and then a
Sak Yant. *"What I was looking for was not enlightenment. It was better luck."*

The episode's axis is CONTROL (the episode plan §2). Chapter 1 is **ASKING**:
the only chapter in which he reaches for the unseen entirely on purpose and is
fully in control. The one thing that must happen in it: **the first
stirring** — as the last line of his first yant is struck, it warms. Nothing
more. Chapter 2 is when his hands move.

Episode 1 = boy (Aaron). Episode 2 = recruit (Aaron). Episode 3 = the man:
the equipment figure falls back to the ADULT `zav` scan already
(`ZAV_FIGURE`), and his voice is a grown man's (§5).

### 3.2 PLAY — the wat at dawn

The world (§4) is one compound: gate → courtyard → the offering stall on the
left → the steps up to the SALA (shoe rack at the foot) → inside, the Ajarn's
raised platform at the far end under a Buddha altar, mats on the floor, a
devotee already being tattooed.

Objectives, in order (the HUD beat for each, v8.7):

1. **"Buy an offering set at the stall"** — the stall auntie (`au1…`). A
   hotspot on her counter. She hands the tray: flowers (a lotus bud and
   jasmine garland), incense, a candle, a small packet of cigarettes and an
   envelope — the traditional *kan khru*. Line: **au1sell** *"Offering for
   Ajarn? This one. Flower, candle, incense. Put some money inside the
   envelope, okay?"* Item: `kanset` goes into the hand slot (a bag item, the
   torch's pattern — it is what he is carrying). +2 awareness.
2. **"Take off your shoes at the steps"** — a zone at the foot of the stair
   (the v8.7 red zone look). If he climbs in shoes, the auntie calls
   **au1shoes** *"Eh — shoes! Shoes outside!"* and the objective waits; −2
   awareness, once. Taking them off: a hotspot at the rack, **shoesoff** sound,
   barefoot steps from here (a new footstep set, `barestep1..4`? — no: the
   existing `step` rotation is shod; **the barefoot steps are one new sound
   `barestep` rotated by pitch**).
3. **"Pay respect at the altar"** (optional in spirit, on the path in fact —
   the Ajarn will not take a man who walks straight past the Buddha) — kneel
   and wai at the altar: a hotspot; the camera lowers (`kit.pose`, kneeling),
   **e3bell** once, a line **z1wai** *"I didn't really know what I was doing.
   I copied the man beside me."* +2 awareness.
4. **"Present your offering to the Ajarn"** — the Ajarn is working on another
   man. The assistant (`ls1…`) takes the tray from the player's hands
   (**ls1tray** *"Give to me. Sit there, wait."*), lays it before the Ajarn;
   the item leaves the bag.
5. **"Wait your turn"** — sit on the mat (a zone; the kit's pose). Watching:
   the rod's taps on the other man's back, the Ajarn murmuring, the man's
   jaw set. A short timed wait (~25 s) filled by the room: **aj1next** when it
   is done, *"Next."* The other man wais and goes.
6. **"Sit with your back to the Ajarn"** — the seat: a cushion on the floor in
   front of the platform, facing AWAY from him (that is how it is done: the
   master behind you, an assistant either side holding the skin taut). The
   pose locks; the view is the sala's open side, the courtyard, the dawn.
7. **THE YANT — the minigame.** A briefing card: *"The Ajarn tattoos with a
   long rod. Breathe out as each strike lands — tap on the strike."* The
   heartbeat kind re-skinned: `label: 'Hold still'`, the tick is the rod's
   own tap (**yantap**, a new optional `tick` sound on the kind — the one
   engine seam), 14 strikes from 70 bpm accelerating to ~110 (a rod is fast),
   `missCost` sanity (a flinch). Between phases the Ajarn murmurs the katha
   (**aj1katha**, *"Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa"* —
   murmured, the one Pali line every Thai blessing opens with; correct and
   verifiable). The screen breathes red at a miss (the pain), white at a
   perfect. Awards: sanity by score (the ladder).
8. **THE STIRRING.** The last strike. The Ajarn blows on the yant
   (**yantblow**) — and on the frame after, **it warms**: a gold wash from the
   screen's top edge (his back), a low shimmer (**yantwarm**), the view
   brightening a touch, a tingle in the hands (the viewmodel's fingers curl
   and uncurl — `setHandCurl`), haptic. Line **z1warm** *"It went warm. Not
   the sting. Something under it."* The assistant: **ls1done** *"Finish.
   Turn around, sit in front of Ajarn."*
9. **THE DECISION.** He turns to face the Ajarn (the lens turns), who puts
   his hand on the new yant, and asks, through his thin English:
   **aj1ask** *"Now it is on you. What you ask of it?"* The decision opens
   by itself (the Ajarn IS the pile).

Ambience through play: **watamb** (dawn temple: birds, a far road, leaves
swept on stone, wind chimes), **e3chant** (monks' morning chanting from the
ubosot across the courtyard, keyed to distance), the Ajarn's rod whenever he
is working (**yantap** at its working rhythm, positional). A music bed only
under the waiting and the tattoo: **e3wait**, sparse and warm, never over the
chant.

### 3.3 THE DECISION — "Now it is on you. What you ask of it?"

| | option (his words) | verdict | the Ajarn's answer (scene) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | "Luck. For my business." | good | He laughs, not unkindly. *"Luck come, luck go. Business is business. The yant is not for money. Keep five precepts, and it will look after you."* |
| B | "Protection. From whatever's out there." | bad | *"Out there?"* He taps his own chest. *"What you are afraid of, you bring with you. Protection starts here."* |
| C | "Next time, give me the strongest one you have." | worst | He takes his hand off the yant. *"Strong for what? Yant is not a weapon. A man who wants the strongest does not keep even the first."* |
| D | "What does it ask of me?" | best | He looks at him a long time, and nods. *"Good. First one who ask me this today."* The rules: *"No lying. No stealing. No hurting. No drunk. Do good. Then it will ask more of you, later. You will see."* |

Each scene then plays the same ending with its own card line: he wais, stands,
takes his shoes, and walks out through the gate into the sun, the lens rising
to the sala's roof and the sky (the v15.3 ending shape), his closing line
**z1close** (all four): *"Software engineer. Business owner. Amulets.
Blessings. Sak Yant. Looking back now, I can see how quietly one step led to
the next."* — and, over black, **z1next** *"But something else had to happen
first. That is where the story really begins."*

Card lines (`sayPrefix: 'z1'`): **z1A** *"I asked for luck. He told me what it
would cost."* · **z1B** *"I asked for protection. He pointed at my own
chest."* · **z1C** *"I asked for more. He showed me I had not understood the
first."* · **z1D** *"I asked what it wanted from me. That was the right
question."*

Deltas on the ±30 scale, same rank formula: A {s:+12, a:+15, w:+18}, B {s:−6,
a:+9, w:−12}, C {s:−15, a:−9, w:−27}, D {s:+18, a:+24, w:+30}.

Teaching (`core`), S1·06's lesson verbatim, three sentences (Chad's v11.8
length): *"Almost nobody arrives at a spiritual path by deciding to. There is
no shame in starting from self-interest; that is where most of us start. What
matters is what you do once the path opens."* No Pali tag (the LESSON LINE
rule: the term comes from Chad; Master Z's own sheet lists Anicca, Paṭicca

Each option's `teach`: A *"Luck is a fine reason to begin. It is a poor

inside."* · C *"Wanting the strongest is wanting power you have not earned
the understanding of."* · D *"Before you ask what a practice will give you,
ask what it will ask of you."*

## 4. THE WORLD — every set and prop

### 4.1 The wat (play), units metres, y up
- **Compound:** a whitewashed boundary wall with a red-tiled coping; the GATE
  (two white pillars, a small tiered roof, two guardian statues — yak
  stand-ins, two big frangipani/banyan trees beside it).
- **Courtyard:** pale stone paving (canvas: large slabs, worn), a bodhi tree
  with a raised planter and coloured cloths tied round the trunk, a bell
  tower (a small open pavilion with a hanging bell), potted bougainvillea,
  a stray dog asleep in the shade (a static low-poly dog — model slot),
  the ubosot's corner across the courtyard (a tall white wall, gold
  window frames, red-gold tiered roof with chofa finials), a line of
  lanterns.
- **The offering stall:** a wooden stall with a striped awning, trays of
  offering sets (lotus buds, marigold/jasmine garlands, incense bundles,
  candles, small envelopes), a cooler box, the stall auntie behind it.
- **The steps and the shoe rack:** three wide steps up to the sala, a naga
  balustrade (primitive serpent rails in gold/green), a wooden shoe rack with
  a dozen pairs of slippers and shoes, and a sign **"PLEASE REMOVE SHOES"**
  (English, and Thai: "กรุณาถอดรองเท้า").
- **The sala:** an open-sided pavilion, 14 × 9 m, polished wood floor (canvas
  planks), 10 red-lacquer pillars with gold stencil bands, a tiered roof
  (three layers, dark-red and green tiles, gold bargeboards, chofa finials),
  ceiling fans, pendant lamps (off at dawn), reed mats in rows, a low table
  of amulets and water bottles.
- **The altar** at the far end: a stepped altar (gold-and-red), a seated
  golden Buddha (model slot; stand-in: a lathe-built seated figure in gold
  leaf with an emissive halo), smaller Buddha images, vases of lotus,
  candles, incense pots with smoke (the chapter's particles), garlands,
  a framed photograph of the old abbot.
- **The Ajarn's platform:** a raised wooden dais (0.4 m) with a cushion, a
  low lacquer tray of rods (the long *mai sak* — thin steel needles on
  a rod, ~60 cm), ink pots (black ink and a sesame-oil pot), cotton wool,
  a spirit lamp, his water, the pile of offerings from the day.
- **The waiting devotees:** 3–4 men sitting on the mats (model slots), one
  on the tattoo cushion with two assistants either side.
- **Light:** DAWN — low sun from the gate side, gold, long shadows into the
  sala; sky pink to blue; the altar's candles; `daylight` declared.
