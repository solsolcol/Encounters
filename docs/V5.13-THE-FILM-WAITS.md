# v5.13 — THE FILM WAITS FOR ITS SOUNDS, and six smaller things

Chad, on v5.12: *"why when i select chapters from the chapter selector,
the intro cutscenes that play have no voicelines or sound effects or
other sounds when the cutscenes play? what other bugs can you find?"*

## 1. The silent film — measured, then fixed

A cue in a cutscene is sample-only: `sting()` asks `sndBuf()` for the
sample and, if the buffer has not decoded yet, kicks the decode and plays
NOTHING (`docs/AUDIO-PLAN.md`; that is why `startDecision()` warms the
whole cutscene set). A chapter's own sounds live in its own pack, fetched
by `setChapter()`.

On the advance path that is fine: `startDecision()` fetched the NEXT
chapter's pack a whole decision before its film, and `warmIntroSet()`
found it present. From the selector — and on Continue into a chapter the
player sealed into but never entered — `setChapter()` starts the fetch
moments before `enterWorld()`, `warmIntroSet()` runs before the pack
exists and warms nothing, and every cue in the film fires into the gap.

Measured with a cue log (`stingLog`, read through `__enc.stings()`; each
entry says what became of a cue: `sample`, `step`, `synth`, `decoding`,
`no-pack`, `muted`, `no-ctx`): replaying chapter 2 from the selector on
this box, the pack was already present at film start (localhost is fast)
and the clock and the fan played — but `v2wake1`, "It followed me home",
reported `decoding` and was lost. On a phone, with a real network and a
slower decoder, every cue lands in that gap. Chad heard exactly that.

**The fix, in `enterWorld()`'s film path:** the pack first
(`packLoad(CH_KEY)`, capped at 12 s), then `warmIntroSet()`, then
`whenDecoded(introSamples(), …)` — every named sample that is in the pack
has decoded, or 4 s have passed — then the models (`whenWorldReady`),
then the film. Each stage is capped so nothing holds the black forever.
`introSamples()` is the intro's own cues mapped to their samples plus the
bed the warm set always carried. Re-probed: every cue `sample`.

The same wait covers the resume path, so the older bug — a player who
sealed chapter 1, closed the tab, and pressed Continue into chapter 2's
film — is fixed by the same lines.

## 2. The other bugs, found by reading the new paths

- **The menu during a faint.** `fainting` runs with `state === 'play'`,
  so M opened the menu mid-faint, and Back to the title would have saved
  the zero sanity the faint exists to rewrite — Continue would then faint
  again on landing. `menuOpen()` and `returnToTitle()` refuse while
  fainting.
- **The room tone followed you to the title.** `silenceChapterLoops()`
  is for SWITCHING chapters: it keeps the current chapter's own loops.
  Leaving to the title with the same chapter left the void deck's crickets
  and the burner running under the title video. Every live loop goes to
  zero now; the ambient frame re-asserts them on the next entry.
- **His opening line on the title screen.** `queueVoice()` arms a
  two-second timer at play start; leave within two seconds and the line
  landed on the title. The timer is cleared on the way out.
- **The red of a low sanity followed you out.** `ui.panic`'s `critical`
  class and opacity are cleared the way `restart()` clears them.
- **A deep link unlocked everything.** `?ch=ch5` is a preview and writes
  no save; it now opens nothing in the selector either (`markReached`
  returns early on `CH_ASKED`).
- **The volume slider sat over the panels.** On a laptop `#volWrap` is a
  separate fixed element at the same z-index as the menu and inventory;
  it hides while either is open.
