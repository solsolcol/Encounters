## 1. v24.10 · the incense sticks (episode 3 chapter 5, ending D)

**The cause.** `giveStick(i)` hides each joss stick in the bundle in his hand as it is given (`St.g.children[n].visible
= false`) — right for play: the bundle thins as each child gets its stick — and the third gift lets the bundle go
(`kit.hold(null)`). Ending D's shot d1b (`dState('d1b')`) sets the SAME bundle out on the desk again with
`deskIncense('stage')`, which reparented and leaned it and never set the sticks out. After a real play-through the
lighter was struck over three hidden sticks: 0 meshes drawn, the flame lit over nothing.

**Why five reviews missed it.** Every photograph of the endings came from the probes' shortcut, `applyPhase('decide')`
straight after boot: nothing has been given there, all three sticks are drawn, and the shot looked right. The one real
play-through that reached ending D (v24.2) logged its voices and errors and never looked at its pictures. The bug
needed two places read together — the gift in play (~line 2930) and the re-staging in the ending (~line 6970) — and the
review lenses were split by topic. It has been so since v23.0 CP8, when the shot was written (`git log -S`).

**The fix.** `deskIncense('stage')` sets all three sticks out (its own comment always said "'stage' sets all three
out"). Line 6967's tips-off and the scale reset stay load-bearing (the audit, §2).

**The proof, through the real presses** (phone): the run put at the incense order, the packet pressed through the
engine's `interactNow()`, the three sand bowls pressed — the bundle read 111 → 011 → 001 → 000, exactly as play leaves
it — the decision, option D, the shot seeked. On v24.9 the bundle drew 0 meshes under the flame; on v24.10 all three
sticks lean into it and catch (6–9 meshes, the tips' embers and flames on their cue). Deploy
`6ae38894e9e085242e614e8c`, byte-verified 268 of 268.

**The check that would have caught it, kept:** `tools/probes/scenesig.mjs` (every drawn thing in the world's scene,
keyed by what it is and where it stands), `e3c5drive` (`SIG=`, `ENDTS=auto`: an ending photographed and recorded after
a REAL play-through), `e3c5ends` (`SIG=`, `TS=auto`: the same after the jump), and `sigdiff.mjs` comparing them.

## 4. v24.12 · the storm, watched (episode 4 chapter 1)

**What WHAT CHANGED? was for** (the plan, §4.5): the chapter's lesson as something you do. By the storm he has noticed
four signs; the power cuts and the lightning shows the room in flashes; between some flashes things really change (the
cup, the cloth, the clock, her a step closer), between others nothing does; tap only what changed — a false tap cost
sanity: certainty ahead of the evidence, the question the decision then asks.

**Why it failed.** On a phone it was a spot-the-difference memory test in 2.6-second flashes of a dim room: hard for
eyesight and memory, not for the lesson — and the four signs had taught the lesson a minute before.

**What shipped.** The storm is a film from play (`kit.film('storm')`, the twenty-sixth seam), 17.4 s, every picture a
function of its clock (`STORM`, `stormFrame`), the hush and the decision after it in play exactly as before:

| t (s) | shot | what is shown |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3.3 | his eyes on the master (play's kneeling look) | flash 1 at 0.9 (lit 1.3 s): the room as it is — the baseline; on a desktop, her by the door at the frame's edge |
| 3.3–6.3 | close on the low table | the cup has moved in the dark (on the cut); flash 2 at 4.2 shows it beside the WET RING where it stood (new, `room.cupRing`) |
| 6.3–7.95 | close on the covered bowl | the cloth lies differently (moved on the cut); flash 3 at 7.2, lit longer (1.75 s) |
| 7.95–9.7 | the clock | in the same flash: the pendulum hangs still (stopped with the cloth); the case has a glass window now, so it can be seen |
| 9.7–13.7 | his eyes on the master, pushing in | she stood a step closer while the lens was on the clock (9.3); she is at the frame's edge in the candlelight, and flash 4 at 11.3 shows her plainly, on every screen |
| 13.7–16.3 | the master's face, chanting | flash 5 at 14.4, the loudest crack: nothing has changed |
| 16.3–17.4 | his eyes on the master | the frame play takes over on |

Every change is made on a cut or in a shot that cannot see it, so nothing moves in front of the lens; the lit beats are
the room's FLASH light, the rest the candles' DARK, and each flash's flicker fires once as its moment passes.

The thunder is the film's own cues (a far roll, four cracks, the loudest last); the drone (`w4storm`) rises from the
film's first frame and stops with the rain; the room's rain beds stay up under the film (`mixBeds`: every other film
cues its own sound). A Continue saved in the storm plays it again; refused while play is not running (the menu up on
the frame it began), it waits for play; a skip lands on its last frame's room. `stormEnd()` sets the room as the storm
leaves it whether the film ran, was skipped or never played.

**Found by the photographs, and fixed before it shipped** (phone and desktop, every beat, then the film let run into
play and the decision):
- A Continue into the storm began the film under the arrival's black, which a film never lifts — all seventeen seconds
  played black on a phone. The film now waits for the black to lift (`stormWatch`).
- The first cut moved the cup at 2.9 in the opening shot, where it could be seen to jump, and she stepped closer at
  10.0, inside a shot a desktop could see her in. Every change is on a cut now, hers inside the clock shot.
- The clock's pendulum hung inside its solid case, so "the clock stopped" showed nothing; the case has a glass window.
- The silver offering dish beside the cup drew only its rim from above — a lathe's floor faces down — so the new close
  shot showed a hollow grey ring. It is two-sided now, 4 mm off the wood: at 0 mm its floor shared the table top's plane
  (a jagged star of wood through the silver) and at 2 mm the candles' painted pool's (a pinwheel of its wedges).
- Her figure fades as the rain stops (`stormEnd`), on the frame play takes over — the rain, the drone, the chant and
  her all go at once, as the storm always ended.

**Gone:** the `change` event from this chapter, its two words (`chgLabel`, `chgBrief`) and its three card notes
(`noteStorm*`) — sheet v132. **Kept:** the engine's `change` kind (S44) and its picture, proven by the fixture, for a
chapter that wants it.
