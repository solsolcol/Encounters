## 1 · The models

The v5.05 crowd recipe, unchanged, through `tools/prepwoman.mjs`: drop every
map `rescueTextures()` cannot restore (it only ever rescues base colour, so a
normal map is pure download), textureCompress the body sheet, weld, simplify,
then `KHR_mesh_quantization` — the one compression this game gets for free,
handled inside three.js's own GLTFLoader with no decoder and no blob.

| asset | who | shipped |
|---|---|---|
| `sitman.glb` | a seated man | 595 KB |
| `sitwoman.glb` | a seated woman | 1338 KB |
| `sitshout.glb` | a seated man shouting | 389 KB |
| `scold.glb` | the scolding granny | 1257 KB |
| `zavyoung.glb` | young Master Zav | 1811 KB |

`sitwoman` would not simplify below ~26k triangles. The first diagnosis was
the simplifier's `error: 0.02` cap, and `prepwoman.mjs` gained an eighth
argument to raise it — which changed nothing (26309 tris at 0.02, 26331 at
0.08). The real floor is topological: her UV seams are locked and the
simplifier will not collapse across them. 1338 KB was accepted. **The new
argument was kept** — it is a genuine improvement to the tool even though
this model was not what it was reached for. Recording the wrong first guess
matters more than the fix: raising a cap that is not the binding constraint
produces a number that looks like it worked.

**Corrected at v5.31.** The "topological floor" above was wrong: at the tool's
own defaults the same source simplifies to 47k triangles at ratio 0.08 and
165k at 0.28, so the shipped 26k came from a far lower ratio than this doc
believed — and 26k on a 512-pixel sheet was, in Chad's words, "deformed".
`sitwoman.glb` and `scold.glb` were re-encoded gentler; docs/V5.31-TWO-FACES.md.

`kana.glb` is DELETED. Nothing else referenced it.

## 2 · The seating, solved

Chad said *mathematically*, so this is an optimisation, not a shuffle.

`KINDS` is now six: `encik`, `sitclap`, `sitangry`, `sitman`, `sitwoman`,
`sitshout`. Three steps, all inside one block in `ch3.js`:

**A balanced deal.** `bag[k] = k % KINDS.length`. At 19 seats that is
`[4,3,3,3,3,3]`; at `LOW`'s 10 seats it is `[2,2,2,2,1,1]`. Every kind gets
at least one seat **by construction** — which is Chad's "every model
represented at least once, don't accidentally remove any", enforced rather
than checked afterwards.

**A seeded shuffle.** One LCG (`0x9E3779B1`, 1664525/1013904223) drives both
the Fisher–Yates and the search, so the tent is byte-identical on every
load, every device and every test run. A crowd that reshuffles per session
is a crowd no probe can defend.

**A local search.** Two seats are neighbours when they are within **2.30 m**
— measured in the car park, not in grid indices, so it picks up the row
(0.9 m), the row behind (2.0 m) and the diagonals (2.19 m) and correctly does
NOT bridge the 1.8 m aisle as though it were an ordinary gap. Each pair is
weighted `1/d`, because the closer two identical faces sit the more they read
as a copy. Cost is the weighted count of neighbour pairs sharing a kind. A
swap only changes the cost around its two seats, so each step is O(1) and
4000 of them are free.

Measured on the shipped build:

| density | seats | neighbour pairs | after shuffle | after search |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FULL | 19 | 39 | cost 3.08 | **0.00 — 0 pairs** |
| `LOW` | 10 | 9 | cost 0.50 | **0.00 — 0 pairs** |

Not "fewer". None. That is the optimum, reached well inside the cap.

`stage.seatStats()` reports `{seats, counts, samePairs, pairs}` so the claim
stays checkable rather than remembered. **A `samePairs` that is not 0 is a
regression.**

## 3 · The granny at the brazier

`scold` takes `standers[2]` — the figure at `[4.6, -3.9]` feeding the fire —
exactly as kana took it at v5.22. The whole contract is kana's, unchanged,
because it was right: the primitive stays visible until the real bytes land,
the model inherits its position and sway phase, and it faces the FIRE
computed from where the brazier is rather than inheriting a yaw.

She is the right casting and kana never was. Kana's one take is a pleasant
talking loop; the line delivered here is a shout at a boy who has walked
somewhere he should not have. This model's single clip is literally
`Stand_Talking_Angry`. Idle is its first frame held; the take runs only while
a scene sets `stage.brazierTalk`, which scene B does at 15.0 with the camera
on the brazier.

Height: `BRAZ_H = 1.66`, a woman's height beside the standers' 1.74, measured
from POSED BONES with the crowd's loose `/Head/` — which on her Mixamo rig
catches `HeadTop_End`, the crown, which is what is wanted here. (The
technically-correct `HEAD_RE` would stop at the head JOINT and stand her
~23 cm tall — the v5.05 lesson, in the direction that bites.)

## 4 · Young Master Zav in the panel

The rotating figure in the equipment screen is the man the player is
*becoming*, so his age tracks the story. `ZAV_FIGURE` in main.js is the
table and `zavKey()` reads it:

| episode | chapters | figure | asset |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ch1–ch5 | young | `zavyoung` — shipping now |
| 2 | ch1–ch5 | teenager | `zavteen` — **Chad supplies the model** |
| later | the adult phase | adult | `zav`, the 990k-tri scan |

Keyed by CHAPTER because the chapter key is what the engine actually knows;
the episode is the reason behind the grouping, not a thing the engine tracks.
`ZAV_ADULT` is the fallback for any chapter not listed, so a chapter added
without a row gets the adult rather than nothing.

**The adult is kept, not deleted** — Chad's explicit instruction. Every
number that shaped him survives with him: the meshopt pack and its
WebAssembly decoder under the strict CSP (v5.10), the no-mipmap rule for his
atlas (v5.11 — the white hair streaks), the seam padding. He returns when the
adult phase does, with nothing to rebuild.

Swapping ages is a real swap, not a preference: `zavLoad()` disposes the
figure standing there — geometry, materials and maps — whenever `zav.key` no
longer matches `zavKey()`, then fetches the right one. Within episode 1 that
branch never fires, because all five chapters name the same figure.
`setChapter()` re-arms the prefetch (`zavPrefetched = false; zavPrefetch()`)
so the panel still opens with him already standing there.

`__enc.zav()` reports `{key, want, model}` for probes — the figure lives in
its own renderer's scene, unreachable from the world graph, so it cannot be
measured the way the tent is.

## What this build is worth remembering for

- **"Mathematically" is an instruction you can actually satisfy.** A
  balanced deal guarantees coverage; a weighted neighbour graph turns "looks
  repetitive" into a number; a local search drives that number to zero. The
  result is defensible — `seatStats()` prints it — where a shuffle would only
  ever be *probably* fine.
- **A cap you raise that changes nothing was not the constraint.** `sitwoman`
  sat at 26k triangles at both 0.02 and 0.08 error; the floor was her UV
  seams. Two measurements, not one, is what told the difference.
- **Reach for a character's OWN clip.** `scold` ships exactly one take,
  `Stand_Talking_Angry`, and it is the scene. The v5.20 lesson, again.
