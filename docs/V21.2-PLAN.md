## CP1 · why the lights could not be hit (measured, not guessed)

Every earlier probe of STAY PRESENT had tapped the light by reading its
hidden position (`getBoundingClientRect`) on a box that renders about one
frame a second — so it could pass even when a person cannot see the light,
and it never saw the light MOVE. `dbg-e3c3aware.mjs` runs the page on
Playwright's fake clock: it flows normally to the brief, then is stepped
16 ms a frame, so what it records is exactly what a 60 fps phone draws. A
simulated person taps 0.75 s after a light first shows, where it was drawn
0.25 s earlier.

v21.1, on a 390 × 844 phone: **five lights of six came on at the screen's
right edge** (x 361–374 of 390 — the dot half off the glass, its ring
further; only the first, the Buddha, already ahead of him, came on inside, at
144), because the trance always turns right and the light switched on the
moment its thing crossed the edge of the view, and then **slid 87–194 px
inward** (the first 39) while the turn carried the thing toward the middle
and the dance's sway, nod and foot-drift moved it about. A person aims at where a light came on;
by the time the finger lands it is somewhere else.

## CP2 · the steady light

Engine (`src/main.js`, opt-in `steady: true` on a graded focus event — no
other chapter opens one; the fixture proves it):
- `evFocusSteady`: a light comes on only when its target's `ready()` says so
  AND its projection lies inside a safe `box` (NDC); it comes on with a
  flash (`#evDot.pop`: a filter flash and a ring thrown out by a
  pseudo-element — never an animated transform, which would move the light,
  see LEARNINGS), the HUD's chime (`popSound`) and a tick under the thumb;
  it is held where it came on, eased toward its thing (`ease` 0.08 s).
- Nothing runs before it is shown: no life, no `lost` charge. A light the
  game never put on the screen is skipped after `waitMax` (12 s) — NOT
  charged.
- `gap` (0.7 s) between one light resolving and the next coming on, so the
  word it earned is read first.
- `evState()` reports `idx`, `placed`, `slotT`, `shown`, `skipped` for probes.

Chapter (`tranceTick`): the dance brings each thing to REST a third of the
way out from the middle (alternating sides) and a little above the middle,
between the raised hands below and the panel above; `readyFor` is set once
it has held there 0.12 s; the sway, the nod and the feet's drift quieten as
it arrives and drop to 8 % while the light is up. The light's life is 2.4 s
with bands 0.8 / 1.2 / 1.7 / 2.3 s (a fair reaction to a still light on a
phone is 0.6–1.0 s).

Measured after, same probe, on the build that shipped: lights come on at
x 140–249, y 392–452 (both thirds, under the panel), travel 3–16 px while
up; the simulated person's real touch taps land 1–3 px from the light; and
the event's OWN result is `[3,3,3,3,3,3]` — six PERFECTs of six. (The last
light's grade cannot be read off `evState()`, because the event closes on
the frame it is graded; the probe wraps `kit.event` to keep the result.)
Fixture: `focusSteadyWaits`, `focusSteadyPops`, `focusSteadyHit`,
`focusSteadyNeverFree`, `focusSteadyLookGoes`.

## CP3 · the radio chant

The eerie bed was `shopchant`, made at v21.0 by the SOUND-EFFECTS model from
"lay people chanting in Pali" — which makes wordless voice texture: measured,
a continuous wall with no breath in 20 s, at 216 Hz, men and women together.
Replaced from flow `qapQMCX9SGeqWVMjA91j`: the music model with the
**Itipiso as its lyrics** ("a group of monks reciting Pali in unison, a
cappella, one steady low reciting tone"), four takes; transcribed to prove
the words are sung (take c chants the whole text, 0.18–74.96 s); picked c —
f0 IQR 0.25 st (one reciting tone), every syllable on an even pulse, 85 % in
the band a phone plays. Previews of c, a and d went to Chad. Recipe:
`masters/v21.2/make.sh`.
