# v6.16 — THE LEAVES GOT THEIR ALPHA BACK

> Chad, on v6.15: *"the trees looks way too heavily compressed, cant it look
> better?"*

Yes — and "compressed" was the right word for the wrong reason. The geometry
was barely reduced and the sheets were only half size. What was actually
missing was the **alpha channel**: every leaf sheet had been re-encoded as a
JPEG, and JPEG has no alpha. A leaf card whose cut-out is gone is an opaque
rectangle, so each crown was a pile of solid textured cards instead of leaves.

## The bug, in one line

The prep tool decided which sheet was foliage and which was bark **by the
texture's name**:

```js
const nm = (t.getName() || '') + ' ' + (t.getURI() || '');
if (/leaf|leaves|crown|branch/i.test(nm)) continue;   // ...JPEG the rest
```

All three of Chad's files embed their textures with **no name and no URI**, so
that test matched nothing, every sheet fell through to the bark branch, and
every leaf sheet was flattened to JPEG. Measured on the shipped v6.15 files:

| file | leaf sheet as shipped |
|---|---|
| tree1 | 512×512 **jpeg**, `hasAlpha=false` |
| tree2 | 512×512 **jpeg**, `hasAlpha=false` |
| tree3 | 512×512 **jpeg**, `hasAlpha=false` |
| tree4 | 512×512 **jpeg**, `hasAlpha=false` |

Against the source, where the low-poly sheet is a 512×512 PNG whose alpha runs
the full 0–255 with **50.9 % of its texels under half** — that half is the gaps
between the leaves, and it was thrown away.

The materials were right the whole time (`MASK`, cutoff 0.45, double-sided) —
they were testing an alpha channel that no longer existed, so nothing was ever
cut out.

## The fix

**Which sheet is which is decided by the MATERIAL that uses it**, never by the
texture's own name. A material is foliage if its name says so *or* if the file
shipped it as `BLEND` — recorded before the prep rewrites the alpha mode, so a
model with unnamed materials still sorts correctly. Its base-colour texture
stays PNG with its alpha; everything else becomes JPEG.

Palette mode is what keeps that affordable: a foliage sheet is a few greens and
a hard alpha edge, and `alphaCutoff` discards every partial value anyway, so
256 colours costs nothing visible and about a quarter of the bytes.

The same law now holds in the engine (`treeKit`): a part is foliage if its
material carries `alphaTest > 0` or `transparent`, with the name test as the
fallback. A model whose materials are unnamed can no longer be mistaken for
bark.

## What else got better while the tool was open

- **Full geometry.** No simplification at all now: tree1 is back to its whole
  19,921 triangles (v6.15 shipped 12,799), tree2 to 7,112. Simplifiers are
  particularly bad for leaf cards, which are quads whose silhouette *is* the
  model.
- **1024-pixel sheets** for the two big trees, both leaf and bark (v6.15 halved
  them to 512). The low-poly pair keep 512, which is their source size —
  upscaling would buy bytes and no detail.
- **Bark at JPEG quality 88**, up from 82.
- **Anisotropic filtering** on every tree sheet. A crown is nothing but grazing
  angles, and without it the far leaves crawl. Same setting the hell notes have
  had since v3.8.

## The cost

| | v6.15 | v6.16 |
|---|---|---|
| tree1 (realistic) | 572 KB, 12,799 tris | 1065 KB, 19,921 tris |
| tree2 (oak) | 176 KB, 6,106 tris | 477 KB, 7,112 tris |
| tree3 (low poly A) | 330 KB, 3,519 tris | 412 KB, 3,519 tris |
| tree4 (low poly B) | 278 KB, 1,862 tris | 360 KB, 1,862 tris |
| **the kit** | **1.36 MB** | **2.31 MB** |

Just under a megabyte more, once per session, shared by every chapter that
plants a tree — which is the whole game. Worth it for foliage that is foliage.

## Verified

- Every leaf sheet re-measured after prep: PNG, `hasAlpha=true`, alpha 0–255,
  80.2 % / 89.1 % / 50.7 % / 50.7 % of texels under half.
- Rendered in chapter 3's car park in full daylight — at a tree's foot, looking
  up into a crown, and along a row at distance. The crowns break into leaf
  clusters with sky and branch structure through them; nothing reads as a card.
- The live materials read back out of the running game: every foliage part
  `alphaTest 0.45`, `side: DoubleSide`, `transparent: false`, `anisotropy 8`,
  with 1024-pixel sheets on the two big kinds and 512 on the low-poly pair.
- `chapter`, `leak`, `csp`, `hosted` green in 136 s. `csptest` matters most
  here: the kit is loaded by hand-parsed GLB under the strict no-blob/no-data
  policy, and PNG-with-alpha is a different path through it than JPEG.
  `leaktest` matters second: the sheets got bigger and the kit is shared, so
  a chapter must still hand the GPU back everything it took.
- Deployed from inside `dist/` and byte-verified against the live site:
  **65/65 match, 0 mismatch** (deploy `6a9f9f14b73f33bce0521bf7`).
