## 0. What was measured before anything moved (v24.8, from the chapter's own code and its measured line lengths)

- **The whole chapter runs about 9½ minutes:** the film 72 s, the yard about 3 min, the receiving room about 3¾ min,
  the decision, an ending of 52–82 s and the 24 s coda.
- **The yard took about 3 minutes whichever way it was played.** The numbers were called one at a time: the first
  24 s after the greeting, then 30 s apart (only 3.5 s after a conversation). A player who asked nobody stood in the
  sala for about 2½ minutes with nothing to do. A player who asked everyone heard 64 s of conversation (Khun Joy
  16.5 s, Uncle Lim 14.3, Ta Sombat through Pong 21.3, Pong 12.3), four note pages and five calls.
- **The notes stopped the game nine times:** four after the yard's conversations, one after the reading and four
  after the room's signs. Nothing later depended on them. A "fact" paid +2 awareness, a "meaning" nothing, and two
  lines went on the outcome card. The decision and all four endings were the same whatever was written. Endings A and
  D drew the pages in the book shot, and chapter 2 (not built) was planned to read them back.
- **The birthday pick** changed only what the master chalked on his slate; the reading's words were the same for all
  eight days.
- **Why they were there** (docs/V24.0-E4C1-PLAN.md §4.2): the case file's lesson, "Ask the ordinary questions you
  would ask about anyone you are about to trust … who can genuinely vouch for them?", turned into a yard of people to
  question, and the trial's "evidence first, interpretation second" turned into a notebook. One question to Pong carries
  the first; the room's signs, found by looking and said aloud, carry the second.
