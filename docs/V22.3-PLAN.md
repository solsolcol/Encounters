# v22.3 · Episode 3 chapter 4, debugged in depth

Chad, 24 Oct 2026, straight after reporting v22.2's three bugs (the Yin amulet gone after Continue, the five-foot way's
ceiling flickering, the roofs across the street incomplete):

> *"Also do a very deep and comprehensive debugging of this chapter"*

"This chapter" is episode 3 chapter 4, THE PIECES THAT ANSWERED (`src/chapters/e3/e3c4.js`, 10,401 lines; its build
memory is docs/V22.0-E3C4-PLAN.md). v22.2 shipped first (deployed, its own record is that plan's §12.4), so this pass
reads the chapter as v22.2 left it.

## The method (v21.1's shape, which found the most last time)

1. **Code lenses, read-only, in parallel**, each one narrow, each finding with its line, the code quoted, a concrete
   failure (what the player does → what goes wrong) and how sure it is:
   1. save, resume, phases and reset (every phase and tag written and read back; the v22.2 same-sitting Continue
      path; receipts; what reset forgets);
   2. World Y and the Yin experience (geometry, blockers, anchors, the cold effects' teardown, the girl, STEADY);
   3. World H, the Hanuman experience and the run-out film;
   4. World T, the tray, the street and the decision's pile;
   5. the four endings (the cutscene laws: pure functions of the clock, held glides, late cues, warmed cues, the lens
      and the hands handed back, silences, the standing rules);
   6. the opening film and the merit film (the same laws, plus a film from play's hand-off);
   7. sound, words, HUD and the standing rules (rows, warm lists, SECS, dropped lines, beds in the wrong world,
      objectives that congratulate a non-completion, the sheet);
   8. the engine seams the chapter uses, per-frame cost and disposal.
2. **Phone photographs of every beat** on the deployed build (the films at steps, each world's play, each minigame,
   each ending, look-arounds), with desktop where framing matters, read for what is wrong on screen.
3. **Every finding verified before it is touched**: default to refuted, quote the line as it stands today, and where it
   is a picture, photograph it. Findings that do not survive are written down with the reason, so the next pass does
   not chase them again.
4. **Fix** the confirmed ones, each the smallest change that removes the cause.
5. **Release**: build, boot-check the chapter, DEPLOY and byte-verify, re-photograph what moved, the harnesses the
   fixes can reach, docs (this file, CLAUDE.md, LEARNINGS for any law), sheet only if words moved, commit, push,
   report to Chad.

## Left alone, and why

- **L1-3, the other bookmarks that move at a line's start** (the looks, the third look's promotion, the arrivals,
  `ownerShows`, `hanRelease`): a Continue saved inside one of those lines loses that line's words only — the order, the
  phase and every payment land right. Re-saying each would need its own guard against hearing it twice once the bookmark
  has moved on; the one that carries an order (her `yk4gentle`) is re-said.
- **L1-4 for the Yin set-down**: its single −6 cannot reach zero — the cold drains for seconds, not minutes, before it.
- **L5-7, the light of ending B's morning**: the shop keeps its one afternoon light for a six-second beat; a morning of its
  own (another sun, the painted patch of sun moved) is a design change, not a defect. The clock and the cups are fixed.
- **L8-5, skeletons never disposed**: engine-wide (no chapter disposes them), a few bone textures; a change to every
  chapter's teardown for kilobytes.
- **L8-6, ~1.46 GB of textures on a phone**: the five 4096² maps are the amulets and the shelf, kept at full detail by the
  standing rule ("ALL amulets must always show full quality", v14.14). Nothing to take without breaking it; worth
  watching if a phone reports a lost context.
