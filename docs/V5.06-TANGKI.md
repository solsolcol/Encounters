# v5.06 · THE TANG-KI, REMADE

Chad: *"I want to replace the tangki medium in all scenes and chapters with
this rigged fbx model."* One file, `edited_rigged_oldman_1.fbx`, 828 KB.

## What arrived

A hand-painted, low-poly Taoist master: white beard and brows, a topknot
with a hairpin, an ochre wrapped robe with taiji emblems on the shoulders
and seal-script down the hem, a bamboo scroll in one hand and a 仙人指路
("the immortal points the way") signboard with paper talismans in the
other. About a thousand triangles across three meshes, two 512² sheets,
and a full `mixamorig:` skeleton — the file went through Mixamo's
auto-rigger (`/home/app/mixamo-mini/` in its own texture paths).

Two things it did NOT arrive with, and both had to be supplied:

1. **Its textures were beside it, not in it.** FBX2glTF wrote them out to
   `oldman.fbm/0.png` and `1.png` and left both materials untextured. They
   were reattached in material order (the FBX names them `0` and `1`),
   with `alphaMode: MASK` at 0.5 — the beard, the hair strands and the
   talismans are alpha cutouts on flat cards, and OPAQUE rendered every
   card as a solid rectangle (the first close-up looked like a cardboard
   beard). The signboard sheet dropped to 256²; the face stays at 512
   because chapter 5 puts it across a table from the player.
2. **No animation.** Not one clip. The praying man it replaces was chosen
   at v4.8 *because* of his clip ("his animations must be preserved and
   shown"), and chapter 3 ties the medium's rate to the drum. A model with
   no clip stands in its bind pose — arms held out, frozen — which is not
   a replacement, it is a regression with a nicer face.

## The clips

The v5.02 bake (`docs/V5.02-MOTHER-ANIM.md`) retargets Mixamo takes onto a
skeleton offline by renaming tracks to the target's node names and scaling
the hips translation by the measured rig ratio. Pointed at this rig instead
of the mother's it produced `tangkianim.glb` — 150 KB, two clips — from
Chad's own Standing Idle and Talking takes. His bones are plain
`mixamorig:Hips` (no `_NN` suffix), so the rename is a no-op and the only
real work is the ratio: his rig sits at hips-Y 0.011 in file units against
the clip's 1.043, ratio 0.011, and the engine scales the whole group ~100×
to his 1.68 m. Rotations are unit-free and pass straight through.

Measured on the baked clips, not guessed:

| clip | length | largest swing off frame 0 |
|---|---|---|
| idle | 5.04 s | 11° |
| talk | 6.96 s | 99° |

So the two chapters use different takes for the same man:

- **Chapter 3** plays `talk`, rate-tied to `drumBeat` as before. A man
  mid-ritual with a god in him needs a take the drum can visibly quicken;
  an 11° breathing idle at 2.4× is still a man breathing. The procedural
  trance (head tremor re-laid onto the head bone, torso lean, group roll)
  lays on top exactly as it did.
- **Chapter 5** plays `idle`, at FULL rate — the old `dt * 0.5` was tuned
  for the praying man's wide slow loop, and halving an 11° idle left him
  reading as a photograph (1.8 mm of hand travel in two seconds). The scene
  verbs (`tangBow`, the head-look, the glides, the note into his hand) all
  drive the GROUP and the head bone, so every scene kept working with no
  timeline touched.

Both chapters load the model, ground it from posed bones, then load the
clips and **re-ground** — the v5.02 law: the pose changes when the clip
lands, so the measurement is taken again.
