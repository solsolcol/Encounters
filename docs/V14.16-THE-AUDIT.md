# v14.16 — THE AUDIT

Chad, after v14.15: *"What other improvements can you think of and implement?
Architectural improvements? Without compromising anything. How sure are you
that your improvements have not silently broken anything? Created
regressions? Or accidentally removed anything? Or caused any niche bugs that
you have not discovered?"* — and then: *"If there are any rules, you have to
reconsider if it makes sense instead of taking it blindly. If a change is a
clear and objectively superior solution, then the objectively superior
solution should be adopted … But you have to be very sure about it, and do it
cleanly."*

## 1. How it was looked for

Three independent angles, so no one of them had to be right on its own:

1. **Adversarial review of v14.12–v14.15.** Eight reviewers, each on one
   seam (the load tracker, the meshopt workers, the curtain, the download
   ahead, the save, the assets, the minigame guard, and every REMOVED line
   of those four releases), told to find what broke and to default to
   "not a bug" unless they could trace it. Each finding was then traced by
   two more agents, one asking "is the mechanism real?" and one "can a
   player reach it today?".
2. **Three idea rounds** — runtime, architecture, tests — each told to
   propose only what is objectively better, with the evidence, the risk and
   whether it changes the base game's feel.
3. **Measurement on the hosted build**, before and after every change:
   pop-ins per chapter on both entry paths (`__enc.loads()`), GPU uploads on
   the walk to ch3's amulet (`renderer.info`), the JS heap over a
   six-chapter journey with forced garbage collection, the voice log across
   a return visit, the sound packs' sizes, and a sweep of every chapter's
   film, play, decision and ending with every page error recorded.

## 3. What was improved — the architecture

- **The dialogue left the shared sound pack.** build.py read the four BUS
  tables in main.js (which bus a line plays through) as "the engine plays
  these", so every one of 273 spoken lines was downloaded by every player at
  boot. The shared pack: **19.6 MB → 6.4 MB** (opus), 33.2 → 9.4 MB (mp3);
  a new player's first sound download for chapter 1 about 21.6 → 8.8 MB;
  the single-file build 134 → 112 MB. Each line now lives in the one
  chapter that speaks it (the v4.2 rule, restored). The curtain waits for
  the shared pack and the chapter's own (never more than 20 s), so a
  chapter's lines are there before its world is shown.
- **A chapter left lets its sounds go.** Its loops are STOPPED (they ran at
  gain 0 for the rest of the session — thirty-odd by the end of episode 2),
  its decoded audio is released, and a warm list never re-decodes another
  chapter's. The raw bytes of a sound pack go once parsed.
- **Every model's bytes go once the world is built**, the figure's every
  map on an episode swap.
- **A loader that fails is never silent.** The one class every parse goes
  through records a failure and a throwing callback, says so on the
  console, and walktest fails on any.
- **Three things the tests could not see, they now check** (chaptertest,
  no new harness): every cue a cutscene fires is one the engine warms (all
  54 films and scenes, 422 cues); every model or picture a chapter loads is
  in its own list (91 loads); every take a character plays is a clip in its
  own file (59 takes — CLAUDE.md v11.2 wrote this down as uncheckable).
  Each check was proven by breaking the thing it guards and watching it
  fail.

## 4. Considered, and left for Chad

- **Pausing the sound when the phone is put away** — Android keeps a page's
  audio running in the background; iOS already pauses it. Not done: the
  fix calls `suspend()` from a script, and whether an iPhone always comes
  back from a script suspend without a tap cannot be tested here, so it is
  not "very sure".
- **The equipment screen draws the full-detail amulet twice a frame** (the
  worn box and the description), and the world behind a full-screen panel
  keeps drawing at 60 fps. A lower panel rate would cool the phone, but the
  frozen world behind the blur would update less smoothly — a base-game
  feel change, his call.
- **Chapter 3 parses the full Phiboon twice** (the table's close-up level
  and the item viewer). The peak is what v14.14 already had after the
  pickup; sharing one parse would couple the chapter to the engine's item
  viewer, a larger change than the saving is worth unless the phone shows
  memory trouble.
- **Replaying an episode-2 chapter from the selector could REBUILD it**
  under the curtain instead of trusting its `reset()` — the whole "reset
  forgot X" class (v8.1, v8.2, v9.x …) gone by construction, at the price
  of a short loading curtain on a replay.
- **An engine-owned clock, scheduler and speech queue for episode 3's
  chapters**, cleared by the engine on every restart — the same class, gone
  for new chapters.
- **CLAUDE.md is ~84k tokens loaded into every session.** 87 % of it is the
  release history, out of order. Moving the history verbatim to
  docs/HISTORY.md (proved line by line) would give every session far more
  room to work — his contract file, his call.

  followed up: **walk** was the new load check doing its job (the boot and
  `voiceDecode` still asked for pack lines as FILES — fixed, row 17);

  final, sound, hosted, csp and leak 7/7 on the final build. **restart**

  number that animates on requestAnimationFrame, at ~1 fps, with the survey
  agents running — and passed alone (every episode-card check true).

  guards (a take renamed, an asset undeclared, a warm removed, a play
  misspelled) and watching it fail.
- The probes in §5 on the final build.

  its v14.16 form).
