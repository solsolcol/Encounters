## Ghost behavior spec (workstream 7)

Ghost-movie logic, one state machine (replaces the walk-at-you chase):
1. `hidden` → player crosses 14 m from burner: ghost placed BEHIND the
   burner (shrine side, opposite the player), fades in fast (~0.5 s),
   `strings` sting (first time per run) + whisper layer up.
2. `standing`: holds 1.2–2.0 s, facing the player.
3. `dart`: flies away on an accelerating curve (ease-in cubic:
   slow lift-off then whips), hovering (y rises 0.3–0.6), fading out
   over the last 30 % of the path. Whoosh sting.
4. `gone`: 2–3 s pause.
5. reappear at a new spot chosen from candidate points around the deck
   that are INSIDE the camera frustum but near its edge (corner of the
   eye), at 5–12 m from the player; back to `standing`. Repeat 2–5.
- Sanity drain continues while she is visible (standing or darting):
  keep ghostDrainRate driven by reveal + distance, unchanged maths.
- She never leaves the deck (z ≤ GHOST_DECK_EDGE, |x| ≤ 20.5), never
  within GHOST_MIN_DIST of the player.
- Retreat rule stays: walk far enough away (>14 m) and she fades out,
  state back to hidden (drain stops — walking away remains an answer).
- Cutscenes still own her completely (state 'cine' bails).
- ghosttest.mjs rewritten to the new behavior: reveal near burner,
  position changes between appearances, never out of bounds, never
  closer than GHOST_MIN_DIST, fades when player leaves.

## Faint sequence spec (workstream 8)

Trigger: sanity hits 0 in play (the only lose() path). Sequence
(~4.5 s, all TRACKS + STINGS on the cine engine, skippable after 2 s):
1. 0.0–0.4 s: camera pitch whips UP (+0.5 rad above horizon), roll
   kicks ±, heavy shake (decaying noise on yaw/pitch/roll), `boom`
   sting, `vfaint` line starts, flatline ECG already handled by state.
2. 0.4–1.6 s: fall — camera y 1.62 → 0.45 with a bounce at impact
   (`kick` sting at floor contact), shake decays.
3. 1.6–3.2 s: settle into lying-on-the-side view: roll → ~1.35 rad
   (head on the ground, horizon vertical), pitch → slightly below
   level, slow drift. World keeps rendering (she can be in frame).
4. 3.2–4.5 s: fade to black, then the Case failed card.
- state during it: 'lost' immediately (drain, ghost updates, input all
  already gated on 'play'), but the card waits for the sequence.
- sanitytest.mjs updated: card no longer instant — poll for it within
  ~6 s of the drain hitting 0; camera roll asserted during the fall.

## Outcome sequence spec (workstream 9)

On result card open: `#say` visible at once, deltas area replaced by
three full-width animated stat rows (icon + label + track + value),
hidden until their turn. Order: sanity → awareness → wisdom, only stats
whose delta ≠ 0. Each row: bar fills from OLD value to NEW value over
~0.9 s with the value counting; +N/−N chip pops beside it (green/red).
Next row starts when the previous ends. Then the teaching writes itself
letter by letter (~28 ms/char, skippable), in italics, serif. Then
Continue fades in — but only once the outcome voiceline (`v`+k) has
ended too (speech-end promise; if muted or the buffer failed, gate on
animations alone). Case failed card: same gating for Retry on `vlost`.
A tap during the sequence fast-forwards animations but not the voice
gate. HUD stays hidden through it (bars ARE the card's bars).
