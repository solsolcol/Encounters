## 0 · Why it changed

Chad's standing rule since v9.5: *"everything in episode 2 ... should all
still link back to spiritual stuff. I don't want to veer too far off into
a military sim game."* The approved E2C2 was the range — the most military
chapter in the episode, and not in the source at all. Master Z's own
account of the middle of S1·04 is one sentence:

> "After night upon night, curiosity got the better of me. One night I got
> out of bed and walked over to check. The shower was running. There was
> nobody there. I went straight back to my bed."

And its lesson states the mechanic: *"acknowledging a presence can create
an awareness or a connection that was not there before."*

Chad (14 Sep): *"greatly simplify chp2 here, everything needs a spiritual
reason to exist as a mechanic or to advance the story from a spiritual
perspective ... The story needs to meaningfully progress from one chapter
to the next. I'm not a fan of repeating the same things."*

## 1 · The chapter, in Chad's words

**Film.** *"Show the same things happening night after night from
different camera angles/scenes/shots to illustrate it being different
nights, but the common thread is that it always starts at 3am. Then
voiceline says, 'I've had enough, today, I decided to bring it up to my
bunkmates...and the encik....'"*

**Play.** *"The playable scene starts at the cookhouse during breakfast
time, every bunkmate is seated at the table and chairs with food on the
table with sitting doze off animation. The first objective is to talk to
3 different bunkmates sitting at the table to gather information. Every
interaction with one bunkmate will cause the bunkmate to say only 1
voiceline with no choosable options, then move on to the next bunkmate.
Objective tracker will update 1/3, 2/3, 3/3, complete."*

The three lines, his:
- *"Siao eh, you think too much la. Where got ghost?"*
- *"I didn't hear anything, but I heard from others that bed one is the
  problem..."*
- *"Bro, you also heard it? It starts at 3am every night I swear..."*

**Decision.** *"When the objective tracker completes, the final task is to
talk to the encik directly who is standing at the corner of the cookhouse
looking at all the recruits, this one will have choosable options, and
upon choosing, each will have its own cutscene ... Then after choosing the
option, play the cutscene, and this chapter ends, and moves on to the next
chapter."*

**The cutscenes, Chad's lines verbatim:**

- **A** — he doesn't laugh. *"Good. Don't know means don't know. Go eat and
  don't think too much... Put your focus on the live range tomorrow. I will
  check your bunk tonight..."* Walks off. The table's still noisy. Nothing
  changed, which is the point.
- **B** — *"You saw?"* — *"No, but—"* — *"Don't talk cock! Go back and eat
  your breakfast!"* The whole table has gone quiet. Internal: *"Guess only
  I know what is happening..."*
- **C** — he looks at you a beat too long. *"Ok."* Cut to 3am: it starts
  again, you're awake, and you know you weren't tired. Internal: *"I should
  have just told him..."*
- **D** — his non-answer, in his two talking takes: *"Old block la....
  Plumbing issue.... Or you're just too tired...."* Then the shrug: *"And if
  it's not those two, mind your own business and don't disturb it."* The
  episode's whole lesson, said early, by a man with twenty years.
