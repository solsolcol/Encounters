# v10.0 · EPISODE 2 · CHAPTER 2 · NOBODY THERE — the plan

*Status: SHIPPED at v10.0 (Chad, 14 Sep 2026: "Go, build it now. All of it.").
Supersedes the E2C2 section of docs/V7.0-EPISODE2-PLAN.md — the range,
strip-and-assemble and the armskote are GONE. Chapters 3–5 are unchanged.*

## 0 · Why it changed

Chad's standing rule since v9.5: *"everything in episode 2 ... should all
still link back to spiritual stuff. I don't want to veer too far off into
a military sim game."* The approved E2C2 was the range — the most military
chapter in the episode, and not in the source at all. Master Z's own
account of the middle of S1·04 is one sentence:

> "After night upon night, curiosity got the better of me. One night I got
> out of bed and walked over to check. The shower was running. There was
> nobody there. I went straight back to my bed."

And its lesson states the mechanic: *"acknowledging a presence can create
an awareness or a connection that was not there before."*

Chad (14 Sep): *"greatly simplify chp2 here, everything needs a spiritual
reason to exist as a mechanic or to advance the story from a spiritual
perspective ... The story needs to meaningfully progress from one chapter
to the next. I'm not a fan of repeating the same things."*

## 1 · The chapter, in Chad's words

**Film.** *"Show the same things happening night after night from
different camera angles/scenes/shots to illustrate it being different
nights, but the common thread is that it always starts at 3am. Then
voiceline says, 'I've had enough, today, I decided to bring it up to my
bunkmates...and the encik....'"*

**Play.** *"The playable scene starts at the cookhouse during breakfast
time, every bunkmate is seated at the table and chairs with food on the
table with sitting doze off animation. The first objective is to talk to
3 different bunkmates sitting at the table to gather information. Every
interaction with one bunkmate will cause the bunkmate to say only 1
voiceline with no choosable options, then move on to the next bunkmate.
Objective tracker will update 1/3, 2/3, 3/3, complete."*

## 8 · v10.2 — Chad's four notes on v10.1

1. *"the encik voiceline does not play when i choose the option for the first
   time, it only plays when i replay the chapter."* — `startDecision()` warms
   a scene's cues by scanning its SOURCE for literal `sfx(t, 'name')` calls
   (`CUE_RE` in main.js). v10.0's `encSay(stage, sfx, step, null, at, name,
   …)` passed the name as a variable, so the six encik takes never entered the
   warm set; on a fresh load they had not decoded at the first decision and
   `snd()` returned null — silence, with the talk take still playing. On a
   replay the pack had long since decoded. Every encik cue is a literal now
   (`sfx(4.6, 'e2A'); encTalk(stage, step, 4.6, 9.92, take)`), and build()
   warms them too. `chaptertest` caught the first draft of the fix's own
   comment (a quoted example name inside it read as a cue).
2. *"encik to shout 'hurry up and eat, fall in soon!' after player talks to 3
   bunkmates ... internal voiceline 'I think I'm not the only one who knows
   about this...' right after ... first"* — `afterThree()`: a gap, `n2known`,
   a gap, `e2hurry` on `Talk_with_Left_Hand_Raised` (back to `Idle_9` after
   its measured length), a gap, then `setPhase('encik')` + the objective. The
   queue (`lineQ`, `runQueue`) waits on `speak.until`, the window the third
   man's own line booked, so nothing overlaps whatever the frame rate.
   `reset()` empties it. Takes: n2known take 1 (2.96 s, flat, clean edges),
   e2hurry take 2 (2.88 s, the shout at one level).
3. *"Why is the music so calming?"* — see masters/v10.2/mk.py: four
   candidates, the pick chosen on energy above 300 Hz (what a phone speaker
   reproduces), 0.24 → 0.20.
4. *"only the middle singapore flag, keep the other 2 flagpoles but remove
   those flags ... chapter 1's parade square too"* — `POLES.forEach` returns
   before the cloth for `pi !== 1` in both e2c1 and e2c2; the flank textures
   are no longer painted.
