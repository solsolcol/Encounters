# v5.11 — THE WHITE STREAKS IN HIS HAIR, AND THE SPIN

Chad, from his phone, on v5.10: *"there are still white streaks on his
hair, on his back and sides and top. how do you fix it without affecting
the face at all? also i want his spin to be slightly faster. and his spin
should start with much lesser delay, almost immediately after user
manually rotates him and lets go."* And when the first answer leaned on
close-up renders: *"it may look fine in your preview but not when im in
the game looking from the inventory view on my phone."*

He was right, and the second remark is the whole lesson of this build.

## 1. Why the close-ups never showed it

Every render used to judge the seams was a 900 px close-up of the head.
That MAGNIFIES the texture: each texel covers several screen pixels and
the GPU samples the atlas at full size. The panel on a phone does the
opposite — the whole man is ~600 device pixels tall, the head ~80 — so
the texture is MINIFIED, and a minified texture is sampled from its
mipmaps: shrunken copies where each texel is the average of a block of
the original.

The scan's atlas packs its patches edge to edge. `shot-atlas-hair.png`
is the proof: a black hair patch sits directly against cream robe patches
with no gutter at all. So every shrunken copy averages hair with cream
along the patch borders, and on a phone that is a white streak along
every seam of the hair — back, sides and top, exactly where he saw them.
Padding cannot help: the cream beside the hair is not empty fill but
another patch's real paint. Rendered at the panel's true phone size
(`?z=panel`, 352 × 600, the panel's lighting), the streaks reproduced in
the first try — `shot-zav-panel-heads.png`, left tile.

## 2. The fix, and why the face cannot have moved

**No shrunken copies for this one texture.** `zavNoMip()` sets
`generateMipmaps = false` and `minFilter = LinearFilter` on Master Zav's
map — on the normal load path, and again when `rescueTextures()` lands a
map under the strict CSP (it gained an optional `onMap` callback for
that; every other caller passes nothing). Plain bilinear sampling never
reaches past one texel of a neighbour, so the cross-patch averaging is
gone. What remains is a one-texel line where hair paint touches robe
paint directly, far fainter than before (`shot-zav-panel-heads.png`,
second tile; `shot-invphone-head.png` is the real game on an iPhone 13
viewport).

**The model file is byte-identical to v5.10.** Not one texel changed.
That is the strongest possible form of "without affecting the face":
the face is sampled from the same paint by the same shader, only without
the mip chain — and at the panel's size the face was never the thing the
mip chain was blurring badly.

Cost: with no mipmaps a minified texture can shimmer as it moves. At
this size, with a mostly flat cream atlas, it does not show; measured by
eye on the phone-viewport probe, not assumed.

Two things that were tried and are recorded so they are not tried again:
- A hair-only rim repaint (dark UV charts only, found on the mesh —
  charts must be found through shared UV vertices, because a flood fill
  over coverage merges hair with the robe it touches). It repainted
  1,312 texels and changed nothing visible: the live texture already
  carries the v5.09 rim treatment, and the streaks were never in the
  rims. Kept in `tools/prepzav-full.mjs` (`DARKMAX`) for a day it is
  wanted.
- Simply eroding or feathering more: already known from v5.09 to draw
  new marks.

## 3. A correction

The v5.10 hand-over said his paint was "untouched" apart from the fill.
Measured now against the original (`covdiff.mjs`, painted texels only):
8,325 of 762,777 painted texels — 1.1 % — differ by more than 12/255,
2,566 of them skin-range, 553 dark, the rest on the robe. That is the
v5.09 rim repaint and the restricted seam feather, both at seams. The
face Chad has been looking at since v5.10, and accepted, carries that
treatment; v5.11 does not change it. The statement was wrong and this
file is where it is put right.

## 4. The spin

- Rate 0.006 → **0.0085** rad per frame (Chad: "slightly faster").
- **He turns again the moment you let go.** The four-second idle wait
  (`idleT > 240`) is gone: `pointerup` sets `auto = true` with
  `ramp = 0`, and `zavFrame()` eases `ramp` to 1 over twenty frames, so
  the hand-off from the finger to the turntable is not a jolt. A
  `pointerup` with no drag in flight is ignored.

## 5. Tests

`invtest` (desktop + phone), `csptest`, `leaktest` green on the build.
Engine seams: `rescueTextures(gltf, buf, onMap)` — an optional third
argument, every existing caller unchanged. No strings; sheet stays v17.
