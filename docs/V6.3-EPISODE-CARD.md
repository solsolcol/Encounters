# v6.3 · THE EPISODE COMPLETE CARD — the case file closed

Chad:

> Yes, have an episode complete card, and show next episode unlocked in a
> progress line with checkpoint/milestones like going through a map or
> something. The scores across all chapters must be properly tallied up and
> brought over. The episode complete card must look visually different from
> the usual outcomes and score cards. It must look impressive and exciting,
> with sound effects.

## When it shows

Continue on the SEALED card of a case's LAST chapter — the fifth
(`isLastOfEpisode`: the chapter's index in `episodeKeys(episode)` is
`CHAPTERS_PER_EPISODE - 1`). An episode with fewer than five chapters built
is not complete, so its last built chapter still goes straight on as it
always did. Until v6.3 Continue on chapter 5's sealed card replayed
chapter 5; now it closes the case first.

## What it shows, top to bottom

1. "EPISODE 1" as a sodium stencil line, the case's name in the display
   serif (`ep{n}.label`, `ep{n}.title`), "Episode complete".
2. The episode's RANK, huge, in Cormorant — scaling in from .6 — with the
   red **CASE CLOSED** stamp slamming down across it (the same `stampin`
   keyframes as the chapter card's Sealed, a bigger stamp, a kick under it).
3. "EPISODE SCORE" and the number rolling up from zero.
4. "Five chapters, tallied": five stops in a row, one per chapter — its
   label, its rank in a filled sodium disc, its score rolling up — landing
   one by one, left to right, a tick each. A chapter with no result on
   record (a store from before v6.2) shows a dashed disc and a dash, and
   is left out of the mean.
5. "The ten cases": the milestone map — a winding dashed trail through ten
   stops (alternating high and low, so it reads as a route, not a ruler);
   a closed case's stop is filled sodium and carries that case's rank; the
   trail LIGHTS from the closed case to the next one (its length measured,
   `stroke-dashoffset` drawn from full to zero over 1.4 s); the next case's
   stop is a jade ring with a pulsing halo and an "UNLOCKED" flag —
   whether or not the case is written yet.
6. The line under the trail says which: "Next · Episode 2 · The 3 AM Shower"
   when it exists, "Episode 2 · The 3 AM Shower is unlocked. It is not yet
   written — come back for it." while it does not, "Every case is closed."
   after the tenth.
7. One button: **Continue** into the next case when it exists (the same
   `advanceTo()` a sealed card uses — `setChapter`, `restart`, `enterWorld`
   with the film), otherwise **Back to the title screen**.

## The tally

`episodeTally(n)` reads the per-chapter results the progress store has kept
since v6.2 (`sealed: { ch1: { score, rank } … }`, written by `finish()`).
The episode's score is the MEAN of the chapter scores on record, rounded;
its rank is that number on the chapter formula (`RANK_OF`: S ≥ 90, A+ ≥ 80,
A ≥ 70, B ≥ 55, C ≥ 40, else D — now one function, used by `finish()` too).
Five chapters at 91 / 62 / 84 / 45 / 79 tally to 72 %, an A. The latest
result per chapter is what counts, so a replayed chapter's new score is
what the card brings over.

## The sound

| moment | sound |
|---|---|
| the card lands | `epfanfare` — NEW: a gong and a rising swell, 7.0 s (ElevenLabs SFX, take A of two; stereo, peak −3.8 dBFS to sit with `uirank` at −4.2 and `noteflight` at −3.9; 113 KB mp3 / 78 KB opus, in the SHARED pack because the engine fires it) |
| the stamp | `kick` |
| each chapter stop | `uiclick` |
| the score settling, the rank starting to glow | `uirank` |
| the trail lighting to the next case | `uiconfirm` |

The fanfare and the ticks are DECODED while the sealed card is up
(`packWarm` in `finish()` when the chapter is a case's last), so the card
never opens on a sample that is still decoding.

## Choreography (from the card's first frame)

| t | what |
|---|---|
| 0.00 | fanfare; the card fades up |
| 0.35 | the rank scales in |
| 0.85 | the stamp comes down, kick |
| 1.50 + 0.23 per stop | the five stops land, each with a tick and its score rolling |
| 2.90 | the episode score rolls for 1.3 s; at its end `uirank` and the rank's glow |
| 4.40 | the trail draws to the next case; the halo starts; `uiconfirm` |
| 5.50 | the button fades in and becomes pressable |

The button is disabled until the end so a tap during the fanfare does not
skip the tally.

## After the button

- Next case exists: `advanceTo(e2c1)` — exactly what Continue on a sealed
  card does between chapters.
- Nothing follows: the card hides, `restart()` puts a fresh last chapter
  under the title, `returnToTitle()` brings the title up, and the save is
  re-written as `{ at: null, done: true }` — what `finish()` had written,
  so the title's Continue behaves as it always did after the final chapter.

## Where it lives

- `shell.html`: the `#episode` layer (z 8, above the sealed card) — its own
  look, not `.card`: a full-bleed red-black radial ground, a bordered
  dossier with bracketed corners, slow conic light rays turning behind the
  rank (masked to a disc; off under reduced motion), the stamp, the stops,
  the trail as inline SVG built by `paintEpisodeMap()`.

  `rollNumber`, `paintEpisodeMap`, `showEpisodeCard`, `advanceTo`; the
  `againBtn` and `epBtn` handlers; `ui.episode` hidden by `restart()`,
  `startChapter()` and `advanceTo()`; `__enc.episodeCard`,
  `__enc.episodeTally`, `__enc.finish`.
- Twelve strings (`episode.*`), sheet v35.
