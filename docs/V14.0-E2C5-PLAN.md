that is both of them. S1·04 (docs/source/site-case-files.md):

## 2 · The place — his infantry camp, days before ORD, afternoon

Not Tekong. Not a bunk. Not night. **The first chapter in either episode
with no darkness in it at all**, and the first with no presence drain.

A small, walkable piece of a mainland infantry camp in the late afternoon:

- a **company line** — a two-storey block with balconies, from e2c2's
  bunk-block recipe, along one side
- an open **tarmac apron** in front of it — e2c1/e2c2's parade-square
  recipe (asphalt with aggregate, patches, joints), smaller and turned
- the **stores block** across the apron: a roller shutter, a counter,
  a queue rail, racks behind it
- the **armskote** beside it: a steel door, a grille window, a counter,
  a rifle rack visible through the grille
- the **company office** at the end of the line: a door, a notice board,
  a bench outside it
- trees from the kit round all of it; a few parked vehicles; a flag stand
  in the distance, past the bounds, so the camp reads as a camp

`daylight`: a LATE AFTERNOON — warm key low in the sky, long shadows, a
sky that is still blue at the zenith and gold near the horizon. Not the
range's dusk, not the cookhouse's 07:00 blue, not chapter 1's evening.
**It is the brightest, most ordinary light in the episode**, and that is
the point.

`bounds` are small. This is a walk between three counters and one man.

## 4 · Play

Four phases. No timer, no event, no minigame, **no presence drain** — the
chapter has nothing to survive.

### Phase `clear` · THE CLEARANCE — the burning, in uniform

The objective is **"Clear your kit · {n}/3"**, and it is the chapter's
physical final act: every item the episode taught him to reach for when he
was frightened, handed across a counter to a bored storeman.

| stop | what happens | engine |
|---|---|---|
| **the armskote** | the rifle out of the hand slot, over the counter, into the rack; a clatter and a stamp | `kit.take('rifle')` |
| **the stores** | the torch back; it goes in a tray with thirty others | `kit.take('torch')` |
| **the company office** | the form signed; a stamp, a line from the clerk | a hotspot with no item |

Each stop is an ordinary hotspot with an ordinary prompt. Nothing is
frightening, nothing is timed, and each one plays its own COMPLETE beat
(v8.7) because each one genuinely is.

**THE BEADS STAY.** The bag's default `beads` — episode 1's, from when he
was twelve — are the only thing he does not hand back, and nothing in the
chapter mentions it. It is there for the player who opens the bag.

**Conduct banked (v7.0):** clearing all three before speaking to the encik
is one note on the card. Clearing none of them is another. It is the
difference between a man who has finished and a man who has not.

### Phase `spot` · the encik

Objective **"Speak to the encik"**, a `kit.waypoint` on him. He is the
chapter's PILE — the same shape e2c2 uses, so the decision is the engine's
own and no new seam is needed. `words.approach` is EMPTY (v13.0's rule) so
no floating label hangs on a person.

He is on `Idle_9` and does not move. He is a different man out of Hawk
Company: standing easy, cap off, nothing to be in charge of.

### Phase `talk` · the conversation

Pressing him starts a SCRIPTED EXCHANGE IN PLAY — not a cutscene, so the
player is inside it and can look at his face. `kit.root(true)` holds him in
place: he is standing in a conversation, which is exactly what root is for.

The exchange runs on a QUEUE gated on the chapter's own speech window,
never on hand-typed times — the v9.5 count-off's shape, and the reason is
the same: `sayLine` refuses a line while another speaks, and on a
one-frame-a-second box several `after` slots flush in one tick, so a
hand-timed conversation loses its middle and reports nothing.

1. **Small talk.** Posting, ORD, what next. He is almost friendly. Two
   exchanges, no weight in them at all.
2. **The turn.** Unprompted, halfway through:
   > **"Remember the question you asked me? In the cookhouse. About the
   > 3 AM shower."**
   A beat of nothing: no line, no music cue, the camp carrying on behind
   him. Then the decision opens BY ITSELF (e2c3's precedent — no press).

### Phase `decide`

The four options are **what the boy says back** to that question. The
encik answers each one fully, and each answer is a different thing —
a story, a confirmation, a correction, a reason (§5, §6).

## 6 · The four scenes — the encik's answers

Each scene is the encik's full answer, then he goes. No scare in any of
them. The words below are the BUILD'S — Chad's edits of 19 Sep applied —
and his to change again on the sheet.

**A · THE STORY** — *"Who was it, encik? What was his background story?"*

> "Before my time. One recruit, bed one. Quiet boy. Every night he's the
> last one in the shower — he don't like people see him wash. One morning,
> first parade, he's not there. They find him inside. Water still running.
>
> No note, no reason. His family come and take him back. The bunk close
> for one month. Then they open it again like nothing.
>
> Since then, every batch, bed one, three o'clock. **Sometimes the departed
> are still stuck at the places they have attachments to.** That shower was
> his. Nobody knows what he's waiting for. I don't know.
>
> That's the whole story. You asked, so I told you. But don't go and make
> it into something. He's not your business."

He goes. **What it did to the boy:** he asked who, and the answer was a
teaching, not a lead — a man held in place by what he was attached to. He
carried the lesson out of the camp and left the story where it was.

**B · THE CONFIRMATION** — *"I don't think about it anymore, encik."*

> "Good. Better than me.
>
> I was in that bunk also. I went to look once — water running, somebody
> standing under it, back facing me. I went back to bed and never look
> again. One month, then nothing.
>
> Every year I get one or two who cannot stop checking. They go down. Not
> because of ghost. Because they cannot put it down.
>
> That morning in the cookhouse I say nothing, because if I tell you, you
> become one of those. You didn't.
>
> After your batch posted out — quiet. First time in years."

He goes. **What it did to the boy:** nothing. He thought about it once on
the bus, then not again. The truth landed on a man who had let go, and had
nothing to hold onto. (The rhyme nobody says aloud: what the encik saw in
that shower is what the player saw at ten at night in chapter 1.)

**C · THE ONE LINE** — *"I think I was just too stressed."*

> "I know it is easier to brush things off with logic and reason. But
> there are many things you simply won't fully understand."

**That is the whole scene.** He goes. The shortest scene in the episode.
**What it did to the boy:** he had buried it, not put it down. One
sentence took the cover off. That night, for the first time in months, he
lay there listening.

**D · THE REASON** — *"Why didn't you tell me then, encik?"*

> "Because I told one boy before you. Same question, same answer. After
> that he checked every night. Two months later he's out. Not sick, not
> injured. Just cannot anymore.
>
> After him I decide — nobody gets told until they're leaving. You stopped
> because you didn't know enough to keep looking.
>
> After your batch posted out — quiet. First time in years. Now you know.
> Now it can't hurt you."

He goes. **What it did to the boy:** he got his reason and it did not
satisfy him. He came in wanting to be owed something, and left still
weighing the encik's account against his own months of quiet.

**No ghost appears in any scene.** Not at the edge of frame, not in a
window, not on the apron. The one figure that ran through chapters 1–4 is
simply not in this chapter, and nothing says so.

| — | `campamb` (e2c1's) and `marchcall` (e2c2's) are ONE CHAPTER'S today | see below |

Every pick by MEASUREMENT (the phone-band measure, v10.4), previews to
Chad before shipping (the v10.6 rule), `duration_seconds` and `loop` as
model PARAMETERS and never only in English (the v9.2 law).

**On reusing a chapter-owned sound:** `build.py` COMPUTES the split — a
sound is a chapter's only when exactly ONE chapter can ask for it. So
cueing `campamb` or `marchcall` here moves it into the SHARED pack
automatically, with no declaration and no duplicate bytes. That is correct
and costs a few KB at boot; the bias to shared is deliberate and
asymmetric (a sound wrongly left shared costs a few KB, a sound wrongly
moved out of it is a cue that plays nothing with no error).

changed a harness. They are written up in `docs/LEARNINGS.md`.

1. **`fireLight: null` threw every frame.** `updateViewmodel` has read
   `stage.fireLight.position` unconditionally since v2.1; every chapter
   until this one had something warm to name. The engine takes a null now.
2. **No harness listened for `pageerror`.** `walktest` does — it is the one
   that walks into every episode-2 chapter and lets it run, and a per-frame
   throw needs a frame. It caught a second bug the same run: `makeConcrete()`
   returns `{ map, rough }` and the whole object had gone in as `map`.
3. **An empty bag is not a receipt.** `applyPhase` read "not carrying the
   rifle" as "handed the rifle in"; on the first frame of a fresh run that
   marked two counters done and wrote it into the phase string, so the arms
   and stores hotspots were dead for the whole chapter. The phase string is
   the only receipt now, and the bag is made to match it — which is what
   issues his kit at the start.
