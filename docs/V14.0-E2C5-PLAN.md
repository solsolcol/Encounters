## 1 · The chapter, in the source's words

This chapter is the END OF BOTH CASE FILES, which is right for an episode
that is both of them. S1·04 (docs/source/site-case-files.md):

> After that, training became so exhausting that I simply slept through the
> nights. I stopped thinking about the shower. I stopped checking. I
> stopped paying attention to whatever might have been happening. And
> strangely, the disturbance eventually stopped too.

and its lesson, which is the chapter's whole thesis:

> From a spiritual perspective, acknowledging a presence can create an
> awareness or a connection that was not there before. Once I stopped
> giving the experience my attention, it stopped giving me its attention
> too. Knowing how to do something does not mean we should. Understanding
> when not to interfere is just as important as knowing what to do.

and from S1·05, the sentence that decides what he may and may not take
from the encik's account:

> Remember that your own experience and somebody else's account are two
> different things.

**The lesson line (`core`) starts from those sentences, cut to card
length. THE PALI TERM, IF ANY, IS CHAD'S TO NAME** — the v11.10/v11.11
rule. §15 describes the beat's structure in plain words for him to name it
or say there is none.

## 2 · The place — his infantry camp, days before ORD, afternoon

Not Tekong. Not a bunk. Not night. **The first chapter in either episode
with no darkness in it at all**, and the first with no presence drain.

A small, walkable piece of a mainland infantry camp in the late afternoon:

- a **company line** — a two-storey block with balconies, from e2c2's
  bunk-block recipe, along one side
- an open **tarmac apron** in front of it — e2c1/e2c2's parade-square
  recipe (asphalt with aggregate, patches, joints), smaller and turned
- the **stores block** across the apron: a roller shutter, a counter,
  a queue rail, racks behind it
- the **armskote** beside it: a steel door, a grille window, a counter,
  a rifle rack visible through the grille
- the **company office** at the end of the line: a door, a notice board,
  a bench outside it
- trees from the kit round all of it; a few parked vehicles; a flag stand
  in the distance, past the bounds, so the camp reads as a camp

`daylight`: a LATE AFTERNOON — warm key low in the sky, long shadows, a
sky that is still blue at the zenith and gold near the horizon. Not the
range's dusk, not the cookhouse's 07:00 blue, not chapter 1's evening.
**It is the brightest, most ordinary light in the episode**, and that is
the point.

`bounds` are small. This is a walk between three counters and one man.

## 4 · Play

Four phases. No timer, no event, no minigame, **no presence drain** — the
chapter has nothing to survive.

### Phase `clear` · THE CLEARANCE — the burning, in uniform

The objective is **"Clear your kit · {n}/3"**, and it is the chapter's
physical final act: every item the episode taught him to reach for when he
was frightened, handed across a counter to a bored storeman.

| stop | what happens | engine |
|---|---|---|
| **the armskote** | the rifle out of the hand slot, over the counter, into the rack; a clatter and a stamp | `kit.take('rifle')` |
| **the stores** | the torch back; it goes in a tray with thirty others | `kit.take('torch')` |
| **the company office** | the form signed; a stamp, a line from the clerk | a hotspot with no item |

Each stop is an ordinary hotspot with an ordinary prompt. Nothing is
frightening, nothing is timed, and each one plays its own COMPLETE beat
(v8.7) because each one genuinely is.

**THE BEADS STAY.** The bag's default `beads` — episode 1's, from when he
was twelve — are the only thing he does not hand back, and nothing in the
chapter mentions it. It is there for the player who opens the bag.

**Conduct banked (v7.0):** clearing all three before speaking to the encik
is one note on the card. Clearing none of them is another. It is the
difference between a man who has finished and a man who has not.

### Phase `spot` · the encik

Objective **"Speak to the encik"**, a `kit.waypoint` on him. He is the
chapter's PILE — the same shape e2c2 uses, so the decision is the engine's
own and no new seam is needed. `words.approach` is EMPTY (v13.0's rule) so
no floating label hangs on a person.

He is on `Idle_9` and does not move. He is a different man out of Hawk
Company: standing easy, cap off, nothing to be in charge of.

### Phase `talk` · the conversation

Pressing him starts a SCRIPTED EXCHANGE IN PLAY — not a cutscene, so the
player is inside it and can look at his face. `kit.root(true)` holds him in
place: he is standing in a conversation, which is exactly what root is for.

The exchange runs on a QUEUE gated on the chapter's own speech window,
never on hand-typed times — the v9.5 count-off's shape, and the reason is
the same: `sayLine` refuses a line while another speaks, and on a
one-frame-a-second box several `after` slots flush in one tick, so a
hand-timed conversation loses its middle and reports nothing.

1. **Small talk.** Posting, ORD, what next. He is almost friendly. Two
   exchanges, no weight in them at all.
2. **The turn.** Unprompted, halfway through:
   > **"Remember the question you asked me? In the cookhouse. About the
   > 3 AM shower."**
   The boy goes still. (A beat of nothing: no line, no music cue, the camp
   carrying on behind him.)
3. **His account.** The bunk has been like that for years. Every batch.
   Bed one. *"First week everyone checks. By the third week, nobody. The
   ones who cannot stop checking — those are the ones I worry about."*
4. **The question**, and the decision opens BY ITSELF (e2c3's precedent —
   no press):
   > **"So. Did it stop?"**

### Phase `decide`

The four options. The decision is the engine's own, on the pile.

## 5 · The decision — four, on Master Z's sentences

**FOR CHAD'S APPROVAL** (§15). Deltas at this game's ±30 scale, shaped
against e2c2's set so one rank formula still serves.

| | choice | S | A | W | verdict | the sentence under it |
|---|---|--:|--:|--:|---|---|
| **A** | *"It stopped, encik. When I stopped checking."* | +21 | +24 | +30 | **best** | Once I stopped giving it my attention, it stopped giving me its. |
| **B** | *"What is it, encik? You must know."* | −6 | +9 | −21 | bad | Not everything needs to be challenged or investigated. |
| **C** | *Tell him everything — the leg, the rider, the flare.* | −18 | −9 | −30 | **worst**, `critical` | Acknowledging a presence can create a connection that was not there before. |
| **D** | *"Why didn't you tell me, encik?"* | +12 | +21 | +24 | good | Your own experience and somebody else's account are two different things. |

**A is best because he already did the work and only needs it named.** D is
good, not best, because the lesson is handed to him rather than found —
which is the same distinction e2c2 draws between its A and its D.

C is the critical mistake and it is the episode's own thesis inverted: he
hands it his attention again, out loud, months after it let him go.

## 6 · The four scenes

Short. No scare in any of them. The consequence is said in a line, which
is a right the game has earned over two episodes.

**A · THE NOD.** *"Good."* And then, because it is safe to now, **the shrug
explained** — the payoff chapter 2 planted:

> *"That day in the cookhouse. If I tell you what I know, you check every
> night until ORD. Some people, once they know, cannot stop. So I say
> nothing."*

Then the line that closes the episode:

> *"After your batch posted out — quiet. First time in years."*

He thinks that is good news about the bunk. **The player understands the
whole five-chapter thread in one sentence, the encik never will, and the
boy does not tell him.** The encik goes. Final teaching to camera.

**B · HE WON'T SAY.** *"Doesn't matter what it is."* The boy pushes; the
encik gets shorter, not angry — bored. He leaves with the question still
open, which is exactly where chapter 2 left him. He has learned nothing in
five chapters.

**C · WHY YOU TELLING ME.** He tells it all — the leg, the rider, the
flare. The encik listens the whole way, and then:

> *"Why you telling me? I asked about the bunk."*

And walks off. The boy alone on the apron. Narration under the card: that
night, for the first time in months, he lay listening.

**D · WOULD YOU HAVE STOPPED.** *"If I tell you, would you have stopped?"*
The shrug explained, but as a rebuke rather than a gift. He gets the
"quiet, first time in years" line too — he has earned the fact, if not the
understanding of it.

**No ghost appears in any scene.** Not at the edge of frame, not in a
window, not on the apron. The one figure that ran through chapters 1–4 is
simply not in this chapter, and nothing says so.

## 10 · The sounds (≈6 new, and one deliberate absence)

**THE DREAD BED IS NOT IN THIS CHAPTER.** `e2dread` has run at full under
every frame of episodes 2's chapters 1–4 since v10.4 — film, play, scenes,
cards. Chapter 5 does not declare it. The player will not consciously
notice it has gone; the chapter will feel different from its first second.
**That is the release, heard, and it costs nothing.**

In its place, an ordinary afternoon:

| sound | what it is | where |
|---|---|---|
| `campday` | an afternoon camp bed — distant traffic, a generator, birds, a far PA | the chapter's bed |
| `storecount` | a rubber stamp on a form | the three counters |
| `riflerack` | a rifle going into a steel rack | the armskote |
| `armsdoor` | the armskote grille | the armskote |
| `e5theme` | a short, warm closing bed under the final teaching | scenes A and D |
| — | `platoonmarch` is already SHARED (e2c1 + e2c3) — reuse it free | reuse |
| — | `campamb` (e2c1's) and `marchcall` (e2c2's) are ONE CHAPTER'S today | see below |

Every pick by MEASUREMENT (the phone-band measure, v10.4), previews to
Chad before shipping (the v10.6 rule), `duration_seconds` and `loop` as
model PARAMETERS and never only in English (the v9.2 law).

**On reusing a chapter-owned sound:** `build.py` COMPUTES the split — a
sound is a chapter's only when exactly ONE chapter can ask for it. So
cueing `campamb` or `marchcall` here moves it into the SHARED pack
automatically, with no declaration and no duplicate bytes. That is correct
and costs a few KB at boot; the bias to shared is deliberate and
asymmetric (a sound wrongly left shared costs a few KB, a sound wrongly
moved out of it is a cue that plays nothing with no error).

## 13 · Resume

(the v11.6 law):
