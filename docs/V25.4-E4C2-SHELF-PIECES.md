## 1. The asks

- **S1 · BIGGER ON THE SHELF.** Whichever of the three he took in chapter 1 (the Nang Kwak, the takrut, the pha yant) is
  made bigger where it sits on the shelf in his shop, so that it is seen: on a phone first, from where the player
  stands and through every film that looks at it. All three, each measured and photographed.
- **S2 · A RED GLOW.** A red glow on the piece while it sits on the shelf. All three.
- **S3 · BLACK SMOKE.** Black smoke on the piece, to make it look more evil. With the glow: while it sits on the
  shelf.
- **S4 · WHISPERING.** A sound on the piece, creepy, like whispering: heard near it, wordless (nothing to caption, as
  the black master's own chant is wordless, v24.25), under every voice. Chosen on taste, so every candidate goes to him
  as a loudness-matched preview and his ear decides (v10.6); the build carries the measured pick until then.
- **S5 · THE SMOKE MORE TRANSLUCENT** (his word on seeing v25.4 live). The same smoke, seen through: its strength
  taken down, not its shape changed, and not so far that it is gone (v22.0: a correction does not overshoot). A look,
  so it is set from photographs at a few strengths and the bracket goes to him (v24.29).

## 2. Checkpoints

- **P1** this plan.
- **P2** measure first: where the piece sits, how big each is drawn now and how many pixels of a phone it fills from
  each place the player can see it (play in the shop by day and by night, each film that shows the shelf); what else

- **P3** S1 and S2 designed from those measures, built in the parts, photographed (each piece, phone and desktop, day
