# v17.6 · THE SAK YANT CHOICE

Chad: *"I want an added gameplay flow when the player sits down infront of the
sakyant ajahn, before the ajahn starts, the ajahn asks the player which sakyant
design the player would like to choose. Then a window frame pops up in the
middle of the screen, and there are 3 different options to choose from, each
with a different effect and power, each with its own description. After the
player confirms which sakyant, then it goes to the flow that you already have,
which asks the player to breathe, and the heartbeat minigame starts. Do not
remove anything that you already have."* And then: *"The 3 sakyant choice is:
Gao Yord, Hatiew Japet, Paed Tidt. Research on what they are before adding
them."*

## 1 · The research

| design | Thai | what it is (the sources agree) |
|---|---|---|
| **Gao Yord** | เก้ายอด | Nine spires for the nine peaks of Mount Meru, home of the gods. The "master yant", the one many others are built on: protection from every side, authority, good fortune. |
| **Hah Taew** | ห้าแถว | Five rows of Khom script, each row its own blessing: (1) against unjust punishment and unwanted spirits, (2) against bad fortune and a bad horoscope, (3) against black magic and curses, (4) success and luck, (5) charm. |
| **Paed Tidt** | แปดทิศ | "Eight directions": eight mantras for the eight compass points round a centre (eight emanations of the Buddha), guarding the wearer from every side harm can come from. |

These three are the classic trio of "master yants". **"Hatiew" is Hah Taew**, the five lines.
**"Japet" matched nothing**, in English or Thai, across eight searches. The
nearest candidates were Krahad Phet (diamond armour, เกราะเพชร) and Chat Phet.
The game ships Hah Taew. If Chad meant a different yant, the fix is a sheet
edit: its name, power line and words are three rows on UI TEXT.

## 2 · The flow (nothing removed)

He sits on the stool → "Sit here. Back to me." (as before) → **the Ajarn
asks: "Before I start. Which yant you want on you? Choose."** (`aj1which`) →
**the window** → he confirms → **"Good. This one, I give you."** (`aj1chosen`)
→ "When the needle go in, you breathe out. Don't move." (as before) → the
gong → the rod (the heartbeat game, as before) → the stirring (as before).
At the stirring, the chosen yant goes on: into the Sak Yant box. It never goes
into the bag, because ink is not carried. When his line about the warmth has
been said, the ITEM UNLOCKED splash shows its drawing in gold, under "Inked on
your back · worn in your Sak Yant slot". Then "Finish. Turn around." and the
question, as before.

The old tail of `sitStool()` (breathe, gong, rod) lives on, line for line, in
`yantBegin()`, which the choice calls. A resume into the yant phase sits him
down again and asks again. A replay takes the yant back, like the torch.

## 3 · The powers

Each yant guards against a different KIND of harm, so no two overlap:

| yant | power | engine field | where it cuts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gao Yord | minigame damage −30 % | `evGuard: 0.3` (v14.13's seam) | every minigame price; the stronger worn guard counts, so it never stacks with the LP Tim |
| Hah Taew | sanity drains 35 % slower | `drainGuard: 0.35` | her drain, a chapter's presence, the hurt bleed |
| Paed Tidt | sudden sanity hits −40 % | `shockGuard: 0.4` | a sighting's bite; a `kit.award` of sanity that is not a minigame's |

Both new guards run BEFORE the amulet's ward, as the minigame guard does. With
no yant worn, both hand back exactly what they were given. Each yant is
`fixed`: once worn, it cannot be dragged, double-tapped or swapped out of its
box (`invFixed`); a chapter's own `kit.equip`/`kit.take` still can.

## 4 · The engine

- **THE TWENTY-THIRD SEAM, `kit.choose(ids, { label, title, confirm })`**
  returns a Promise of the chosen id. It works like the unlock splash:
  - It is a screen STATE of its own (`choose`), so every play gate closes and a chapter's clock waits.
  - The mouse is released, and taken back on confirm.
  - There is no Esc.
  - Confirm is refused until a card is picked, and for 0.6 s after the window opens.
  - Picking: tap or click a card; on a keyboard, 1–9 or the arrows, then Enter.
  - The window is torn down with the run (`kitReset`).
- It gives nothing itself: what the choice means is the chapter's business.
- Layout: cards side by side on a desktop; stacked rows on a phone (drawing left, words right).
- `item.<id>.power`, an optional one-line POWER, is shown in jade on the cards and in the bag's description pane.
- The unlock splash takes the chapter's own sub-line (`opts.sub`). An item with neither a model nor art (`bare`) shows its line drawing large.
- Three new line-drawn icons: `e-ygao`, `e-yhah`, `e-ypaed`.
- Proof: the fixture checks the window opens, refuses an early or empty confirm, answers the id, and hands back play. It checks that a worn yant stays in its box, and that each guard cuts only its own kind of harm (`chooseOpens`, `chooseAnswers`, `yantGuards`).
- Episodes 1 and 2 call none of it. The one shared change is two guard multiplies; with no yant worn, they hand back exactly what they are given.

Paed Tidt is gone (it was v17.6's guess for "Japet"); its power moved to Gao
Yord, whose own meaning is protection against danger. The ids are
`yantgaoyord`, `yanthahtaew`, `yantsroi`; a v17.6 save holding the old
`yantpaedtidt` simply drops it (applyState's known-id filter) and the chapter
gives the choice again on its next run.
