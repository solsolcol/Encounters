# v6.2 · THE SELECTOR, DRESSED AS WHAT IT IS

Chad, on the v6.0 selector:

> Can you make the selector window look visually nicer, and more
> appealing? Its rather plain and boring now. Have clear differentiation
> between episodes and chapters.

## What it was

Ten mono numerals in small boxes, one line of grey text, five boxed rows.
An episode and a chapter were the same box at two sizes, and nothing in
the panel said which chapters were finished or how well.

## What it is

**Episodes are CASE FILES.** A strip of dossier cards you slide through
(scroll-snap, both edges masked so it reads as a strip), one per episode:
a folder tab on the top-left corner, the numeral in Cormorant Garamond,
"EPISODE N" in the small mono, the case's name in italic serif (three
lines, clamped), and five dots for its five chapters — filled sodium for
a sealed one, a jade dot for the one you are in, a sodium ring for one you
have reached, a hollow dot for the rest. A case whose five are all sealed
carries a small red SEALED stamp in the corner. The open case is lifted
four pixels with a sodium glow and centred in the strip when the panel
opens; an unwritten episode's card is dim and dashed and says "Not yet
written" where the dots would be, with its name still on it — the ten
names Chad gave are the point of the ten cards.

**Under the strip, the case:** "EPISODE 1" as a sodium stencil line, the
name in the display serif at heading size, and "2 of 5 chapters sealed".

**Chapters are STOPS ON A LINE.** Five rows, no boxes: a 22-px stop on a
vertical line. Each row draws its own two pieces of the line (from its top
edge to its stop, from its stop to its bottom edge), so the line follows
the rows whatever height a wrapped title gives them, and a sealed stop
lights the line down to the next one. The stops:

| state | class | the stop | the word |
|---|---|---|---|
| sealed | `done` | filled sodium, carrying the RANK the chapter got (a tick if it was sealed before v6.2 kept records) | Sealed |
| the chapter you are in | `now` | jade ring with a dot, breathing (`herePulse`; off under reduced motion) | You are here |
| reached, not sealed | `open` | sodium ring with a dot | In progress (only once there is a run; a fresh profile's chapter 1 says nothing) |
| not reached | `locked` | dim ring | — (the name reads "Not yet reached") |
| not written | `unwritten locked` | dashed dim ring | — (the name reads "Not yet written") |

`sealed` is by the record — a result on file, or a later chapter reached
(a store from before v6.2 has no results, so its earlier chapters still
read as sealed, with a tick). The chapter you are replaying is `now` on
the line but still counts as sealed in the heading and on the card.

## The store

`mz.encounters.progress` used to hold `{ reached, t }`. It now also holds
`sealed: { ch1: { score, rank, t }, … }`, written by `finish()` through
`markSealed()`: the LATEST result per chapter, so a replay overwrites,
and kept across a new game the way `reached` is (a chapter you sealed
stays sealed). `markReached` now preserves the rest of the object instead
of rewriting it. A `?ch=` preview records nothing, as it opens nothing.
This is the data the episode-complete card (v6.3) tallies.

## Words

Three new strings — `chapters.inProgress`, `chapters.here`,
`chapters.progress` ("{n} of {m} chapters sealed") — and three reused:
`complete.sealed` (the stamp's word is the stop's word), `chapters.locked`,
`chapters.unwritten`. Sheet v34.

## The contract the harnesses read is unchanged

`#chTabs .chTab[data-ep]` (ten of them, `.on` on the open one, `.empty`
on an unwritten episode), `#chEpName` (its text still contains "Episode
N"), `#chList .chTile[data-ch]`, `.locked`, `.unwritten`, `disabled`,
`#chClose`, `#chAsk`/`#chYes`/`#chNo`. `menutest` gained step 8 — with
chapter 3 reached and results for 1 and 2 on record: the stops read
done, done, open, locked, locked with S and B on the first two, the case
card is the lit one with the same five dots and is inside the strip's
box, the heading counts 2 of 5. `texttest` now OPENS the selector before
its scan, so the painted words (all from T() and the chapter files) are
guarded rather than never seen; `#chTabs`, `#chEpName` and `#chList` are
exempt as whole subtrees, the way `#choices` always was.

## The trap, for LEARNINGS

The first build put two rows and two dots at the top-right corner of the
panel, rotated, with red borders. `.sealed` is the complete card's STAMP —
`position:absolute; top:2px; right:6px; transform:rotate(-13deg); border:
2px solid var(--seal)` — and the rows and dots had been given the state
class `sealed`. The rule is a grep: before naming a state class in a
shared stylesheet, look for the bare name; here the state is `done`.

## Numbers

Card 128 × 162, strip padding 13 / 10 with a 16-px edge mask; heading
1.56 rem; rows min 46 px; stop 22 px on a 2-px line at x = 16; panel
min(560px, 100%). On a 420 × 760 phone all five stops are on screen with
the strip and the heading — the first cut had chapter 5 below the fold,
and 36 px came out of margins, not out of the type.
