## What "leave nothing behind" is, mechanically

It is not a promise, it is a closed loop that already existed:

- the regeneration list is `who === 'james'` out of `src/voicelines.js` — 79 takes;
- `JAMES_TAKES` in main.js is asserted equal to that set, both directions, by
  `chaptertest`;
- every take has a file, and every file has a registry row, also by `chaptertest`.

So a missed line is a red build, not a silent survivor of the old voice. All
79 were regenerated, encoded, installed and re-measured; nothing was skipped.

## The prompt sheet

`masters/v5.28/prompts.json` holds the exact prompt fired for each of the 79,
and it survived four rounds of Chad's correction. The rules it encodes:

1. **The registry's words EXACTLY.** eleven_v3 has no scene-description
   concept — the prompt IS the spoken text — so prose in brackets gets read
   out loud. (v5.15's bug: *"why is he saying the direction out loud?"*)
2. **No capitals added.** A capital forces a stress the sentence may not want.
   Adding them is what made the chapter 1 opening sound wrong.
3. **A tag names an EMOTION, never only a volume or a body state.** Chad:
   *"whispering is not an emotion"*. A tag that says how LOUD gets a flat read
   at that volume, which is half of what makes a pass sound monotone. Where the
   line also wants a volume, the tag carries both — `[terrified whisper]`, not
   `[whispering]`.
4. **At most one added ellipsis**, only where the line genuinely turns.
5. **Chapter 1's opening carries no tag** — Chad picked the untagged read.

26 of the 79 carry a tag. That the tags were *absorbed* rather than spoken is
measured, not assumed: a spoken direction leaves a pause between itself and the
line, and **none of the 26 has an internal silence ≥0.15 s at -38 dB before 45 %
of its duration**. Zero of 26. (Transcription cannot answer this question —
`creative_transcribe_audio` echoes the prompt.)

## Timing

`masters/v5.28/overlapscan.mjs` scans all 24 films and scenes: every voice cue's
start plus its MEASURED length against the next cue and the scene's end. Aaron
is slower than River on some lines (v2wake1 1.15 → 2.43 s, vD 5.72 → 8.05 s), so
unlike v5.18 a collision was genuinely possible. **There is none.** The one flag
the scan raises — `v3aunt5` overlapping `vpant` by 0.02 s in ch3 scene B — is the
auntie's line, not his, and predates this build. No timeline moved.

## The level, and why the bus grew a limiter

Measured across all 79 takes, against the same 79 as River shipped them:

| | peak mean | loudness mean | crest |
|---|---|---|---|
| River (v5.18–v5.27) | -4.29 dBFS | -20.82 dBFS | 16.53 dB |
| Aaron, peak-matched | -4.74 dBFS | -24.51 dBFS | 19.77 dB |

Aaron reads **3.7 dB quieter with a 3.2 dB wider crest**. Peak-matching (the
v4.8 rule) aligns the loudest instant and says nothing about the average — the
exact trap v5.26 documented for River, hit again by a different actor. Left
alone he would have landed roughly where River was *before* the v5.26 bus, which
is the complaint Chad has now raised twice.

A uniform file lift cannot fix it: the hottest take sits at -2.3 dBFS, so the
set has under 2 dB of headroom, against a 3.7 dB gap. It is a dynamics problem.

Rendered through the REAL chain in an `OfflineAudioContext` (`dbgaaron.mjs`),
18 takes, against River through the shipped bus:

| bus | avg vs River | worst take | max peak | clipping takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.0 / -18 / 4 (v5.26, shipped) | **-1.87 dB** | -4.73 | -0.52 | 0 |
| 2.5 / -18 / 4 | -1.05 | -3.78 | +0.47 | 3 |
| 3.0 / -18 / 4 | -0.42 | -3.06 | +1.29 | 8 |
| 3.0 / -18 / 4 + limiter | +0.93 | -1.55 | -0.19 | 0 |
| **3.5 / -18 / 4 + limiter** | **+1.31 dB** | **-1.05** | **-0.06** | **0** |
| 4.0 / -18 / 4 + limiter | +1.62 | -0.64 | +0.07 | 3 |

More gain alone buys loudness and clipping together. The **limiter** is what
separates them: `VOICE_BOOST` 2.0 → 3.5 and a second `DynamicsCompressor` after
the first — threshold -3, knee 0, ratio 20, attack 1 ms, release 50 ms — sitting
between the compressor and `voiceOut`. 3.5 is the last non-clipping rung.

Result: he is now **1.31 dB louder than River was**, worst take within 1.05 dB
instead of 4.73, and the quietest lines gain most (v4regret +3.6, v4wake3am
+2.6, vrelief +5.5) — it evens him out rather than turning him up, which is the
whole point.

Nothing outside his bus moved. The other three speakers run on `voiceStage`
with no compressor and no limiter; the v5.27 duck is untouched, and his higher
post-bus level only widens the margins it was built to protect.
