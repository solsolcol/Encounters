# v25.3 — Episode 4 chapter 2: no sprinkle, no string, and Lersi Chin's body made natural

## 0. Chad's words (verbatim, 10 Nov 2026, while v25.2 was being checked)

> There is no need for lersi chin to sprinkle water with the leaf thing and no need for the tieing of string. Also i
> find it unacceptable that you find his poses natural, it clearly is not. You are not trying hard enough

And, minutes later, with a phone screenshot of the visit's wai (Lersi Chin standing before the altar, seen from his
right: two flat hands side by side in front of his face, two forearms squeezed together before his chest):

> Look at how ridiculous this looks, and you said it looks natural, unbelievable

What I had told him, and why it was wrong: during v25.2's checks I wrote that his homage, the bend to the bowl, the
bowl held out, the flicks and the thread "read naturally". I judged them small, on contact sheets, as the person who
had written them (the standing rule on pictures: the author sees what he intended). Looked at full size through the
film's own lens, as the player sees them:

- **The wai is not a wai.** The palms are not pressed together: two flat hands stand side by side before his face,
  palms out, and it reads as a man hiding his face. The hand's own turn (which way its palm faces) was never measured on
  his scan; it was taken from the hand's vertices at bind and trusted.
- **The bowl is not held.** The hands hang at its sides, fingers pointing down, palms clear of it, wrists bent: two
  boards beside a bowl.
- His hands have no finger joints (his rig is a 28-joint Mixamo rig: one bone per hand), so no pose may ask a hand to
  close or grip; a pose must be one a flat hand can make (palms together, palms under, palm on).

## 1. The asks

- **B1 · NO SPRINKLE.** The mayom bundle is not lifted, dipped or flicked; no drops; their sounds and cues out. The
  bundle may stay standing in the bowl of holy water as one of his things.
- **B2 · NO STRING.** No thread tied at the client's wrist: the "Your wrist." line, the client's held-out hand, the
  winding and the knot all out; the line retired (its take kept in masters). The thread may stay on the mat with his
  things.
- **B3 · HIS BODY NATURAL, IN FACT.** Every pose he takes made from how a real person does it: the wai from a real
  motion-captured prayer (palms pressed, fingers up, elbows down and in, the head bowing into it); the bowl cradled on
  both palms from beneath, as an offering is held, close to the body and then held out; the examination's hands; the
  packing. Each photographed full size through the film's lens on a phone and a desktop, looked at against real
  photographs of the same act, and every fault seen written down before it is called done. No pose is called natural
  on a contact sheet.
