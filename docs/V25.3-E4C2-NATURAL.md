## 4. The wai: the sources

- Thairath, on the three levels of the wai (Thai): https://www.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/culture/2455692 and
  https://www.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/culture/2814383
- The Government Public Relations Department, on the wai (Thai): https://www.prd.go.th/th/content/category/detail/id/31/iid/463156
- The Thailand Life, "How to do the perfect Thai wai": https://www.thethailandlife.com/how-to-do-the-perfect-thai-wai
- Tasty Thailand, on the wai's levels: https://tastythailand.com/?p=20144

What they agree on, and what the poses use: level one (monks, sacred images) the thumbs between the eyebrows, the index
fingers at the upper forehead, the head bowed; level two (elders) the thumbs at the tip of the nose; level three (equals)
the thumbs at the chin; an elder or a senior answers a wai with the hands at the chest and a slight nod; the elbows are
kept in, close to the body.
