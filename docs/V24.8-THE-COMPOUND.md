# v24.8 — The compound round the garden (episode 3 chapter 5, ending D)

Chad, 29 Oct 2026, on the build v24.7 shipped:

> "For ending d, when it reaches the monk part, why are there hdb blocks in the background? Replace the hdb blocks
> with temple buildings and temple compound instead."

## 1. Why there were HDB blocks

World M is chapter 4's temple (the merit film, v22.0), rebuilt in this chapter at x −250. Its ESTATE is eight of the
game's HDB blocks laid round the temple ("the city beyond: HDB blocks round the temple, their windows coming on"),
which suits film M at night: a temple in the city.

v24.7 moved ending D's morning out of the hall to the great oak on the grass OUTSIDE the hall's west wall, and every
shot of that garden looks west, north-west, north or south-west, straight at the estate. The before-photographs
(phone and desktop, the shot's own clock) show an HDB block in nearly every shot: the grounds at first light, the walk
to his mat, the kneel (on a phone on both sides of the trunk), the monk's teaching (dead centre, behind the monk), the
round of the trunk, the lights' climb and the years' mornings.

**The law** (docs/LEARNINGS.md): a scene moved to a new place inherits that place's backdrop. When a camera moves, list
what it now looks at; the old place's view is no evidence for the new one.

## 2. What changed

- **The estate is not drawn while the garden is on.** `gardenSet` hides it, and so does its own late landing (the parse
  is async; it reads `mLive.gardenOn`). Film M and anything outside ending D's garden still stand in the city:
  `gardenSet(false)` at every ending's start and at film M's begin shows the estate again.
- **The garden now stands in the temple's own grounds**, built inside the garden group (`GG`), so it is drawn only
  while the garden is. Each building was placed for the shot that looks its way, on a phone's narrow frame as well as a
  desktop's:

- **Recipes.** The hall's own: roofs stepped and stacked (tier A meets the wall top at the wall line; each later tier is
  1.5 m in, 2.2 m up and 1.6 m shorter at each end, with a white band under it), carved gables with gilt bargeboards,
  blades, naga curls and chofa, white walls, gilt window frames under pointed crowns, and lotus capitals. The courts use
  the courtyard wall's recipe (white, lacquered coping, gilt-budded piers).
- **Cost.** Every piece is merged by material into **five meshes** with per-vertex colours, the shape of World A's
  `shopBatch`: walls and painted colours, gold, the two roof tiles, and the carved gables. Measured on the build:
  42,538 vertices and 34,854 triangles in total, the gold mesh being 29k of the triangles. There is no download, no
  light and no shadow, and the morning's fog sets each building back at its own distance. The curtain draws the garden
  forced visible, so the new programs compile under it (v14.15 / v15.1).
