## 1 · The hand-off (episode 3 chapter 3, the shop's browse)

### Why it clashed — measured in the code, before anything moved

- **The chapter's clock stands still under the gallery window; the voices do not.** His answer as the window opens
  (`z3masters`, 5.69 s) is booked on the chapter clock (`speak.until`), and the clock does not run while the window
  is up — so when the window closed, the chapter still thought he was speaking for the line's whole length, and his
  "The old masters... watching over the whole shop." (`z3look4`) waited ~6 s behind a voice that had long finished.
- **The owner's order went up on the fourth press.** v21.9's `ownerCalls()` set the phase to `owner` the moment the
  fourth thing was pressed: OBJECTIVE COMPLETE, "Talk to the owner", his mark and his badge — while `z3look4` and his
  call (`ow3call`) still waited in the line queue. Pressing him started the gift, and `giftBegin()` EMPTIES the queue,
  so the owner's call (and, after the gallery, the old-masters line itself) could be skipped outright.
- **Nothing held a press while a line was said.** Four quick presses queued four lines back to back.

### What it does now

- The gallery closes (every page read) → **`z3look4` at once** (the booking brought forward by the time the window
  was up; an answer that never started is dropped) → OBJECTIVE COMPLETE and the box empties → when his line is over,
  0.4 s, the owner's call ("Eh, come, come. I got something for you.") → when that is over, his order and his mark.
  Until then the owner cannot be pressed.
- Whichever look is the fourth, the same: its line in full, then the call, then the order.
- **One thing at a time** in the whole browse: from the press of a look until its line has been said in full — and
  while the owner introduces the masters, and while he answers — nothing else in the browse is offered: no mark, no
  badge, no press (`holdTill`, `browseHeld()`).
- A Continue saved between the fourth look and the order lands on the order (the browse is spent).
