## The axis (the fifth, closing the arc)

DISTANCE → SMALLNESS → INVERSION → INTRUSION → **RELEASE**. For four
chapters something has been owed and unnamed. Chapter 5 names it: the
note from the void deck has been IN THE FLAT the whole time — taped
under the seat of the dining chair he sat on to think. The chapter's
horror is retroactive (it was under him); its relief is real (a man who
knows what to do is standing in the room). The episode ends with the
debt paid: the note burns at the home altar, returned to the fire it
was taken from. Never show her; the TANG-KI shows where she is by where
HE looks. A medium reading a room the audience cannot read is scarier
than any apparition, and it is the character's whole job.

   gaps between lines — phone-call law generalised: turn-taking gaps)

A · SEEK EXPERIENCED GUIDANCE (~52 s) — THE SIT-DOWN. sitDown() at the

B · TRUST FEAR ALONE (~42 s) — THE ROOM IN HIS HEAD. He stays standing,
staring at the note on the table. The morning DRAINS: day sounds duck
to nothing, fog closes a little, heart in. The ch4 poltergeist sounds
replay — hallsteps, the dragged chair — LOUD, panned, circling... and
NOTHING in the bright room moves. The tang-ki's voice cuts through
(t5fearB: "What you saw in the night was real. What you see now — this
fear — is yours, not its."). Everything releases AT ONCE: full morning
back in one beat. v5fearB1, shaky. Fade. (The scare is a lie told by
fear, staged honestly: the audience hears his head, then gets shown
the unchanged room.)

C · DISMISS EVERYTHING (~40 s) — THE FLAT DISAGREES. A short laugh.
v5disC1 — "Paper. It's just paper." He turns his back on the table and
walks toward the window. Behind him, quiet, the tang-ki: t5disC —
"You may call it paper. [beat] Paper burns either way." Then the
daytime poltergeist tell, ONCE: the curtains BILLOW with no wind
(billow), and the fan does a half-turn on its own (fanSpeed pulse).
He stops mid-step, back still turned. Hold four seconds. Fade. No
ghost, no strings — the flat simply declines to be dismissed.

D · LEARN & MOVE FORWARD (~56 s) — THE BURNING. The finale of the
episode. The tang-ki takes the note from the table (notepull, soft),
carries it to the HOME ALTAR. Ma comes to stand at the edge of frame
(glide, small). t5learnD1 — "We return what was kept." matchstrike.
The note catches (`noteburn`; a small flame plane at the altar;
altLight swells; the real art curling — scale/opacity out). ONE ring
of the tang-ki's bell (`bellring`, ch3's — the ceremony come home,
one man, one gesture). As it burns the DAYLIGHT SWELLS — key + hemi
up over four seconds: the flat exhales for the first time in five
chapters. t5learnD2 — "It is finished. What you keep now is the
lesson." v5learnD (quiet): "The first one. Master Z says every case
teaches you the next." chime. Long hold on the altar, the smoke, the
morning. Fade. Card, teaching, rank — and the EPISODE seals.
