## The main hall

- **The admin-shirt man is gone.** He was the waiter dozing on the bench, the
  only use of `admintee` in the chapter, so the asset left the chapter's list
  too (2.4 MB less to download). The bench stays.
- **The man by the walkway no longer vanishes.** His file (`standman`) is
  QUANTIZED: every mesh's positions are normalised shorts in [-1, 1] under a
  0.01 armature. `wideBounds`' default culling sphere, (0, 0.9, 0) r 1.9, is in
  MESH units, so for him it was a ball under two centimetres across at his
  feet. While his feet were in frame he was drawn; walk close enough to look
  at his face and the feet left the frame, the sphere with them, and three.js
  culled the whole man. This is v18.5's law (e3c2's congregation) met again.
  Every rig without a measured table entry now gets `skinCull()`: a sphere
  round its POSED SKIN in world space, then in each mesh's own frame,
  ×1.35 and never under 0.8 of the rig's height. One sphere covers the whole
  body, eyes and teeth included. Photographed from 4.4 m, 1.0 m and 0.8 m:
  drawn every time.
- **The blessing bowl is out of the throne.** It was set on the dais at v16.1,
  beside a 0.62 m seat block. Since v17.1 the throne is built round the monk
  once he lands, and its red footrest (as wide as his knees, as deep as his
  toes) reached over the bowl. `placeBowl()` runs after the throne is built
  and moves the silver bowl, and the alms bowl on the other side, to the
  footrest's own sides, a hand's width clear of its gilt edge. The whisk's
  resting place and `bowlPos` follow. Neither bowl ever moves nearer in than
  v16.1 put it.
- **The altar.** The abbot's framed photograph on the second tier, the gilt
  half-arch and the halo ring are gone. The red board behind the Buddha
  carries a lai thai on its front face only (`makeAltarScreen`, 1024 × 1056):
  a running kanok-flame border between gilt rules, round a diamond lattice
  with a four-petal flower at every crossing and a small star in every
  diamond. Its edges and back stay plain lacquer. It harmonises with the
  temple model's own diamond-lattice ceiling.
- One thing that looked like a bug and is not: from in front, the monk
  resting on the first frame of `Sit_Thumbs_Up_Right` reads as standing,
  because his thighs point at the lens and the robe hides the bend.
  Measured: hips 0.32 m over the cushion and toes 0.50 m ahead of them. A
  photograph from 1 October shows the same pose.

## The Sak Yant room

- **The altar row is on the back wall now, to the masks' left.** There is
  1.4 m of wall to their right and the row is 2.09 m wide, so it goes left.
  It hangs 0.40 m clear of them on a wall shelf (a red board, a gilt lip
  hanging under its front, three red brackets with gilt edges). The shelf's
  top is at F+1.40; the masks' scan starts at F+1.30, so the row stands a
  hand higher. The candles, the incense pot with its smoke, and both garlands
  moved with it. The pot is at the end away from the masks, so its smoke
  rises clear of them, and the candles' warm floor glow is in front of the
  shelf. Nobody walks under a shrine: a blocker fills the space below.
- **The entry view turned** to the gap between the two rows. A phone sees
  about 37° across, and from the doorway the altar, the masks and the Ajarn
  span 49°. So the frame is the altar on one side and the masks on the other,
  with the Ajarn at its edge. Desktop sees everything.
- **The Ajarn's throne has no back** (`seat.userData.noBack`). The monk's
  throne keeps its own.
- **The old masters' three photographs are gone.**
- **Every yant poster is redrawn** (`makeYantCloth`) from the three designs
  Chad gave at v17.7 (bkktattoostudio13.com; the reference images were
  studied side by side):
  - **Gao Yord**: the stepped pyramid of script cells, 9-7-5-3-1. On every
    step's outer cell sit the three ovals with an unalome over them, nine
    peaks in all, the middle one the tallest. Under it are two lines of
    script whose rules end in curls.
  - **Hah Taew Chat Petch**: five vertical rows of script, an unalome rising
    from the top of each and one hanging from the bottom, with the Chat Petch
    diamond net crossing between the rows.
  - **Sroi Sangwan**: three rows of script hung in a U, an unalome rising
    from both ends of every row, and the pendant at the front: a diamond with
    an eye, and three unalomes over it.

  The letters are drawn, not typed: no phone font has the Khom script. They
  use sixteen stroke shapes in the style of the references (a looped head,
  arches, hooks and tails), each with a hand's wobble that is the same on
  every load. Each design is painted once and shared by every cloth of it.
  There are eight cloths: three on the west wall, three on the east wall (one
  where v18.7's altar stood), and two on the front wall. The kuti door's small
  sign is a Gao Yord too.

## The thank-you

`z1thank`: "[grateful] Thank you, Ajahn Krukai." It is Brian on eleven_v3,
four takes, and take A was picked (`masters/v19.1/make.sh`). Take A has the
cleanest end. C ends on signal; B is clean but the quickest read. Its 1.28 s
of trailing air was cut to 0.36 s, giving 1.70 s. It goes through v18.6's
chain.

It is cued in `walkOut()` at T + 0.15, into the bow (the head is down at
T + 0.8 and back up by T + 1.7), in all four endings. Every reply has
finished by T: A 0.4 s before, B 1.2, C 1.6, D 0.7. It is warmed in
`build()`, because it is fired from a helper and not the scene itself
(v14.16). Measured on the hosted build: the cue fires in all four endings,
and in B and C (unmuted) the voice log shows it playing 1.70 s, after the
bell and before he stands.
