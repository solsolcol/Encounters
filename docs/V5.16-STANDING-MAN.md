# v5.16 · THE MAN AT THE TENT EDGE

Chad, with two files: *"Replace 2 of the standing audience characters at the
medium event with these 2 standing models"* — a Sketchfab standing man
(`standing_man.glb`) and a Fab fighter (`kungfu_man_v2.fbx`).

One of the two shipped. The other cannot yet, and the reason is in the file
rather than in the game; it is written down at the bottom.

## Which standers, and why those

Chapter 3's tentage has FOUR standing figures, built at the edges by the
same primitive `figure()` the seated crowd uses:

| index | at | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | (-4.9, 1.4) | left edge — **already Chad's chinese boy** since v4.8 |
| 1 | (4.9, 2.2) | right edge, the one you walk past coming in |
| 2 | (4.6, -3.9) | down at the brazier, feeding it |
| 3 | (3.9, -4.6) | down at the brazier, feeding it |

The standing man took **index 1**, and the choice is not a matter of taste:

- It is the mirror of the boy. The two ends of the back of the tent are
  where the walkable lane brings you closest to a face, and having a real
  character at each end is what makes the tent read as full of people
  rather than full of one person.
- `LOW` stops building standers at three (`if (LOW && standers.length >= 3)
  break`), so index 3 does not exist on a weak phone. A model placed there
  would silently vanish for the players most likely to notice a missing
  man. Index 1 survives `LOW`.
- Indices 2 and 3 have a job — they are the pair at the brazier — and
  breaking that pair up costs the reading of a small piece of staging.

Index 2 is where the second model goes when it can be finished.

## Getting him shippable

He arrived as 10 MB: 68,708 triangles and eleven textures, four of them
1024² PNG normal maps.

| stage | size |
|---|---|
| as uploaded | 10,023 KB |
| maps dropped, welded, simplified 0.24, sheets to 512 JPEG | 1,423 KB |
| **+ quantized (KHR_mesh_quantization)** | **910 KB** |

The first two steps are v5.05's recipe unchanged, and the reasoning has not
changed either: the CSP-safe `rescueTextures()` only ever restores the BASE
COLOUR map, so a normal map is not a heavy map, it is a map that renders
white — pure download. 18,801 triangles remain, which is the same class as
the two seated characters of v5.05 (~14k) and Guan Gong.

The third step is new here and worth the note. **Quantization is the one
compression this game can take for free**: `KHR_mesh_quantization` is
handled inside three.js's own GLTFLoader with no decoder, no worker and no
`blob:` — unlike meshopt, which needs a decoder the chapter loaders do not
carry (only `zavLoader()` does). It cut a third off the file with no
visible change: the same shot, rendered before and after, differs only by
the phase of his idle. For a skinned mesh the dequantization scale goes
into the SKIN's inverse bind matrices rather than onto the mesh node —
checked in the file, because a scale left on the node of a skinned mesh is
exactly the kind of thing three.js ignores in silence.

## How he is wired

`loadGLB('standman', ...)` in `ch3.js`, beside the boy, and the contract is
every other model's in this chapter:

- **Async over a placeholder.** The primitive stander stays visible and is
  hidden only when the real bytes land, so a download that never finishes
  costs a nicer man and never an empty patch of tarmac.
- **His own mixer**, pushed into `specialMixers` beside the two v5.05
  sitters, so he freezes and thaws with the rest of the crowd
  (`dt * crowdLife`). Three mixers is three; the shared-skeleton trick the
  thirty-strong encik crowd needs buys nothing at this count.
- **The wrapper keeps the stander contract**: a `f` group at the old one's
  position and yaw, carrying its `userData.ph`, so the sway loop drives him
  without knowing he is a model.

## The two measurements

Both taken from POSED bones after the idle is playing, never from a box —
`Box3.setFromObject` on a skinned mesh reports the bind pose.

**Facing.** His toes measure +z of his feet (`LeftToeBase` z 0.074 against
`LeftFoot` z −0.058), so he faces +z at identity, like the boy scan, and
the stander wrapper's own `Math.PI` is what turns him to the altar.
`STAND_YAW = 0`.

**Height.** `STAND_H = 1.74`, derived from the primitive he replaces rather
than from a guess about how tall a man is: `figure()` puts the head centre
at `(0.86 + 0.775) * h` and the hair cap adds ~0.11, and the right-edge
stander is built at `h = 1.00`. Measured in the running game afterwards,
the primitive's crown was **1.732** and the model's crown bone is
**1.750** — the 18 mm is his idle's own bob. His lowest bone sits at
−0.002, i.e. on the tarmac.

The crown is measured with the crowd's loose `/Head/i` and not `HEAD_RE`,
deliberately, and the comment in the code says so: on a Mixamo rig the
loose one also catches `HeadTop_End`, which is the top of the skull. That
is the v5.05 lesson — among existing things, the measure that matters is
the one the existing things used.

## The one that could not ship: the Fab kungfu man

Converted (`fbx2gltf`) and inspected: 750 triangles, 34 joints, a clean
low-poly character who would sit in this crowd for almost no download.
Two things are missing from the file, and neither can be invented here:

1. **His texture.** The FBX references
   `C:\ALL\Blender\textures\Fighter_v2\kungfuman\kungfu_man_v2_texture256.png`
   as an EXTERNAL file and does not embed it — there are no PNG or JPEG
   bytes anywhere in the 112 KB. His UVs cover most of a 256² sheet in
   structured islands, so this is a painted texture and not a palette of
   flat swatches: without it he renders as a white mannequin, which is
   the opposite of what replacing a repetitive figure is for.
2. **An idle.** The FBX carries no animation take, so he would stand in
   his bind T-pose. This one IS solvable here — the standing man's Mixamo
   idle can be retargeted onto his rig by the v5.02 bake — but it is not
   worth doing until the texture decides whether he ships at all.

The ask to Chad is one file: `kungfu_man_v2_texture256.png` from the Fab
download. Everything else for him is ready.
