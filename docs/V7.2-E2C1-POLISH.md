# v7.2 · THE WORST BED, POLISHED — the quality pass (the plan and the record)

Chad, 9 Sep 2026, after v7.1 went live: *"do another comprehensive pass
and look at everything in this new chapter that you've just done. Look at
everything that can be objectively improved and enhanced further, whether
it is in visuals, HUD, UI, minigame, props, mechanics, or anything i have
not thought of ... carefully execute and improve upon them, without
changing the story, narrative or overall direction. This is about
improving the quality of the gaming experience."*

The story, the four choices, the lines and the timeline of the day are
NOT on the table. Everything else is. This file is the memory of the pass:
what was found, how, what is done about it, checkpoint by checkpoint, so a
compaction or a pause loses nothing.

## 1 · How the pass was done

Not from memory of building it. The shipped v7.1 was photographed —
the film every two seconds (desktop) and every four (a 390×844 phone
crop), the four scenes every two seconds on both crops, the day at its
nine moments on both, and the room from seven stands by day and four by
night — and every frame was read. The chapter file was re-read end to end,
and the engine's kit (`kitFrame`, the prompt/badge/banner logic, the
presence drain, the event overlay's CSS) was read beside it.

## 2 · What was found

### Visuals — the room
- **V1 · a z-fighting strip on the far wall.** A white vertical stripe at
  x ≈ −0.4 from floor to ceiling in every daytime shot down the room
  (`spawn.png`, `night-row.png`). The shower block's partition wall starts
  at `BLOCK.z0 = 4.0`, coplanar with the bunk's +z wall's inner face, so
  the two fight for the same pixels. Fix: the block's walls start at the
  wall's BACK face (`R.z + R.wall`).
- **V2 · the room is a box lit from one side.** The −x wall is blank; a
  Tekong bunk has louvred windows along both long walls. Five windows
  above the bed heads (one per bay, y 1.85–2.65), pale-sky glass by day
  (a little emissive, so the wall reads lit), dark blue-black at night
  with the room's own `nightK` — the bunk stops looking like a corridor
  and gets a second light direction, and at 03:00 five faint rectangles.
- **V3 · the beds are painted boxes.** A green slab, a white block, a
  green block. Add: a white bedsheet on every mattress top (thin, inset —
  it is one of the standby bed's eight items and never seen), boots under
  every bed at the aisle end (a pair), a field pack on every locker, and
  the TOP BUNK'S UNDERSIDE as a wire-mesh plane — the film's last eight
  seconds and scenes B and D look straight up at a black slab, on both
  crops (`film-phone/f0050`, `f0054`, `sheet-B`, `sheet-D`).
- **V4 · the sleepers lie uncovered.** `night-sleeper.png`: eight men on
  bare mattresses with the blanket box hidden under their bodies. The
  blanket goes over them: raised to the body's height, from the chest to
  the foot of the bed.
- **V5 · the night lighting.** The clock's red (`clockGlow` 1.3 at 3.2 m)
  floods the whole door wall and the block doorway red in every night
  frame (`night-block.png`, scene A 2–6 s, `05-lightsout.png`) — a
  darkroom, not an LED. The block's tube at night (3.2 + the shower's 2.4)
  blows the tiles to white (`sheet-A` 8–14 s). Fix: the glow 1.3 → 0.55
  at 2.2 m (the digits are unlit and stay bright), the block tube at night
  1.6 with a slow fluorescent FLICKER (the dips a dying tube makes — the
  block's own unease, and it costs nothing).
- **V6 · dressing.** A fire extinguisher and a bin by the entrance, a
  broom in the corner, a mop bucket by the block door. Small, cheap, and
  a bunk without them is a render.

## 4 · The record (what shipped at each checkpoint)

**CP1 — THE ROOM.** V1: the block's side walls begin at the bunk wall's
back face (`wz0 = BLOCK.z0 + R.wall`); the stripe is gone from every shot
down the room. V2: five louvred windows on the −x wall (`WIN` 1.04 × 0.8
at y 2.25, four slats at 0.55 rad, a frame), pale-sky glass with a little
emissive by day; `setWindows(k)` lerps the glass to the night's blue-black
with `nightK` at lights out, the film's switch (44.2–48.0) and
`setNightRoom`; the snapshot keeps `winK`. Read on both crops by day and

so the pile ring lifted to +22 mm), boots under every bed at the aisle end,

diamond lattice (`makeMesh`, alpha-tested, double-sided) that the film's
last shot and scenes B and D now look up at; photographed from the pillow

with his knees up and his arms behind his head, 0.57–1.10 m off the floor
against a mattress top of 0.55 — no blanket box covers him without burying
him, so the six statues stay uncovered and the two breathing rigs (flat,
0.57–0.93) take the blanket (0.565–0.765, chest to foot). V5: `CLOCK_GLOW`
