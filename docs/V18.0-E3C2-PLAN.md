## 1. What chapter 2 is

S1·07, "How It All Began · Part Two". After his first Sak Yant he kept going
back; business improved, life got smoother — and he became *"extremely
sensitive in spiritual settings. Something in me was waking up."* When he
heard chanting his hands moved by themselves, *"slow and graceful, almost
like the traditional movements of a Thevada"*. Then in temples: *"I would
kneel to pray like everyone else, and sometimes before I could properly
begin, my body would start moving. My upper body would rotate and sway. My
hands would form gestures I did not know."* He was frightened — *"What if it
happens in front of everyone?"*

The episode's axis is CONTROL. Chapter 1 was ASKING (full control). Chapter 2
is **HANDS**: he loses his body's smallest part. The player keeps the camera
and the legs; the HANDS are taken, and the fight is to keep them.

What the case file does NOT say, and the chapter invents (told to Chad, 2 Oct):
the home-speaker scene in the film, the evening setting, the laypeople in
white, the faces that turn (in the text only his fear), the golden figure (in
the text only a comparison). The four options are new; only D is what he did.

   +3 awareness.
3. **"Find a place in the rows and kneel"** — a free mat in the third row
   between two laywomen in white, a soft gold glow on it (the v8.7 zone
   look, gold not red). Entering it kneels him (`kit.pose`), the book stand
   with his book in front, and the hands go to añjali (`kit.pray`).
4. **THE CHANT SETTLES (~20 s).** The monks begin the Itipiso; the hall
   answers. **z2kneel** *"Just chant. Like everyone else. Nobody is looking at
   you."* The objective: **"Chant with the hall"**.
5. **THE HANDS — the minigame** (§7, the RESIST kind). Brief: *"When the chant
   swells, your hands will rise. HOLD to keep them still. LET GO when it
   ebbs, and breathe."* Three swells, each longer and stronger. A slip: the
   hands sweep wider (`kit.hands`), the upper body sways (the lens rolls and
   yaws), the yant warms, the woman to his left turns her head to look, and
   sanity drops. Holding through an ebb (not breathing) costs too — he is
   fighting, not settling. Score by the ladder.
6. **THE PEAK.** Whatever the score, the chant reaches its height and the
   hands go: up out of añjali into a full slow gesture, the upper body
   turning; in the candlelight his SHADOW on the floor ahead has a crown and
   four arms (the Thevada, seen only as that shadow and at the frame's
   edge); both women and the man in front look; presence drains. **z2peak**
   *"Not here. Not in front of everyone."* The decision opens by itself.

## 7. THE ENGINE — what changes

- **The 24th seam, `kit.hands(k, opts)`** — the HANDS TAKEN: both arms (the
  right viewmodel arm and the prayer left arm) moved out of añjali into Thai
  dance gestures by `k` 0..1 (`pose`: 'jeeb' — thumb and index touching, the
  other fingers fanned back; 'wong' — palm open, fingers bent back), with a
  slow travel of the hands up and outward and a drift between the two hands
  so they are never symmetrical. Built from bone rotations on the rig's own
  finger bones (hyperextension = a negative curl about the across axis; the
  pinch is the thumb's and index's tips brought together about the palm
  axis). Eased on wall time; a scene takes the hands back on its first frame;
  kitReset clears it. The fixture proves it (`handsSeam`).
- **The 25th seam, event kind `resist`** — swells laid out in time
  (`swells: [{ at, len, pull }]`), HOLD during a swell, LET GO in the ebb;
  the hands' rise `k` is the event's own state, reported to the chapter each
  frame (`onRise(k)`), and every slip (`onSlip(i)`) and every unbroken hold
  through an ebb (`onHoldBreath`) is graded on the v9.3 ladder. The brief
  carries a `demo`. The fixture declares one.
- STING_SAMPLE rows, ADULT_TAKES / CAST_TAKES names.
- Nothing else. Episodes 1 and 2 call neither seam.
