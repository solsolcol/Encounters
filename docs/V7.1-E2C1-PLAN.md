## 1 · THE CHAPTER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Hawk Company's bunk, day one of Basic Military Training. A double-deck bed
nearest the toilet door — bed one, the one nobody wants. The day: fall in on
the whistle, make the standby bed against the clock, meet the bunk, lights
out. The night: 03:00, the shower in the block next to his bed turns itself
on. Heart in the throat, and a decision lying in the dark. The trial's THE
SOUND: open the door, lie still, whisper to the buddy, or the blanket over
the head. Every choice a cutscene; D ends with the water still running.
**The escalation axis for episode 2 is EXPOSURE** — episode 1 ran DISTANCE →
SMALLNESS → INVERSION → INTRUSION → RELEASE inside one block and one family;
episode 2 strips the family away: a dormitory of strangers, a sound nobody
else reacts to, and a rule (no talking after lights out) that makes the
obvious human answer cost something.

## 8 · THE DAY, STEP BY STEP (play; `kit.setPhase` at each step)

```
phase 'arrive'   spawn; n1voice at 3 s; objective "Find your bed — bed one";
                 waypoint on the bed. Sergeant idle by the door. bunkday bed.
                 → reaching the bed (hotspot 'bed' or within 1.6 m):
phase 'fallin'   whistle; s1fallin; n1fallin; objective "FALL IN on the line";
                 waypoint on the balcony line; kit.timer(14, onLate).
                 Reach the line in time → conduct {a:+4, note:'Fell in on time.'}
                 Late → s1late, n1late, pushups sfx, conduct {s:−3, a:−2,
                 note:'Late to fall in. Push-ups.'}; the game goes on either way.
phase 'standby'  back in the bunk (waypoint on the bed); s1standby; then
                 kit.event({kind:'sequence', label:'STANDBY BED', items: the
                 eight, each:1.3, accel:0.86, minEach:0.5, award:{stat:
                 'awareness', lo:0, hi:8}}).
                 pass → n1bedok, conduct {a:+3, note:'A good standby bed.'}
                 fail → s1again, n1bedfail, conduct {s:−4, note:'The bunk did
                 it again because of you.'}; the sequence runs ONCE more, its
                 result final.
phase 'free'     objective "Look around the bunk before lights out"; hotspots
                 live: SHOWER (the block door — n1shower; walk in, tiles by
                 day), BUDDY (b1day), BOARD (k1board, n1board), BUNKMATE
                 (k1three), BED (lie down / get up).
                 → the bed hotspot while standing after ≥2 hotspots seen, or
                 40 s: LIGHTS OUT.
phase 'lightsout' s1lights; switchoff; n1lights; kit.daylight(NIGHT, 3);
                 the tubes go dark, the fans keep turning; bunkday → bunknight;
                 b1sleep; the sleepers appear in their bunks; kit.pose('lying',
                 {y:0.62, yaw: facing the ceiling}); fade to black 2 s.
phase 'night'    fade up on the ceiling at 03:00 (the clock canvas repainted);
                 n1wake; 4 s; showerrun starts from the block (worldSfx, panned
                 +z); n1hear; kit.presence(0.35);
                 kit.event({kind:'heartbeat', label:'FEAR CONTROL', n:5,
                 bpm:72, win:0.19, award:{stat:'sanity', lo:−8, hi:+2}});
                 on resolve → n1act → startDecision().
                 (the pile = the bed; interacting it in 'night' also opens the
                 decision, so a player who skipped the event by pause still can.)
```

Saved phase: a resume into 'free' or later re-derives the room (lights, beds,
pose) from the phase in `build()`'s `ready()`; earlier phases restart at
'arrive' (the day is a minute long and its timer is not worth saving).

## 10 · THE FOUR SCENES

**A · GET UP AND OPEN THE TOILET DOOR** (~24 s). The pose lifts; steps to the
block door (bootsrun soft); dooropen2; the tiles under a single tube; the
shower running in the far cubicle — camera pushes down the corridor; n1A1;

camera turns back down the corridor and — for ONE frame at 21.0, 20 m off at
the corridor's end — the `ghostsoldier` stands, then is gone (`dread` at

**B · STAY STILL AND LISTEN** (~22 s). Lying, the ceiling, the fan turning
(the camera holds on it); the clock's red glow on the wall; n1B1; showerrun
ducked to the block's distance; 14.0: showeroff — by itself; n1B2; the fan;
a long hold; drip once at 19.5. → card.

**C · WHISPER TO YOUR BUNKMATE** (~18 s). Lying, the head turns (yawTo) to
the next bed; n1C1 (a whisper — `[whispers]`, the v6.9 stage, `WHISPER_TAKES`);
a beat; the buddy's blanket shifts (blanket); b1huh; he rolls over; the
shower runs on; the camera turns back to the ceiling. → card.

**D · IT'S A GHOST — BLANKET OVER YOUR HEAD** (~20 s, `critical`). blanket
at 1.0; the camera drops into weave-dark (a blanket plane 4 cm from the
lens, its underside lit by nothing); n1D1 twice, quieter the second time;
the shower does NOT stop — showerrun stays up, closer (its duck released,
vol up); kit.presence to 0.8 across the scene; boom at 15.0; black. → card.
The engine's faint/chunk logic is untouched: the −15 sanity is the choice's
own delta.
