## 0.1 The plan on one screen

- **The story.** He has his own amulet shop now, and a worry about its future. A dealer he buys from knows "a very
  strong master" in Thailand. Nobody can tell him anything else about the man. In a house among the rice fields, with a
  storm coming, the master is kind, unhurried and dressed in white. His room shows things it does not explain. The
  chapter ends with the case file's own words: *"When I got back to Singapore, I stopped feeling like myself."*
- **What the player learns to do: LOOK.** Ask the ordinary questions, write down what you saw rather than what you
  think it means, and notice only what is really there.
- **Everything in the world is new.** No HDB block, no Golden Mile, no wat, no Chinnarat Buddha, no Thai kit, no tree
  kit, no congregation and no old music. There are four places nobody has seen in this game: his own new shop at dawn,
  the rice country with its sugar palms under a monsoon storm, a master's compound, and a taxi through Singapore at
  night.
- **Three new ways to play.**
  - **ASK**: the people waiting can be questioned. It is the case file's lesson as something you do.
  - **THE LEDGER**: his shop's stock book. Its back pages become what he saw, and every entry is written as a fact or as
    a meaning. It travels with him into chapter 2.
  - **WHAT CHANGED?**: in the storm the lightning is a blink, and you tap only what really changed. Tapping something
    that did not change costs you.
- **The shape.**
  - A 72 s opening film.
  - About 6 minutes of play, in four parts: the yard, the reading, the watching, and the storm.
  - One decision: Master Z's own trial 5.1, verbatim.
  - Four endings, each a whole story of about 70 s.
  - A 20 s coda.
- **The build.** Three milestones:
  - **M0, direction** (CP0–CP1): the research, the script, the mood board and the model list. You approve these.
  - **M1, built and playable** (CP2–CP10): a preview deploy you can play, with captions where voices will go.
  - **M2, sound and release** (CP11–CP14): the voices, effects and music, then a full review and the release as
    v24.0.
- **What I need from you.** The models in §6.1 (Grandmaster Pho and Lersi Chin are chapters 2–3, not this one). Your
  picks on §2.3. Your ear on the voices and the music.

## 3. The direction

### 3.1 The idea

**One image holds the chapter:** a kind man in white, in a small green room full of things for sale, and behind his
shoulder something you can only see when you are not looking at it.

**The spine:** HOPE → MOMENTUM → WELCOME → DOUBT → INTIMACY → UNEASE → STORM → HUSH → (the ending's own key) → the
first grey of the fog. Each beat has its own light (§3.3), its own sound (§3.4) and its own thing to do (§4).

**What he learns:**
- He begins believing that someone being called a master is enough.
- He ends having written, in his own hand, that nobody could tell him who this man is.
- The full lesson only arrives in chapter 2 (Lersi Chin), and the question behind it — *why am I like this?* — only in
  chapter 3 (Pho).
- Chapter 1 plants all three.

**Fairness (Chad's standing rule: no practice and no practitioner is ever the villain).**
- The master is warm, unhurried, competent in his own practice and hospitable. He offers water, asks about the shop
  with real interest, and never pushes an item (the dealer is the eager one, and sincerely so).
- He does nothing that could be read as harm; his blessing is an ordinary blessing.
- What unsettles the room is only what Master Z perceives, and the chapter never says what it means.
- When the player is rude (ending C), the master is gracious.
- When the player asks (ending B), his answer is sincere and his offer is genuine.
- The case file's *"whether you interpret that spiritually or otherwise"* governs every line. Every line of the
  master, the dealer and the wife is read against the rule twice: at CP0, and again at CP13.

### 3.2 Fresh, by rule — and checked, not promised

**Everything the player sees and hears in this chapter is made for it.** The only things carried in are the five that
are not the world:

1. **The engine and its interface**: the HUD, the cards, the bag, the menus, the decision panel, the interface's own
   sounds. This is the game's frame.
2. **His hands** (`arms.glb`): his body, and continuity.
3. **His voice** (Brian): the same man.
4. **The first master's model**: Chad's decision. Players have not seen it since v18.6, and it is re-prepared here
   (§6.3), so it reaches them new.
5. **What he carries in the bag**: his history (the Sak Yant, his amulets).

**Enforced at CP2:**
- `chaptertest` gains a FRESH check: any asset key this chapter loads that another chapter also loads must be on the
  chapter's declared exception list, or the build fails.
- The same check covers sounds: no sample this chapter cues may belong to another chapter's pack.

So "no reuse" is a test, like "Krukai is untouched".

### 3.3 The look

**Palette.** Sugar-palm gold and paddy green under a slate-violet storm deck. Inside the room: faded mint walls under
one fluorescent tube (cool green-white) against candle amber. The two temperatures clash, and the clash is the unease.
The red of the cloths and the white of the master are the only saturated things in the room.

**Lens.**
- **Long lenses in the films** (28–40° vertical, where the game's default is 72°). Compressed space and heat shimmer:
  the outsider looking in.
- **In the room, slow push-ins** and nothing else.
- **No handheld wobble** except in the pickup.

These are deliberate breaks from episode 3's wide frames. Every shot is solved for the phone's centre crop as well as
the desktop (§8).

**Weather is the clock.** The storm deck crosses the sun during play (a daylight tween over the waiting), so the yard
darkens with each number called. The rain arrives with the chant and peaks at WHAT CHANGED?. It stops at once for the
decision.

**The colour script:**

| Beat | Light | Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Shop, dawn (film) | clean white, pale timber, soft daylight under a rising shutter | a new page |
| The road (film) | low gold sun under a violet storm wall; green paddies mirroring it | momentum, an omen |
| The yard, arriving | warm gold, wind rising, long shadows | welcome |
| The yard, waiting | the gold going grey, number by number | doubt |
| The room | mint and fluorescent, candle amber, blue through the louvres | intimacy, then unease |
| The storm | candlelight and black between lightning flashes | the storm |
| The hush | washed silver-blue, dripping eaves, frogs beginning | the decision |
| The coda | Singapore at night (sodium and LED on wet glass), then dawn in the shop, drained grey | the fog begins |

### 3.4 The sound and the score

**A theme of its own for Episode 4.**
- **The instrument is the KHAEN**, the bamboo mouth organ of the north-east: a breathing chord, earthy and strange,
  never heard in this game. Episode 3 lived in the central Thai court sound (piphat, mahori, khim, the bowed fiddle).
  Under it sit a low bowed bass drone and a frame drum.
- **The motif is four notes that rise and stop on the second degree, unresolved.** It is the question he carries: why
  am I like this?
  - It appears in every chapter of the episode.
  - It resolves only in chapter 3, when he meets Pho.
  - In this chapter it never resolves.

**Where music is, and where it is not.**
- **The opening film is scored:** the khaen's breath under the shop, then the theme's first full statement as the
  pickup runs at the storm.
- **The yard has no score.** Only what is there: a radio playing luk thung (Thai country music, instrumental and
  tinny) from the stilt house, a TV soap inside, wind in the palms, the paddies' frogs and insects, thunder.
- **The room has none at all.** Rain on a zinc roof, the wall fan's click, the fluorescent hum, a small fridge, his own
  breath, and the master's chant. Silence is scarier than a score.
- **The storm:** the roof's roar and one low drone rising under WHAT CHANGED?.
- **The hush:** nothing, then frogs.
- **Each ending has its own cue** (A khaen alone; B a curious, unsettled motif; C a tight pulse; D a gentle phin, the
  Isan lute).
- **The coda:** one unresolved khaen chord under the last line.

**The sound palette (all new, §9.2).** Zinc-roof rain at three strengths and its sudden stop, the dry clatter of
sugar-palm fronds in wind, thunder near and far, the paddies at dusk, a pickup's diesel, gravel and doors, a dog, a TV
through a wall, a whiteboard marker, a wall fan, a fluorescent tube's tick and death, the master's mala, a match, a
candle, cloth, and a cheap ballpoint on ledger paper.

### 3.6 The tone rules for this chapter

- No jump scares. Nothing lunges, screams or cuts at the lens.
- The camera never confirms. No shot shows the presence head-on, and no line says what she is.
- Every stretch of 1.5 s or more without a voice is held by a sound with a reason (the v21.30 rule).
- Every ending is a whole story: what he does, how it is answered, what it costs or gives him, and what he
  understands.
- Every master's title is spoken in full ("Ajahn", never "Aj.").
- Pali is used only where it is chanted, and captioned. No dhamma tag goes on the lesson unless Chad names one.

### 4.1 THE COMPOUND (World C — the play world, at the origin)

**One small dense place**, about 40 × 30 m, walkable end to end. The paddies and palms run to a painted horizon past
the fence.

**The lane and the gate.** Red earth with puddles; two concrete gate posts; the sign; the pickup parked; four more
cars under a big tree.

**The old house on stilts** (left): teak, a steep gable, plank walls, a veranda. In the open space under it:
- a bamboo bench and a hammock
- water jars (ong)
- a motorbike
- a fridge
- laundry on a line
- a television playing a Thai soap (heard)
- chillies drying on a mat

**The waiting sala** (centre): an open wooden pavilion with two benches and a whiteboard of queue numbers ("NOW 9"). A
water dispenser with paper cups and a standing fan. The three waiting people sit here, with the soi dog lying under it.

**The receiving room** (right): a newer concrete room with a zinc roof, louvred glass windows and one door at the top
of three steps, with sandals by it (NICE; otherwise none).

**The takhian tree:** a big tree with women's dresses and coloured cloths hung on its trunk and a small shrine at its
foot. This is the folk custom for the female spirit believed to live in takhian trees. It is shown respectfully and
never commented on, and it plants "not everyone who stays here is a person" before the room does.

**The compound's own spirit house:** weathered concrete on a pillar, a different style from anything in episode 3.

**Light:** the yard presets (§10.2) tween from gold to grey through the waiting.

  ask."*).

  last.

**P3 · `called`.**

  - Pong: *"He ask what day you were born."*

### 4.4 PLAY III — THE WORK (`work`)

**The rite begins.**
- The master lights two candles with a match, takes up his mala, closes his eyes and begins to chant: a business
  blessing, chosen and sourced at CP0, captioned in Pali over its meaning.
- **The rain arrives on the zinc roof** at the first line, light, then heavier.

**Objective:** *Watch. Write down what you see · 0/4.*

**The four signs.** These are the trial's practitioner, presence and ritual object, made into things a room does. Each
is a press-aimed look-spot (the twenty-eighth seam: E, the badge, or a tap on the mark or the thing). Each is followed
by a ledger entry on its second page, WHAT I SAW.

1. **The smoke.** The incense smoke leans toward the covered bowl, against the wall fan's air.
   - He: *"The smoke… it's leaning toward that bowl. The fan's blowing the other way."*
   - FACT *"The smoke leaned toward the covered bowl, against the fan."* / MEANING *"Something under the cloth was
     pulling at it."*
2. **The cloth.** The red cloth over the bowl rises and falls once, like a breath.
   - He: *"That cloth… did it just move?"*
   - FACT *"The cloth over the bowl moved."* / MEANING *"He keeps something alive under there."*
3. **The shadow.** On the mint wall, his shadow from the candles moves a beat after he does (§7.4).
   - He: *"His shadow… it moved after he did."*
   - FACT *"His shadow moved after he did."* / MEANING *"His shadow isn't his own."*
4. **Her.** At the edge of the frame, a woman stands behind his left shoulder by the curtained doorway: still, head
   bowed.
   - Turning to her fades her out, so by the time the reticle arrives nobody is there (§7.3). The press is made at an
     empty doorway.
   - He: *"Someone's standing behind him— …No. There's no one."*
   - FACT *"Someone stood behind him. When I looked, no one was there."* / MEANING *"He keeps a spirit that works for
     him."*

The four can be found in any order; the order counts them. The master chants through all of it and never looks up.

### 4.5 PLAY IV — THE STORM (`storm`): WHAT CHANGED?

**The lead-in.**
- At the fourth sign the storm breaks fully. The fluorescent tube ticks, buzzes and dies; only the two candles are
  left.
- Pong, nervous: *"Power cut. Normal, normal — every time rain."*
- The master's chant does not stop.

**The event** (S44, kind `change`; its picture is §5).
- **Brief:** *"The lightning shows the room. Remember it. When it flashes again, tap only what has changed. If nothing
  has changed, tap nothing."*
- **Five flashes.** Each is lit for about 2.5 s, then dark for about 1.5 s, and the changes happen in the dark:

| Flash | What has changed | Its lesson |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | nothing: the baseline — remember the room | — |
| 2 | his water cup has moved along the table | an ordinary change: notice it |
| 3 | the red cloth lies differently, and the clock has stopped | two at once |
| 4 | she is one step closer, at the edge of the frame | the one that matters, and only peripheral |
| 5 | nothing, and the thunder is the loudest yet | fear makes everything look different; tap nothing |

**Scoring:**
- A correct tap: +3 awareness.
- A false tap: −3 sanity, and the place he tapped flashes red. That is the lesson exactly: certainty moving ahead of
  the evidence. (Never Wisdom: play moves Sanity and Awareness only.)
- A miss: −1 awareness.
- A minigame decrease is cut by a worn `evGuard` item as always.

**The end of the storm.**
- After the fifth flash the rain stops all at once. A second of silence, the roof ticking as it cools, drips.
- The master opens his eyes and waits, with the kind face he had at the start.

### 4.6 THE DECISION (`decide`)

**It opens by itself, in the hush.** Prompt: *"The rain has stopped. He is waiting for you."*

The trial's 5.1, verbatim:

| | Option | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Observe quietly before reacting** | good |
| B | **Ask directly about what you notice** | bad |
| C | **Assume the master is dangerous** | worst, critical |
| D | **Leave without getting more involved** | best |

### 4.7 THE FOUR ENDINGS (each a whole story, ≈ 65–80 s)

#### A · STILL — he observes and decides nothing

1. He lowers his eyes and keeps still: his breath, the drips.
2. The master finishes the blessing. He leans forward and blows three times onto the crown of his head (the pao), and
   the hands come up in a wai.
3. **The master** (Thai), then **Pong:** *"He say your shop will be busy. Put the [item] facing the door."* The line
   follows whichever piece he took.
4. He looks round the room once, and every sign has settled: the smoke straight, the cloth still, the doorway empty.
   He does not decide what any of it was.
5. He opens the ledger and writes one more line: *"I don't know what I saw."*
6. Outside, the yard is washed and the paddies are mirrors, with a gold seam at the horizon. Pa Noi presses a bag of
   mangoes on him.
7. In the pickup, Pong: *"Strong, right?"* He: *"…Maybe."*
8. **VO:** *"I kept what I saw as what I saw. I didn't decide what it meant."*
9. The music: the khaen alone.

#### B · THE QUESTION — he asks, and is seen

1. He, to Pong, low: *"Pong… ask him who's standing behind him."*
2. Pong freezes: *"Behind? …Okay, I ask."* He asks, quietly, in Thai.
3. The master does not turn round. He looks at the player for a long time (the cast's look, v5.03), then smiles and
   answers.
4. **Pong**, uneasy: *"He say… she has been in this house longer than him. He say not many people can see her."*
5. A beat. **Pong:** *"He ask — who teach you?"* He: *"…Nobody."*
6. **Pong:** *"He say you should come back. He can teach you to use it."* He: *"Thank you, Ajahn. Maybe another
   time."* The master's smile does not change.
7. At the door, Pa Noi puts a card with the master's number into his hand: *"Ajahn say, call anytime, ka."*
8. In the pickup, Pong is thrilled: *"Brother! You can SEE? Why you never tell me!"* He turns the card over in his
   fingers.
9. **VO:** *"I'd asked to understand… and shown a man I didn't know what I could see."*
10. The music: the curious motif.

#### C · CERTAIN — fear decides first

1. His heart takes over (`kit.pulse`). The room tilts a degree, and every sign joins up into one story at once: the
   bowl, the shadow, her.
2. He (inner): *"He's doing something to me."*
3. He gets up mid-blessing, too fast; the cup tips over.
4. The master stops, surprised, and opens his hands, calm. **Pong:** *"He say… go well. The rain is still heavy."*
5. He backs out without a wai, leaves the piece on the mat and steps into his shoes wrong. Across the yard in the
   last of the rain the dog barks, and Pa Noi calls after him with an umbrella.
6. At the pickup, Pong catches up, embarrassed: *"Brother, what happen? He is a good man!"* He: *"Just drive."*
7. On the road, every dark shape is her: the wing mirror, the black windscreen, a woman at a roadside stall turning
   round. She is only a woman at a stall; the camera lets that be seen.
8. **VO:** *"I was certain before I knew anything. And the certainty came home with me."*
9. The music: the tight pulse.

#### D · ENOUGH — he knows when to go

1. He waits for the master to finish the chant he began (courtesy), wais, and speaks through Pong: *"Thank you,
   Ajahn. This is enough for today."*
2. He takes nothing more, and his offering stays on the tray. The master nods, unoffended, and returns the wai.
3. Outside, Pong is puzzled: *"Finish already? He haven't even done the full one!"* He: *"It's okay. Let's wait out
   the rain over there."*
4. They sit at the roadside shelter across the lane. The compound's lights sit across the wet paddy, frogs begin, and
   the storm clears to a sliver of dusk gold.
5. He writes in the ledger: *"Some things I don't need to understand today."*
6. **VO:** *"I didn't need to understand it to know it was time to go."*
7. The music: the phin.

## 5. Engine work

All of it is opt-in. Episodes 1–3 never reach it, and the fixture proves each seam.

**S43 · THE LEDGER — `kit.ledger`.**
- **Calls:**
  - `write(id, { page, prompt, fact, meaning })` answers a Promise of `'fact' | 'meaning'`.
  - `entries()` and `open(page)` read it back.
- **The book:**
  - A 3D book held up in his hands (a new `handPose`, `'ledger'`), its pages drawn live on a canvas in a handwriting
    face. The face is self-hosted through `tools/fontsnap.mjs`, which the build asserts.
  - The two sentences are drawn on the page, and a tap or click on either inks it with a pen sound.
  - Tap targets are the lines' own rectangles in screen space (the `hits` shape).
- **The save:** the run state carries the ledger to the end of the episode. It is cleared by New game, and a resume
  restores it.
- **In the bag:** the ledger is an item (`ledger`), and its view opens the book.
- **Fallback** (Q6): a flat page over the screen.
- **Fixture:** write, read back, save, resume, and a tap on each line.

**S44 · `change` — WHAT CHANGED?**
- **The chapter supplies:**
  - `spots` (id, a world point or a `hits` test)
  - `rounds` (the ids that change in each)
  - `apply(round)` (alters the world in the dark)
- **The engine supplies:**
  - the flash (`kit.flash` and a daylight spike on the lights that already exist, so no new light count — v15.1)
  - the dark, the tap window, and a ring on a right tap or a red mark on a wrong one
  - the grading (`hit`, `miss` and `falseCost`, through `evCut`)
  - **its picture**: a small room that blinks, one shape moving, a finger tapping it; then a blink where nothing moves
    and the finger holds back. That is a new `EV_DEMO` row and class, as the minigame rule requires.
- **Fixture:** a right tap scores, a false tap costs, a round with no change and no tap is perfect.

**S45 · WEATHER — `kit.rain(k, { secs })` and `kit.lightning({ power, near })`.**
- **Rain** is instanced streaks round the lens: unlit, a phone budget, `LOW` thinner. There are splashes on named
  ground planes and droplets running down named glass.
- **Lightning** is a hemisphere/key spike plus a white wash, and the thunder follows by the distance.
- `kitReset` clears both.
- **Fixture:** rain mounts and is gone after reset.

**S46 · TREE KITS — `plantTrees(parent, spots, { kit })`.**
- A chapter can plant its own vegetation (sugar palms, bananas, bamboo) through the same instancing, per-tree
  culling, `lowKeep` and `planted` record (v16.5).
- Every existing stand keeps the default kit, so nothing else moves.

**S47 · PICK — `kit.pick(options, opts)`.**
- A small window of plain options (a word, a colour) answering a Promise: the day of birth.
- **Picture:** none needed (it is a question, not a minigame).
- **Fixture:** pick and resume.

**S48 · A PHONE IN A FILM — `api.phone(messages | call)`.**
- A phone screen over a film: chat bubbles typed in on the film's clock, or a call screen. It is a pure function of
  the clock, so seek- and skip-safe (v19.5's law).
- Episode 5 needs it for case five's video call, and this film uses it for Pong's message.

**Not seams (chapter-side):** her edge-of-sight fade (§7.3), the lagging shadow (§7.4), the breathing cloth, the
leaning smoke, the queue montage.

## 14. Risks, and what guards each

1. **Twenty-odd models to find.** Every one is invisible until real, so the chapter is built and played with whatever
   has landed; MUST first. If a few never come, the scene is designed to stand without them.
2. **Thai voices.** If no Thai voice is good enough, Q2's fallback is simple English for the master, and Pong keeps his
   role as the man who brought him.
3. **Rain, palms and paddies on a phone.** Instancing, per-instance culling, `LOW` thinning and the curtain's warm,
   all measured at CP5.
4. **Lighting compiles in the storm.** No new lights: the lightning drives the existing ones, and every state is
   warmed (v15.1).
5. **Her fade on narrow screens.** Keyed in screen space (§7.3) and photographed on both screens. If dithered alpha
   reads as noise on a phone, an opaque fade by distance is the fallback.
6. **The ledger's legibility on a phone.** Sized for the phone first; the flat page is the fallback (Q6).
7. **The fairness line.** The master, Pong and Pa Noi are read twice against the rule (CP0, CP13). The chapter never
   tells the player what anything means.
8. **Concept frames promising more than the engine gives.** They are targets for palette and composition, said so when
   they are sent.
