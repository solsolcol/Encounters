## 2 · The intended flow, written down

Arrive (find bed one) → whistle, 14 s fall-in on the balcony line →
standby bed: eight items against a tightening clock → free time (the
block, the buddy, the board, the bunkmate) → lights out, lie down →
03:00, the shower turns itself on in the block, a heartbeat to hold →
the decision from the pillow. Four scenes; four card lines.

**The time of day, made coherent (CP1):** the film's first line says
"seven in the morning" and the wall clock in play says **21:58**. So the
film crosses a DAY — morning arrival, the bunk, bed one, then dusk
falling over the bunk with the tubes still on — and play begins in the
EVENING: lit inside, dark outside, before lights out. The film must NOT
switch the lights off (that is play's own lights-out phase); it hands
back an evening, not a morning. The chapter's declared `daylight`
becomes that evening. The cast standing in a lit bunk at 21:58 is then
correct, not a bug.
