## CP2 — retargeting: the math works, one rig still fails

`tools/retarget.mjs`. **This is not v5.02's bake.** That one renamed the
clip's nodes and was done, because the mother's rig WAS a Mixamo rig and
glTF had only sanitized the names. gracy_lee is ValveBiped and
fearful_woman is a Blender rig: two rigs can pose a body identically and
still hold their bones at different REST orientations, so copying local
rotation numbers across copies the numbers and loses the meaning. The tool
retargets in world space, the only frame the two rigs agree on:

    Dw(t) = Ws(t) · Wsb⁻¹      the delta the SOURCE bone turned through
    Wt(t) = Dw(t) · Wtb        the same delta from the TARGET's rest
    Rt(t) = Wtp(t)⁻¹ · Wt(t)   back to local, under the target parent's
                               already-retargeted world rotation

Parents are solved before children, so the joints are walked in hierarchy
order. Rotations only — a clip's translations belong to the source's bone
lengths and would tear a differently-proportioned skeleton apart.

### The open problem: fearful_woman

Her left arm renders as a flat stretched shard and her face smears, while
her dress and torso are correct. Two things are already ruled out by
measurement rather than guessed:

- **Not the simplification.** Retargeted onto her UN-simplified 350k
  original the mesh is a clean woman, and re-shrinking at 0.10 (35k, three
  times the geometry of the first attempt) tears identically.
- **Not the retargeting math**, which produced a correct gracy_lee from
  the same take through the same code path.

So it is something specific to her rig. The strongest candidate, untested:
`restWorld()` composes rotation only, and her Blender export has **eight
skins** and may carry non-uniform or negative SCALE on the armature or on
joint nodes — under which a rotation-only world composition is simply
wrong, and would be wrong exactly for the bones furthest down a scaled
chain (arm, face) while a short chain (spine, hips) survives. Next step is
to measure the node scales in her file before changing any code.
