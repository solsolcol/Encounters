### tools/retarget.mjs — real retargeting, and where it stops

**Not v5.02's bake.** That renamed the clip's nodes and was done, because
the mother's rig WAS Mixamo and glTF had only sanitized the names.
gracy_lee is ValveBiped: two rigs can pose a body identically and still
hold their bones at different REST orientations, so copying local rotations
copies the numbers and loses the meaning. This works in world space, the
only frame the two rigs agree on:

    Dw(t) = Ws(t) · Wsb⁻¹      the delta the SOURCE bone turned through
    Wt(t) = Dw(t) · Wtb        the same delta from the TARGET's rest
    Rt(t) = Wtp(t)⁻¹ · Wt(t)   back to local, under the target parent's
                               already-retargeted world rotation

Parents solved before children. Rotations only — a clip's translations
belong to the source's bone lengths and would tear a differently
proportioned skeleton apart.

It is proven on gracy_lee and **it is not needed anywhere else**, which is
the lesson: reach for a character's OWN clips first, and retarget only
when there is nothing else. A bought character usually ships takes that
are better than anything retargeted onto it, because they were authored
against its own rest pose.
