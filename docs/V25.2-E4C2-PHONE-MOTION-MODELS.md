## 1. The four asks

- **A1 · THE PHONE.** Everything a shot or a play view is about is seen on a 390×844 phone, in the opening film, the call,
  the visit, the examination, the clearing, the four endings and the coda, and in play (day, night, the clearing, the
  decision). Method (CLAUDE.md, camera rules): each shot NAMES the things that must be in frame and solves its lens and
  distance for the phone's aspect (a portrait phone sees about a third of a desktop's width); measured in NDC on the phone
  through the film's own camera, then photographed. Play: what each objective sends him to is inside the phone's view from
  where he stands, and nothing the HUD covers is the thing being asked for.
- **A2 · LERSI CHIN'S MOVEMENT.** The wai, taking up the water bowl, the water flicked from the mayom, the reach to his
  wrist, the thread, the packing up, every pose change: natural poses (elbows low and in, wrists turned as a hand turns,
  his head and shoulders taking part) and natural timing (no pose arriving in a frame: every change eased in and out over
  a human time, overlapping, with the hold and settle a body has). Judged by dense frame sequences (every 0.2 s) from a
  review camera on him, phone and desktop, before and after.
- **A3 · ONE TELLING.** The four-card pick ("Tell Lersi Chin — What happened there?") goes. After "Tell me what happened
  there." the player presses once, Tell him what you saw, and says what he saw (q2tA: the smoke, the cloth, the shadow, the
  one behind him), and Lersi Chin answers (lc2tA: "Good. You tell me what you saw, not what you think..."). Its awareness
  and its note stay (TELL_A.A 9, note52A). Old saves that told B, C or D load and go on as told. The three unused pairs of
  takes (q2tB–D, lc2tB–D), the pick's words and notes leave the game, their takes kept in masters (the retire rule).
- **A4 · REAL PROPS.** Every prop the player sees close that was built in code and does not read as the thing — the
  wallet, his jacket, his bag, and every other one the audit finds (§3) — made his way: a reference picture on white
  (GPT Image at its best), LOOKED AT, then Meshy image-to-3D at its best settings and its remesh, sources and job IDs
  into masters/v25.2 the day they are made, prepared to real metres, placed where the code-built one stood (fitted to
  the dimension the chapter's numbers depend on), photographed where the player sees it. The code-built one stays only as
  the stand-in drawn if the file never lands.
