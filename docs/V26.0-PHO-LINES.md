# Grandmaster Pho's lines, for your yes

Episode 4 chapter 3. These are drafts: tell me the number of any line to change, cut or re-word. The case file
(S1·12) says what he taught, not his words, so every line here was written for him; lines 7 to 12 each stand on a
sentence of the case file ("to help others", "remember the order these things go in", "What he taught me first was
control", "without control it will overwhelm you and it will hurt you", "With control you can open and close at
will", "Learning when to open myself and when to close"). Every line is on screen as a caption in the preview.


**He receives him (play)**

1. Sit. Ton tells me you carry questions. Put them down here.

**The answers (film)**

2. When it takes your hands, you are awake. You watch it happen. You did not tell me that part.
3. That is what frightens you. Not the moving. The watching.
4. You ask why. What you feel was not put into you. It was there already, asleep.
5. The ink, the chanting, the pieces you held... they did not give it to you. They woke it.
6. The rest of that answer is heavy. I will give it when you can carry it.
7. What is it for? A lamp is not lit for the lamp. It is lit for whoever is in the room.
8. And how to use it... you ask that first. It comes last.
9. First, control.
10. To feel is not enough. A door that cannot close is not a door. It is a hole in the wall, and everything walks through.
11. With control you open when you choose. You close when you choose.
12. When to open. When to close. That is the whole practice.

**Open and close (play)**

13. I show you. Breathe with me.
14. Open.
15. Close.
16. Again. This time you choose when.
17. Once more. It will pull. Close anyway.
18. Close. *(only if he lets it run too long / never opens)*
19. You did not open. Do not be afraid of it. *(only if he lets it run too long / never opens)*
20. Good. Now it is yours to open, and yours to close.

**The road (play)**

21. This is a path. It does not end this morning.
22. First you learn to hold it. That is years.
23. Then you use it for others. Not for yourself.

**Ending 1 · Accept your path**

24. You say yes quickly. Yes to what?
25. Good. Remember the order when it is slow. Slow is where people leave.

**Ending 2 · Challenge the path**

26. Nobody asks.
27. Tell me again how you came to this mat. Slowly.
28. Who carried you?
29. A man who walked here can walk on.

**Ending 3 · Ask why**

30. Why me... that question has no floor. A man can fall in it his whole life.
31. Ask a better one. For whom?
32. What you have is for them. Not for you. One day. Not yet. First you hold it.

**Ending 4 · Ask to make it stop**

33. You are tired of it. I understand.
34. I can teach you to close. I cannot take the door away.
35. Close. And this time, stay.
36. Fear never closed it. You did.

**The close (all four endings)**

37. Yesterday you would not kneel to a man you did not know. Good. Keep that.
38. It is not the flowers you give a teacher. It is your trust. Give that slowly.
39. Tomorrow we begin.
