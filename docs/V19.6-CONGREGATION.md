## Applying the two takes (C3)

`tools/retarget.mjs take.glb character.glb out.glb CLIP meshy --align --hips`
— two opt-in flags added at v19.6 (without them the tool is unchanged):

- `--align` — **two rigs at different rest poses.** The tool applies "how
  far the source bone turned from ITS rest" to the target's rest, which is
  only right when both rests are the same body pose; from Mixamo's T-pose onto
  Meshy's A-pose every arm came out 45° off. With the flag, each target bone's
  rest is first swung onto the source rest's bone direction (bone → its mapped
  child, world space, from the files' own TRS chains); the bone's animated
  direction then equals the source's, its twist stays its own.
- `--hips` — the hips' translation in WORLD space: the source's offset from
  its rest, scaled by the two rigs' measured rest hip heights, added to the
  target's rest and brought into the target's parent frame. Without it a
  kneeling body stays at standing height. Measured: rest 0.93 → kneeling 0.47.

Then `tools/prepmonk.mjs` (the v16.8 Meshy recipe: skin weights diffused so a
bending arm does not shear the cloth, normals shared across UV seams, the
metal-roughness sheet dropped, roughness 0.78) at two cuts, with a new opt-in
`TEX` for the sheet size:

| cut | ratio | triangles | sheets | size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| crowd | 0.018 | 10.7k (woman 2 floors at 20.8k) | 1024 base colour, no normal map | 353–626 KB |
| close (the four who act) | 0.08 | 47–48k | 2048 base colour + normal | 1.35–1.72 MB |

Both takes ride in every file (`pray`, `kneel`). Photographed praying, all
eight: clean, no tearing, hands together at the chest (they have no finger
bones, so the palms meet as wholes — which reads as prayer).

## v19.7 — the review's two findings, fixed

- **The slide.** 'pray' and 'kneel' root the body ~22 cm apart (each was
  retargeted from its own file), so a head turn's crossfade skated the
  kneeler across the mat. mkSitter reads each take's knee midpoint alone and
  animTick moves the model by the weighted difference (`kneelOff`, `root0`).
  Measured on a phone through a full turn and back: 1 mm.
- **The doubles.** The seats are solved (balanced seeded deal + swap search
  over a 1/d² neighbour graph, role places weighted 3×): 0 same-model pairs
  within 1.6 m on desktop and phone, down from 5. `stage.seatStats()`.
- Deploy `6ad282b7e556b6636da1bb5f`, byte-verified (216 of 216).
