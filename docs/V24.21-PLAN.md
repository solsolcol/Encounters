## §2 The plan for what is open

**v24.21 — the drivers, the lens, the hand-over, the taxi's seats**
- **8d, the hand-over (found by reading the engine):** a film that hands over to the chapter card keeps its black only if
  it says it ENDS on black — episode 1–3's films set `c.endFade = 1` beside `c.keepFade = true`; this one set only
  `keepFade`, so on its last frame the engine set the black to 0 (`cineEnd`: `cineFadeEl.style.opacity = c.endFade`) and
  the yard showed through, snapped to where play begins, until the card's 0.9 s fade covered it. And its music (75 s,
  started at 0.4) was cut in 0.3 s at the film's end. Fix: `endFade = 1`, and the music brought down with the picture.
  Checked by scanning every chapter's films: this was the only one (episode 3 chapter 5's possession film ends unfaded on
  purpose).
- **8b:** Pong at the wheel was a child of the cabin's root, which is shown only for shots FROM inside — so every shot
  of the moving pickup from outside had an empty driver's seat. He rides the pickup itself now, shown whenever a film
  drives it, hidden when it is parked for play.
- **8a:** the taxi uncle at the wheel (front right, as Singapore drives): one of the game's own people wearing the role
  until Chad's model (the placeholder rule), seated on the driver's seat; listed in E4-MODELS as NEEDED.
- **8c:** every shot from inside the pickup and the taxi on a wider lens — measured on a phone and a desktop by
  photograph, the aim moved where a wider frame shows roof or floor instead of the road, the driver and the windscreen.
- **7a, the taxi:** its two front seats are a real model (a reference picture of a taxi's seat on white, Meshy), in place
  of the drawn backs.

**v24.22 — the shop**
- **9a:** real amulets, each made from photographs of real ones (the standing rule): Phra Somdej, Phra Khun Paen, a rian
  (a monk's coin medal), Phra Pidta, Jatukham, a meed mor; Buddha images; his own takrut, pha yant and Nang Kwak (Chad's
