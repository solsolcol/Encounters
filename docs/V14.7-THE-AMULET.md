## 2 · THE DESIGN

### 2.1 The amulet in the world (chapter 3)
On the paper table's near (south, +z) end, facing the east aisle the player
walks down: a small red-cloth display cushion and the amulet standing on it,
face to the aisle. Its gold is made to GLOW (emissive from its own colour
map, pulsing) with a warm halo sprite behind it and a few motes rising — and
the engine's `!` hotspot marker over it. A waypoint diamond on it while it is
the objective. Scale is set by photograph from the player's approach (the
real thing is ~5 cm; it must read at 2 m).

### 2.2 The flow (all DERIVED per frame from the bag, v11.6's law)
1. Play opens: objective **"Take the amulet on the auntie's table"** + waypoint.
   The medium is LOCKED: `inView()`, `hits()` and `interact()` answer false and
   his mark and ring stay dark until the amulet is worn.
2. Pickup (E, the badge, or a tap/click ON THE AMULET — new optional hotspot
   `hits(x, y)`): `kit.give('amulet')`, the table copy hides, the engine's
   **Item Unlocked** splash opens with its sting, and 0.9 s later the auntie
   says her line on her talking take, turning toward the boy.
3. Splash closed → the bag button pulses (`kit.give` already does this) and
   the objective turns (COMPLETE beat) to **"Open your bag and equip the
   amulet"**.
4. Equipped in the AMULET box (the neck slot) → COMPLETE → **"Watch the ritual
   at the altar"**; the medium unlocks; the objective clears when the decision
   opens.
5. Unequipping it before the medium re-locks him and asks again, WITHOUT a
   COMPLETE beat (`{ complete: false }` — the v8.7 lie).
6. A replay or resume that already WEARS the amulet has no flow at all (the
   chapter plays exactly as it did before v14.7); one that carries it unworn
   starts at step 3. A reset puts nothing back — the amulet is the player's
   (the bag carries across chapters and replays by design; restart() never
   touches the inventory, v12.3).

### 2.3 The ward (the engine; `ITEM_DEFS.amulet.ward = 15`)
- One charge pool `ward = { charge, ep, enter }`. The charge belongs to an
  EPISODE: entering a chapter of a different episode than `ward.ep` refills it
  to the maximum (setChapter, and applyState for a save written at a boundary).
- Every sanity decrease listed in §1 goes through `sanityLoss(n)`, which takes
  what it can from the charge first and returns the rest — only while a ward
  item is WORN. Unworn = no effect, charge kept (re-wearing does not refill).
- A faint rewinds the charge to `ward.enter` (its value when the chapter was
  entered), exactly as the faint rewinds the stats; Retry keeps that.
- `newGame()` refills it. It rides the save (`ward: { charge, ep, enter }`,
  tolerated absent).
- It improves the chapter's score only by the sanity it saved (the rank
  formula reads sanity) — that is the protection working, not a side effect.

### 2.4 The HUD bar
Yellow segment `#bArm` inside the sanity track, right after the red. The bar's
total length never changes: red = min(sanity, 100 − ward), yellow = ward, so at
full sanity the last 15 % of the red is yellow; as the ward is spent the yellow
narrows and the red underneath shows. A small yellow `+15` beside the sanity
number while it is active. Nothing is drawn when no ward is worn or it is spent
(episode 1 before chapter 3 is pixel-identical to v14.6).

### 2.5 The outcome card
The sanity row's track gains the same yellow segment, animated with the red;
a yellow chip **"Amulet −N"** when the ward took part of the choice's cost.
Rows without a ward are unchanged.

### 2.6 The splash — "Item unlocked" (engine verb `kit.unlock(id)`)
A semi-opaque dark takeover with slowly turning gold rays behind the item, the
label ITEM UNLOCKED in the sodium small caps, the item's name in the display
serif, the item's MODEL turning in the middle (the `iv` renderer), "Added to
your bag", and a **Close** button bottom-centre. Its own sting (`itemunlock`,
new). A new screen state `unlock`, so every "is it play?" gate closes; the
pointer lock is released while it is up and taken back on close (desktop);
Enter/Space/Esc/E close it after a 0.6 s guard (the E that picked it up must
not also close it).

### 2.7 The inventory
`amulet: { icon 'e-amulet', slot 'neck', art 'iconamulet', model 'amulet',
ward 15 }`. The description pane and the zoom window carry a status line:
"Protection left: N of 15" or "Spent — recharges in the next episode".

### 2.8 Sounds and words
- `v3aunt6` — the auntie (Alice, `Xb7hH8MSUJpSbSDYk0k2`, eleven_v3), his words
  exactly; picked by measurement, level-matched to her four shipped takes.
- `itemunlock` — the sting (ElevenLabs SFX), shared pack, warmed by the engine.
- Strings (the sheet): the item's name/desc (his words), `unlock.*`,
  `inv.wardLeft`/`inv.wardSpent`, `card.ward`, and chapter 3's words
  (`takeAmulet`, `objAmulet`, `objEquip`, `objAltar`; `act`/`actTouch` made
  neutral so the boot hint stops naming the altar).

### 2.9 What is deliberately NOT changed
- Episode 2 chapter 3's torch pickup keeps its flow (no splash) — the splash is
  an engine verb any chapter can call; offer it to Chad for the torch.
- No credit row for the model until Chad says where it came from.
