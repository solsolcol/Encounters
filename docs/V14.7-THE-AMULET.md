## 2 · THE DESIGN

### 2.1 The amulet in the world (chapter 3)
On the paper table's near (south, +z) end, facing the east aisle the player
walks down: a small red-cloth display cushion and the amulet standing on it,
face to the aisle. Its gold is made to GLOW (emissive from its own colour
map, pulsing) with a warm halo sprite behind it and a few motes rising — and
the engine's `!` hotspot marker over it. Scale is set by photograph from the
player's approach (the real thing is ~3 cm; it must read at 2 m).
AS SHIPPED (CP3): `AM_H` 0.105 m, leaning back 0.22 rad on a 3 × 5.5 cm easel
hidden behind it; the halo 0.19–0.24 m (the first, 0.40 m additive halo blew
her cream paper stacks out to white in the photograph from the aisle); the `!`
at y 0.95 + `markY` 0.14 so it stands over the amulet, not the stack behind.
NO waypoint: the engine's `!` IS the exclamation mark Chad asked for, and it
is drawn from the spawn (13.3 m, inside `HOTMARK_FAR` 16 — measured: one mark
on the phone's first frame of play); a second diamond on the same point would
be clutter.

### 2.2 The flow (all DERIVED per frame from the bag, v11.6's law)
1. Play opens: objective **"Take the amulet on the auntie's table"**.
   The medium is LOCKED: `inView()`, `hits()` and `interact()` answer false and
   his mark and ring stay dark until the amulet is worn.
2. Pickup (E, the badge, or a tap/click ON THE AMULET — the hotspot's `hits`,
   through the engine's `hotspotTap`): `kit.give('amulet')`, the table copy
   hides, the engine's **Item Unlocked** splash opens with its sting, and
   0.9 s later the auntie says her line on her talking take, turned toward
   the boy, then turns back. The turn and the take are timed from the moment
   the LINE STARTS, not from the pickup (a stall at the first splash on a slow
   box used to spend the beat before her voice began).
3. Splash closed → the objective turns (COMPLETE beat) to **"Open your bag and
   equip the amulet"**, the bag button pulsing (`kit.give`/`kit.urge`).
4. Equipped in the AMULET box (the neck slot) → OBJECTIVE COMPLETE and the
   objective CLEARS. There is no "watch the ritual" order after it (the first
   plan had one): episode 1 never had objectives, and once the amulet is worn
   chapter 3 plays exactly as it always did — the altar's own mark and ring
   come on, which is what pointed at it since v4.1.
5. Unequipping it before the medium re-locks him and asks again, WITHOUT a
   COMPLETE beat (`{ complete: false }` — the v8.7 lie). This found an ENGINE
   quirk (fixed, §4 CP3): the objective box hid still holding "OBJECTIVE
   COMPLETE" and slid those stale words out before the next order.
6. A RESET — any fresh run of chapter 3: Retry, a replay from the selector,
   arriving from chapter 2 by Continue — takes the amulet back and she gives
   it again (e2c3's torch precedent, v11.6; the v8.1 law in the bag's form).
   This REVERSES the first plan ("a reset puts nothing back"): with the amulet
   kept, a replay showed an empty cushion and no flow, which reads as broken.
   A RESUME (Continue from the title) is not a reset: it restores the bag as
   saved — worn → no flow at all, carried → step 3, none → step 1.
7. If the decision or a scene opens while she is still turned to him, she is
   put back at her table on that frame and the beat ends (a scene's `snap()`
   must never record her mid-turn).

### 2.3 The ward (the engine; `ITEM_DEFS.amulet.ward = 15`)
- One charge pool `ward = { charge, ep, enter }`. The charge belongs to an
  EPISODE: entering a chapter of a different episode than `ward.ep` refills it
  to the maximum (setChapter, and applyState for a save written at a boundary).
- Every sanity decrease listed in §1 goes through `sanityLoss(n)`, which takes
  what it can from the charge first and returns the rest — only while a ward
  item is WORN. Unworn = no effect, charge kept (re-wearing does not refill).
- A faint rewinds the charge to `ward.enter` (its value when the chapter was
  entered), exactly as the faint rewinds the stats; Retry keeps that.
- `newGame()` refills it. It rides the save (`ward: { charge, ep, enter }`,
  tolerated absent).
- It improves the chapter's score only by the sanity it saved (the rank
  formula reads sanity) — that is the protection working, not a side effect.

### 2.4 The HUD bar
DECIDED (CP2, after weighing three readings of "the total length of the
sanity bar should not be changed at all ... you cut out part of the red bar to
replace it with this yellow armour effect"): the red `#bSan` is drawn EXACTLY
as today (width = sanity %), and a yellow `#bArm` is laid OVER its right end —
from (sanity − ward) % to sanity %. So the bar's total length is always the
sanity value, with or without the amulet; the last 15 units of the red ARE the
yellow. When the ward absorbs a hit, sanity (the total) does not move and the
yellow narrows (the red reclaims it) — the bar shows the truth: the armour took
it. Once it is spent the bar is plain red again, identical to no amulet.
Rejected: (A) red = min(s, 100 − w) with yellow after it — additive at low
sanity, carved at high, inconsistent; (B) everything scaled by 115 — the red
could never reach the end of the track again while a spent amulet was worn,
and it GROWS if the scale changes when the ward runs out. A small yellow `+N`
beside the sanity number while charge remains, and a yellow tick when the ward
takes a hit (the red tick is sanity's). Nothing is drawn when no ward is worn
or it is spent, so every player without the amulet sees exactly v14.6.

### 2.5 The outcome card
The sanity row's track gains the same yellow segment, animated with the red;
a yellow chip **"Amulet −N"** when the ward took part of the choice's cost.
Rows without a ward are unchanged.

### 2.6 The splash — "Item unlocked" (engine verb `kit.unlock(id)`)
A semi-opaque dark takeover with slowly turning gold rays behind the item, the
label ITEM UNLOCKED in the sodium small caps, the item's name in the display
serif, the item's MODEL turning in the middle (the `iv` renderer), "Added to
your bag", and a **Close** button bottom-centre. Its own sting (`itemunlock`,
new). A new screen state `unlock`, so every "is it play?" gate closes; the
pointer lock is released while it is up and taken back on close (desktop);
Enter/Space/Esc/E close it after a 0.6 s guard (the E that picked it up must
not also close it).

### 2.7 The inventory
`amulet: { icon 'e-amulet', slot 'neck', art 'iconamulet', model 'amulet',
ward 15 }`. The description pane and the zoom window carry a status line:
"Protection left: N of 15" or "Spent — recharges in the next episode".
