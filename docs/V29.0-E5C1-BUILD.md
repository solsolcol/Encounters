## 2 · The rules of the road (CLAUDE.md has them in full; these are the ones every piece of this build turns on)

- ONE generation of each model. A model that comes back with a fault is NOT made again: it is recorded with its
  pictures and the price of another, and put to him (v28.0). A job that failed outright is re-run unchanged.
- CREDITS (read 27 Nov 2026; §8): every piece reads its service's balance before it spends, and STOPS at the floor:
  Meshy 60 left, Higgsfield 5 left, ElevenLabs 10,000 left. A reference picture is priced first (estimate_only).
- Every build is a full release in the run (never `preview`), deployed when it boots its chapter at a milestone
  (M5, M6, M7, M8, M10); his own asks go live as soon as they are built and seen to boot; my own small fixes are batched.
- ONE BUILD AT A TIME: a working build only through `sh tools/buildlock.sh <snapshot-dir>`; a release build only
  through `sh tools/relbuild.sh` (CP1.5). Tests and probes run on a snapshot (`ENC_DIST=<snapshot-dir>`), never on dist/
  while another may build.
- At most two browsers at once: every browser goes through `sh tools/bslot.sh <command>`.
- Several helpers work in ONE tree: each saves only its own paths, with `sh tools/gitsave.sh "v29.0 (E5C1 <piece>):
  <message>" <paths>`; never `git add -A`; never another piece's files.
- ROOM: 2.3 GB free on 27 Nov 2026 (after the two old build snapshots were cleared). Before each model, `df -h /`: under
  700 MB free, stop generating and report. Nothing is compressed to make room; only what is totally safe and redundant is
  cleared (an old build snapshot Netlify holds, a probe's leftover profile), never a source, a tool or his files.
- Privacy and keys: no key printed; no signed link (an ElevenLabs flow link, a Higgsfield or Meshy download link) in a
  file; no real person's face or name to a picture service; Grace and Marcus are made up, and their pictures may go to
  the services. The repository is public.
- The words: every word the player reads plain and direct (R112); his own words used exactly; Grandmaster Pho's name
  said "Puu"; "thank you", never anumodana, outside a monk's mouth; a master's title in full.
- Every picture that judges anything is full size (phone 390 x 844 at two pixels a point; desktop 1280 x 800), opened one
  at a time; a body is judged by the measures (jointscan, feetscan, fingerskin, headturn, clipscan), never by eye alone.
