## What v5.21 shipped

The probe walks every seat and measures the SKIN as it renders — every
vertex pushed through the clone's own bones and matrices, the way the GPU
sees it — and reports the box. v5.21, as deployed:

| seat kind | rendered box (y, metres) | where |
|---|---|---|
| encik (6 seats) | **22 to 58** | a 44 m wide, 69 m deep tangle in the sky over the car park |
| sitclap / sitangry (12 seats) | **-1.0 to 1.4** | a metre under the tarmac, scaled up |
| gracy | 0 to 1.29 | on her chair, correct |
| the standing man, the boy | correct | |

Two faults, both mine, both in the one function v5.21 rewrote.

**1. The bind matrix.** v4.8's shared-skeleton crowd sets a clone's
`bindMatrix` and `bindMatrixInverse` to identity. v5.21 "fixed" that by
folding the source mesh's own `matrixWorld` into the bind matrix, on the
theory that identity was only right for a file whose mesh node sits at the
origin. The theory was wrong. A skinned vertex renders as

    clone.matrixWorld · bindMatrixInverse · Σ wᵢ Bᵢ · bindMatrix · v

where each `Bᵢ` is a BONE's world matrix times its inverse bind — and the
bones' world matrices already carry the whole node chain above them. The
pose `Σ Bᵢ v` is in world space whatever the mesh node's transform is;
that is why a skinned mesh "cannot be moved by its node" in the first
place. Identity is right for every file. On the encik's file the mesh
sits under a Sketchfab root scaled 0.018, so folding it in computed
`inv(0.018) · Σ Bᵢ · (0.018 v)`: the rotations cancel and every bone
TRANSLATION is multiplied by 55. Six men, fifty-five times further from
the origin than their chairs — the mess in the sky. The two Mixamo
sitters were unaffected by this one (their mesh nodes are at identity).

**2. The measure.** v5.21 measured a kind's height and hips by walking
down from `poseMeshes[0][0].skeleton.bones[0].parent`. On the encik's
one-skin file that is the rig root. On the two Mixamo files, whose first
skin starts at `Spine2`, it is the SPINE: no feet, no hips, `lo` a hand's
height off the floor. Every one of them was scaled up to make the
truncated span 1.30 m, then dropped by the missing distance. Sunk a
metre.

**How the v5.21 verification missed both.** Its numbers were real and
they all passed: they measured the offstage POSE skeletons (correct) and
counted meshes per seat (correct), never the skin the clones actually
drew. Its five renders were framed on seats from two metres and never
included where a missing body had gone. So the rule written at v5.21 —
measure, then look — gets its second half made exact here: **measure
what RENDERS, and when a thing is missing from where it should be, find
where it went.** The probe now measures every seat's skin box against the
chair it sits on, every skinned part against a ceiling, and photographs
the tent from the spawn, the back, a row, the front, the burner, the
table and the SKY.

## The five asks

**Gracy and the fearful woman are deleted** — `assets/gracy.glb`,
`assets/fearful.glb`, their `build.py` rows, their embed tokens, their
loaders (the whole `SPECIALS` mechanism goes with gracy; nothing else used
it), their credit rows. Her seat went back into the shuffle: 19 seats,
7 encik / 6 clapping / 6 unimpressed, same fixed seed.

**The encik is back and everyone sits.** Identity bind matrices, with the
derivation above written into the comment; the measure walks the whole
pose clone. Every seat's rendered box now sits between -0.02 and 1.25–1.46
(a raised clapping hand is the spread), centred on its chair.

**Kana is the woman at the burner.** She takes `standers[2]` on the
standing man's contract (the primitive stays until her bytes land). Her
file has one take, a talking one, so idle is its first frame held —
v5.21's answer for her, kept — and the take runs only while a scene sets
`stage.brazierTalk`. She faces the FIRE, computed from the brazier's
position rather than inherited from the primitive, which had looked past
the drum at the car park: a featureless figure got away with that and a
face does not. And a scene does ask her to talk: **scene B's shout at
15.0 ("Boy, come out of there now. You cannot stand there!") is hers** —
the camera has been sitting on the brazier since 14.2, so she turns from
the fire to the boy over 0.6 s and her take runs under the line;
`snap/restore/reset` carry her facing and stop the take, so a skipped
scene leaves her at the fire, idle.

**The granny is the auntie.** Chad's `Granny_animated_rigged.glb`: 597k
triangles, a 4.7 MB 2048 sheet, five clips. By the v5.20 rule (reach for
a character's OWN clips first) she is a better fit than kana ever was —
she brings two takes authored against her own rest pose, `Stand_and_Chat`
(a calm standing idle that turns the body slowly) and `Talk_Passionately`
(ten seconds of gesturing), so idle and talking are two takes rather than
one take frozen and unfrozen. Shrunk to 17.9k triangles and a 1024 JPEG,
1055 KB, keeping only those two clips. She joins the `auntie` group as
kana did, so scene C's turn to the boy and the state save carry her; the
talking take crossfades in over 0.5 s while scene C holds `auntTalk`
(2.9–27.4) and fades back out.

Three things about her were measured, not trusted:

- **Her height** from posed bones to the primitive's own 1.658 m — the
  loose `/Head/` catches `head_end`, her crown — with `lo` the LOWEST the
  feet go across the idle take rather than on one frame of it. The take
  lifts her a hand's width and settles; grounding on the high frame would
  have sunk her on the low ones.
- **Each take's root.** The two takes were authored half a metre apart —
  feet at (0.54, -0.42) in the idle, at the origin in the talk — and a
  plain crossfade would have walked her through the table. Each take's
  foot centre is read at its first frame (`auntRoot`) and `auntSlide`
  moves the model by the mixer's blend weight every frame: what the mixer
  blends in pose, the slide undoes in place. Measured mid-fade at
  w = 0.2 the slide sat at exactly 0.2 of the way; after the fade, at the
  talk root within 3 cm.
- **The prep tool's two blind spots**, both hers. `tools/prepwoman.mjs`
  drops every map but base colour — and dropping her EMISSIVE map left the
  emissive factor at 1,1,1: no map, full emission, a solid white lamp in
  the first render. It sets the factor to black now. And its alpha test
  (a sheet with real transparency is a hair card, so PNG at 256 px) fired
  on her single atlas, which carries transparent padding between its
  islands, and made her face a 256-colour PNG at 256 px; a seventh
  argument, `opaque`, says every sheet is a body sheet.

**The auntie faces the table.** Found by the render, not the ask: since
v4.1 her yaw was `-PI/2 + 0.25`, which faces -x, and the table is at +x.
The primitive stood with its back to the paper table for twenty builds and
nobody could tell; the first real face (v5.20's kana) stood the same way
and it showed, and the granny would have too. It is `+PI/2 - 0.25` now —
the table in front of her and her back three-quarters to the ritual,
which is what the paragraph above her has always said. Scene C's turn to
the boy is unchanged in code and now turns her further, which is right:
she looks up from the folding.

  faces, the burner, the table, scene B mid-shout, scene C at 8 and 24 —
  all looked at.
