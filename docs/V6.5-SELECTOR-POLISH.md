## The open case file breathes

Three animations on `.chTab.on`, all sodium, all slow, all off under
`prefers-reduced-motion`:

- `caseGlow` (3.6 s): the halo swells from the v6.2 lift-shadow to a
  wider, brighter one and settles — the "around".
- `caseSweep` (7 s): a second background layer, a soft diagonal band of
  light 260 % of the card's width, whose `background-position` runs from
  right to left across the paper for about two seconds and then waits
  off-card — the "on". A background, not a pseudo-element, because the
  strip is `overflow-x: auto`, which makes its vertical overflow `auto`
  too: any element hanging outside a card would give the strip a
  scrollbar. Shadows and backgrounds are ink, not scrollable overflow.
- `numGlow` (3.6 s): the numeral's text-shadow, in step with the halo.

The folder tab lights with the card and carries a small upward glow.

## Completed, and the tally

- `chapters.progress`: "{n} of {m} chapters completed".
- `chapters.completed`: "Completed" — the word on a finished stop. The
  chapter card's stamp, the episode card and the other screens keep
  `complete.sealed` ("Sealed"), as Chad asked.
- A fully completed case file in the strip is stamped with
  `episode.stamp` ("Case closed") rather than "Sealed" — the episode
  card's own word, and one that never needed to be "sealed" here.
- Under the count, `.epBar`: five segments, one per chapter — sodium and
  lit for a completed one, jade for the one you are in, a dim amber for a
  reached one, hollow for the rest. `aria-hidden`; the sentence above it
  is the accessible text.

## The chapters are buttons now

Each reachable row is a bordered card on a dark gradient with a **Play
pill** (`chapters.play`, "Play ▸") at its right — sodium, or jade on the
row you are in — and on hover or focus the row lifts three pixels to the
right, its border turns sodium, and the pill glows. A locked row is
dashed, dim and has no pill. The rail still runs through the stops: each
row draws its line pieces seven pixels past its own edges so the line
crosses the gaps, and a completed stop still lights the line down to the
next. The rows enter with a 0.36 s stagger (`rowIn`, 60 ms apart,
`animation-fill-mode: backwards` — `both` would pin `transform: none`
after the entrance and cancel the hover lift).

On a phone (≤ 480 px) the state word ("You are here", "Completed", "In
progress") moves under the chapter's name instead of standing beside the
pill, so "The Hell Note" stopped wrapping to two lines next to "PLAY".
