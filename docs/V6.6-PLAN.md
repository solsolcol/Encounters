## The three base-game fixes

**The grass in squares.** `makeGrass()` draws one 512-pixel noise tile
and repeats it 46 times across the 220 m ground, and the tile is not
periodic: its left edge does not meet its right, so every 4.8 m there is
a seam and the field reads as a grid. The fix is a SEAMLESS tile — the
noise field cross-blended with itself shifted by half a tile in each
axis, with weights that fall to zero at the edges, so the tile wraps —
plus a larger blotch layer inside the same tile so the eye finds no
grid. Chapter 4's grass is a separate canvas and is untouched.

**The faint is silent.** `lose()` speaks `vfaint` ("No... my head...
everything's... spinning...") through `speak()`, then starts the faint
cutscene — and `playCineFn` stops every play-time narration on its first
line (v4.91), so the line it had just started is cut a millisecond later.
When the buffer was still decoding the line survived, which is why it was
sometimes heard. The line becomes the faint scene's own cue (`sfx(0.3,
'vfaint')` in `scFaint`, a `STING_SAMPLE` row), where nothing cuts it.

**Skipping.** A tap or click anywhere skipped a cutscene after 0.8 s.
Now only the Skip button does (and, on a keyboard, E / Space / Enter /
Escape — a deliberate key, not a stray touch; Chad's rule names the
button, and the key is the button's shortcut), and both appear at 3.0 s
of every cutscene, film or scene. `testlib.toPlay` waits past 3.1 s
before its key. The film's re-lock on a gesture skip (v6.4) is unchanged.

## What shipped (v6.6), against the plan

Everything above, with these departures — each measured before it moved:

- **The leaf is pinched, not flat on the palm.** The pick take carries its
  object palm-down at the hand-up frame (the palm is the bone's +z; a
  render along −z showed the knuckles), so a leaf lying on the palm would
  be hidden under the hand or fall off it. It hangs from the fingertips by
  its stem, blade toward a lens beyond the fingers (`shotFingers`), the
  sunset behind — how a person holds a leaf up to look at it.
- **The five's macro is parked a beat AFTER the grab (33.55 s), not at the
  chest.** At the chest the walkpick take's palm faces down and in; at
  33.55 it faces up (+z = (0.65, 0.71, −0.29)). The note lies open and flat
  on it (`foldedNote(-0.10, flat)` — the far half's in-plane turn is for a
  FOLDED note; open, it read upside down), the take is parked there and
  never reaches the pocket, and `shotPalm` pushes in for 4.7 s. Everything
  after shifts 3.0 s; the film is 62 s.
- **Bubbles of 18, 14 and 20 m** (not 14), spaced 0 / −34 / −74 so none
  intersects; the pocket-local coordinates of every prop and shot are
  unchanged, so the film's numbers did not move for the spacing.
- **The look is absolute** and writes only while its track runs — see
  LEARNINGS (two entries). His eyes are on the leaf, the bear, the five and
  then his own hand; the pass's pitch is capped at 0.25 up.
- **Feet:** grounded per FRAME from the lowest of four foot joints, not per
  scene — the walk rolls the feet and the pick plants them lower; per scene
  would have left the walk floating. Measured floor + 0.012 in every frame.
- **Sound:** `duration_seconds` + `loop` on the beds (AUDIO-PLAN); the
  cicadas stay under the two outdoor memories at 0.3.
- **A contact shadow** under the boy (a soft disc), because the memories are
  lit by point lights that cast none.
- **leaktest** waits for the actors at both ends (LEARNINGS).
- The harnesses' contract is unchanged; sanitytest gained the faint's cue
  check.
