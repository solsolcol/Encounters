# v6.8 — THE FACE

Chad, on v6.6's prologue film: *"Did you even look at how bad the new chp
1 intro cutscene is? ... Head tracking totally broken, chin still facing
so high up at the last scene, head spinning to the back, clouds look like
some shit, every single frame has a problem. His voice when picking those
items is so loud. The leaf is not properly placed in his hand. 5 dollar is
totally mangled up. The hellnote should fly past his face before he starts
looking at it. His head is not looking down at the thing he is picking up.
... Did you even look at every single frame?"*

No. v6.6 was judged from twenty-two chosen frames. This build was judged
from all of them: the 62 s film rendered at half-second steps on desktop
(112 frames) and one-second steps in the phone crop (55), laid out on
contact sheets, read before anything moved and read again after.

## The measurement under most of it

The head bone's +Z is not his face. From the bind pose — the inverse bind
matrix inverted — the bone's +Z points 30° BELOW the character's forward
(Z = (−0.006, −0.506, 0.862) in mesh space), so the face in the bone's own
frame is R_bind^T (0,0,1) = (0.011, 0.506, 0.862). v6.6 aimed +Z at the
target, which put the face 30° above everything: a level paper read as a
raised chin; his hand, below the eyes, as a look past the lens; the leaf at
his feet as a look at the sky; and the yaw clamp about the body, fighting
a pitch that could never be reached, as a twist over the shoulder.

Proven by render, not argued: four candidate axes (+Z, the bind face, +Y,
the bind face negated), the look target set ON THE CAMERA at full weight,
photographed at 52.5 and 12.8 (`dbg-facecand.mjs`). One looked into the
lens. `boyLook.face` is that axis, `setFromUnitVectors(face, +Z)` is
multiplied onto the aim, and the neck's range is `down` 1.25 (his feet),
`up` 0.30, `side` 1.2.

## The rest

- **The pass, in Chad's order.** The paper is nearest his eyes at 51.3 and
  the look weight now begins at 51.5 (was 49.0): it crosses his face while
  he looks ahead, then his head goes after it. Released 57.2–59 as before.
- **The five.** One `PlaneGeometry(0.138, 0.066)` on `noteMat5`, its
  length down the fingers, resting ON the curled fingers — the hand mesh
  measured in the bone's frame puts the palm heel at z 0.020 and the
  fingertips at z 0.053, so the plane sits at z 0.042 tilted 19°, 4 mm
  clear, and nothing pierces it. The folded note on the grate is unchanged.
- **The leaf.** The rig has NO finger bones (the claw is the mesh), so the
  pinch is a fixed point: stem at (0.03, 0.135, −0.005), between the thumb
  tip (0.054, 0.11, −0.03) and the index (0.004, 0.15, 0.03); the blade on
  world-down as the hand holds it at 12.8 s, its face toward the lens
  beyond the fingertips. Marker discs photographed first (`dbg-markers`).
- **Clouds.** `cloud()` paints a cluster of soft radial puffs — a shaded
  base row under a lit cap, jittered per cloud — a third the old size
  (0.03–0.056 S wide; the old 0.09 S ellipse spanned 32° of sky); the
  evening stratus at the horizon is a faint gradient streak.
- **Pocket one's tail.** Black by 13.7 (was 14.2); the look at the hand
  ends at 13.2. The pick take drops the hand from ~13.5 and the fingertip
  shot follows it.
- **The reactions.** `vpick1–3` at 0.35 into the voice bus (−9 dB).
