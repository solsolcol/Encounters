## 0.1 The plan on one screen

- **The story.** Back in Singapore after the first master's house, he is not himself: his mind fogs, he loses his
  wallet and cannot remember what he did with it, and a shirt in the dark becomes a figure until he looks again. He goes
  to someone he already trusts — Lersi Chin, a true Lersi master — and tells him what happened. Lersi Chin examines him
  by his own practice, says what he believes ("something negative was placed on you"), clears him, and warns him: do not
  go back to prove it. The decision is whether he can leave it there.
- **What the player learns to do: LET IT REST.** Chapter 1 taught looking. This one teaches the two things after it:
  tell what you saw, not what you think it meant; and when you have enough to decide, stop proving.
- **Fresh.** Two places nobody has seen in this game: **his new flat in Singapore, in the fog**, and **Lersi Chin's
  hermitage at the foot of limestone hills, by a stream, in the morning**. Declared reuse: Pong (the friend who took him
  to the first master), the first master and his compound (only in the endings that go back to him — that IS the point
  of those endings), and his charm from chapter 1 (the bag carries it).
- **One new way to play, from a shipped seam: THE FOG.** Play I is played inside episode 3 chapter 4's cold (blur,
  smear, drained colour) given a GREY tint instead of blue — his own sight dulled — and it lifts during the clearing,
  colour and focus coming back. Everything else is the kit.
- **The shape.**
  - A ≈ 56 s opening film, *The Fog*.
  - About 5 minutes of play in two places: the flat (the wallet, the corner, the call) and the hermitage (the bow, what
    he tells him, the examination, the clearing, the warning).
  - One decision: Master Z's own trial 5.3, verbatim.
  - Four endings, each a whole story of ≈ 60–75 s.
  - A ≈ 25 s coda: the case file's closing paragraph, in his own words.
- **The build.** Three milestones: **M0 direction** (this plan, the voices auditioned — Chad approves), **M1 built and
  playable** as a preview deploy with captions where the voices will go, **M2 the sound and the release** as v25.0.

## 3. The direction

### 3.1 The idea

Chapter 1 ended on the line *"When I got back to Singapore, I stopped feeling like myself."* This chapter starts inside
that sentence and ends on its answer. The fog is the player's own sight, so the clearing is felt as colour, focus and
sound returning, not told. Between the two, the player does the lesson twice: once in the dark corner (check the new
situation on its own evidence), once before Lersi Chin (what you saw, not what you think it meant). The decision then
asks the last thing: whether to go back and prove it.

### 3.2 Fresh, by rule — and checked

New: his flat (a resale HDB flat, three rooms, the shop's stock boxes in the second bedroom, his altar shelf), the
hermitage (the sala on stilts by a stream, limestone cliffs, the altar with the Buddha highest and the hermit images
below, Por Kae heads on a shelf at head height, a holy-water bowl, a mayom tree), the airport arrivals glass, the road
out of Bangkok at dawn. No void deck, no HDB corridor already shown, no wat, no rice country, no storm. `chaptertest`'s
FRESH list (v24.0) is extended to this chapter: every asset key it loads that another chapter also loads must be on the
declared list (Pong, the first master and his compound for A/B, the charms, the presence, the adult figure).

### 3.3 The look

- **The flat in the fog:** late afternoon, the blinds half drawn, a grey that takes the colour out of everything; the
  cold's blur at the edges, the smear on every turn (his eyes lag), the light flat. The one warm thing is the altar's
  red lamp, dimmed by the grey like everything else.
- **The hermitage:** clear morning; the limestone pale against a hazy sky, the stream loud, dappled light on the sala's
  boards; white, yellow and gold, red only as accent (the standing temple ruling applies to his altar too: no green
  banners, nothing Chinese).
- **The clearing's lift:** grey to colour over ≈ 20 s while he is still bowed, the stream's sound coming forward as the
  fog's muffle leaves it.

### 3.4 The sound and the score

Beds: the flat (a fridge, a far TV through the wall, the corridor's lift, rain on the ledge at dusk), muffled by the fog
(a low-pass on the beds, lifted in the clearing); the hermitage (stream, birds, cicadas, a wind chime, far temple bell).
Music: *the fog* (a slow pad with a pulse that drags), *the hermitage* (plain, open), *the clearing* (under the chant,
never over it), and one cue per ending. Every candidate goes to Chad as loudness-matched previews; his ear decides.

### 3.6 The tone rules for this chapter

- Lersi Chin is sincere, competent within his own training and kind. He says what he believes ("I believe…"), never
  what is certain; he never names the first master as a sorcerer; he asks about doctors.
- Efficacy is BELIEF throughout: no line claims the clearing cured anything; "afterwards I gradually felt like myself
  again" is his, not the world's.
- No jump scare: the corner's movement is a shirt, and the camera never confirms anything supernatural.
- Respect: no Por Kae head or hermit image is ever set low, damaged or shown as evil; one bow to Lersi Chin, three
  prostrations only to the Buddha.

The VO is the case file verbatim. The film ends on the light play begins in (the flat at dusk, the grey on).

### 4.1 PLAY I — THE FLAT, IN THE FOG (`flat`)

- **P1 · The wallet.** Objective: *Find your wallet.* The places he'd keep it (the key bowl, the bag, the sofa) each
  show it isn't there; the objective's own words blur and re-form between searches (the fog eats the line, never the
  marker). It is in the fridge, beside the milk. Him: "…What is it doing in here?" No timer, no failure; the search is
  three presses, and the marker shows where to look next.
- **P2 · The corner (the trial's 5.4 as play).** As he turns back, something moves in the dark corner by the window.
  The banner observes; it never orders. He can switch on the light, or stand still and watch: both are the trial's good
  answers, and both find the same thing — a shirt on a hanger, moving in the fan's air. Him: "…A shirt."
  (If he walks at it in the dark, the light comes on as he reaches it. There is no wrong press.)
- **P3 · The call.** His phone: a new message from Pong, in chapter 1's voice ("Brother!! You OK? Long time no reply
  🙏"), left unanswered. He calls Lersi Chin instead. Him (VO): "Eventually I approached another Thai practitioner I trusted, Lersi
  Chin, a true Lersi master." Lersi Chin (phone): "Nong, you sound tired. Come and see me." — the fog's grey stays.

### 4.2 THE WAY IN — a film (≈ 25 s)

Bangkok at dawn from Pong's pickup (Chad's Rivian, declared reuse: Pong's car), the road west into the hills, the
limestone rising out of the haze. Pong: "Ratchaburi side. My uncle goes to him for years." Him: "Last time I went on
someone's word. This time I know who I'm going to." Pong, a beat: "…Ya. Sorry, bro." (Pong is not a villain; he took him
to the first master on his cousin's word, and he knows it.)

### 4.3 PLAY II — THE HERMITAGE (`sala`)

- **P4 · The bow.** The sala; Lersi Chin seated cross-legged before his altar. Objective: *Pay respect at the altar.*
  Three prostrations to the Buddha (`kit.pray` and the bow), then a wai to Lersi Chin. Lersi Chin: "Sit, sit. You look
  like you haven't slept."
- **P5 · What he tells him — the trial's 5.2, a pick.** Lersi Chin: "Tell me what happened there." The four options,
  verbatim:
  - A **Tell Lersi Chin exactly what you saw** → Lersi Chin: "Good. You tell me what you saw, not what you think. Then
    I can look for myself."
  - B **Ask Lersi Chin what he thinks it means** → "Seeing is not understanding, nong. Someone near a master — you
    don't know yet what he is to him."
  - C **Assume the first master is using black magic** → "You don't know that. Suspicion is not evidence. I will look
    — then we talk."
  - D **Ask Lersi Chin what you should do next** → "First, we look at you. Not at him."
  Each answer carries the trial's lesson in a caption. Awareness only (§2.2 #5).
- **P6 · The examination — watched.** He takes his wrist, asks the ordinary questions: sleep, food, the wallet, "you
  saw a doctor?" ("Yes. They said stress."), then sits with his eyes closed (his practice — นั่งทางใน is not named).
  If the charm is in the bag (Q8), he asks to see it; holds it with both hands, wais it, sets it on a clean cloth:
  "This one, you give to a temple. Don't keep, don't throw." Then: "According to my practice, I believe something
  negative was placed on you." Him: "By him?" Lersi Chin: "I didn't say that. I said on you."
- **P7 · The clearing — a bow, then watched.** Objective: *Bow your head.* The press bows him; the clearing plays as a
  film he cannot skip the first time (`kit.film`, noSkip until sealed once): homage to his teachers; Namo tassa ×3; the
  blessing (Q7) captioned, Pali over its meaning, timed on the take's breaths; the holy water drunk from his cupped
  hands; the mayom bundle sprinkling it over his head and shoulders; the white thread tied at his wrist. The fog lifts
  under it over ≈ 20 s, colour, focus and the stream's sound returning. Lersi Chin: "Go to a temple this week. Make
  merit. If the fog comes back — doctor first, then me."
- **P8 · The warning.** Lersi Chin, serious: "One more thing. Don't go back there to prove anything. Don't test him.
  Don't call him." Out at the pickup, Pong: "If you want to see for yourself, I know the way." The decision opens.

### 4.4 THE DECISION (`decide`) — the trial's 5.3, verbatim

*"After hearing the full account, Lersi Chin becomes serious and warns Master Z not to return simply to prove whether
his suspicion is correct. Master Z now has to decide whether curiosity is still useful—or has become unnecessary
exposure."*

- A **Return to test your suspicion** (worst) · B **Observe from a distance without entering** · C **Contact the first
  master and confront him** (bad) · D **Follow the warning and walk away** (best). Deltas §2.2 #9.

### 4.5 THE FOUR ENDINGS (each a whole story, ≈ 60–75 s)

**A · BACK THROUGH THE GATE.** Pong drives him back to the compound (declared reuse, Q6). The gate, the yard, the door.
The first master looks up from his low table: "You came back." (his chapter-1 voice, nothing more). He sits; the jar
breathes once under its cloth. The grey creeps back in at the edges of the picture as the master smiles. Him (VO): "I
went back to prove I'd seen it. I proved nothing." Out in the yard, the colour still thin. Lesson: *Curiosity can
become unnecessary exposure.*

**B · ACROSS THE PADDY.** He has Pong stop on the road. From the bund across the flooded field he watches the compound:
a figure at the window, too far to know. Pong: "So?" Him: "So… nothing. Why did I even come?" They drive on; the
compound in the mirror. Lesson: *Distance reduces interaction, but ask whether returning was necessary.*

**C · ONE MESSAGE.** In his flat that night he types it, deletes it, types it again: "I saw the woman standing behind
you." Sent. The ticks turn blue. Minutes. A voice note: the first master, in Thai (captioned): "You see well, nong.
Come back. I can teach you." He doesn't answer; the screen's light on his face, the grey at the edges again. Lesson:
*Physical distance does not automatically make confrontation wise.*

**D · ENOUGH.** At the pickup he shakes his head at Pong. He goes back to the sala and wais Lersi Chin: "Thank you,
Lersi Chin." Lersi Chin: "Go well. Make merit when you get home." The drive out, the limestone in clean light; then the
flat, the blinds up, the colour full; he puts the charm in an envelope for the temple and the wallet back in his
pocket. Him (VO): "I had enough to decide. There was nothing more worth proving." Lesson: *Wisdom can be knowing when
there is nothing more worth proving.*

Every ending is directed shot by shot at M1 and measured for silence (no stretch of 1.5 s without a voice or a sound
with a reason) before it is voiced.

### 4.6 THE CODA (every ending, ≈ 25 s)

His desk at night, months later: a laptop with a lineage chart, printed accounts, names crossed out. The case file's
closing paragraph in his own voice, verbatim, from *"Whether you interpret that spiritually or otherwise…"* to *"…until
the day I met someone who could finally help me understand."* The last image is a Wai Kru notice on his corkboard
(chapter 3's door), with no words drawn that the sheet does not carry.

### 4.7 The words

- **Card lines** (his own terse first-person reflections, never the card read aloud):
  A "I went back to prove it. I only brought it home again." · B "I kept my distance. I still had to ask why I'd

- **Core:** §1 (Q5).
