## The record

### CP1 · the whisper (masters/v24.24/make.sh)

eleven_v4 whispers this voice only in the first phrases of a take and drifts back to his voice after: `[whispering]`
once at the head gave a hushed voice (68–82 % voiced), `[whispers]` before every phrase whispered the homage (4–18 %)
and went back to voice at the trade formula in all four takes (64–82 %); a take of ONE phrase whispers through (the
formula 9–24 %, the closing line 4–23 %). So each chant is laid phrase by phrase from whispered phrases only, cut at
the words' measured edges, each brought to one loudness, the model's own half-second breath between, then levelled as
the game's other whisper is (−21 dB RMS). Measured on the shipped files: the blessing 24.3 s, every phrase 4–10 %
voiced (the chant it replaces: 33.8 s, 75 %), −19.6 LUFS against the old −18.4; the loop 14.6 s, 10–24 %, −19.9
against −20.5; ending D's 6.52 s, inside the 6.8 s before its bell.

### CP2 · the subtitles
