**It keeps the no-villain rule by construction.** The trial never says what the first master is or did: what he

strangeness only in what the player perceives, and lets the player's own certainty be the danger.

## 2. The episode's one axis: CERTAINTY

Episode 1 escalated distance → smallness → inversion → intrusion → release; episode 3, how much of himself he loses.
Episode 4 escalates **how far what he SAW is allowed to become what he is SURE of** — the danger moving from outside
him (a master he cannot read) to inside him (his own conclusions and fear):

1. **SEEING** — something unusual around a master. Unusual is not understood.
2. **TELLING** — evidence first, interpretation second.
3. **PROVING** — the urge to go back and prove it; curiosity becoming exposure.
4. **FEARING** — at home, fear turning ordinary shadows into confirmation.
5. **CARRYING** — what he takes forward: "I don't know" can be wisdom, and a suspicion is never a claim to spread
   about another person.

## 3. The five chapters (the house shape: one small dense place, an opening film, play with the kit, four options
that are four KINDS of answer, the case file's lesson first)

Ranks are the trial's own (its wisdom deltas): best / good / bad / worst.

### Chapter 1 · The First Master  (trial 5.1)
- **Film.** His own amulet shop in Singapore at opening time — glass counters, his first stock, a student's shop.
  Then Thailand: the flight, a driver, a provincial town, a master someone recommended, whom he does not know well.
- **Place.** The master's ritual room — a back room crowded with items for sale and blessing, an altar, candles.
- **Play.** The master is polite and talks fortune and business; he offers piece after piece (the player takes
  little — the case file). Something is **at the edge of sight**: a presence behind the master that is there only
  when not looked at directly, and one unusual ritual object, covered. **NEW — OBSERVE:** a look mechanic that rewards
  NOT staring — what the player notices is logged as plain observations, never as conclusions (the trial's ep 5 used
  its "spot" challenge here).
- **Decision (his).** A **observe quietly** (good) · B **ask directly about what you notice** (bad: it reveals what he
  can perceive) · C **assume the master is dangerous** (worst) · D **leave without getting more involved** (best).

### Chapter 2 · A Second Opinion  (trial 5.2)
- **Place.** Back in Singapore: his shop, and his own mind in a fog.
- **Play.** **The fog** (the case file): he cannot think properly, the wallet is gone and he can barely remember what
  he did with it — a search of his own shop with the picture blurred and dimmed (the cold's seams, `kit.chill` /
  `kit.smear`), retracing his steps. Then Lersi Chin's shrine — the hermit masks (Chad's `lersimask` already ships),
  incense, a tiger-skin cloth — where he tells what he saw. **NEW — EVIDENCE FIRST:** a sorting minigame — what he says
  arrives line by line and is sorted into **WHAT I SAW** or **WHAT I THINK IT MEANS** ("a figure stood behind him" /
  "he uses black magic"), graded; its own picture (the minigame rule).
- **Decision (his).** A **tell Lersi Chin exactly what you saw** (good) · B **ask what he thinks it means** (bad, though kind) ·
  C **assume the first master is using black magic** (worst) · D **ask what you should do next** (best).

### Chapter 3 · The Warning  (trial 5.3)
- **Place.** Lersi Chin's shrine, the inner room.
- **Play.** He examines it **according to his practice** and says what he believes (each reads through his own
  training); **the clearing** (the case file) — the player sits through it, head bowed, while he chants and blows;
  afterwards the fog lifts by degrees. Then the warning: do not go back to prove anything. **NEW — BOWED:** keep the
  head down and the breath slow through the clearing (a look-held, breath-paced kind — not a repeat of episode 3's).
- **Decision (his).** A **return to test your suspicion** (worst — "curiosity can become unnecessary exposure") ·
  B **observe from a distance without entering** (good) · C **contact the first master and confront him** (bad) ·
  D **follow the warning and walk away** (best).

### Chapter 4 · What Followed  (trial 5.4)
- **Place.** His home at night — **DECIDE:** his own room from episode 3 chapter 5 (the three kumanthong still on
  his altar, cared for "for life"), which is the quiet echo; or a new flat.
- **Play.** Movement in a darker corner. The ordinary causes are all in the room — a shirt on a hanger, the fan's
  air, a curtain, a reflection — and they show themselves only to someone who waits. **NEW — STILL:** a patience
  mechanic: the evidence appears while he does NOT move; turning, walking or pressing resets it. The lights are
  there for the one who checks calmly.
- **Decision (his).** A **check the room calmly for an ordinary cause** (good) · B **assume something followed you
  home** (bad) · C **test the room to see if something responds** (worst) · D **stop, observe and wait before
  deciding** (best).

### Chapter 5 · The Real Lesson  (trial 5.5)
- **Place.** His shop the next morning. Nothing further has happened.
- **Play.** A customer asks him about a powerful master she found online. **NEW — THE FOUR QUESTIONS:** he answers
  her with the case file's own questions — who are they, what is their character, what are their intentions, who can
  genuinely vouch for them — picked from a set that also holds the tempting ones (how famous, how strong, how fast);
  then he looks up a lineage himself, the way he does from now on. The close: the one question none of them could
  answer — *why* he has these abilities — over a Wai Kru poster on his wall, the man in white not yet met (S1·12).
- **Decision (his).** A **accept that you may never know the full truth** (good) · B **decide the first master must
  have been dangerous** (bad) · C **tell others the first master practises black magic** (worst — a suspicion
  turned into a claim about another person) · D **keep the lesson, respect the boundary and move forward** (best).

**The teaching** of each chapter starts from the case file's lesson, verbatim (*"Do not choose a spiritual master simply
because somebody calls them a master. Ask the ordinary questions…"*), with the trial's per-option lessons under the
cards. No Pali tag unless Chad names one (the LESSON LINE rule).
