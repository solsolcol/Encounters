3. The taxi, in every coda: "the time is 1860" — it is the fare meter, drawn S$ 18.60 on a 128 x 48 sheet: on a phone the

5. Music: a serious lack of background, ambient and mood music through the whole episode — in play and in every film:
   leaving the samnak, the taxi, back in his shop, and the rest. It feels flat.
6. The samnak's amulet display (the master's cabinets): real amulet models in place of the drawn boards and the
   generated stock — the yin, barang (black-magic), ghost kinds — every model made from photographs of REAL yin amulets
   (his standing rule: a sacred piece begins as real photographs, then a checked picture on white, then Meshy).
7. His own amulet shop can be improved more, inside and outside: why are the other stores closed? Show real people
   (the game's own character models) walking past the shop.
8. Ending B: no business card — none given at the door, none shown in the pickup. He leaves the samnak normally, Pa Noi
   says goodbye to him, and he gets into the pickup with Pong. (Her card line, `pn1card` "Ajahn say, call anytime, ka.",
   goes; a goodbye in her voice takes its place — one new take.)
9. Ending C, back in his shop (the coda): no shadow appearing, and no shock — neither the shadow nor the start he gives.
10. A film's brief appearance of the stall woman is abrupt and awkward, and what it shows is not clear.
