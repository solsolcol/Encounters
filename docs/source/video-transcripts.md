# The case-file VIDEOS — what they say and what they show

Until 8 Sep 2026 the source notes were made from the site's TEXT only.
Chad asked whether the videos had been watched; they had not. These are
the two army-era files, downloaded from encounters.triplegem.asia
(`assets/ep4.mp4`, 1080x1920, 39 s; `assets/ep5.mp4`, 720x1280, 48 s),
transcribed (ElevenLabs Scribe) and read frame by frame. The videos are
AI-generated films with a comic-strip panel after each, and they SHOW the
entity the text leaves unseen — which matters for the game.

## S1·04 — The 3 AM Shower (ep4.mp4)

**Narration, verbatim (Master Z, on camera):**
> During my BMT days, something strange kept happening. The shower would
> suddenly turn on. At first, I tried to ignore it. After it happened
> night after night, curiosity got the better of me. One night, I decided
> to get up and check. The shower was running, but there was no one.
> Another night, something else happened. I suddenly felt as though
> someone was tugging at my blanket. I couldn't see anyone, so I simply
> stayed there and didn't react. But as army training became more tiring,
> eventually I became too exhausted to care. I stopped checking. I stopped
> reacting. I simply slept through it.

**What the film shows, in order:**
- A long platoon bunk at night with DOUBLE-DECK beds in two rows, men
  asleep, a red digital wall clock reading **03:00**.
- The tiled corridor to the toilet block, a light at the far end, water
  on the floor.
- **A pale soldier in uniform standing in the shower corridor**, lit
  from the front, face grey, still. (The text never says this; the film
  does.)
- Him sitting up in bed, walking barefoot down the corridor, looking
  into the empty running shower, walking back.
- Lying under a green army blanket; **a translucent grey hand pulling
  the blanket** from the foot of the bed.
- **The pale soldier standing over his bed** while he lies still.
- Daytime: a route march on Tekong with field packs ("DAYTIME · MASTER Z
  · PULAU TEKONG"); captions "NIGHT ONE 03:00 — abuse or dangerous
  activity: none", "NIGHT THREE — dormant, normal"; him asleep, calm.

**The comic's lesson panel** (the site's lesson, condensed): "Not every
unexplained presence needs to be acknowledged. Always rule out ordinary
causes first. Don't engage, don't provoke, don't feed your own fear.
Knowing how to do something doesn't mean that we should do it."

## S1·05 — The Ghostly Cyclist (ep5.mp4)

**Narration and dialogue, verbatim:**
> During infantry training, we had an outfield exercise deep in the
> jungle. Then suddenly, I felt something pressing against my leg. It
> wasn't just a sensation, there was actual pressure. I could still feel
> the pressure against my leg. For a moment, I thought I saw something.
> Then whatever was there disappeared.
>
> *(other soldiers, shouting)* Eh, what is that? The bicycle, it's
> floating. Look over there! What is that?

**What the film shows, in order:**
- The section sitting in the jungle at night, him at the rear with a
  torch, facing out.
- The torch on his own leg and boot; nothing there.
- **A pale, grey-skinned soldier in an old uniform crouched against a
  tree beside him, one hand on his leg**, then standing behind him with
  a torch of its own — then gone when the beam swings.
- **A glowing, translucent soldier on a bicycle**, riding slowly through
  the trees past the other platoon, who scramble up and point; wide shot
  of the cyclist crossing their position, luminous, no sound of its own.
- The lesson card: **"WHAT YOU CAN'T SEE DOESN'T MEAN IT DOESN'T EXIST.
  Stay calm, observe. Don't let fear control your response. — Master Z"**
- The comic's icons: stay calm · observe · stay grounded · don't let fear
  control the response; "Be aware, but not afraid. Be curious, but not
  reckless. Be strong, but not arrogant."

## What this changes for the game

1. **The entity is SHOWN by the source itself.** The site's own films put
   a pale soldier in the shower corridor and at the bedside, and a
   luminous cyclist in front of the other platoon. Showing the cyclist to
   the player is therefore not a departure from the source — only from
   its prose — and the film gives the look: grey skin, an old uniform,
   lit from within, translucent, slow.
2. **The bunk has double-deck beds** in the film. (Chad served — his
   memory of Hawk Company's bunk decides the set.)
3. **The 03:00 wall clock** is a prop worth having.
4. The **video lesson** for S1·05 is a shorter, sharper sentence than
   the text's and is the right closing line for the episode.
5. The other platoon's dialogue exists in Master Z's own film — "Eh,
   what is that? The bicycle, it's floating. Look over there!" — and can
   be voiced as written.
