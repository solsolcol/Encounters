# The 2D trial game — every chapter, choice and score

Lifted from `gametrial.html`. Three episodes, five chapters each, four choices
per chapter. `d` is the score change as **[sanity, awareness, wisdom]**.
Each choice carries its own action line, consequence line and teaching.

Every episode starts at **sanity 70 · awareness 10 · wisdom 5**.
