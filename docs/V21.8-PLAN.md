## His two picks (asked before any work)

  experience the same way, and being called a master does not make a reading
  infallible. Do not simply accept the first explanation you are handed. If
  you want the truth, seek it, learn it, and find it for yourself.* (The case
  file's own lesson, second person, a third of the length; no Pali tag.)

  everyone, leading the chant toward the statues, as in a temple.

in every voice line, every speaker, every chapter (Chad: "Always remember
this"). On-screen text may abbreviate (the item's name stays "LP Tim Khun
Paen"); a voice says the words. Into CLAUDE.md's audio section.
