## What the model is (measured, CP0)

The Lanna temple (Sketchfab, masters/v16.6/src/lannatemple.glb), after the
v16.8 bake (masters/v16.8/temple: every face turned outward, classed, AO in
COLOR_0). Model units, front at +z:

- Base foot y 21.15; hall and landing floor **y 33.43**.
- The HALL is a walled box: inner faces x −26.87 / +26.14, z −23.38 (front)
  / −79.89 (back); walls to ~57, the ceiling rising to 70.3 in the middle.
  Walls are hollow shells 0.3–1.6 m thick.
- One FRONT DOOR, x −3.5…5.0, y 33.4…49.5, in a recess (the facade at
  z −15.2, the door wall at −20.8…−23.4, a threshold 0.18–0.26 m high).
  A BACK DOOR the same, behind where the altar goes.
- THREE WINDOW PAIRS a side, sill 41.1, head 52: z −37…−30, −55…−48
  (a mullion at −51.5), −73.5…−66.
- **No walkable veranda.** Outside the walls is a decorative balustrade
  hugging the wall and a 0.65 m ledge to the base's edge. The only outdoor
  floor a person uses is the front LANDING (x ±12.6, z −9.5…−15.2) and the
  back one.
- The front STAIR is four giant blocks (risers 0.3–0.7 m at scale) between
  two white cheek walls with a gold saw-tooth crest and a stylised naga head
  at the foot — the model's own nagas.

## Decisions

- **Scale 0.2** (hall 10.7 × 11.3 m, floor 2.456 m up, door 1.7 × 3.2 m,
  windows from 1.5 m to 3.7 m). World x = model x × 0.2; world z =
  (model z + 9.5) × 0.2, so the landing's front edge is world z 0.
- **Chad's nagas stay.** The model's own stair (its four blocks, its cheek
  walls and its stylised nagas) is CUT from the mesh, and a proper staircase
  is built in its place — fourteen risers of 17.5 cm over 3.9 m, the width
  of the model's opening — with Chad's naga re-baked (`BEND`) for the new
  flight and lying on its cheek walls, heads rearing at the foot.
- **The walkway leaves from a SIDE DOOR.** With no veranda, the walkway to
  the Ajarn's room starts at a door opened in the hall's west wall where the
  middle window pair is (the wall under the sill, the mullion and the
  balustrade in front are cut from the mesh; a lacquered frame covers the
  cut edges). The walkway, the kuti and the private room keep their
  recipes, at the new floor height (`SALA.floor`).
- **The monk sits on the EAST (side) wall**, facing the congregation, the
  principal Buddha alone on the back wall on the door's axis.
- **Interior colours follow Chad's standing rulings**: the plain pale teak
  ceiling (v16.4 — "the star pattern ... too much"), no mural (v16.4 — "it
  looks bad"); a lacquered red wainscot with a gold rule, limewash above, a
  red-and-gold frieze, the teak floor and the runner from the door to the
  altar.
- **The compound grows north**: the north wall moves from z −13 to −23.5
  (the temple is 20.6 m deep behind its landing).

## What shipped (CP1–CP3)

- **Full detail, at Chad's word.** The first prep simplified the carving
  (451k → 72k) and Chad stopped it: *"why did u cut the temple down? now it
  loses all its details"*. Nothing is simplified now — 473,896 triangles
  ship, the original's 488,827 less exactly the cut stair and its nagas
  (3.3 MB). The gold carving has no NORMAL (it was flat-shaded in the file,
  every triangle its own normals — the v8.0 attribute-seam law) and is drawn
  `flatShading`, which loses nothing: its facets are its relief.
- **Materials** (`templeMats` in e3c1): the refinement viewer's patterns,
  computed in the model's own units (`vMP` undoes the placement), with two
  fixes found by photograph: the window-reveal mask had painted the whole
  upper half of the side walls (it matched the room-facing faces — the
  viewer had the same bug), and the limewash's grain read as grey granite
  in the game's light (whiter now, a faint bump).
- **Collision from the mesh**: `masters/v16.9/blockgrid.mjs` — a 10 cm grid
  over the base, a cell solid where the temple is within 0.2 m at knee or
  chest height or under 2.3 m overhead, merged into 148 rectangles
  (`TEMPLE_BLOCK`). Rebuild it whenever the temple file changes.
- **The staircase and Chad's nagas**: 14 risers of 17.5 cm over 3.9 m, 0.8 m
  cheek walls running back 1.1 m onto the landing as parapets, the naga at
  ×2.8 re-baked `BEND=-0.93,0.463,0.877` (the ramp from the landing's edge to
  the foot, the head 1.4 m off a 1.45 m pedestal).
- **The west door and the walkway**: the walkway's posts 3.4 m (were 2.6) so
  its roof clears the door head; lacquered red jambs with a gold edge over
  the cut, a red lintel, and the two leaves open flat against the hall wall.
- **Inside**: the altar on the back wall on the door's axis; the monk's dais
  TURNED onto the east wall (`MD.ry`, `mdW()`; everything on it placed in
  its own frame); eight mats either side of the runner; the waiting bench
  and its man along the west wall; the helper by the west door facing the
  monk; the amulet table and the cushions along the east wall; fans and six
  lamps on long rods from the 9.7 m ridge; shafts of dawn through the door
  and the first east window. The extra space: a black-and-gold scripture
  cabinet, a gong on its red frame, rows of small images either side of the
  altar with a candle each, a donation box by the door.
- **The compound**: the north wall 10.5 m back (z −23.5), the paving and
  verges with it, trees kept outside it, the ordination hall 0.4 m east,
  bells under the temple's side eaves (measured: x ±8.0, 6.76 m), marigold
  strings on the two front columns, the shoe rack west of the stair's foot,
  pots, pigeons, the dog and the lantern string moved clear of the flight.
- **Chad's slippers** (his second ask mid-build): `tools/prepslipper.mjs`
  splits his pair into one slipper (toe +z) and keeps the kicked-off pair;
  0.1 of its triangles (2.7k a slipper — ~18 pairs are on screen at once),
  drawn as instances, a colour a pair, on the rack, on the paving, at the
  kuti and his own. Credited.
- **The walk out** (all four endings): out onto the landing, down the longer
  flight in 3.2 s (was 1.6), the rack in its new place, the yard further
  out, the last look and the rising lens aimed at the taller temple.
