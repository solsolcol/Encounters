# v6.9 — THE NECK

Chad, on v6.8's prologue: *"the chp1 intro is better now, but you need to
look every frame with even greater precision, like at least 10 times a
second for the first 3 scenes, the 4th scene is perfect now. The problem is
the head stays fixed and tracked on the object from before picking it up
all the way till he picked it up. So it looks awkward because his neck is
bent and fixed in a static awkward position as his body move but his neck
and head stays fixed like that. The final position of the head and neck is
correct now and that's fine, but the initial position should be the neutral
and default one, and slowly change to the final position or something. You
should look at more frames to decide the speed and linearity. Also, his
pick up reactions voice should be even softer, like almost whispering to
himself excitedly."*

## Measured first

`dbg-headtrack.mjs` reads the head ten times a second over 4.4–38.4 s,
twice per step: with the look muted (the clip's own head) and as the film
plays. Pitch and yaw of the face in world, the head's angle from its REST
rotation in the neck's frame, the look weight. Before anything moved
(`view/headtrack-v68.txt`):

| where | the clip's own head | the film's head |
|---|---|---|
| leaf, 8.2–9.8 | −10° → −72° as the body bends | pinned at −71.6° for 1.6 s (ramp 0.6 s from 7.6) |
| bear on the steps, 17.1–18.9 | back to centre by 17.7, then still | −33° from 17.1, still |
| bear, walking, 19.3–20.3 | level (the walk) | −40° at 19.3, −71.6° by 20.1, held through the bend; 48°–76° off the neck's rest while he still walks |
| five, 31.0–33.3 | −60° to −74° (the walk-pick take looks down itself), yaw to the side | −59° → −71.6° by 32.5, yaw to the note, 61°–105° off the neck's rest through the stoop |
| all three | | ramps of 0.3, 0.4 and 0.8 s: snaps |

Two things were wrong at once. The look began at or near full weight
before the body moved, so the body bent under a head that did not; and
the pitch clamp (72°, right for a head that must look at its feet) let the
head reach an angle no neck reaches while the torso is upright.

## What changed

- **A look starts on the clip's own head and eases in.** `lookAt(t0, t1,
  get, { ramp, w0, w1, cone, ease })`: the weight goes from `w0` (0 — the
  take's own head) to `w1` on a smoothstep over `ramp` seconds. Leaf: 1.8 s
  from 7.4 (full at 9.2, the bend from 8.4). Bear on the steps: 1.3 s from
  17.3 (after the alert take's own glance comes back to centre). Bear after
  the cut: from three quarters to full over 1.6 s while he walks and
  bends. Five: 2.0 s from 30.6, full at 32.6, the stoop from 32.5.
- **A neck cone.** `boyLook.cone` (0.8 rad, 46°) limits the aim, in the
  neck's own frame, to that angle from the head's bind rotation (`restQ`,
  from the bind matrices: `neck_bind^-1 * head_bind`). Inside the cone the
  head goes to the thing; outside it the head goes as far as a neck goes
  and stays ON THE BODY — the torso's bend carries it the rest of the way.
  Per look, default off: the hand looks (the "final positions", which Chad
  called correct) and the whole of the pass keep v6.8's head exactly.
- **The hand looks start at full weight** (`w0: 1, ramp: 0`) on the hand,
  which is at the thing at the grab, so the switch of target is not a jump.
- **The reactions are whispers, round the bus** — docs/AUDIO-PLAN.md v6.9.
