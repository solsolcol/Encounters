# v28.0 · Episode 4 chapter 5 · THE CLEANSING, the tag-team fight · THE SPECIFICATION

Written 21 Nov 2026 for Chad's R14 (docs/V28.0-E4C5-PLAN.md §0): "Zavier needs actual abilities that do something or
synergize with pho abilities so that it is a real reason to switch back and forth between characters for real gameplay.
Like an ability to lock a prai in a bubble to stop it from escaping." With it, his "Behind and above" (the camera) and
"Fight restarts" (a loss). It replaces the plan's §5 (the second draft) wherever the two differ, and keeps everything of
§5 that still holds: Grandmaster Pho's FIRE, his golden SHOCKWAVE on a long wait, the BAG; nothing destroyed, only
gathered; the one not played never idle and never playing the player's part; a first use waits for the player;
instructions first, with START. The direction it follows is the build tracker's §3 (docs/V28.0-E4C5-BUILD.md). The
plan's camera (5.6: behind and above the played one, 4.0 m back and 3.1 m up, following, gliding 0.4 s at a switch),
its screen layout and its clocks, pauses and saves (5.11) stand as written there; this page is the fight's RULES.

**Its rules are ONE file**, `masters/v28.0/parts/c_fightrules.js` (plain JavaScript, no page, no THREE, no clock and no
randomness of its own; the chapter's seeded stream is passed in), assembled into the chapter inside `build()` after
b_world.js and before c_story.js, and loaded as it is by **`tools/probes/fightsim.mjs`**, which plays it 10,000 times
for each of ten simulated players. What is tested and what is played are one function (v8.7). Every number on this
page is that file's `NUM` table (§7); where the two ever differ, the file is right and this page is stale.

How the chapter drives it: `FIGHT.create({ sanity })` makes one plain object (a retry, a replay and a probe's diff all
read it as it is); every frame of the mode `FIGHT.step(fight, input, dt, { rand, secs })` moves it on and answers a list
of events to draw and sound; `FIGHT.hud(fight)` is the screen (every button's face, wait, lock and pulse, the cage, the
count, the objective's key); `FIGHT.need(fight)` is what the moment asks for. The input is one act a frame (a button
by its place, `slot`, or by its name, `act`), the stick (`mx`, `mz`, by the screen), a tap on a Prai (`pick`) or on a
stale cloud (`pickCloud`), and the engine's own sanity after its guards (`sanity`). A `drain` event is a cost the
chapter passes through `kitAward('sanity', -amt, { minigame: true })`, so a worn piece guards here as everywhere
(v14.13); a `say` event is a line of the script, also a caption.

## 1 · The idea in five lines

1. **Nothing is destroyed.** Every Prai is gathered into the black bag, by Grandmaster Pho's BAG one at a time or by his
   golden SHOCKWAVE several at once: nine to nought.
2. **They come for Master Zavier**, whose senses are open. His Lersi's violet cage holds them at its bars and wears; what
   gets past it, or comes up under him unseen, takes his sanity.
3. **Each does what the other cannot.** Grandmaster Pho's FIRE only CHASES a free Prai away (it hides and comes back
   unseen), and one kind slips it altogether; once Master Zavier has BOUND a Prai in a bubble, the fire always lands
   and TIRES it. Only Grandmaster Pho BAGS a tired one; only Master Zavier SMELLS out one that hides.
4. **The gold, under the lent cage.** The golden SHOCKWAVE gathers every bound Prai it passes and drives the rest back.
   While it is gathered they rush to break it, so Master Zavier LENDS his teacher his cage for those seconds and stands
   with nothing round him. Twice the Prai cling to the house, and then only the gold can take them.
5. **Losing begins the fight again** (R14: "Fight restarts"); nothing is ever played for him.

## 2 · The two of them, and every act

Each has three buttons in the same three places (main, second, third) and the switch, which carries the OTHER one's
face and name (the plan's 5.6). On a desktop: Space or a click is the main act, Q the second, E the third, F the switch
(N is SMELL as well, as on the walkway); WASD or the arrows walk. A button appears the first time it is needed; its
wait is a ring on it; the button the moment asks for grows and shrinks (chapter 4's R47); a button that cannot be
pressed says why on its face and is never pressed (v21.28). **Every wait runs on whoever is played**, so what one began
keeps coming ready while the other works: that is the reward for switching, and the only one (no gift for arriving).

## 3 · The nine Prai

Three of each kind. None has a name, a face or a history; his teacher never names a kind (R38): he says what to do.

| kind | which | how it moves | what answers it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **comes** | the lesson's; the 1st and 4th of the fight's eight | straight at him, 0.6 m s⁻¹ | FIRE chases it away (it hides and comes back); BIND, FIRE, BAG gathers it |
| **slips** | the 2nd, 5th and 7th | weaving at him, 0.8 m s⁻¹ | the fire passes through it while it is free: only BIND holds it; then FIRE tires it and BAG gathers it |
| **hides** | the 3rd, 6th and 8th | 0.55 m s⁻¹; 1.5 s after it rises it sinks into the paving and comes on unseen (0.5 m s⁻¹), up under him | SMELL shows it for 4 s; then BIND, FIRE, BAG (fire on it free chases it, and it sinks again) |

- Any kind goes unseen when it is chased (it sinks where it fled) or when it rises from tired; only SMELL, or the gold,
  shows it again.
- **At his cage** at most three press its bars, each wearing it 4 a second; the rest wait in view 2.5 m out. With
  nothing at the bars or at him it mends 6 a second. At nought it breaks and they pass; it is whole again at 30.
  While it is lent they pass.
- **At him** (past the cage, up under him, or shown beside him) a Prai gathers itself for 2 s, then takes 2 of his
  sanity a second, each.
- **The fight's eight** come in the kinds' order above, each at its time (0, 12, 26, 42, 58, 74, 90 and 106 s into the
  fight), at least 1.5 s apart, one free on the court at a time at first and two from 24 s (bound, held and tired ones
  are not free); a court left empty has the next within 8 s. In a hold they come on at 0.45 of their pace.

## 6 · What the moment asks for, and how it is said

`FIGHT.need` is ONE answer for the pulsing button and the voice, read from the court, never from a count of seconds.

**Grandmaster Pho played** (the first that holds): an act in hand: nothing · the cage lent and the gold ready:
SHOCKWAVE · the gold ready with something bound: switch to have the cage lent (or SHOCKWAVE, if nothing free is near
him) · a Prai at his student that the fire can chase: FIRE · a slipping one at him, while a bubble is free: switch ·
one under him: switch · the first gold, something bound: switch · a bound one (not in a hold): FIRE, walking into
reach · a tired one (not in a hold): BAG, walking into reach · one the fire can chase at the bars, the cage under 35:
FIRE · the first gold, nothing bound: switch · a Prai at the bars: switch (with three bubbles already: FIRE, to chase
it off) · one the smell showed, a bubble free: switch · the gold ready and worth it: switch, or SHOCKWAVE · one that
slips (a bubble free), one that hides, one on its way (a bubble free): switch. A bound one is left bound in a hold
(the gold's to gather: fire would tire it, and a tired one rises again).

**Master Zavier played** (each BIND here asks for a bubble free and his BIND ready within 1.5 s, each SMELL for his
smell ready within 1.5 s): the cage lent and the gold ready: switch · the gold ready with something bound: LEND · a
Prai at him: BIND · a Prai at him that the fire can chase, his BIND far off: switch · a slipping one at him: BIND, as
soon as it is back · one under him: SMELL · one at his bars: BIND · one the smell showed: BIND before it sinks · the
first gold: BIND one, or LEND · one hidden: SMELL · one that slips: BIND · a bound one (not in a hold): switch · a tired
one whose ring is half closed: BIND it (keep it for the bag); any tired one (not in a hold): switch · the gold ready
and worth it: LEND · one on its way: BIND. With three bubbles already, no BIND is asked for: what frees one is his
teacher's fire and bag (in a hold, the gold).

**The voice** (the plan's 7.1, kept, with one rule added): Grandmaster Pho is the one voice that instructs, whoever is
played. One voice at a time; a line is never cut. Each need has three wordings for whoever is played: the first, other
words, and a short one said from then on, every 12 s while the need stands. A need that **costs him while it stands**
(something at him, under him, at his bars, the cage nearly gone, the gold to loose under the lent cage; in a hold, the
gold's own call) is said 0.5 s after it stands, and **while one stands nothing else begins** (a long lesson line begun
in such a moment had left the next order unsaid). Other needs wait 1 s, and 4 s after the line before. A ONCE line
(a lesson, praise, the colour of the court) waits for its moment (a lesson or praise 10 s, the holds' two 30 s, the
colour 4 s, a few their own) and is dropped if its moment passes. No line names an input; every line is also a caption above the thumbs.

## 8 · What is at stake, and losing

- **His sanity**, the game's own stat and its own bar: each Prai at him takes 2 a second after its 2 s of gathering,
  through the engine's evCut (a worn piece's guard counts here: v14.13). Nothing else costs it in the fight but the
  surge, which presses it down to 13 and gives it all back when the Naga has laid the three down; nothing else gives
  it back.
- **At 12 the fight is lost**: the voice stops, his teacher says "Enough. Breathe. We begin again.", and the card says
  "The cleansing was broken." with BEGIN AGAIN. **The fight begins again** (R14), at stage 3 with eight left: the
  lesson is not sat through twice; his sanity is what he opened the fight with; every clock, wait, place, bubble, ring,
  cloud, fireball, hold and the voice's counts are put back, and the eight are made new. What he has learned stays
  learned: a button once shown stays shown, and the first gold is not taught twice. The chapter is never restarted.
- **After three losses** the card carries "Let Grandmaster Pho lead": then only one Prai is free at a time. Said on the
  card, never done silently (v8.1).

## 9 · The requirements, and what the simulation measured

`tools/probes/fightsim.mjs` plays the rules' own file on a fake clock (v21.2), a tenth of a second a step (the fuzz
player on random frames of 4 to 250 ms), 10,000 seeded fights for each player (v6.15). A loss is followed by BEGIN
AGAIN, up to six tries a campaign (after three, a player that would asks Grandmaster Pho to lead). After every step it
checks the fight's invariants (the plan's 14.1): the count never rises, never falls by more than one a step, and is
never under four in the fight; no number is not a number; no more than three at the bars and no more than three
bubbles; no Prai outside the court or nearer the doorway than the thread; Grandmaster Pho never inside the cage and
neither body outside the court; every wait inside its range; no switch inside the lock; one act a step; the surge
never under its floor; sanity never above what he opened with, and never at the loss without a loss; only the stage
moves the rules allow. It reads every move a Prai makes against the state list (§4), the longest stand in each state
against its bound, and every line the rules can say against the script and §10. The same seed gives the same end,
bit for bit. It exits 1 if a requirement fails. `--n`, `--players`, `--sanity`, `--trace <player> --seed <n>

## 13 · What I am unsure of

1. **The length.** A careful first-timer's fight runs a median of 3:31 from START to the last bag (the brief's reading
   not counted), against "about four minutes". A real first-timer on a phone is likely slower than the simulated one
   (it has no camera to learn, no thumb to move and no wrong buttons beyond 4%). The lever is the holds' 40 s: in the
   sweeps of 300 fights a player, 36 s gave 3:20 and 44 s gave 3:38, with the careful player's first-try wins near 86%
   at both and within two tries 98 to 99%; 40 s was kept because a longer hold is more of the same binding again, not
   more fight.
2. **The simulated careful player is a model**, not a person: it hears each new instruction to its end and then acts,
   or reads the pulsing button after about 4.5 s; each campaign draws its own pace (0.75 to 1.35 times). Before the
   model drew its own pace, a three-second change in one number moved its first-try wins from 94% to 30%: one person's
   fight measured 10,000 times. The model is no substitute for the plan's phone play-through (§14).
3. **The last hold is where careful players lose** (1,099 of their 1,187 first losses in 10,000 fall there): the bag
   shut for 40 s, bubbles kept up by binding again as they end, a hiding Prai coming up under him, and then the lend
   that lets what was at his bars through. That is the climax by design, and within two tries they win; whether it reads as tension
   or as chores is a question for a real hand.
4. **A button masher** won a quarter of first tries; at Chad's R21 a smell opened for nothing now costs sanity, and the
   short run of §9 has the masher at 2.3 % (4.3 % within two). The careful player stands at 79 % first try on the voiced
   lines (97.3 % within two), under the 85 % asked: not tuned further, at R22.
5. **The words of 45 new and 17 changed lines** are drafts (§10): Grandmaster Pho's lines go to Chad before they are
   voiced (v26.0), and "Zavier" in his mouth is the plan's §16 question.
6. **A once-line queued in a crowded moment and dropped is not said on a retry** (the fight remembers it as said). The
   holds' lines wait 30 s for a gap, and the objective line and the BAG's face say the same, but a first-timer who
   lost the moment may not hear them at all.
7. **The look and sound of each act are words here.** The violet of his cage and bubbles, the ruby of the fire and the
   gold of the shockwave are the plan's colours (§11 of the plan); nothing is drawn yet.
8. **The part has no `module.exports`.** A guarded one was asked for, but the name `module`, even behind
   `typeof module`, fails the build's own undefined-name scan (tools/probes/undefscan.mjs, which `npm run build` runs
   over every chapter: tried on a scratch file). Node loads the part exactly as it ships, through `vm.runInNewContext`
   (fightsim's `loadRules`), so nothing is added for Node's sake.
