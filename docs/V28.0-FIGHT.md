## 10 · The lines: what the redesign adds, changes and drops against the FIGHT-DRAFT rows of masters/v28.0/script/lines.json

How a line is chosen (§6): each NEED has three wordings for whoever is played: the first, other words, and a short one
said from then on, every 12 s while the need stands, never over another voice. A need that costs him while it stands
(something at him, under him, at his cage, or the cage lent) is said at once and is short, so that the words always
come before the cost. A ONCE line is said at its moment, or dropped if its moment has passed. Every line is also a
caption above the thumbs (the plan's 7.1), so a muted game is told the same. No line names an input. No line says
"Pho" (so none needs `say: Puu`); his student is "Zavier" (the plan's 16, 8: his to rule before a line is voiced).

`keep` is a row whose words do not change (its `where` is said again here where its moment moved). `change` is new
words under the old key. `add` is a new row. `drop` is a row the fight no longer says. The WORDS helper applies this
table to lines.json; until then fightsim reads the words of `add` and `change` rows from here, to time them.
