The reference photographs, read: **the ferry cabin** — rows of blue/teal
high-back seats four across with a centre aisle, a white ribbed ceiling with
a long light box down the middle, chrome poles, a continuous band of side
windows blown out white by the sea light, standing passengers in the aisle.
**The jetty** — a covered walkway, green steel trusses, WELCOME TO PULAU
TEKONG in black capitals across the beam, people walking away from camera
between railings. **The parade square** — a four-storey cream-and-orange
block (SAF BMTC crest on the stair tower), a squad marching across the
foreground on a pale square. **The bunk** — cream walls, big black-framed
windows with trees behind, grey steel lockers standing BETWEEN the beds
along the wall, single beds with white sheets, a long table with stacked
chairs down the middle of the room, two ceiling fans, paired tube lights,
a tiled floor.
