# v4.7 · The bought models — how 31 MB became 4, and where each one went

Chad uploaded five Sketchfab models on 31 Aug 2026 with exact instructions:
the rigged woman is the MOTHER in every scene that shows her; the plastic
chair replaces ALL the chairs at the medium event; the three low-poly cars
replace the box cars there; the Guan Gong scan replaces the main altar
statue; the sitting encik replaces the seated audience. All five are
credited in the credits panel — the links are in `shell.html` and the sheet.

## The shrink pipeline

`gltf-transform` (Node API + sharp), script preserved in the session
scratchpad and re-runnable from the originals. Per model: strip every
texture slot except base colour (and ZERO `emissiveFactor` when stripping an
emissive texture, or the whole material glows white), drop TANGENT and spare
UV sets, dedup/weld, meshopt-simplify, resample the animation where one is
kept, base-colour texture to JPEG at 512–1024.

| guangong.glb | 6.2 MB | 1.33 MB | 35k tris |

Total download added: ch2 +1.38 MB, ch3 +2.6 MB — each chapter pays only for
its own, same as the sound packs. The chair's red is `baseColorFactor`, not
a texture — its two PNGs were normal+ORM detail maps, which this game's flat
look never reads, so 2 MB became 20 KB with nothing visible lost.

encik arrives as UNINDEXED soup (30k unique verts for 10k tris), so exact
weld finds nothing and simplify cannot collapse; drop NORMAL first, weld,
simplify, regrow normals.

## How each lands in the game

All five load like the hdb block always has: async through `assetBytes` +
`GLTFLoader.parse` + `rescueTextures`, with the primitives they replace kept
in `world` but turned invisible — the fallback is instant, a failed download
costs a nicer prop and never the chapter, and dispose()'s one traverse still
frees everything (leaktest's contract). Sizing is never trusted from the
file: every model is measured and scaled to the real size of the thing it
is, and orientation fixes are baked into GEOMETRY so placement code keeps
thinking in position + yaw.

- **The chairs** are ONE more InstancedMesh driven by the same
  `placeChair()` — the odd chair, the film's empty chair and `turnChair`
  work unchanged. `chairIMs` is the list every needsUpdate/dispose loop
  iterates, so the fourth member joined without touching them.
- **The audience** is the encik's sitting pose BAKED into one plain
  geometry (`applyBoneTransform` per vertex, skeleton posed 0.4 s into the
  clip), then ~25 tinted meshes share it — no skeletons at runtime, fewer
  draw calls than the old six-box figures. Half are mirrored (scale.x −1;
  the material is double-sided in the file) because flipped asymmetry does
  more against thirty-of-one-man than any tint. The seating plan (TAKEN),
  per-person phase, `userData.head` sway and crowdLife freeze carry over
  exactly; standers, auntie, priest and the MEDIUM stay primitives (only
  the seated audience was asked for).
- **Guan Gong** stands on a red-cloth plinth at the effigy's exact spot —
  the altar table is 0.94 m of occlusion and a floor-standing 2.05 m statue
  reads as furniture from every seat; 0.72 m of plinth puts him above the
  tabletop the way deity statues actually stand at these events.
- **The cars** are re-centred per mesh onto their own origins, scaled to a
  sedan's 4.35 m, and parked at the two box-car spots plus two more, mixed
  nose-in/nose-out. Outside `bounds`, not blockers, same as the boxes.

## THE MOTHER, and the trap that cost the evening

She is the same `mother` GROUP contract as v4.6 — scenes, snap/restore/
reset and the walk/sway tracks drive the group and never knew the body
changed. The GLB is a child; the primitives are invisible siblings. Her
mixer ticks in `updateNotes` only while she is visible.

Three facts about the file, hard-won:

1. **Its three meshes are PARTS (body, dress, hair), not variants.** Hiding
   any one of them renders a woman missing that part — the dress alone is
   crumpled floating fabric. Ship all three.
2. **The idle clip's first ~1.5 s is a LEAD-IN** easing out of the bind
   pose with the root at a DIFFERENT OFFSET. Play the clip whole and she
   starts grounded, rides 0.8 m into the air when the real idle begins, and
   drops back every loop. The loop is cut with
   `THREE.AnimationUtils.subclip` (30 fps, keep 1.6 s → 8.2 s) so the
   lead-in never plays.
3. **Ground and size her from BONES IN THE POSED SKELETON**, never the bind
   box — the bind stands 0.8 m below where the idle stands. Feet = the
   lowest Toe/Foot bone; height = HeadTop_End to 1.56 m. This is the arms
   rig's lesson (CLAUDE.md v3.8) striking a second time, now written twice.

Diagnosis pattern that cracked it, for next time a rig misbehaves: sample
BONE world positions (head/foot/hips) at several scene times — bones tell
you floating-vs-giant-vs-crumpled in three numbers where screenshots
argue; and toggle each mesh visible alone to learn what the meshes ARE
before concluding what is broken.
