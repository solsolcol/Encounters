## The list (one line each, for ticking off)

1. **The merit film, the water to the tree:** the emotional music louder from the pour onto the roots; the glowing
   orb slightly more prominent as it flies up into the tree and the sky; the shot held 2–3 s longer on the climb so the
   music can swell. ("small extra refinements … this scene now is already very good" — change nothing else.)
2. **The merit film, the monk:** a flickering, glitchy texture behind the monk — find it (z-fighting is the usual
   cause in this game, v19.3/v22.2's coplanar law) and fix it without affecting anything else.
3. **World H, the Hanuman:** when it appears, the picture moves slightly with it — a small shake or drift so the
   figure seems to move along with his eyes, as if trying to get into him.
4. **World T, the Thao Wessuwan:** the floor's shake stronger, and the stomps louder.
5. **Ending A:** the man seen in the window's reflection becomes the female ghost (World Y's own model).
6. **Ending B:** the shop's brown paper bag carries no text — a plain brown paper bag.
7. **Ending D:** the owner's card with the temple's and the monk's details slides UNDER the tray of amulets — it must
   lie on top of / clear of the tray.

   player's look still owns the base), and the figure's follow tightened under it, so it moves WITH his eyes and the shop

   it would float over the amulets).
