## The design

### M1 · the lorong's buses (v21.17)

What is there: ONE night bus (`busG`, `busK(k)`), driven only by ending C's
timeline (x 50 → −80 in the far lane at z 11.45). Play never moves it, so a
player who does not choose C never sees a bus at all.

- **Passes in play.** A scheduler on the chapter's own clock (`dayClock`, wall
  time — the v7.4 law) sends a bus past while he is in play at the shrine
  (`arrive`, `wait`, `called`): the first a few seconds after he arrives, then
  every 24–40 s from a seeded stream (`busRand`, rewound by `reset()` — the
  v8.1 law), alternating the lanes: the near lane east (+x, z ≈ 5.6, close
  behind him, its doors on our kerb) and the far lane west (−x, z 11.45). One
  at a time; a pass caught by a scene, a card or the decision is ENDED on that
  frame (e2c3's far runs, v11.4) and the next is booked after it.
- **The buses are the night bus.** A clone of `busG`'s geometry and materials
  for the passes (`coachA.clone()`'s shape — no second set of canvases), its
  passengers seated after the clone (`seatPeople`: a clone carries the seats,
  not the people). Ending C keeps `busG` for itself.
- **Its own sound, following it.** A bus engine LOOP (`busnight`), driven every
  frame from where the bus is: the volume by distance (1/(1 + (d/r)²)², the
  street lamps' own falloff), quieter the deeper he is inside (the room's
  `outK`), and the PAN by its bearing on his screen. The engine can pan a loop
  (`loopPan`, her loops since v3.3) but a chapter cannot reach it: ONE small
  seam — a bed may carry a third value, its pan (`[name, vol, pan]`), which the
  ambient frame hands to `loopPan`. No other chapter declares one, so nothing
  else moves (episodes 1–2 unchanged by construction).
- **The sighting that is guaranteed: the bus that brought him.** At the
  arrival (a fresh one, not a Continue), the black lifts on the STREET: the
  night bus he came on is stopped at a stop on our kerb a few metres down, its
  doors closing (`busdoor`), and it pulls away (`busaway`) past him; the lens
  turns to the shrine over two seconds as the line comes, and play is his.
  It mirrors the film's opening (M3: off the bus at Golden Mile in the
  morning), and "That night... I went." is how he went. A bus stop pole on our
  kerb, so the stop is a place.
- Sounds: `busnight` (loop), `busaway`, `busdoor` — generated on the effects
  flow, picked by measurement (the phone band, a loop's swing and seam, clean
  ends), levelled; e3c3's pack.
