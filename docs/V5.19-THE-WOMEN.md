## The four jobs, and what each one actually needs

1. **yinn → the auntie at the paper table.** She replaces the primitive
   `auntie` in `ch3.js`, who speaks in scene C (`v3aunt1`, `v3aunt2`,
   `v3aunt3`, `v3aunt4`). No clips of her own, so `Talking.fbx` is
   retargeted onto her skeleton by the v5.02 bake. Her bone naming must be
   checked first — nine separate skins is Character Creator's shape, and
   the take will only bind if the names match.

2. **fearful_woman → one seated encik.** 349k triangles is fifty times
   what this crowd costs per person, so she takes the v5.05 recipe hard:
   drop every map `rescueTextures` cannot restore, weld, simplify to
   ~8k, 512 sheets. Her bones are Mixamo-named, so `Sitting_Talking.fbx`
   binds by name with no remapping.

3. **gracy_lee → the other seated encik.** Light already. **Her skeleton
   is ValveBiped**, so a Mixamo take will NOT bind to it by name — this
   one needs a name map (ValveBiped.Bip01_Spine → mixamorig:Spine and so
   on) or it silently does nothing, which is the v5.01/v5.02 failure
   family exactly.

4. **kana_talk → the standing woman who shouts.** She brings her own
   10.27 s talking clip and Mixamo bones; she needs no retarget at all.
   She takes a standing spot in the tent (the brazier-side stander that
   survives `LOW`), and the line she is the face of is `v3aunt5`.

## Seat and stand choices (to be settled by the v5.05 rule)

Both sitters must land on seats that are OCCUPIED in the scatter, EVEN
(so they survive `LOW`), and aisle-side in the middle rows where the lanes
run — the same three constraints `sitclap` and `sitangry` were placed by.
They must also not collide with those two.
