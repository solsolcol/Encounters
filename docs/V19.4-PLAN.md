## How it is verified (Chad, 16 Oct 2026)

> "If your probes are taking way too long, you need to be smart about it.
> Either avoid having to rely on too much probing or find other ways to verify
> things ... it makes zero sense to spend 45mins probing such simple changes."

- The probe box draws about one frame every 2.4 s, so a full play-through
  costs most of an hour. **Never play a chapter through to check a simple
  change.**
- Prefer, in this order: reading the code path; `node --check` and
  `chaptertest` (no browser, under a second); a probe that JUMPS straight to
  the state with `stage.applyPhase(...)`, `__enc.cine.seek(t)` or a direct
  call, takes one or two photos and exits; the harness that covers the seam.
- Photograph only what changed, on the phone crop (390×844 @1.6) first.
- One probe at a time, and each probe should run in under five minutes.
